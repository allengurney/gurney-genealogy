Evidence — West Barsham wardship: Edward Gournay a minor ward to c.1628 (WARD 7 / C 142)

The West Barsham heir was a royal ward through the later 1620s. Thomas Gurney III (Henry G15's eldest son, m. Martha Lewkenor of Denham) died in his father's lifetime, c.1621×1623 — Henry G15's will of 1 May 1621 still names 'my eldest sonne Thomas' among the living — and Henry himself died in 1623, so his grandson and heir Edward Gournay (b. 1608) succeeded as a minor: a ward of the Crown under his widowed mother Martha, coming of age about 1628. Edward held West Barsham to his death on 6 August 1641 (inquisition C 142/613/60). Earlier 'Gurney, Thomas: Norfolk' entries in the Court of Wards series (WARD 7/57/157, /80, 1616/17–1617/18) cannot be Thomas III's death inquisitions — he was living in 1621 — and are carried only as unresolved catalogue leads.
