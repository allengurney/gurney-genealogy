Evidence — The Great Berkhamsted father-John baptism run, 1597-1637, and the parish's Gurney burial record

The Hertfordshire baptism index records twelve children baptised at Great Berkhamsted to a father John Gurney between 1597 and 1637 - Hugh 1597, Anne 1599, Amye 1602, Bridgett 1606, Henrye 1610, Sara 1615, Jhon 1624, Richard 1626, Elizabeth 1629, Michaell 1631, Sarah 1634 and Francys/Francis 1636/7 - with the mother not indexed in any row. The run is forty years long, which is not one man. The parish's burial record, read across seven surname stems rather than one, holds sixteen Gurney-variant burials between 1579 and 1643, among them Jhon Gourney in 1612 and again in 1620; no John Gurney-variant burial occurs at Berkhamsted after 1620, and none anywhere in Hertfordshire between 1620 and 1690 except Jonathan Gurney at Bovingdon in 1682-3. That post-1620 absence is not interpretable as departure, because the record set states that many Hertfordshire registers are defective for 1643-1660 and the parish's Gurney burials stop in 1643 and resume only in 1711.
