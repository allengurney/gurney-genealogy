Evidence — Arrival window: 1636–early 1641

John Gurney arrived in New England between 1636 and early 1641, most likely in 1638–40. He was absent from the 1636 Weymouth property grants, received ordinary town land there later, and was living at Weymouth by 30 May / 2 June 1641.
