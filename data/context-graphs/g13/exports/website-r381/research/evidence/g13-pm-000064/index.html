Evidence — Newgate, master of the 1636 'John Gurney' apprentice, was of Horningsheath by Bury St Edmunds

John Newgate - the Boston feltmaker who in July 1636 brought a runaway apprentice named John Gurney before the governor - was himself of Horningsheath (Horringer), Suffolk, about three miles south-west of Bury St Edmunds, and lived at Hessett and Bury St Edmunds (then Southwark) before emigrating to Boston in 1633. An apprentice he carried was therefore most plausibly a young man from Newgate's own Suffolk neighbourhood around Bury.
