Evidence — Great Berkhamsted held four contemporaneous Gurney households, distinguished by the father's forename

Read whole rather than by child, the Great Berkhamsted baptism corpus carries four contemporaneous Gurney households in the emigration window, distinguished by the father's forename: John/Jhon (twelve children 1597-1637); Thomas (John 1588, Eliz 1591, Willm 1594, Thomas 1604, Nicolas 1607, Richarde 1635, Thomas 1638, Thomas 1640, mother Elizabeth); Edward/Edwarde (James 1603, Elisabeth 1606, Susanna 1611); and William/Willyam (Anna/Anne 1594, Willyam 1632). That governs how the parish's burials may be read: they cannot be assigned wholesale to the John line, and the Thomas household was actively baptising in 1638 and 1640. The parish attribution itself is settled - FindMyPast indexes the family at Great Berkhampstead and FamilySearch indexes the same children at St Peter, Hertfordshire, and St Peter's is Berkhamsted's own parish church, so the two labels name one place.
