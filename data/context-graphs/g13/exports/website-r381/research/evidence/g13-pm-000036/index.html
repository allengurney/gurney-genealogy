Evidence — Analysis: the open program reconciled to the leads catalogue

The open research program for John Gurney reduces to a small set of standing questions — his English origin and parentage, his first wife Mary's identity and English marriage, and the untested Y-DNA discriminator — after the colonial record was worked to exhaustion at the printed and indexed level. The concrete record-location residuals are already homed in their own topics: the emigrant-record residual (Coldham's Complete Book of Emigrants and TNA E 157/20) in g13-research-source-coverage, the primary page behind the May 1645 Braintree petition, and the post-death Mendon proprietary stream. The operational record-pulls that would test these questions are maintained in the research-leads catalogue, which is the canonical de-duplicated index; this research-state topic surfaces only the work-in-progress subset not yet assimilated into the library, grouped by the question it serves.
