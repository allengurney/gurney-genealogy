Evidence — Standing: C(i) eliminated on age at about 1 percent, C(ii) held at about 4 percent

The elder Berkhamsted John is eliminated on his own age - fathering from 1597 puts his birth about 1570-75, twenty-five to thirty years before the colonial John - and that ground is immune to the same-name masking problem that weakens eliminations built on continuing residence. He holds at about 1 percent. The younger is a documented man of the right age whose household is documented dying rather than leaving, and he holds at about 4 percent. For him: the head himself has no recorded burial, inside a register window known to be defective. Against him: no Mary Gurney is baptised in the parish at any date, no John Gurney marriage occurs anywhere in the county in the window, and a Richard Gurney was buried in the parish in June 1638 who may well be his own son.
