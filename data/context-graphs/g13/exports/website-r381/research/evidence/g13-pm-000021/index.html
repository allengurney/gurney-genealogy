Evidence — Negative: no record states John's English origin

No record of John Gurney in New England states his English origin, parentage, or pre-migration residence; the colony, town, court, and probate records that document his American life are uniformly silent on where he came from, and Anderson's Great Migration Directory, having reviewed that surviving set, records the origin as 'Unknown.' This delimits the coverage of the record, not the identity question itself: the English-origin identification (Banks's Bury St Edmunds lead; the Candidate-B reading making John a son of Francis Gurney the Merchant Taylor) is an open matter pursued in the dedicated origin/identity work and is neither resolved nor advocated here.
