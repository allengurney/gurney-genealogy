Evidence — Shared matching-criteria profile: tailor, wife Mary, three English-born children, birth c.1602–1608, gone from England by 1641

Any English candidate for the Braintree emigrant must satisfy one shared profile drawn from his colonial baseline: a tailor by trade; married to a wife named Mary; father in England of Mary, Richard and John Jr., with Sarah possible; born in England about 1602-1608, read off his own 1652/3 age deposition; and gone from the English record by the time he first appears in Massachusetts in June 1641. Peter and Isaac are excluded from the English child test — neither has a baptism anywhere in Britain and both were almost certainly born in New England — so a candidate household is tested against three named English children rather than five. A settled Puritan household is a soft fifth criterion. This whole profile, not a bare surname match, is the shared test applied to every alternative candidate.
