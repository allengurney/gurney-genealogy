Reception network is a weak lever for English origin

The reception network fixes the mode of John's settlement but not his English origin: his documented colonial associates and landlords span Buckinghamshire, the West Country, Essex, Warwickshire, Suffolk, and Norfolk with no single stream claiming him, so association is strong enough to show he entered an existing community yet too diffuse to name the corridor that carried him — the English-origin corridor question is therefore held to the identity work, not resolved from the migration record.
