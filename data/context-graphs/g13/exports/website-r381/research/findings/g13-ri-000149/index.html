Francis Gurney's failed King's Lynn worsted venture, 1622

On 17 October 1622 Francis Gurney, citizen and merchant-taylor of London, agreed with Ambrose Tompson and Martin Hill to teach the poor children of King's Lynn to spin worsted yarn and to employ the borough's poor in that industry. The venture failed, and Sir Hamon Lestrange had to pay Francis's £100 bond.
