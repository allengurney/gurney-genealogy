1652/3 'aged 50' deposition

In a Suffolk County deposition in Wilson v. Faxon, entered under Suffolk Court Files no. 188 and dated 17 (1st month) 1652/3 — 17 March 1652/3 old style — 'John Gurney of Brayntree' declared himself 'aged 50 Yeares or thereabouts'. It is his only surviving first-person statement of his own age.

Type: Evidence
