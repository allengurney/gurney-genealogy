Banks (1937) p.151 attributes John Gurney to Bury St Edmunds on 'Banks Mss.' alone

Charles Edward Banks's Topographical Dictionary of 2885 English Emigrants to New England, 1620-1650 (ed. Brownell, 1937), p. 151, enters 'GURNEY, John | Bury St. Edmunds | [no ship] | Braintree, Massachusetts | Banks Mss.' - attributing the Braintree emigrant's English origin to Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk, on the authority of Banks's unpublished working notes alone, with no ship, register, will, or indenture cited. The same page sets him amid a genuine Bury St Edmunds emigrant cohort (Newgate, Clement Chaplin, William Knapp, John Goodrich, Henry Bright, William Townsend, Theodore Atkinson, Peyton Cooke, Samuel Wright), most of them documented from primary records.
