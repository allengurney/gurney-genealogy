Was Ann Gurney (Gilman) John's sister?

Whether the Ann Gurney who married John Gilman at Hingham in 1626 was John Gurney's sister is unresolved. The Pease genealogy identifies her as a daughter of Francis Gurney by his first marriage - which, if correct, would make her John's sister - but no primary record links her to Francis, her Gilman children were baptised at West Dereham some twenty miles from Francis's East Dereham parish, and a separately recorded Ann Gurney married Peter Woodcocke at West Dereham in 1618/19 and may be a different woman. The kinship rests on a single compiled genealogy, not a record.

Type: Open question
