1663 probate inventory: itemized movable estate (£55 14s 6d)

The manuscript inventory of John Gurney Senr's goods and estate, Braintree, 16 March 1663 (taken by Gregory Belcher, Edmund Quincy, and Thomas Faxon), itemizes his movable estate line by line — apparel, boots and hat; bedding, blanket, pillows and cases; pewter, pincers, brass skillet and kettle; a cupboard and a musket; plough-irons, coulter, axes, mattock and chisel; a chair and frame goods, barrels and trays; wheat and Indian corn with grain-boxes, hoops and tubs; a horse and yoke of oxen; sows, pigs and young cattle; an old barrow, wheels and axletree; and one unvalued land line 'an estate layd out in land at Quinapaug wch we know not' — appraised in all at £55 14s 6d.

Type: Evidence
