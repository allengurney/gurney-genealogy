When the Jenner debt may have begun

The debt probably began during Jenner's Weymouth years rather than after he moved to Saco, but only by a modest margin. The current arrival range allows John and Jenner to overlap at Weymouth in 1638–40. By 1644, John was leaving Weymouth or had recently left for Braintree, so an older debt being settled then makes sense. But the account does not date the debt, explain Thomas Lake's part in it, or show that John filed the claim because he was moving.
