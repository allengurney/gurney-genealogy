The recorded child lists are not closed; the "eldest sonne" attestation does not exclude an older son

Francis Gurney's recorded child list is demonstrably incomplete, so the 1633 "eldest sonne" attestation does not exclude an older son of the first marriage. Daniel Gurney called Dorothy the eldest child "mentioned in" the St Benet Fink register, judged Roger likely to have died young, and added beyond the register a son John of Maldon whom Francis "seems" to have had and a George who was "probably another son." Bernau warned outright that no complete record of the children of Francis and Anne Browning survives. Neither compiler knew of the 1611 marriage, and a visitation recorded the children a man presented, not all who existed.
