Margaret's Rivett kin at Garveston and Gressenhall (two candidate baptisms), two to three miles from East Dereham

Margaret Rybett is identifiable as a Rivet of Garveston — either Margaret Rivet, baptised 21 May 1586, daughter of Francis Rivet, or Margarett Rivet, baptised 1 February 1577/8, daughter of Robert Rivet (working weights ~55/40) — a mid-Norfolk family, not the Suffolk gentry line. Garveston lies two miles from East Dereham and Gressenhall three, and the Rivett presence in both is continuous. This gives the long-standing hypothesis a documentary basis: John, left motherless about 1616–17 when Margaret died and Francis remarried and moved to London, had maternal kin a few miles from his birthplace. Richard Ryvett of Gressenhall, who married Faith in 1602, is a documented source for the name John gave his eldest surviving son — Margaret's first cousin under the 1586 reading, her full brother under the 1577/8 one.
