The head's missing burial falls inside a documented Hertfordshire register defect and is not evidence

No John Gurney-variant burial occurs at Great Berkhamsted after 1620, and none anywhere in Hertfordshire between 1620 and 1690 except Jonathan Gurney at Bovingdon in 1682-3. That absence cannot be read as departure. The record set's own description states that many Hertfordshire registers are 'sadly defective during the Civil War and Commonwealth period (1643-1660)', and the Berkhamsted Gurney burials stop in 1643 and do not resume until 1711 - the signature of that defect rather than of an empty parish. Coverage statement, so this is not re-run as a search: the burial set covers Berkhamsted across 1583-1660 and again from 1711, and any Berkhamsted burial negative for 1644-1660 falls inside a known defect and is not evidence. A head of household who died in that window would be invisible.
