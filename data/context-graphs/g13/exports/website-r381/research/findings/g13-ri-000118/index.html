No English John Gurney household outside the named candidates carries a Richard, John Jr., Peter, or Isaac

A search anchored on the colonial John's two rarest children's names -- Richard and Isaac -- across every English collection FamilySearch indexes returns exactly one Richard Gurney baptism with a father named John (Richard Gurnie, baptized 15 December 1626, St Peter, Hertfordshire -- Candidate C's own already-eliminated Richard) and zero Isaac Gurney baptisms with a father named John. No English John Gurney household anywhere, outside the candidates already named, carries a Richard, a John Jr., a Peter, or an Isaac in any combination. This is not treated as a definitive elimination on its own: it is equally consistent with the colonial John fathering his distinctive children entirely in Massachusetts after his June 1641 emigration, which is what the colonial record in fact shows.
