Anderson GMD p.158 - published coverage summary; origin 'Unknown'

Anderson's Great Migration Directory (2nd ed., 2025), p. 158, summarizes John Gurney's documented New England presence — arrival 1636, settlements Boston and Braintree — records his English origin as 'Unknown,' and rests the entry on six cited records (Winthrop's Journal 2:422; Massachusetts Bay Records 1:331; NEHGR 62:94; Suffolk Probate Case #338; History of Weymouth 3:251; The American Genealogist 10:70-73); it omits the Weymouth residential phase the manuscript town-land records attest.

Type: Evidence
