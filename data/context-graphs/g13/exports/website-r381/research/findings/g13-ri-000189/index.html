Ann Gurney x John Gilman, Hingham Norfolk 1626

Ann Gurney married John Gilman, a worsted weaver, at Hingham, Norfolk, on 1 October 1626; the couple's children were baptised at West Dereham and Hingham, Ann was buried at Hingham on 23 November 1651, and their son John Gilman Jr. later emigrated to Exeter, New Hampshire. West Dereham, in western Norfolk near Downham Market, lies about twenty miles from the East Dereham parish of Francis Gurney's children.
