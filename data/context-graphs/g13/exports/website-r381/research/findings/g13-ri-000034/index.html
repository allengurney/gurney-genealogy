Finding: estate wealth concentrated in livestock and the working farm

John Gurney's working wealth at death was in husbandry, not his named tailoring trade: livestock alone (horse £9 10s + yoke of oxen £12 10s + swine £6 + young cattle £6) accounted for about £34 of the £55 14s 6d estate, and with plough-gear, cart equipment, and stored wheat and Indian corn the farm economy comes to roughly three-quarters of the whole appraisal.
