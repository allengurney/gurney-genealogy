1636 Ackworth marriage of John Gurnoe to Mary Burton; 1637 baptism of John Thomas Gurnoe

John Gurnoe married Mary Burton at Ackworth, Yorkshire, on 6 June 1636. A son, John Thomas Gurnoe, was baptized at the same parish on 19 January 1637, seven months later; the baptism transcript does not index a mother's name. No burial of either spouse and no further child are indexed for this household in the present Findmypast coverage.
