John Gurnell (freeman 1643) is a distinct surname, confirmed by an 1675 Dorchester death entry

A separate 'John Gurnell,' admitted freeman in Massachusetts on 10 May 1643, is not John Gurney-1 under a different spelling. Surviving 1600s Massachusetts Gurnell records are thin enough to invite a Gurnell-to-Gurney misreading, but a 30 July 1675 Dorchester death entry, checked directly, reads 'Gurnell' plainly -- confirming Gurnell as its own surname. The colonial John is recorded under Gurney/Gurny/Garny variants throughout his New England career, never Gurnell.
