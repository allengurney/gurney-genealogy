Mendon town birth record: Samuel son of John & Ruth Gurney (14 March 1671)

The Mendon, Massachusetts town book records the birth of a son to the John Gurney household: "Samuel son of John & Ruth Gurney — born March 14, 1671," the primary record fixing the wife's name Ruth and adding a third child, Samuel, to the household alongside John and Mary.
