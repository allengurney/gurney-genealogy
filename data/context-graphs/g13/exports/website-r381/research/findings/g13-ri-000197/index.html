Is the 1693 Samuel Gurney John Jr. and Ruth's Mendon-born son?

Whether the Samuel Gurney who married Sarah Shapley at Boston in 1693 is John Jr. and Ruth's Mendon-born son (b. 14 March 1671) is unresolved. No record names either Samuel's father. The identification is chronologically clean - a Samuel born in 1671 would have been twenty-two in 1693, an ordinary marrying age - and no other Samuel Gurney appears anywhere in the surviving colonial-era record to compete for it, but age and the absence of a rival are not the same as a document naming the connection outright. The identification is probable, not proven.

Type: Open question
