Occupation: a tailor by trade, a husbandman by economy

John Gurney's trade was tailoring — he is styled 'John Gurney, tailor' in his own 12 February 1661/2 Braintree deed, and Sprague's compiled Braintree genealogy likewise enters him as a tailor — but the working economy of his last years was a husbandman's: his March 1663 estate inventory held no tool of the tailoring trade and its value lay in livestock, plough-gear, and grain. His occupation is best read as a trained trade overtaken in practice by mixed farming: a tailor by name, a husbandman by the measure of his goods.
