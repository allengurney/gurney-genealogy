The 28 June 1638 Richard Gurney burial: three readings, and only the register image discriminates

A Richard Gurney was buried at Great Berkhamsted on 28 June 1638, and which Richard he is decides whether the younger John survives as a candidate at all. Three readings are open. He may be the Richard baptised there 15 December 1626 to a father John - in which case the candidate's best name-match child died in England at eleven while the colonial Richard Gurney of Weymouth was alive and a freeman into the 1680s, and the candidate collapses outright. He may be Richarde Gurney baptised 1635 to the neighbouring Thomas household, a three-year-old whose death in a mortality year is equally consistent with the entry. Or he may be an adult Richard of the parish never baptised there, which is the weakest reading, since no Richard Gurney baptism other than 1626 and 1635 appears in the whole run. The index cannot discriminate: the burial transcript carries name, date and place only, with no age and no parent. The register image normally gives 'son of', and it is the discriminator.
