Record coverage: a continuous 1641-1663 civic/land/court/probate footprint

John Gurney's surviving documentary footprint is broad within four record classes — colony court and account records, town land books (Weymouth, Braintree, Billerica, Mendon), a registry deed, a court deposition, and probate (as witness/appraiser and as decedent) — and runs continuously from his earliest secure record (the 2 June 1641 Weymouth gunpowder-fine remission) to his 16 March 1663 estate inventory.
