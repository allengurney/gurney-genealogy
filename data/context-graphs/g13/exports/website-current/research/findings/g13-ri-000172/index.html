Candidate C (Berkhamsted) eliminated on age, child set, and child chronology

Candidate C, the Berkhamsted John Gurney, is eliminated on three independent grounds. Henry's 1610 baptism puts the father's own birth no later than about 1585-1590, thirteen to eighteen years before the colonial John's c.1603. Three of the eight Berkhamsted children - Henry, Michael, and Francis - have no counterpart in the colonial family, and the colonial John's Mary and Peter have no counterpart at Berkhamsted. The three shared names miss on date: Berkhamsted's John Jr. was baptised in 1624 against the colonial John Jr.'s c.1633, Richard in 1626 against c.1630, Sarah in 1634 against c.1628. Any one ground would suffice. Candidate C is the last of the principal named alternatives to be cleared.
