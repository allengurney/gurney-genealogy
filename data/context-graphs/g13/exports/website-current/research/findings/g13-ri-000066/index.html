Torrey p. 331 cross-check of the John1 family group

Torrey's New England Marriages Prior to 1700 (p. 331) gives a one-page cross-check of the John1 Gurney family group, corroborating John's dates (born c. 1603, died 1663) and preserving John Jr.'s Mendon household (wife Ruth; children John, Samuel, Mary).
