Eleven Gurney households in the London hearth-tax returns, 1662-1666

The London hearth-tax returns of 1662-1666 record eleven strict Gurney or Gurny household entries across the City of London, Westminster, and inner Middlesex. Among them: Walter Gurney at Greene Dragon Court in St Margaret's Westminster, 1 hearth, 1664; William Gurny on the south side of Fleet Street in St Bride's, 7 hearths, 1666, returned empty; William Gurney at Cock and Key Alley in St Dunstan in the West, 3 hearths, 1666; and Ann Gurney at Two Crane Court in the same parish, 5 hearths, 1666. All post-date the colonial John's death; none is a candidate for him.

Type: Evidence
