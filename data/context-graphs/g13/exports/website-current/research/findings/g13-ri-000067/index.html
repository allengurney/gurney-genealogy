Isaac (c. 1643) most plausibly John-1's son, by elimination (moderate)

The young Isacke Gurney of the 1663/4–1667 colonial court records is most plausibly John Gurney-1's son: no record states his parentage, but an Isaac born c. 1643 needs a father of John's generation, and the only other regional Gurney male, Richard (G12, b. c. 1630–34), was himself a child in 1643 — a moderate identification the dedicated subject file still holds cautiously as a distinct person.
