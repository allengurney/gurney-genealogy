No John among the seven St Benet Fink children of Francis and Anne Browning, 1619–1637

No John appears among the seven children baptised to Francis Gurney and his second wife Anne at St Peter le Poer with St Benet Fink, London, 1619–1637: Dorithy (2 March 1619), Roger (20 December 1621), Frances (18 January 1625), Francis (13 November 1628), Lucretia (28 October 1630), Thomas (19 April 1636), and Margaret (28 July 1637). The silence supports rather than undercuts Candidate B: if John was Francis's son, he must belong to the first marriage, which is where the East Dereham entry places him.
