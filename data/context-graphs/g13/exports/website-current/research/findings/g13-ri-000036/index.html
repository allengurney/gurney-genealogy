Negative: no tailoring tools or cloth in the estate

The 1663 estate inventory contains no tool or stock of the tailoring trade — no shears, needles, thimble, pressing- or goose-iron, yard-measure, or cloth/fabric — despite John being styled 'John Gurney, tailor' in his 1661/2 Monatiquot conveyance. The absence does not unsay the trade (the deed is direct evidence) but qualifies its centrality to his late-life economy, whose appraised value sat in land-rights and livestock; the 'pincers' listed beside the pewter are a general household/smith's tool, not a tailor's implement.
