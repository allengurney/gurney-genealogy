Anderson GMD (2025): arrival 1636, Boston/Braintree, origin Unknown

Robert Charles Anderson's Great Migration Directory (2nd ed., 2025), p. 158, records John Gurney with an arrival of 1636 and New England residences at Boston and Braintree (Weymouth not listed), while leaving his English origin 'Unknown' — the standing published assessment of John's arrival and settlement.
