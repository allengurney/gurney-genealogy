Candidate B: John was probably the son of Francis Gurney and Margaret Rybett

John Gurney of Braintree was probably the son of Francis Gurney, a Merchant Taylor of West Barsham, Norwich and London, by his first wife Margaret Rybett, whom Francis married at Norwich on 23 September 1611. The identification rests on two primary records — that marriage and a degraded East Dereham baptism entry read as "John the sonne of ffrancis Gurnie" — supported by a father-to-son trade, an emigration corridor, a documented financial motive, and a Gurney kindred settled within five miles of the register. No record names John as Francis's son; the case is cumulative and indirect, and four offsets hold it below certainty.

Type: Identity hypothesis
