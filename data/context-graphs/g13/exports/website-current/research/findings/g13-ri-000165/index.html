The parentage claim attaches to Francis the Merchant Taylor, not to the two same-named contemporaries

The Candidate B parentage attaches specifically to Francis Gurney the West Barsham gentleman and Merchant Taylor, and is protected against two same-named contemporaries — the Norwich laceweaver of St Peter Mountergate and Parmentergate, and a St Giles Cripplegate joiner. Keeping the three apart is what stops the argument from absorbing the wrong household's records, and it is the reason the Costessey property and the 1640 Cripplegate infant John belong to neither the emigrant nor his father.
