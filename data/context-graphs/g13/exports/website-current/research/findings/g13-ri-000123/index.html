Candidate D eliminated: 1662 London residence incompatible with the colonial John's 1662/3 death

Candidate D -- John Gurney, son and executor of Robert Gurney of Old Change -- remained visible in London records through the 1662 hearth tax, while the colonial John Gurney of Braintree had already lived a documented Massachusetts life and died at Boston about March 1662/3. That continuing London tax presence is incompatible with the Braintree emigrant's colonial residence and death; Candidate D and the colonial emigrant are not the same person, and Candidate D is eliminated.
