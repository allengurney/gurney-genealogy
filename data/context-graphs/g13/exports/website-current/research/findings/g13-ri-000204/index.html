Why the arrival range begins in 1636

John was not among the men receiving Weymouth property grants in 1636. Earlier Massachusetts arrivals are more likely to leave records, and the early population was small. The well-preserved Boston records also contain no John Gurney of his generation. Taken together, those facts make an arrival before 1636 extremely unlikely. They support 1636 as the beginning of the plausible range and 1638–40 as the best estimate.
