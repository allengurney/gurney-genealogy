Negative: First Church Boston (CSM 39) records no Gurney, 1630-1778

The Records of the First Church in Boston (CSM Publications 39) - the town's only church of the 1630s-1640s, its admissions, votes, and baptisms running 1630-1778 - contain no Gurney of any spelling, though John Newgate, the feltmaker in whose Boston household the 1636 apprentice John Gurney lived as a servant, was himself a member. The last Boston-side record class thus closes against Anderson's 'Boston' settlement attribution for the elder John.
