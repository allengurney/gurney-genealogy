Isacke Gurney court cluster: Scituate 1663/4; Suffolk file 792, 1667

A young Isacke Gurney appears in a tight cluster of colonial court records — whipped for pilfering at Scituate (Plymouth court, 1 March 1663/4) and jailed for vagrancy and burglary at Medford, committed to the Boston prison under Daniel Gookin's warrant (Suffolk County court file no. 792, June 1667); neither record states his parentage.
