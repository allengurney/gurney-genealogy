Finding: Quinapaug line = unrecorded Providence-jurisdiction frontier share

The 1663 inventory's 'estate layd out in land at Quinapaug wch we know not' is best read as an unrecorded right or share in the Providence-jurisdiction Quinapaug land speculation (Foote to Hudson to Boston men) - an unvalued frontier proprietary interest, not appraised Braintree real estate.
