MBCR 2:79 - 1644 £5 'Mr Ginner' bill (Gurny + Lake)

The Massachusetts Bay colony accounts for the General Court session beginning 30 October 1644 record: 'John Gurny his byl for 3. 16. 4d & Tho: Lake his note for 1. 3. 8d are accepted for £5 fro Mr Ginner.'
