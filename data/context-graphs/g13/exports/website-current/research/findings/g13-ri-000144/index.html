Negative: no true Gurney household at colonial Dorchester

No independent Gurney household existed at colonial Dorchester. Both the 1664 "Jane Gurnet" legacy and the 1676/7 "Goodman Gurney of Dorchester" surety resolve to the Gornell/Gurnell family under ordinary clerk-level spelling drift, confirmed across three independent Dorchester record streams; no Dorchester "Gurney" record should be read as evidence of John Gurney-1's family.
