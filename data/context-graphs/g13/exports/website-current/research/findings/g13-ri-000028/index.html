Suffolk Deeds IV - Quinapaug (Providence-jurisdiction) tract, no Gurney in the recorded deeds

Suffolk Deeds Liber IV records the Quinapaug tract: Joshua Foote bought 4,000 acres 'att Quinapaug within ye Jurisdiction of Providence' from the Indians Sacont and George Sacount by deed of 18 June 1655; by 1660 it passed to William Hudson, who sold it in 500-acre parcels to Boston men. No Gurney appears among the recorded Quinapaug parcels.

Type: Evidence
