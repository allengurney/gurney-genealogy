1633 Heralds' Visitation: Francis attests Roger as his "eldest sonne"

In the 1633 Heralds' Visitation of London, Francis Gurnay attested his own pedigree and children and named Roger as his "eldest sonne." Roger was baptised at St Benet Fink in December 1621. Taken as written, the attestation leaves no room for an older son, and it is the sharpest documentary objection to the Candidate B parentage.

Type: Evidence
