Sixteen Gurney-variant burials at Great Berkhamsted, 1579-1643, five of them in eighteen months

Great Berkhamsted's burial register survives, is transcribed, and holds sixteen Gurney-variant burials between 1579 and 1643: Jhone Gernnye 1579; Isabell Gourney 1601; Henry Gourney 1607; Jhon Gourney 1612; Jhon Gourney 1620; Thomas Gorne 1620; Joane Gurney 7 November 1637; Richard Gurney 28 June 1638; William Gurney 1 October 1638; Elizabeth Gurney 21 February 1638/9; Francis Gurney 9 March 1638/9; Thomas Gurney 1639 (two rows); Margaret Gurney 1643; and Thomas Gurney 1643. Five fall between November 1637 and March 1638/9. Two of those five - Elizabeth, baptised 2 August 1629, and Francis, baptised 1 March 1636/7 - match to the day the death dates independently recorded for two children of the younger John Gurney, on two providers and two record classes. The parish is distinct from Little Berkhamstead twenty miles east, which the same place keyword also matches and which carries no Gurney entry in this window.
