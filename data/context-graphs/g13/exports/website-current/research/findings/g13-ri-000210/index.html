The pre-existing Gurney household at East Dereham is the most economical explanation yet found for why Francis Gurney settled his first family there rather than near the Great Ellingham/West Barsham seats, and it removes any residual oddity in a Gurney child being baptised in that parish. It supplies context, not kinship: whether Margery's household connects to Francis's line is untested (her gated will is the test), and the complete 1585-1650 Norfolk probate census shows hers to be the only unplaced in-window Gurney probate - the whole parish-level probate signal.
