Evidence — C(ii)'s household stopped because his children were dying, not because he left

The younger Berkhamsted John was opened as a candidate on a departure-shaped gap in the record, and that gap does not exist. His household's last baptism is Francis in 1636/7; within the following two years the parish buried Joane (November 1637), Richard (June 1638), William (October 1638), Elizabeth (February 1638/9) and Francis (March 1638/9), and two of those are demonstrably his own children. A household that loses four or five members in eighteen months and then stops appearing in the baptism register is displaying mortality, not departure. The finding matters beyond this candidate: a departure-shaped gap is only evidence where the burial series for the same parish and window has been read and shown to be covered.
