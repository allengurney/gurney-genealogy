Evidence — Admitted Weymouth inhabitant (c. 1638/9-1642/4)

John Gurney was an admitted, resident inhabitant of Weymouth c. 1638/9-c. 1642/4: early-Massachusetts town land divisions went only to admitted inhabitants, so his three original East/Mill-Field grants mark him as a recognised member of the Weymouth community before his removal to Braintree.
