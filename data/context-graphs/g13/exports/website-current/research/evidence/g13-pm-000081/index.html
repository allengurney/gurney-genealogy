Evidence — Fourteen further Norfolk John-Gurney baptisms across six parishes, 1623-1643, none matching the full child set

A Findmypast UK Parish Baptisms search restricted to Norfolk, surname Gurney, father John, 1623-1643, returns fourteen further baptisms across six parishes: Denton (John + Rachell, children Mary 1638, Thomas 1639, Sarah 1644), Hempnall (Anna 1640, Elizabeth 1641), Stanfield (Mary 1634), Norwich St Margaret & St Swithin (a 1630 child), North Runcton (Beniamin 1625, Frances 1628), and Earsham (John + Elizabeth Singler). None of these six households reproduces the colonial John's full, distinctive child set -- Sarah, Mary, Richard, John Jr., and Peter -- by name and date, but the density is consistent with Norfolk having been a Gurney heartland with several parish-level Johns active in the same decade.
