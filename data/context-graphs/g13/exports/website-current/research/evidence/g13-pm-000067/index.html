Evidence — American tradition: John Gurney born 29 Sept 1615, Southwark/"Brent" origin

Three American genealogical sources give John Gurney a birth of 29 September 1615 in England: American Biography (1926), locating him "probably from Southwark, near London Bridge"; the Lysander Franklin Gurney sketch (1912, an earlier printing of the same tradition, framing John as John Newgate's apprentice); and a Find a Grave memorial (no. 252975617), locating the birth in the anachronistic modern place name "London Borough of Brent, Greater London" (Brent was created in 1965 and cannot describe a seventeenth-century parish). All three repeat the identical date across compilations a century apart.
