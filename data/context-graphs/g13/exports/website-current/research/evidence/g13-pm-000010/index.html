Evidence — Finding: Billerica proprietorship (house-lot surrendered; Dudley-Farm share + meadow lots persist); never removed there

John Gurney was admitted a proprietor of the new town of Billerica in 1659 - a standing carrying a personal ten-acre house-lot (granted 15 August 1659 on condition he inhabit it, his son Richard accepted as fellow inhabitant), a rate-paying share in the communal Dudley Farm purchase, and two great-meadow lots. He surrendered the house-lot within four months, handed back through his son-in-law Daniel Shed, and never removed to Billerica; the meadow and upland allocations appear in the records independently of the surrendered house-lot, but no Billerica parcel is shown passing to or occupied by a Gurney.
