Evidence — Boston silence: every surviving Boston-era record class holds no John Gurney

Anderson's Great Migration Directory assigns John Gurney a Boston settlement beside Braintree, but no Boston record of any class names him. An exhaustive sweep of every surviving Boston-era record class - First Church admissions and baptisms, the Book of Possessions and Boston town records, the Aspinwall notarial register, Lechford's note-book, Suffolk Deeds Libers I-III, and the Boston vital records 1630-1699 - returns no Gurney of John's generation, and a manuscript-level full-text search adds none. This converts the 'Boston' attribution from an inference-level qualification into a record-level negative: John's documented life belongs wholly to his four towns, and the attribution is best read as a conflation with the 1636 Newgate apprentice, a Boston household servant who would have generated neither a church admission nor a property record.
