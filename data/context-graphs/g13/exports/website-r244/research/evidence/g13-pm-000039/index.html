Evidence — First wife Mary (maiden name unknown), m. England by mid-1620s, d. Braintree 20 Sep 1661

John Gurney's first wife bore the name Mary in the compiled Braintree genealogy (Sprague, p. 695); the primary Braintree town record and Torrey both leave her unnamed - the death record enters only 'the wife of John' - and the 'Mary Richards' of later derivative trees is untraced to any primary record. He married her in England by the mid-1620s, bounded by their daughter Mary Gurney's 1647 Braintree marriage to Daniel Shed (baptized Finchingfield, Essex, 1620), which places the daughter's birth by c. 1628. By every indication she was the mother of his children, though no birth record survives to establish the maternity of any of them. She died at Braintree on 20 September 1661.
