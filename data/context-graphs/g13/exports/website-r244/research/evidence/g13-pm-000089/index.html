Evidence — Which John died at Mendon in 1675: G13's son, not Richard G12's son

The 1675 Mendon casualty is John Gurney Jr., son of the emigrant John Gurney-1 (G13), not the identically-named eldest son of his brother Richard Gurney (G12). Richard's own son John was still living on 27 October 1691, when he declined to administer his father's estate — a record that rules him out as the 1675 casualty and resolves a confusion that had attached the Mendon death to the wrong generation.
