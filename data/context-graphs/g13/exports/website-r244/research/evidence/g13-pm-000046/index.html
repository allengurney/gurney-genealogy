Evidence — No John Gurney in the Norwich freemen or apprentice registers

The Norwich city freemen register (Millican, 1548-1713) and the enrolled Norwich apprentice-indenture index (Rising & Millican, NRS 29) record no John Gurney freedom admission and no enrolled apprenticeship in the colonial-emigrant window, c.1615-1660. The registers' Gurney content is otherwise Elizabethan (a John Gurney freed not by apprenticeship in 1560/61; a John Gurney cordwainer dead by 1580) plus a single seventeenth-century entry - James Gurney, son of Francis of St Peter Parmentergate, bound at Michaelmas 1627 for seven years and admitted freeman under Henry Bond in January 1648 - which is a Norwich-side record of a Francis Gurney household, not the emigrant. The East Anglian formal trade-transmission pathway thus carries no record of John's training.
