Evidence — 1646 Braintree meadows petition (imaged; Mass. Archives vol. 45)

In 1646 John Gurney was among the signers of a Braintree inhabitants' petition to the General Court against Thomas Gilbert's cattle grazing the petitioners' meadow lots; the imaged document (Massachusetts Archives vol. 45, p. 11, with the answer slip at p. 11a endorsed over Gov. John Winthrop) places John among the town's founding cohort — Martin Sanders, Peter and Richard Brackett, Henry Adams, Samuel Bass, Samuel Adams, Benjamin Albee, John Dassett, James Penniman, and Thomas Meakins.
