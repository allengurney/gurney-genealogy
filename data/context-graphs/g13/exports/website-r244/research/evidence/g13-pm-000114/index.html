Evidence — Ann/Gilman as a Gurney instance on the Norfolk corridor

The Ann Gurney x John Gilman marriage threads a Gurney-surname connection through the same Norfolk-to-New-England corridor that received John. It took place at Hingham, Norfolk - ancient Gurney manorial territory (the manor of Hingham Gurneys) and one of the parishes that fed the Norfolk cohort settling Hingham, Massachusetts - into a Norfolk worsted-weaving family, and the couple's son emigrated to New England. Whether or not Ann was John's own sister (an open question), the connection is a concrete Gurney instance on the corridor; if the sisterhood holds, it places a second Gurney sibling on the Norfolk-to-New-England route, reinforcing the corridor reading of a Norfolk origin. The origin conclusion itself is held to the identity work.
