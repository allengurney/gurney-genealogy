Evidence — Second marriage: John Gurney m. widow Grizzell, Braintree 12 Nov 1661

John Gurney married the widow Grizzell — 'Grissell of the many marriages' — at Braintree on 12 November 1661, by Peter Brackett, as the fourth of her five husbands (Thomas Jewell, Humphrey Griggs, Henry Kibby, John Gurney, and, after John's 1663 death, John Burge in 1667). The marriage is attested directly as John Gurney by Holman's TAG marriage-chain study and by Torrey, independent of the misread town record. Grizzell's compiled maiden name Fletcher (daughter of Robert Fletcher of Chelmsford, Essex) is a colonial attribution and carries no bearing on John Gurney's English origin.
