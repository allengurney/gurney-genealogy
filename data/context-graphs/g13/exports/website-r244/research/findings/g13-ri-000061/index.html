'John Cheny' of the 1661 Braintree record = John Gurney

The 'John Cheny' of the 1661 Braintree marriage and wife-death lines is John Gurney. The fair-copy manuscript reads 'John Girny, Senior' in ink, with 'Cheny' only a later pencil margin annotation that the 1886 print mistook for text; Pope 1900 independently ruled the printed Cheny a typographical error; no John Cheney family is otherwise attested at Braintree in 1661; and the surrounding facts fit Gurney — Mary Gurney's death three weeks earlier, the officiant Peter Brackett (a documented Gurney associate), and John Gurney's 1663 Braintree probate. Confidence is roughly 85-90%, the residual being the ambiguous deaths-page letterform.
