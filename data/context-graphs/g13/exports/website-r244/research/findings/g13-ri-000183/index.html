Negative: Early Providence records hold no Gurney; bounds the Quinapaug interest

The Early Records of the Town of Providence (town-meeting and probate volumes swept) hold no Gurney of any spelling; the only near-name is a distinct Thomas Garnet family of Providence. The 1655-1675 town and deed volumes are clean, which bounds John Gurney's unlocated 'Quinapaug' interest - a Providence-jurisdiction frontier share carried into his 1663 inventory - as one the Providence record never registered. (The separate 1690s Providence John-and-Sarah Gurney household sits in volumes outside this sweep and is unaffected.)
