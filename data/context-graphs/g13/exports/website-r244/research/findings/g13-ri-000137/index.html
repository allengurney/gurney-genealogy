1714 Providence will and inventory of Sarah Gurney; husband John Gurney alive

Sarah Gurney of Providence died 16 April 1714; her will of 31 March 1714, proved 30 April 1714, names her husband John Gurney as still living — he witnessed the will and co-appraised the inventory by mark — and leaves everything to her children by a prior marriage, all surnamed Field.
