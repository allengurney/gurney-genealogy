Entry E is probably John Gurney-1's baptism

Entry E is probably the baptism of John Gurney-1, making him the eldest known child of Francis Gurney by Margaret Rybett, born at East Dereham before the family moved to London. The reading is the strongest single link between the Massachusetts emigrant and the Norfolk Gurney line, and it is an inference from letterform comparison, not a legible entry.
