Recognised standing in the Braintree community

John Gurney held recognised standing in the Braintree community — signing the 1645 plantation petition, serving as probate witness and inventory-taker for Gregory Baxter (1659), and appearing across a dense Braintree creditor network in his own 1663 estate accounts.
