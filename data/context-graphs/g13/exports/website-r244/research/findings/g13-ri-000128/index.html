John Jr. and Ruth's Mendon household, 1671-1676

John Gurney Jr., son of the emigrant John Gurney-1, had settled at Mendon by the early 1670s with his wife Ruth and children John, Samuel (b. 14 March 1671), and Mary. He was among the dead when Nipmuc forces struck Mendon on 14 July 1675, the opening bloodshed of King Philip's War, and Ruth remarried John Bundy of Taunton on 9 January 1676.
