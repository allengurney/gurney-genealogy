MA Archives gunpowder petition (30 May 1641, 'of Weymouth')

The underlying petition, calendared in the Massachusetts State Archives colonial collection and dated 30 May 1641, names "John Gurny, Richard Porter, and James Ludden of Weymouth" seeking remission of the gunpowder fines.
