Francis Gurney sells all his Norfolk and Suffolk lands, 11 July 1634

By an indenture of 11 July 1634 between Fraunces Gurnay of London, Merchant Taylor, and Sir Owen Smith of Irmingland, Norfolk, Francis conveyed all his Norfolk and Suffolk manors and lands for £1,000 — a forced liquidation conducted through the Court of Wards.
