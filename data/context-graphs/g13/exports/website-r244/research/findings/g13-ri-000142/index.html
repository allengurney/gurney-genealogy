1664 Margery Laver will: bequest "to Jane Gurnet"

Margery Laver of Dorchester's will, dated 4 (6) 1664 and proved 10 (9) 1664, bequeaths 2 shillings "to Jane Gurnet, 2s for a memorandum," among legacies to Braintree's minister William Tompson, Dorchester's minister Richard Mather, and the Wiswall and Preston families.
