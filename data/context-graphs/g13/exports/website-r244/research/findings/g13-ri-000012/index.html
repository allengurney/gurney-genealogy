Principal Braintree land was the leased Tyng farm (future Adams seat)

John Gurney's principal Braintree landholding was leasehold, not freehold: the Tyng farm he held continuously c. 1647–1662 was a tenancy, and the northern part of 'the Gurney farm' later became the Adams family seat (the Old House, Quincy).
