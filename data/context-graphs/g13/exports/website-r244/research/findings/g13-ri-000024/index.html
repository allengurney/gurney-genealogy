Billerica town book - 1659 grant-on-condition & surrender

The Billerica manuscript town book records a ten-acre house-lot granted to John Gurney 15 August 1659 'upon condition' he inhabit it himself (his son Richard accepted as a fellow inhabitant), and on 26 December 1659 its surrender: 'Daniel Shead in answer for his father John Gurney; the said John Gurney surrendred up the Lott.'

Type: Evidence
