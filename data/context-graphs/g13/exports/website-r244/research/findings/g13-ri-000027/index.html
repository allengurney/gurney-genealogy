SPR #338 - the 1663 'Quinapaug wch we know not' estate line

John Gurney's 1663 Braintree probate inventory (taken 16 March 1663; total 55 pounds 14s 6d) records a land-interest line reading 'an estate layd out in land at Quinapaug wch we know not' - an interest the appraisers could not value.

Type: Evidence
