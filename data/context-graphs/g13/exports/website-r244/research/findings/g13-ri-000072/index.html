No Merchant Taylors' binding or patrimony freedom for John

The Merchant Taylors' Company of London apprentice and freedom records for 1583-1800 (Scott's full transcription of the binding books and freedom registers, UKDA-SN-9263) contain no binding of a John Gurney son of Francis Gurney to any Merchant Taylor master and no Gurney freedom by patrimony at all: the two John Gurney apprentices in the dataset are a glover's son of 'Moborne', Worcestershire (bound 1602) and an Aylesbury ironmonger's son (bound 1655), neither the emigrant, and the 3,391-record patrimony sheet returns zero Gurney variants. The colonial John's tailoring was therefore not transmitted through his proposed father's own livery company, whether by apprenticeship to a fellow Merchant Taylor or by patrimony freedom after Francis Gurney's death.
