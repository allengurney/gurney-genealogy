Born c.1602-1610 (probably c.1603-08)

John Gurney was born about 1602-1610, most probably about 1604-1608. Three independent estimators of his birth year - his 1652/3 deposition age, the identity work's probable East Dereham baptism (a 'John son of ffrancis Gurnie' about 1609/10), and the bound set by his daughter Mary's 1647 marriage - overlap around 1604-1608, the most probable window, and widen to about 1602-1610 at their possible extremes. A fourth handle, the September 1611 marriage of his probable parents, sits in tension with the baptism reading rather than adding a vote. No colonial record states his birth or age beyond the deposition, and his baptism is not independently fixed apart from the contested Norfolk register reading.
