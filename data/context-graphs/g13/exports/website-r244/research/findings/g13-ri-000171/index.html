Candidate C: the eight-child Berkhamsted Gurney family, 1610-1636, and the absent Berkhamsted burial

The Hertfordshire baptism index records eight children baptised at Berkhamsted to a father John Gurney between 1610 and 1636: Henry (1610), Sara (1615), Jhon (1624), Richard (1626), Elizabeth (1629), Michael (1631), Sarah (1634), and Francis (1636); the mother is not indexed. The Hertfordshire burial index records no John Gurney burial at Berkhamsted between 1640 and 1700, the only seventeenth-century Berkhamsted burials under the name being two entries for a Jhon Gourney, in 1612 and 1620, both too early to be this householder.
