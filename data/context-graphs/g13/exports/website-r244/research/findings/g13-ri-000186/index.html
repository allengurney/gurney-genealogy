Adam Winthrop's diary records the 1605 deaths of Martha (Lewkenor) Gurney's Denham parents

Adam Winthrop - John Winthrop's father - recorded in his diary the deaths of Sir Edward Lewkenor of Denham and his wife on 3 October 1605: 'Sir Edwarde Lewkenor of Denham in Suff[olk] Knight died of the smalpocks. Vir bonus et doctus fuit et patriae amans. The lady his wife died 2 dayes before him.' The couple were Sir Edward Lewkenor (d. 1605) and Susan (Heigham), the parents of Martha (Lewkenor) Gurney - so the Winthrop family personally memorialised the widowed Mrs. Gurney's own parents, in warm terms, a generation before the 1627 Gurdon-Winthrop letter carried a Gurney suit to John Winthrop's chamber.
