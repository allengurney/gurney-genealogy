Bodge: Peter Gurney, Capt. Isaac Johnson's company, Dedham muster 1675

George Madison Bodge's roll of King Philip's War soldiers lists "Peeter Gurnay" under Weymouth in Captain Isaac Johnson's company, mustered at Dedham on 10 December 1675 for the Narragansett campaign, and absent from that company's list of the Great Swamp Fight's December 1675 dead and wounded.

Type: Evidence
