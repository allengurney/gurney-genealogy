Mendon Proprietors' Records: John Gurny's 20-acre lot, sold 1692

The Mendon proprietors' records list "John Gurny, a twenty acre lot" among the town's earliest purchasers, and trace the same lot's chain of title through recurring meadow and swamp-lot abuttal references to its sale, by deed of 3 May 1692, to Josiah Thayer.

Type: Evidence
