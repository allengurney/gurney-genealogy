The Rybett/Ryvett family, St Martin at Palace, and the corrected Heydon connection

The Rybett or Ryvett family were Norfolk and Suffolk gentry, documented at Fritton in Norfolk and at Rishangles, Rattlesden, Stowmarket, and Bildeston in Suffolk. St Martin at Palace was their own Norwich parish, with Rivett entries running 1539–1603, so Margaret almost certainly married from her family's church. A reported marriage of Myrable Ryvett to Sir John Heydon is not borne out by the Visitation of Suffolk, which gives her husband as William Burd, Customer of London; the genuine Gurney–Heydon tie runs through Anne Heydon's marriage to William Gurney V. Francis Gurney was buried at St Botolph Bishopsgate, London, on 9 January 1646/7.
