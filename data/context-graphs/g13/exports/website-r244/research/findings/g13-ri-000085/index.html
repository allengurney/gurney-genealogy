Open: identity of 'mr. Warford' and the advowson (CCEd unexhausted)

The clergyman 'mr. Warford' whom Mrs. Gurney presented in 1627 is unidentified: no Warford of any spelling appears in Venn's Alumni Cantabrigienses (to 1751), and none in Foster's Oxford register beyond too-early Bristol men. The Norwich diocesan institution registers (the Clergy of the Church of England Database) are the remaining route, and his institution would name the Gurney-held advowson.
