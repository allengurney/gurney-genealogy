Two distinct Braintree properties: leased Tyng farm vs. Monatiquot freehold

John held two distinct Braintree properties in his final years — the leased Tyng farm (the future Adams seat) and his own Monatiquot-River freehold sold to Thayer in 1661/2 — so the 'Gurney farm' of local tradition and his personal homestead are separate parcels.
