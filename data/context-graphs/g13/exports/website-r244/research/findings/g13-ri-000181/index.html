Negative: Boston town, notarial, deed, and vital record classes hold no John-era Gurney

The printed Boston-era civil record classes hold no Gurney of John Gurney's generation: the Book of Possessions and Boston town records, the Aspinwall notarial register of 1644-1651, Thomas Lechford's note-book of 1638-1641, the earliest Suffolk Deeds (Libers I-III, 1640-1660), and the Boston vital records 1630-1699 are each silent. The single Gurney in the Boston vital records is the 26 October 1693 marriage of Samuel Gurney and Sarah Shapley - probably John's grandson, an identification tested (not asserted) in the family Mendon-descendants unit - married thirty years after John's death, so no Boston civil record touches the emigrant himself.
