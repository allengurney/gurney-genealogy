Reconstructed family group: five children (Sarah, Mary, Richard, John Jr., Peter) + Isaac uncertain, all Mary's

John Gurney's children form a reconstructed family group of five — Sarah, Mary, Richard (the direct-line G12), John Jr., and Peter — with Isaac a probable but unproven sixth; all were born to his first wife Mary, and no single colonial record enumerates the family, which is assembled from compiled genealogies (Sprague, the Weymouth town history, Rigler) and per-child primary records.
