Plymouth/Taunton primary record: Ruth (Gurney) m. John Bundy, 9 Jan 1676

The Plymouth Colony court's printed record of town returns and the Taunton town's own vital records both carry the 9 January 1676 marriage of John Bundy to a Ruth Gurney of Mendon, under the colonial spellings "Surney" and "Garney"; Charles Henry Pope's 1900 compilation independently records the same marriage but misnames the bride "Jane."

Type: Evidence

Sources

  • plymouth-colony-records-vol8-shurtleff-pulsifer-1857 — vol. 8, Taunton town return, printed p. ~60: "John Bundey married to Ruth Surney, of Mendum, this 9th of January, 1676."
  • pope-pioneers-of-massachusetts-1900 — BUNDY entry: "he m. 9 Jan. 1676, Jane Gurney of Mendon."
  • taunton-vital-records-to-1850-vol2-marriages-1928 — vol. 2 (Marriages): "BUNDY, John ... and Ruth Garney [Gurney] of Mendun, Jan. 9, 1676."
