1886 Braintree print: 'John Cheny' marriage & wife-death lines, 1661

The 1886 printed Braintree town record (Bates ed.) enters John Gurney's second marriage and his first wife's death under a Cheny surname: p. 717, 'John Cheny Senior and Grizell Kidbee,' 9th month 12, 1661 (12 November 1661); and p. 638, 'Cheny the wife of John Cheny,' 7th month 20, 1661 (20 September 1661). Both dates match the Gurney chronology but the printed surname is Cheny, not Gurney.
