Bury St Edmunds cleared as the emigrant's birthplace - no natal Gurney household in its registers

No John Gurney natal household capable of fathering the Braintree emigrant (born about 1602-1612) survives in the Bury St Edmunds parish registers. Across both Bury parishes searched - St James (a full Gurney-variant register sweep) and St Mary (National Burial Index) - the Gurney presence is either post-dated (the Thomas x Lidda Broddish household married about 1633-35) or an unconnected fragment, and the one Bury 'John Gurney' of the period was buried there in 1653. Bury St Edmunds is therefore cleared as the emigrant's birthplace: whatever Banks recorded, it was not a Bury natal record.
