1661/2 Gurney–Thayer deed ('John Gurney, tailor')

On 12 February 1661/2 "John Gurney, tailor" conveyed to Richard Thayer, for £14, a Braintree house and orchard, a five-acre Monatiquot-River parcel, and an adjoining half-acre; possession given 16 April 1662, signed by mark, witnessed by Peter Brackett and John Rockwell.
