26 Oct 1693 Boston marriage: Samuel Gurney x Sarah Shapley

Samuel Gurney married Sarah Shapley at Boston on 26 October 1693, per the printed Boston vital records (Ninth Report of the Record Commissioners, p. 210) - the only Gurney entry of any generation in the volume.
