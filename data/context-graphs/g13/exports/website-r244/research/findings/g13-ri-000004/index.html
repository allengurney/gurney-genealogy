Nash (1885): John an original Weymouth grantee before 1643

Nash's Historical Sketch of Weymouth (1885), Appendix C, records East Field (p. 258) and Mill Field (p. 278) parcels "first granted to John Gurny/Gurnie" but already held by other men by the 1643 partial property record.
