## Corpus

Daniel Gurney *Record of the House of Gournay* (1848, Yale/HathiTrust scan) processed into three text files:
- `sources/corpus/daniel-gurney-part-1.md` — Part I (Norman origins through Gerard G32). 725K chars.
- `sources/corpus/daniel-gurney-part-2.md` — Part II (Junior Norfolk Branch, West Barsham medieval). 448K chars.
- `sources/corpus/daniel-gurney-part-3.md` — Part III (Tudor through Keswick Gurneys). 188K chars.

OCR quality: excellent. Known quirks: "Wilham"/"William" (~6%), "Basiha"/"Basilia" (~16%), hyphenation breaks. 584 book pages, zero gaps. Page markers preserved as `## p. N (#M) ##`.

**Not yet in corpus:** 1858 Supplement, Rye appendix (PDFs in project knowledge, text extraction pending).
