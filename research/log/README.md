# research/log/

Historical operational logs. **Not currently maintained.** The log discipline was retired during the 2026-05-25 consolidation pass (see AGENTS.md §2 repo map and the deletion of `.claude/rules/research-log.md`). Existing dated entries are preserved as a historical record.

## If logging is ever revived
Logs are pointer-only — record what changed and where the substantive content landed, never substantive research content itself. Substantive findings belong in the relevant `research/people/`, `research/places/`, or `research/topics/` file.
