
# The National Archives (UK) — Probate Records Analysis for John Gurney Emigrant Elimination

## Purpose

Best-effort probate-only analysis from records reviewed in this thread, focused on whether each record helps **eliminate** or **validate** possible candidates for emigrant **John Gurney** (c. 1637), especially by identifying:
- a **John Gurney still in England after 1637**
- a **wife / children / kin cluster** tying a John Gurney to England
- a **place cluster** that may intersect with the candidate table
- any **Buckinghamshire / Stewkley / Edlesborough** intersection relevant to **Candidate A**

**Important:** this is a research memo, not a full diplomatic transcription. Some readings remain uncertain because the supplied scans are faint or partial. Uncertain readings are marked with `[ ]` or described as probable.

## Source

**The National Archives (UK)**, Prerogative Court of Canterbury wills and related probate acts, digital images consulted from the user-supplied PDFs in this thread.

## Candidate-A Flag (Stewkley / Edlesborough, Buckinghamshire)

**Flagged intersections:**
- **PROB 11/337/37** — *Sentence of John Gurney of Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire*
- **PROB 11/347/122** — *Will of Daniell Gurney of Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire*

These do **not** directly match Candidate A as currently defined (**Stewkley/Edlesborough; b. 21 Feb 1602/3; wife Alice Collindridge**), but they are important because they show a **separate Buckinghamshire Gurney cluster** still active in England later in the century. That does not eliminate Candidate A by itself, but it increases the need to keep **Aylesbury-area Bucks Gurneys** separate from the **Stewkley/Edlesborough** line.

No probate record reviewed here directly named:
- **Stewkley**
- **Edlesborough**
- **Alice Collindridge**
- or a clearly matching **Jonathan** tied to Candidate A’s Bucks line

A **Jonathan Gurney** appears in the **Winkfield, Berkshire** will, but that is a Berkshire record and should not be conflated with Candidate A.

## Analysis

### 1) PROB 11/382/271 — Will of John Gurney, Merchant
- **Type:** will
- **Core identity:** **John Gurney**, merchant
- **Location:** **parish of St Botolph without Aldgate, Middlesex** (London orbit)
- **Date made:** **23 April 1666**
- **Probate:** later probate act on the same item; still clearly post-1637
- **Key names / places visible:** **Mary Gurney**; probable **John Gurney** (son); **John Duncomb of London, gentleman**; probable **Mortlake, Surrey** reference
- **Notes:** the testator states he is **“upon my voyage for my Native Country England”** and writes as a merchant with London ties. This is a strong post-1637 English John Gurney.
- **Emigrant relevance:** **ELIMINATES a separate John Gurney**. This John was alive, identifiable, and tied to London/Middlesex after 1637.
- **Candidate-table alignment:** **new John**; not Candidate A/B/C.
- **Candidate A intersection:** **none**

## Working Conclusion

For the emigrant-John problem, the probate set materially helps by documenting multiple **separate English John Gurneys after 1637**, especially in:
- **Middlesex / London**
- **Berkshire**
- **Buckinghamshire**
- **Hertfordshire**
- **Sussex**

The strongest county-level caution remains **Buckinghamshire**, because the **Aylesbury** probate records show an additional **Bucks Gurney cluster** that must be distinguished from **Candidate A (Stewkley/Edlesborough)**. At present, though, these Bucks probate records do **not** directly tie to **Alice Collindridge**, **Stewkley**, or **Edlesborough**, so they are best treated as **important regional context**, not a direct identification of Candidate A.
