## Why it was retired

Version 4 published the identification at **about sixty-five percent** for
Candidate B, resting on two primary records: the 1611 Norwich marriage of
Francis Gurney and Margaret Rybett, and a baptism entry in the East Dereham
register read as "John the sonne of ffrancis Gurnie."

**The second of those does not exist as described.** A re-reading of the full
image run against recorded coordinates shows that the line adjudicated is on a
different image and belongs to an Enderby family, and that the record the index
describes is six years later and belongs to a Horne family. Version 5 states
that finding at §5 and carries Candidate B at about eleven percent, inside a
model in which three rows describing the state of the record — rather than any
named man — hold forty-four percent between them.

Version 4 also predates the splitting of Candidate A into two men, the splitting
of Candidate C into two men, the completion of the parent-name and mother
sweeps, the first search of Essex and the West Country, and the correction of
the surname-variant scale.

## What is not carried forward

Two passages in version 4 were dropped rather than rewritten, and neither has a
home elsewhere:

- **A modern-currency equivalence for Francis Gurney's 1634 losses** ("plausibly
  the equivalent of $7+ million US dollars"). Unsourced, and an order-of-magnitude
  estimate that the file could not support.
- **The section-level structure of §10.3 and §10.4** (the Coleman Street
  treatment as a standalone section). Its content is carried in version 5 at
  §10.1 row 7 and §10.3.

Everything else that was trimmed is carried in the John Gurney identity research
units at `research/people/g13-john-gurney/topics/identity/`.
