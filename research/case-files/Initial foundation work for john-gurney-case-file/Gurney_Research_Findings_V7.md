## MAJOR DISCOVERY: FRANCIS GURNEY'S FIRST MARRIAGE

**"Franc Gurny & Margaret Rybett nupt 23 Septemb[er]" — St Martin at Palace, Norwich, 1611.**

Source: Norfolk Record Office, PD 12/1 (parish register). Original register image obtained and verified by Allen Gurney via Findmypast.

This is the single most important primary source discovery of the entire research project. It confirms that Francis Gurney (b.1581, Merchant Taylor) married Margaret Rybett at Norwich on 23 September 1611 — BEFORE his known marriage to Anne Browning (first SBF child 1619). Daniel Gurney, who spent decades researching the family, never found this record.

### Implications
- Francis had a first wife from Norfolk gentry (the Ryvetts)
- Children from this marriage would have been born c.1612-1617
- The Pease genealogy (Charles E.G. Pease, 2021) claim of children John and Anne from this marriage gains credibility
- The Ann Gurney who married John Gilman at Hingham 1626 is likely Francis's daughter
- John Gurney-1 of Braintree MA may have been born c.1612, not c.1603

### Birth Date Revision
The 1611 marriage requires revising John's estimated birth from c.1603 to c.1612. The 1653 deposition "aged about 50 years" would reflect an age of ~41 — within normal error range for period depositions. All other data points (marriage, children, emigration, death) work equally well with c.1612.

## OTHER KEY FINDINGS THIS SESSION

### 1. Buckinghamshire Contributions for Ireland 1642 (BRS Vol. 21)
Complete tax list examined. Gurney entries found at Stewkley (Walter, Thomas, Anthony), Edlesborough (John — £1 0s 0d), and Cheddington (Isaac — 4s 0d). NO John Gurney at Stewkley. The Edlesborough John could be Candidate A's father or Candidate A himself. Isaac Gourney Sr. (1596-1644) confirmed at Cheddington with son Isaac II (b.1638). Candidate A weakened to ~8-10%.

### 2. VCH Buckinghamshire — Stewkley Manor
John Gurney held the manor of Stewkley by 1687. Isaac Gurney, identified as "son of John," was seised during his father's lifetime. Both sold to Anne Robinson of Stepney in 1701. Father/son ambiguity prevents full elimination of Candidate A.

### 3. Naming Pattern Analysis
John-1's children (Sarah, Mary, Richard, John, Peter) show NO Norfolk Gurney naming patterns. No child named Francis (expected for eldest son of Francis). Sarah and Peter are Puritan/biblical. Peter is COMPLETELY ABSENT from every documented Gurney family across 500 years. England-wide search found zero Peter Gurney baptisms 1620-1645. Peter likely comes from wife Mary's maternal family.

### 4. London Apprenticeship Search
Findmypast search found Haberdashers' Company Gourneys (John/Johanni 1590/1592) and other London Gurneys — proving the London Gurney universe extended beyond Francis's Norfolk branch. Francis himself not found (MT records not in Findmypast collection).

### 5. Grizzell Fletcher Correction
WikiTree research confirmed Grizzell Fletcher was daughter of Robert Fletcher of Chelmsford, Essex. John Gurney was her FOURTH husband (after Thomas Jewell, Humphrey Greggs, Henry Kibby). The Chelmsford connection is purely colonial — no English link to John.

### 6. Ann Gurney / John Gilman Marriage Confirmed
Ann Gurney married John Gilman at Hingham, Norfolk, 1 October 1626. Their children baptized at West Dereham and Hingham. Ann buried Hingham 23 November 1651. Son John Gilman Jr. emigrated to New England. Hingham was Gurney family territory ("manor of Hingham Gurneys"). The Gilmans were Norfolk textile workers — same social world as the Merchant Taylors.

### 7. F.G. Gurney Papers
Frederick G. Gurney (1878-1947) of Eggington, near Leighton Buzzard, left 121 notebooks at the Buckinghamshire Archaeological Society. ~70 concentrate on "the history of his family and its connections" — the Eggington/Stewkley/Cheddington Gurneys. Physically at Discover Bucks Museum, Aylesbury.
