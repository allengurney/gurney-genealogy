# West Barsham, Norfolk

Village in north-west Norfolk, England. Coordinates: **52.867826, 0.830094**.

**The principal Gurney family seat from 1372 until the extinction of the direct male West Barsham line in 1661** — approximately 290 years of continuous Gurney lordship. Entered the family through the **Wauncy inheritance**: Katherine de Wauncy, wife of Edmund Gurney (G23), was daughter and eventual heir of the Wauncy family who had held West Barsham since before Domesday.

Francis G14's 1634 alienation is the end of continuous Gurney tenure at West Barsham by Allen's direct line. Per AI-Rules §7, the estate continued under other holders until the senior West Barsham line extinguished in 1661.

## The Wauncy inheritance — precise chronology

- **Before Domesday:** Wauncy family hold West Barsham.
- **1356:** Sir Edmund de Wauncy lord of West Barsham (30 Edw. III).
- **1357:** Sir William de Wauncy settles 100 marks p.a. from West Barsham and Denver on Edmund Gurney and Katherine (31 Edw. III).
- **1367:** Fine levied re moiety of West Barsham, settled on Edmund and Katherine in tail (41 Edw. III).
- **1372:** Sir Edmund de Wauncy dies; his infant son Edmund also dies. Lordship passes to Katherine and thence to Edmund Gurney.
- **c. 1375/77 (51 Edw. III):** Indenture of 180-year lease signed at West Barsham.

The Wauncy family is covered in DG-II Appendix LXIII (pp. 364+), including their Norman origin (possibly from Wanchy near Neufchâtel in Normandy) and their Domesday holdings. Worth reading in full for the pre-1372 context.

## West Barsham Hall

Surviving 16th-century north wing of West Barsham Hall is Grade II listed and still stands. Built by a later Gurney generation. This is the most tangible physical survival of the family's 290-year tenure and the strongest hero-image candidate for G23 onward.

## Primary-source hooks

- **DG-II Appendix LXV** — 180-year lease indenture text. Direct documentary evidence of Edmund Gurney at West Barsham.
- **Edmund Gurney's 1387 will** — Reg. Harsyke, fol. 34, Bishop's office at Norwich. Will dated "Thursday the feast of the Ascension of our Lord" 1387. Executors: Osbert de Mundeford and Thomas Kemp. Bequest: 8l. distributed to the poor on burial day. Burial in the Church of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin, West Barsham.
- **Blomefield vol. vii, pp. 42–47** (West Barsham entry).

## Church of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin

The parish church where Edmund (G23) directed his burial, and almost certainly the burial site of many subsequent Gurney lords through 1661. Any surviving monuments or ledger stones would be valuable — but no field survey has been done in the project.
