# City of London, England

### St Augustine John Gurney and Haberdashers' Gournay charity

Two London leads are useful but should remain carefully separated. The 1638 St Augustine inhabitants return lists a John Gurney in the parish rents at 10 pounds; this is same-name evidence only and does not identify the Massachusetts emigrant.[^london-st-augustine-place]

A later Charity Commission report on the Haberdashers' Company describes "Gournay's Charity," a loan charity arising from Richard Gournay's gift of 300 pounds, with annual interest intended for the poor of the Company, Christ's Hospital, and a poor scholar studying divinity at Oxford or Cambridge. The report says the money was no longer lent at interest and had been represented by a nominal stock appropriation producing 15 pounds per year.[^haberdashers-gournay-charity]

The Richard Gournay charity is not yet tied to the direct line, but it is useful London mercantile/civic context for the broader surname network and should remain visible as a lead.
