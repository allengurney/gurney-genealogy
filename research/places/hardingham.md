# Hardingham, Norfolk

Village in central Norfolk, England. Coordinates: **52.5708, 0.8508**.

Location of **Swathings manor**, held continuously by the junior Gournay line from the mid-12th century through the 14th century. One of two long-standing Norfolk holdings (the other being Harpley) that defined the junior branch's territorial identity before West Barsham was acquired in 1372.

## Primary-source hooks

- **Norfolk fines** naming successive Gurney holders c. 1243 (27 Hen. III) onward.
- **Plea: Matthew v. Gilbert de Runhall** (DG-I Appendix LIII) — legal record establishing Matthew as son of William I. Cited but not transcribed in project files.

## Significance

Swathings represents the junior branch's earliest continuous Norfolk holding — entering family tenure under Walter (G31) when the junior branch separated from the senior Gournay line in the mid-12th century. The 250-year Gurney presence at Hardingham and Harpley together forms the core of the pre-1372 junior branch territorial identity, before the Wauncy inheritance moved the principal seat to West Barsham.
