# La Ferté foundation endowment cluster

This aggregate place note preserves the five church endowments named in the La Ferté foundation charter: Fry, Argueil, Saint-Samson, Boulay, and Bruquedale.

## Why this cluster matters

The La Ferté charter is not only a family-naming document. It also shows the territorial and ecclesiastical footprint of the Gournay / La Ferté family in the late 10th century. The five church endowments demonstrate that Gautier de La Ferté's foundation drew on a real network of local churches and lands.

## Why this is an aggregate first

The identities of all five places are not yet equally stable. Fry is now strong enough for its own place record. Argueil appears strong enough for near-term follow-up. Saint-Samson requires disambiguation. Boulay and Bruquedale need more work. This aggregate record preserves the geography without over-claiming precision.
