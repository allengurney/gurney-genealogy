# Weare, Somerset

Weare is a collateral Gurnay/Gournay market/fair place. The IHR Gazetteer of Markets and Fairs records a 21 May 1241 charter grant by Henry III to Robert de Gurnay for a Friday market at Weare manor and a fair on the vigil and feast of the Translation of Thomas the Martyr.[^gazetteer-weare]

This is not direct evidence for the Norfolk junior line, but it belongs in the broader Gournay place library because it gives a dated administrative record for Robert de Gurnay in Somerset.

[^gazetteer-weare]: Samantha Letters et al., *Gazetteer of Markets and Fairs in England and Wales to 1516*, Somerset, Weare entry, https://archives.history.ac.uk/gazetteer/somer.html#Wea. Source ID: `history-ac-uk-markets-fairs-gazetteer`.
