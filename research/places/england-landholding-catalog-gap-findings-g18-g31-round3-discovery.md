## Round 3 conclusion

The most productive round-3 result is **Pockthorpe / St James the Less**. Round 2 had already found the Pockthorpe church/family-network lead. Round 3 confirms it with **official Historic England and Heritage Gateway records**, including:

- Historic England List Entry **1372521** for the **Former Church of St James, Whitefriars**, Grade I, National Grid Reference **TG 23415 09358**.
- Heritage Gateway / Historic England Research Record **Hob UID 132668**, Grid Reference **TG2341409351**.
- Archaeological watching-brief evidence from the 1979 conversion to Norwich Puppet Theatre, including a clay boundary wall possibly of the 11th/12th century and an earlier simple nave/chancel church possibly of the 12th century.

This makes St James the strongest **official archaeology/church anchor** for the Pockthorpe residence hypothesis, but still does **not** locate The Lathes / Hassets' Hall itself.

Round 3 also found an important **East Barsham Manor** official listing. It is a Grade I Tudor manor house, but it is later Fermor-era fabric and should be treated as **Barsham-cluster context only**, not as evidence for G20 Thomas Gournay II's 1445 Waldgraves feoffee/seal evidence unless a separate Waldgraves-to-Manor continuity source is found.

## Phase 1 implications after round 3

1. **Pockthorpe should now receive a separate church/place-detail candidate** for `St James the Less / Former Church of St James, Whitefriars`, linked to G19 only as residence/network context, not ownership.
2. **The Lathes / Hassets' Hall remains unresolved**. Add it as an alias/research lead under Pockthorpe, but do not geocode to St James or any other parcel.
3. **East Barsham should remain secondary/feoffee context** until the specific Waldgraves manor site is found. East Barsham Manor is probably too late and too Fermor-specific to use as the G20 site.

5. **Future round priority:** direct NHER / Norfolk Heritage Explorer extraction for North Barsham DMV, Pockthorpe settlement, Letton, and Thuxton; direct Suffolk HER / HE extraction for Depden.
