## John Gurney's two Braintree properties

John Gurney held two separate properties in old Braintree, in different precincts.

**The Tyng leasehold (north precinct, now central Quincy)** — full place record at [`gurney-tyng-farm-quincy-ma.md`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/research/places/gurney-tyng-farm-quincy-ma.md). In **1647 William Tyng leased John Gurney a 45-acre farm for ten years**; Gurney kept leasing it from Tyng's daughters **Bethia and Mercy** until **1662**, and the northern part of this "Gurney farm" later became the **Adams Old House** estate ("Peace field"), now in the center of Quincy. This is the holding in William Tyng's 1653 probate inventory recorded as land "in the possession of John Gurney" — read as **45 acres** by the National Park Service / Ezekiel Sargent and as **48 acres** in the *NEHGR* abstract (a transcription discrepancy to settle against the inventory image). It is one continuous tenancy ~1647–1662 (the original 1647 ten-year term from William Tyng, then renewed from his daughters), the same property documented in both the 1653 inventory and the 28 March 1661 estate-division indenture. Approximate location: the Adams Old House, ~42.2565, -71.0028.[^braintree-clr-1997][^braintree-nehgr30]

**The Monatiquot freehold (middle precinct, now Braintree center)** — full place record at [`gurney-monatiquot-freehold-braintree-ma.md`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/research/places/gurney-monatiquot-freehold-braintree-ma.md). A house, orchard, a five-acre parcel on the **Monatiquot River**, and an adjoining half-acre, which Gurney owned outright and sold to Richard Thayer in 1661/2. Approximate location on the Monatiquot, ~42.210, -70.992.[^braintree-thayer]
