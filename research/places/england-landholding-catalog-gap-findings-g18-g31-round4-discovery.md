## Round 4 conclusion

Round 4 did not locate a new medieval Gurney/Gournay manor-house site. It did, however, improve the **church-anchor layer** in several ways:

1. **East Barsham / All Saints is now a stronger official church anchor** for the G20 East Barsham/Waldgraves feoffee/seal lead than the nearby East Barsham Manor listing. Historic England's Church of All Saints listing includes Norman, c.1200, C13, C15, and C17/C18 fabric/features, plus a Calthorpe memorial.
2. **Runhall gains a useful round-tower church anchor** for the older G27–G31 Runhall continuity. This does not add a new catalog place, because Runhall already exists, but it strengthens future narrative and imagery work.
3. **North Barsham remains a likely place-entry candidate**, but still lacks a strong direct Historic England/NHER extraction in this pass. The strongest online anchors remain the current All Saints church existence and the lost-settlement/DMV lead requiring NHER verification.
4. **St Gregory's Norwich remains verified as a Grade I medieval church through secondary sources**, but a clean Historic England list-entry extraction still did not surface. Treat St Gregory as a parish anchor only until its official list entry is extracted.
5. **Waldgraves remains unresolved**. No online source found in this pass connects the 1445 Waldgraves feoffee/seal evidence to East Barsham Manor or any modern parcel.

Recommended precision note:

> Use All Saints as the East Barsham parish/church anchor for the Waldgraves feoffee/seal research lead. Do not identify it as Waldgraves or as a Gurney holding unless deed/site continuity evidence is found.

## Phase 1 implications after round 4

1. **East Barsham/Waldgraves should use All Saints, not East Barsham Manor, as the safer church/parish anchor.**
2. **East Barsham Manor should be retained as visual/locality context only**, because it is later Fermor-era standing fabric.
3. **Runhall already exists in the catalog, but its narrative should be enriched** with the All Saints round-tower church lead if a direct official or specialist source is later verified.
4. **North Barsham still needs direct HER work** before exact status or mapping is asserted.
5. **St Gregory's remains unresolved at official-list extraction level**, despite being a strong parish anchor from secondary sources.
6. **No manor-house survival claim should be added** for Waldgraves, Pockthorpe/The Lathes, North Barsham, Depden, Denver, Drayton, Saxthorpe/Loundhall, or South Wootton on current evidence.
