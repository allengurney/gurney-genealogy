## Research Appendix

### Lineage Status
**Confirmed.** William I is named in the *Registres Olim* (French royal court records) as holder of Montigny-sur-Andelle in parage; confirmed as lord of Runhall in Placit. 8 John; his designation as "Dominus" in the Gaywood deed confirms knighthood. Father-son relationship to Matthew is established by the plea in DG Appendix LIII (a legal record).

### Sources Consulted
- DG-I, pp. 277–278 and pedigree p. 286.
- *Registres Olim, par Le Comte de Beugnot* (cited by DG) — not independently verified in project files.
- Placit. 8 John (cited by DG) — not independently verified.
- DG Appendix LIII (plea with Gilbert de Runhall) — cited but not transcribed in project files.
- Ancestors_v3.json; Gurney_Research_KnowledgeBase_1.md.

### Key Findings from Hannay (1867)
- Hannay does not cover the junior Norfolk branch in detail (his book focuses on the senior baron line through Hugh VI). However, the parage connection to Montigny-sur-Andelle that DG documents places William I squarely in the Gournay family tree.
- Note: The Montigny-sur-Andelle Norman holding would have been lost when Philip Augustus expelled the English from Normandy in 1204 — roughly the generation of William I's son Matthew.

### Negative Results
- No wife named.
- The Gaywood deed is cited but not transcribed in project sources; the specific archive location is not given by DG.
- DG Appendix LIII (the plea establishing Matthew as son) is referenced but not quoted at length in the passages reviewed.

### Open Questions
1. The *Registres Olim* (Beugnot ed., Paris, 1839) — this is a published source and could be searched for the Montigny-sur-Andelle entry naming William de Gournay. This would provide the most rigorous independent confirmation of his Norman holding.
2. The Gaywood deed: Gaywood is near King's Lynn, Norfolk. Are there surviving charter collections for this area in the Norfolk Record Office or British Library that might contain or reference this deed?
3. Could the Appendix LIII plea (Matthew vs. Gilbert de Runhall) be located in the DG project PDF for transcription?
