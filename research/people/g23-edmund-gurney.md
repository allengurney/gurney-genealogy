# Edmund Gurney (G23)

d. 1387. Harpley & Hardingham, Norfolk; Bishop's Lynn (King's Lynn). Lineage status: **Confirmed**.

## Known facts

- Successful lawyer. **Steward of John of Gaunt's East Anglian estates**, 1372–1387 [HoP-Gurney]. John of Gaunt = Duke of Lancaster, father of Henry IV, most powerful man in England after the king.
- Counsel sought by cities of Norwich and Bishop's Lynn (King's Lynn) [DG-I-280ff].
- 15+ appearances in Patent and Close Rolls [DG-I, project knowledge].
- Married **Katherine de Wauncy** (daughter and heir of Sir William Wauncy, Knt.). Through her, **West Barsham entered the Gurney family** — the manor held continuously until 1661 [DG-I-280].
- Principal documented holdings: Harpley manor, Hardingham manor, land interests at Bishop's Lynn.
- Two sons: Sir John Gurney Knt. (d. 1408, **collateral**) and Robert Gournay (G22, **direct line**).
