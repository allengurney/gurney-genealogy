# research/people/

Research files for people: ancestors, spouses, non-ancestor subjects, and candidate identities.

## File types
- `g{NN}-{slug}-fact-sheet.research.md` — paired companion to a published fact sheet
- `g{NN}-{slug}.md` — pre-fact-sheet ancestor staging file
- `{descriptive-slug}.md` — non-ancestor or candidate subject

## What belongs here
- working notes with substance
- source extracts
- transcriptions
- open questions
- conflicting information
- negative results
- hypotheses
- crosslinks

## What does not belong here
- published ancestor narrative
- purely geographic files
- broad cross-cutting topic files
- thin source-validation worksheets

See `research/README.md` for the cross-subdirectory destination guidance (multi-destination cases, when to use places vs. people vs. topics).
