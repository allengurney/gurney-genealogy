# research/people/

**NOT for ancestors who have fact sheets.** The fact sheet (`fact-sheets/g{NN}-{slug}.md`) is the single canonical per-ancestor document — narrative plus research appendix. See AI-Rules.md §3 delineation rule.

This folder is for non-ancestor research subjects and pre-fact-sheet staging only.

## Who belongs here

- **Non-ancestors with substantive research:** Margaret Rybett, the laceweaver Francis, Ann Gurney of Hingham, Earsham John Girney 1636, other candidate matches, disambiguation targets.
- **Spouses** with enough independent research to warrant their own file (Katherine de Wauncy, Edith de Warenne).
- **Pre-fact-sheet staging:** accumulating findings about an ancestor before producing their fact sheet. Once the fact sheet is produced, this file gets absorbed into the appendix or deleted.

## Who does NOT belong here

Any direct-line ancestor who already has a fact sheet. New findings for those ancestors go into `fact-sheets/g{NN}-{slug}.md` § Research Appendix.

## Naming

Descriptive slug, no generation prefix (these aren't direct-line fact sheet subjects):

- `margaret-rybett.md` — Francis G14's first wife
- `francis-gurney-laceweaver.md` — the other Francis (disambiguation target)
- `hingham-ann-gurney.md` — possible sibling of John Gurney-1
- `earsham-john-girney-1636.md` — candidate match
- `katherine-de-wauncy.md` — Edmund G23's wife, Wauncy heiress

Exception: pre-fact-sheet staging files may use `g{NN}-{slug}.md` naming to make the eventual migration obvious. Delete or absorb once the fact sheet is produced.

## Structure inside the file

Same sections as the fact sheet research appendix:

```
# {Name}

{One-line summary: relationship to direct line, dates, geography}

## Known facts
## Open questions
## Records examined
## Negative results
## Conjectures
## Crosslinks
```
