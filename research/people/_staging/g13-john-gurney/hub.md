# John Gurney-1 (G13)

**Tailor. First Gurney in North America.** John Gurney (Generation 13) was in
New England by 1641 — an admitted inhabitant of Weymouth, Massachusetts, then
settled at Braintree from about 1645 until his death in the winter of 1662/3 — a
tailor by the trade his deeds name and a farmer by the measure of his estate.[^banner-record]
He was born about 1604–1608.[^banner-age] His English origin is the line's
central open question: no surviving record states where he came from, and the
leading identification is probable rather than proven.[^banner-origin]

## Identity status

The identity work reaches its conclusion — John Gurney of Braintree as the son of
Francis Gurney, Merchant Taylor, and Margaret Rybett of Norfolk (Candidate B) — by
elimination and circumstantial fit rather than by any record naming his father, and
holds it as **probable, not proven**. The current probability model is maintained
in the case file, which assesses the identification at roughly sixty-five percent;
that model is canonical there and is not restated here.[^identity-xref]

Source ID: `nehgr-62-94`.
