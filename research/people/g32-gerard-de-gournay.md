# Gerard de Gournay (G32)

c. 1040 – d. before 1104, Palestine. Lineage status: **Confirmed**.

## Known facts

- Son of Hugh de Gournay III and Basilia Flaitel [DG-I-46].
- Present at **Battle of Hastings** (1066) with father [DG-I-intro-p.i].
- Married **Edith de Warenne**, daughter of William de Warenne, 1st Earl of Surrey — one of the richest men in Domesday England [DG-I-46].
- Witnessed foundation charter of William I and Matilda's nunnery, Holy Trinity at Caen, 1082, alongside father [DG-I-48].
- Joined the **First Crusade** (1096). Captured Jerusalem July 1099 [DG-I-58ff].
- Died in the Holy Land before 1104 [DG-I].
- Founded **Lessingham Priory**, Norfolk — attached to Abbey of Bec [DG-I].
- Daughter **Gundred de Gournay** married Nigel de Albini, 1118. Patroness of Byland Abbey and Rievaulx Abbey, Yorkshire [DG-I].
- English holdings: Caister (Norfolk), Cantley (Norfolk). Norfolk manors expanded through Edith de Warenne marriage connection.
- Retained Gournay-en-Bray seigneury and surrounding Pays de Bray, Normandy.
