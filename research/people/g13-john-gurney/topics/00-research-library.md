# John Gurney Research Library

John Gurney (Generation 13, born about 1602-1610) is the emigrant ancestor of
the American Gurney line: in New England by 1641, first an admitted inhabitant
of Weymouth, Massachusetts, then settled at Braintree, MA from about 1645 until
his death in the winter of 1662/3 — a tailor by trade and a farmer by the
measure of his property and goods. His English origin is the line's central open question:
no surviving record states where he came from, and the leading candidate
identifications remain unproven.

This research library is the comprehensive working research on John, organized
by topic. It is the deepest of three layers:

- The **[fact sheet](/fact-sheets/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet)** is the concise published biography — start there for the
  settled narrative.
- The **[case file](/key-research/john-gurney-case-file)** is the polished identification argument for who John was and where he came from.
- This **research library** is the working evidence itself: every record,
  negative search, qualification, and open conflict, organized so each topic
  can be read on its own.
- The **[Context Graph Explorer](/research/notes/g13-john-gurney/explorer/)** is the
  library's relational data layer: every research item, source citation, and
  relationship as a structured graph — useful for exploring the evidence
  visually, and built for optimal AI consumption.

The material here is working research. Conclusions carry stated confidence,
conflicts are left visible rather than smoothed over and open questions are stated as open. Evidence supporting  a passage has footnoted citations as well as an **Evidence** link which opens a context graph relational research provenance with
findings, sources, and qualifications behind it.
