# East Dereham PD 86/41 — what the register can and cannot answer

The East Dereham parish register is the single document the Candidate B identification was built on, and
its limits are now fixed. This unit records what the register contains, what it never contained, and
which repo statements those two facts govern.

## The register is one file, and it is complete

The Norfolk Record Office catalogues **PD 86/41, "Indented register bills, 1593–1641"** — loose annual
certificates, not a bound book — and catalogues its digitisation image by image as **110 contiguous
images, `4034129-00692` to `4034129-00801`**.[^nrocat] The March–July 2026 analysis worked from images
`00693`–`00768`, which is 76 of the 110. The remaining 34 were pulled in July 2026 and read.[^tail-pull]

Every membrane carries a modern pencil year at the head of its mount, and most annual returns also carry
a contemporaneous in-parchment heading. The pencil years are transcriptions of those headings, not
archival guesses: on image `00793` the pencil reads `PD 86/41/20 · 1640-41` and the parchment beneath it
reads *"Bills Indented of all the Christinings, Marriages and Buriall[s] in y[e] towne of East Dereham in
y[e] County of Norff[olk] **from our Ladie 1640 unto our Ladie 1641**, Mr Jo: Bretton being vicar…"* —
Lady Day to Lady Day, matching exactly.[^pencil]

## Five annual returns are lost, and nothing else ever recorded them

The file runs from the 1610 return directly to the 1616-17 return. **The returns for 25 March 1611 to
25 March 1616 do not survive.**

No second witness exists, and this is the part that makes the loss absolute rather than inconvenient.
The Norfolk Record Office's own per-parish summary gives East Dereham (PD 86) as: original registers
**c.1549–1596** (unfit for production), then nothing until **1679**; archdeacon's and bishop's
transcripts **from 1698**.[^nro-summary] There is no bound parish register for East Dereham between 1596
and 1679 at all, and the transcript series begins eighty-two years after the missing window. The loose
register bills of PD 86/41 are the only witness the parish ever had for 1593–1641. FindMyPast's East
Dereham archdeacon's-transcript film confirms it from the other direction: its target card reads
"NORWICH DIOCESE / NORFOLK ARCHDEACONRY / HINGHAM DEANERY / ARCHDEACONS TRANSCRIPTS / ITEM 5 / **EAST
DEREHAM 1725—1811/12**".[^at-film]

**This is a permanent evidentiary limit.** It is not a pending pull, and no future access — subscription,
record office, or otherwise — will change it.

## What that governs

**The 1612–1615 birth window is permanently unobservable.** Francis Gurney married Margaret Rybett at
Norwich on 23 September 1611; a first child of that marriage falls in roughly 1612–1615. Candidate B is
therefore neither confirmed nor refuted from East Dereham for those years, and never can be. The absence
of a Gurney baptism in the window is not evidence against the hypothesis — and equally, the hypothesis
can never draw support from the window. Any argument that treats the gap as a reason for optimism is
treating the unobservable as favourable, which it is not.

**The expected son Francis is partly unrecoverable.** The reconstruction that places a son Francis born
at East Dereham c.1611–1618 — the man buried there 8 November 1633 — falls half inside the lost window.
Only a 1616–1618 birth remains testable against surviving membranes.

**The chronology lattice built in May 2026 is superseded.** Its interpolated rows
(`00728/29/30 = 1617`, `00731/32 = 1618`, `00733/34 = 1619`) rest on an assumption of one page per annual
return, which the file disproves: each return runs across several membranes and each membrane is
photographed twice. Images 00726–00731 all belong to the 1616-17 return, and 00732–00734 to the 1618-19
return. Years should be taken from in-parchment headings only.

**"Page 00725 = the 1615 register year" cannot stand.** Images 00724 / 00725 are the marriages-and-burials
membranes of the **1610** return. No 1615 return exists, so the assignment was never available. Every
date derived from it needs re-deriving — including the years assigned to the Marye and Agnes burials.

**A year conflict on the East Dereham Francis burial is unresolved.** The repo carries Francis Gurney,
buried 8 November **1633** (FamilySearch index VNN2-H8S, no parent named). Ancestry's Norfolk collection
indexes what is almost certainly the same line as *"Francis Garndy, burial, 8 November **1649**, East
Dereham"* — same day and month, sixteen years apart, surname read `Garndy`. PD 86/41 ends at 1640-41, so
a genuine 1649 burial cannot sit in this file; either Ancestry's year is wrong or the row derives from
another source.

## The reading that failed, and why it matters here

The "John the sonne of ffrancis Gurnie" baptism that the case file called its strongest single anchor
does not exist. The line adjudicated in March 2026 is on image `00722` and reads *"Amorye the daughter
of Robart Enderby, Ianuarij 10"*; the record the FindMyPast index actually described is on `00726` and
reads *"John the sonne off Nicholas Horne bapt — Janua 10"*, 10 January 1616/17.[^audit] Two findings of
that audit survive intact and are load-bearing here: **Edward, son of ffrancis Gurney, baptised 27 May
1610** is D-class, fixed by the in-parchment "East Dereham 1610" heading on image `00721`; and **Marye,
daughter of ffrancis Gurnoe, baptised 25 May 1618** sits in the 1618-19 return.

Edward's date is the sharper of the two for the identification, because it is sixteen months *before*
the September 1611 marriage. The East Dereham household was producing children in May 1610, and the
register can no longer be asked whether it produced more between 1611 and 1616.
