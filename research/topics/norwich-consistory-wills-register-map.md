# The Norwich Consistory Court will registers in FamilySearch Full-Text Search — film map and sweep log

The Norwich Consistory Court (NCC) was the bishop's probate court for Norfolk, Suffolk, and part of Cambridgeshire — the court that proved the wills of Anthony Gurney G17 (1557), John Gurney of Earsham (1639), Henry Gurnay G15 (1623), and the Waveney-family wills around the Earsham Gurneys. Its registered-copy-will volumes (1370–1858) are digitised and machine-transcribed in FamilySearch Full-Text Search as year-labelled films ("Norwich. Wills 1638–1639," etc.). This file maps register years to film (DGS) numbers so any probate year is a two-step pull: take the DGS from the table, film-scope the query (`q.groupName=<DGS>`).[^ncc-register-map]

## Gurney sweep log (2026-06-11)

- **1615–16, 1617–18, 1619–20** (`Gurn* Gourn*`): no Gurney testator. Coverage confirmed per film by `+wife` probes; the only cards are garble ("Gurnance" ≈ *governance*; a "do bene Gurne" token inside a Latin probate formula, [ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-T942-7](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-T942-7?view=fullText), image 528). Consequence: **the 1621–23 will of Henry Gurnay of Great Ellingham is the only Gurney will registered at Norwich in 1615–1623** — there is no 1615/16 Henry Gurney probate.
- **1621–23**: two cards — Henry Gurnay G15's will (see the [G15 companion](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/research/people/g15-henry-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md)), and an unrelated will at image 365 with legatee token "the said Gourne my [sonne?]" and a "Henry Porter of Barton" ([ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C3SQ-X](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C3SQ-X?view=fullText)) — unexamined minor card.
- **1643–46 and 1647–51** (`Gurn*`, `Gourn*`, `+Harpley`): zero. Coverage confirmed. **Edmund Gurney the Divine's will (d. 1648, rector of Harpley) is not findable at machine level in the NCC registers**; given the Interregnum gap after 1651, the next stop is the Prerogative Court of Canterbury (PROB 11, TNA) for a 1648–1659 probate. (Lead L-6.)
