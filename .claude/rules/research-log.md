# Research log rules

Human-facing overview:
- `research/log/README.md`
- `research/README.md`

## Purpose
The log is an operational pointer layer.

## Keep logs small
- Log what changed.
- Log where substantive content landed.
- Log what still needs review if useful.

## Do not let logs become notebooks
- If substantive research content starts growing in a log entry, move it to the correct people/place/topic/case file.
- The log should point, not store.

## Naming
- Prefer small dated files.
- Multiple files per day are fine when that keeps logs atomic and readable.
