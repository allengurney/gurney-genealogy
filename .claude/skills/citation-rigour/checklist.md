# Citation rigour checklist

## Coverage
- Does each factual sentence in a fact sheet have a nearby footnote?
- Are inferential claims cited?
- Are quotations and transcriptions cited at exact source level where possible?
- Are multiple-source claims fully supported?

## Proximity
- Is the footnote attached to the sentence or clause it supports?
- Are there any ambiguous end-of-paragraph notes covering too much material?

## Readability
- Does the note use human-readable citation text?
- Are unexplained abbreviations avoided?
- Is the note concise but specific?

## Traceability
- Does the note identify the exact page, folio, image, entry, or URL when possible?
- If the source exists in `data/sources.json`, is that alignment visible?

## Web sources
- Is the URL included when available?
- Is the URL stable and specific?

## Integrity
- Are any claims unsupported?
- Are any citations mismatched, duplicated, or stale?
- Are any notes pointing to the wrong source, wrong page, or wrong work?

## Publication fit
- If support is thin, should the claim remain in the fact sheet?
- Would this be better in research only?
