Gurney Genealogy Library

Thirty-seven generations of Gurney ancestry, from the rise of the Vikings to modern America. This public research library follows the direct Gurney line through sourced fact sheets, companion research notes, place records, maps, source catalogs, and the active John Gurney case file. Developed by amateur genealogist Allen Gurney. Contact me.

Family History Research Library

The site is organized as a working genealogy library rather than a single pedigree chart. Each generation has a published fact sheet, and many ancestors also have companion research notes preserving source problems, contradictory evidence, and open questions.

Core Research Questions

The central research thread is the identity and English origin of John Gurney-1 of Braintree, Massachusetts. The library also traces the older Norfolk and Norman Gurney line through documented people, places, source records, and geographic context.
