## 1. Source-format anatomy

**Shortest export in the batch (4 pages)**, reflecting how thin the primary record is for this generation.

Three layers:
- **Structured FGR** (p. 1): husband **"Eudes ou Hugues de GOURNAY EN BRAY (PWPZ-VK1)"** with conflated identity, b. about 0930, d. about 0995. Wife: Marthe de Foucarmont (L2TC-TFZ), b. 0944 Foucarmont, d. 1000 Gournay-en-Bray, parents Clotaire de Foucarmont II + Sylvette de Sénarpont. Children: Giselberthe de Gournay (G17J-BBT) and Renaud de Gournay (GC1N-CQ3).
- **Sources** (p. 2): three Racines Histoire / Pattou citations (one redundant); a TNG entry (Our Royal Titled Noble; "Eudes de Gournay, Sire de Gournay-en-Bray was born circa 935… died in 995"); and a long FMG MedLands quotation that is the **single most important transcript in the export**:
  > [EUDES . According to Gurney, the town of Gournay and adjacent land were granted by Rollo to Eudes, in 912, but he adds in a later passage that the existence of Eudes and his supposed son Hugues "is a matter of tradition" and that there is no surviving contemporary document in which they are named[881]. m ---. The name of Eudes's wife is not known. Eudes & his wife had one child:
  >  a) [HUGUES de Gournay . According to Gurney, the existence of Eudes and his supposed son Hugues "is a matter of tradition" and no contemporary document survives in which they are named[882].]
- **Notes** (pp. 2–3): two contributor flags — *"Please, please, PLEASE!!! Check your birth/death dates....a father cannot be 5 years older than his son!!!!!"* (a contributor exasperated by data-quality), and *"Maybe the father of GC1N-CQ3?"* (uncertainty about parent-child link).

**Layer peculiarity**: the FMG MedLands transcript on this PID is bracketed **[EUDES…]** — i.e., FMG itself treats both Eudes and his supposed son Hugues with full brackets indicating uncertainty. FMG cites Gurney (1845) twice: pp. 26 (citation [881]) and pp. 26 and 31 (citation [882]). MedLands is more cautious about this generation than about any other in the chain.

The FS PID "Eudes ou Hugues" conflation is **not just an FS-tree artifact** — it reflects real scholarly uncertainty about whether Eudes and Hugues are distinct historical figures or the same person under variant names (Hannay's observation: "Hugh's name was convertible with Eudes or Eude in the chronicles," repo G36 companion line 31). FMG's bracketed treatment matches.

## 2. Cross-walk: FS export vs. repo

### 2.1 Concordant facts (FS confirms repo)

- Eudes / Hugues active in the 10th century at Gournay-en-Bray.
- Tradition only — no contemporary document survives.
- Son Renaud de Gournay (GC1N-CQ3 = repo G35).
- Existence of Eudes and Hugues is "a matter of tradition" per FMG via DG (1845).

### 2.2 Net-new content worth promoting

1. **The FMG-via-Gurney "matter of tradition" attestation**, verbatim:
   > [EUDES . According to Gurney, the town of Gournay and adjacent land were granted by Rollo to Eudes, in 912, but he adds in a later passage that the existence of Eudes and his supposed son Hugues "is a matter of tradition" and that there is no surviving contemporary document in which they are named[881].

   Citation: [881] Gurney (1845), p. 26; [882] Gurney (1845), pp. 26 and 31.

   This is a **direct admission embedded in MedLands** that DG himself (in the 1845 *first* edition / Supplement) classified Eudes and Hugues as tradition rather than primary-source-attested. The repo's existing G36 companion (line 32, "the existence of Eudes and Hugues is 'a matter of tradition'") and G37 companion both already use language matching this. **Strengthens the existing classification.**

2. **The Pattou chart structure for this generation** (companion p. 2):
   > "? Eudes (de Gournay) seigneur investi du pays de Gournay (-en-Bray, 76) par le duc Rollon (~912) ép. ? (Marthe de Foucarmont ?)"
   > [...]
   > "? Hugues de Gournay" (with question marks indicating uncertainty)
   > [...]
   > "Hugues 1er de Gournay + après 989 (1046 ?) seigneur de Gournay (fortifie Gournay dès 984) ép. ? Bathilde de Gerberoy + 1059"

   Pattou's chart structure: Eudes (`?`) → Hugues (`?`) → Hugues 1er → Hugues II → Hugues III. So Pattou treats:
   - **Eudes** as *probably* the original Rollon investee, with `?` (parallel to FMG's brackets).
   - **A "Hugues" of uncertain status** between Eudes and Hugues 1er, also with `?`.
   - **Hugues 1er** as the *real* fortifier, with named wife Bathilde de Gerberoy (`?`), active 984.

   The repo's chain (G37 = Eudes c. 860; G36 = Hugh I c. 945–950, the fortifier) collapses Pattou's three uncertain generations (Eudes, ?Hugues, Hugues 1er) into two (Eudes, Hugh I). Pattou implies a possible *fourth* generation — an unnamed/uncertain "Hugues" between Eudes and the fortifying Hugues 1er.

   French Wikipedia mirrors Pattou: "Eudes de Gournay (existence contestée), cité vers 912, épouse Marthe de Foucarmont" → "Hugues (?)" → "Renaud (douteux)" → "Hugues Ier de Gournay, vivant en 984, décédé après 989 (peut-être en 1046)…" So French Wikipedia has *Eudes → Hugues(?) → Renaud(?) → Hugues 1er*. The Wikipedia structure shifts Renaud one generation later than the repo, and inserts an extra "Hugues(?)" between Eudes and Renaud.

3. **Marthe de Foucarmont** as wife of Eudes:
   > "Eudes de Gournay…He married Marthe de Foucarmont, daughter of Clothaire I de Foucarmont, Seigneur de Foucamont, circa 960." (TNG site via FS p. 2)

   Pattou's chart (p. 2) entertains this with `?`: "ép. ? (Marthe de Foucarmont ?)". So Pattou is **tentatively** willing to attribute a Foucarmont marriage at this generation. The Foucarmont attribution is real (Foucarmont is a place near Eu/Aumale, with a 12th-century Cistercian abbey founded by Henri I de Eu) but the specific link to Eudes/Hugues at the founding generation is unsupported by contemporary documents. **Worth recording as a candidate, not adopting.** If adopted, would resolve the existing G36 companion's negative finding ("No spouse named in any source consulted").

4. **Pattou's treatment of "fortified Gournay from 984"** as Hugues 1er's, not Hugh-I's-as-Eudes'-son:
   > "Hugues 1er de Gournay + après 989 (1046 ?) seigneur de Gournay (fortifie Gournay dès 984)"

   The repo G36 fact sheet attributes the fortification ("La Tour Hue") to Hugh I. **Pattou's "Hugues 1er" matches repo's Hugh I both by date and by the fortifier role.** The naming difference is just notation — Pattou uses "1er" (i.e., the first of the four Hugues he chains: 1er, II, III, IV); the repo uses "Hugh I" (the first of six Hughs in the family). Both refer to the same person.

5. **The Pattou-French-Wikipedia-vs-repo structural disagreement** is real: Pattou treats Hugues 1er as wife of Bathilde de Gerberoy, but the repo's G36 has no recorded wife. **The Bathilde de Gerberoy attribution should be considered for the repo G36 fact-sheet** with `?` qualifier and clear sourcing to Pattou + French Wikipedia.

6. **Bathilde's death year 1059** is the only specific date Pattou gives for her, paralleled by French Wikipedia. Note the FS structured field at G34 also had Berthilde dying 1059 — the year is consistent across the bad-FS-tree-attribution and the better Pattou attribution. The 1059 death year is therefore plausibly real (sourced somewhere in the deeper Pattou substrate); the *attachment* to Hugues 1er rather than Hugues II is the correct disposition.

### 2.4 Spurious / over-claimed structured-field content

- **The reversed parent-child chain** (Renaud as father of Eudes/Hugues) — possibly the most internally inconsistent structured-field error in the entire batch. The FS tree at this PID is structurally inverted relative to all scholarly sources.
- **Marthe's parents** (Clotaire de Foucarmont II + Sylvette de Sénarpont) — community-tree extrapolation.
- **Marthe's specific dates** (b. 0944 Foucarmont, d. 1000 Gournay-en-Bray) — community-tree extrapolation.
- **Giselberthe de Gournay** as daughter — unsupported.
- **Alberade's "de la Ferté" surname** — community-tree extrapolation (la Ferté is the place where her son Gauthier founded the priory, not her family of origin).

The contributor's own exasperation note on p. 2 (*"Please, please, PLEASE!!! Check your birth/death dates....a father cannot be 5 years older than his son!!!!!"*) flags exactly the data-quality problem at this PID: the structured-field dates are internally inconsistent and the parent-child links are inverted.

## 6. Open items still requiring human judgment

1. **The fundamental Eudes/Hugues conflation**: FS treats them as a single uncertain person (PWPZ-VK1). Repo treats them as two generations (G37 = P8MV-L5C, G36 = PWPZ-VK1). FMG brackets both. Pattou keeps them as separate `?` figures. French Wikipedia keeps them as separate. Hannay observed the names were "convertible" in the chronicles. **Resolution**: the repo's two-generation chain is defensible (and standard genealogy practice for tradition-only ancestors is to follow the named chain), but it's worth recording that a one-generation reading is also possible. The G37 export (P8MV-L5C, not in this batch) will give more data.
2. **Pattou's possible extra "?Hugues" generation between Eudes and Hugues 1er**: Pattou's chart shows three uncertain generations (Eudes ?, Hugues ?, Hugues 1er) where the repo has two (Eudes G37, Hugh I G36). If the repo adopts Pattou's structure, it would need to insert an additional G36.5 generation. **Likely best handled by leaving the repo's chain as-is and footnoting Pattou's variant.**
3. **Bathilde de Gerberoy at Hugh I level**: Pattou and French Wikipedia name her with `?`. Repo currently has no wife for Hugh I. Adopting her with `?` would be consistent with Pattou.
4. **Marthe de Foucarmont at Eudes / pre-Hugh-I level**: TNG and Pattou (with `?`) name her. The Foucarmont attribution is geographically plausible (Foucarmont is in the Pays de Bray near Gournay) and would give a sensible local-marriage explanation for the early Gournay establishment. Worth recording with `?`.
5. **Birth date range for Hugh I**: c. 920–940 is now well-supported (FS 930, TNG 935, Hannay's Longsword-contemporary framing, the repo companion's own flag). The fact sheet's "c. 945–950" should be widened.
6. **The 1750s "La Tour Hue" demolition date** (DG-Supp Note 7 already in the repo) is independently corroborated by Pattou and French Wikipedia, both of which describe Hugues 1er as the fortifier. The repo's narrative is well-supported on this point.
