**Why these matter:** the three pre-1525 wills extend the documented Gurnay presence across
medieval/early-Tudor central Norfolk (Loddon 1373, Heigham 1434, Great Ellingham 1454) —
the geography the family occupied *before* the 1525 Lovell/Great-Ellingham inheritance that
brought the direct line (G17 Anthony) there. They speak to the L-68 question (what Gurnay
presence predates the Lovell inheritance, and how it relates to the West Barsham direct
line).

**Recommended next step (discovery, not yet promotion):** the NCC registered-copy wills are
a mapped FamilySearch film series (catalog `koha:278818`; Harsyk = DGS 008076261 per the FTS
skill). Resolve the DGS films for registers **Heydon, Surflete, Aleyn** via the catalog
film-table, then FTS-probe or image-walk for these three folios to pull the actual will
text. The 1373 Loddon and 1434 Heigham wills could name kin tying the central-Norfolk
Gurnays to the West Barsham line.
