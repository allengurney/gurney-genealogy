The four additional pages substantially improve and correct the prior interpretation.

Most important changes:

Page 4’s earlier “11-43” should now be read as either a misread, a mistaken shorthand, or possibly a poorly formed “11-42.”

3. **G2 Lester Hayes Gurney’s birth location is more precise.**  
   Page 5 says he was born **11-16-45 in New York City, St. Vincent’s Hospital**, while G3 was stationed in Manila.

4. **G3’s second marriage is newly captured.**  
   Page 5 records that Lester Sawyer Gurney III married **Dorothy Lillian Haben/Haden?** on **6 June 1965**. The surname is not fully secure from handwriting.

5. **Edith Walberg Scott Gurney’s profile is much clearer.**  
   Page 8 identifies her as **Edith Walberg Scott Gurney**, states she died **27 Dec. 1963 in Chatham, New Jersey**, says her mother died when Edith was 12, and says she married Scott to get away from “Papa.” It also gives her parents as:
   - **Edward Godfrey Walberg**, born 11 Nov. 1889.

   Page 8 says she completed a course on 8 June 1939 for **“Customers’ Men”** at the **New York Stock Exchange Institute**, possibly with a foreign-exchange specialization. About six months later, foreign-exchange trading closed due to the war and she quit.
