## Decisive result: Mary Wales married George Hill

The 1628 marriage page contains this faint but legible entry:

> George Hill & Mary Wallis were married the seventh day of August A[nn]o p[rae]dicto.

The annual heading is 1628. FamilySearch indexes the bride as Mary Wales and gives August 1628, Great Dunham, source detail `v1 p14`.

Original-image and index locators:

- ARK: `3:1:S3HY-61VS-VNK`
- Mary record: `CFCP-8KW2`; principal George record/citation: `CFCP-8K3Z`
- Digital folder: `004034287_001_M9KD-49J`
- Microfilm: `2262434`
- Image number: `25`
- Indexing batch: `I07439-6`

This **corrects Packet 32's high-confidence negative**. The entry was present on the relevant opening but was exceptionally pale.

Because the known Mary Wales was baptized at Great Dunham in 1611 and no second local Mary Wales has been established, this marriage is strongly inconsistent with her being John Gurney's emigrant wife. It reduces that identification from a live standout candidate to **unlikely (~5-10%)**, unless evidence emerges for another same-name Mary or for George Hill's early death followed by a second marriage and migration.

## Effect on the hypothesis

The absence of a Mary Wales burial no longer preserves the emigrant-wife hypothesis in the way anticipated by the brief, because a positive local marriage has now been image-confirmed. The stronger evidence is that Mary Wallis/Wales married George Hill at Great Dunham on 7 August 1628.

Confidence:

- Marriage reading and date: **very high (~98%)**.
- Identification with the 1611 daughter of Peter Wales: **high but not absolute (~90-95%)**.
- No Mary Wales burial in images 53-77: **high (~95%)**.
- No Gurney-form burial: **high for legible entries (~90%)**.
