# Packet 23 - East Dereham court Gurney hunt

Date of this report: 2026-06-17

## Scope and status

This packet tested two East Dereham court-book questions from lead L-98:

- whether FamilySearch Full-Text Search exposes any new pre-1636 male Gurney candidate before the known 1636 widow/free-bench problem;
- whether the later East Dereham copyhold references support Matthew or Michael Gurney.

The search was run against the two relevant FamilySearch image groups:

- DGS `004389483`, East Dereham court records, 1623-1689.
- DGS `004389277`, East Dereham court records, 1689-1820.

New local evidence saved:

- `sources/media/east-dereham-court-books-fts/_local/dereham-1758-michael-gurney-recital-2PP.jpg`

## Method

I used FamilySearch Full-Text Search inside the authenticated browser session, then opened the strongest hit image in the FamilySearch viewer. The OCR is used only as a locator and finding aid; the page image controls the surname reading.

## Findings

### Pre-1636 male Gurney hunt

No new pre-1636 male Gurney candidate was found in this pass.

The DGS `004389483` `Gurn*` results surfaced the already-known or already-suspected East Dereham material:

- `3:1:S3HY-D1S9-WWM`, tagged 1636, OCR token `Gurner`.
- `3:1:S3HY-D1S9-7H8`, tagged 1640, OCR token `Gurney`.
- `3:1:S3HY-D1S9-QX9`, tagged 1662, OCR token `Gurney` / Margery.
- `3:1:S3HY-D1S9-7CR`, tagged 1694, OCR token `Gurney`.

The variant probes did not reveal an earlier male. `Gourn*` returned no scoped hit. `Gorn*` produced only non-actionable noise in this context. `Gurner` returned the 1636 hit and one late false/irrelevant hit.

The `Margaret Gurn*` and `Margery Gurn*` probes mostly returned Margaret-only noise, plus the known East Dereham Gurney hits.

### Matthew / Michael reconciliation

The strongest new hit is FamilySearch ARK `3:1:S3HT-6179-2PP`, image 867 of 2,388 on DGS `004389277`.

The page carries a court dated 31 August 1758. In a property recital on the lower half of the left page, the text reads:

> "One Messuage Customary with one purpreshire and Twenty one acres and three roods of Land and Pasture Customary lying in diverse pieces in East Dearham aforesaid late in the Tenure of Michael Gurney ..."

This is a positive `Michael Gurney` reading on the 1758 page. It does not by itself overturn the earlier local reading of the 1697 image `3:1:S3HT-6179-KKL` as `Mathei`/Matthew Gurney; instead, it shows that the later East Dereham court-book chain contains at least one distinct or later-recited `Michael Gurney` reference.

The 1758 passage appears in a long admission/recital for Sarah Strudwick through Matthew Clements, her attorney. It lists several East Dereham copyholds and prior tenures. I did not attempt a full transcription of the whole 1758 entry because the packet question turns on the Gurney name and copyhold context.

## Answers to packet questions

1. New pre-1636 male Gurney candidate: none found in this FamilySearch FTS pass.
2. Does the packet add a male name behind the 1636 widow/free-bench problem: no. The 1636 and 1662/1665 targets remain the principal images for that question.
3. Matthew or Michael: both forms now have image-level support in different later East Dereham materials. The new 1758 image supports `Michael Gurney`; the existing 1697 media README records `Mathei`/Matthew for `KKL`.
4. Next best use: treat the 1758 `Michael Gurney` recital as a collateral copyhold clue, not as a direct solution to the pre-1636 husband problem.
