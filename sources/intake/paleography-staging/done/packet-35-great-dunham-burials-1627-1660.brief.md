# Packet 35 — Great Dunham burials 1627–1660: the Mary Wales death-elimination test + Gurney/Wales sweep

**Status:** Open brief, 2026-06-22. Companion to Packet 34 (Bawdeswell). Lead L-161 (G13 emigrant wife Mary). Flagged as a follow-up in Packet 32.

## The question
The candidate for the emigrant **John Gurney (G13)**'s wife **Mary** is **Mary Wales, chr. 1611 Great Dunham, dau. Peter Wales**. Packet 32 read the Great Dunham 1623–1633 marriages (no Wales/Gurney marriage) and Packet 33 the Bawdeswell registers. The Peter Wales household straddled both parishes (Mary 1611 + Elizabeth 1614 [bur. Gt Dunham 1616] at Great Dunham; Ann 1618 + Peter 1629 at Bawdeswell), so a death of Mary Wales could fall in *either* parish's burial register.

**Decisive test:** is there a **burial of a Mary Wales** — or a Mary of any married surname — in the Great Dunham burial register **1627–1660**? A local burial in this window would *eliminate* Mary Wales as the emigrant's wife who died in New England. Her **absence** keeps the candidacy alive and is itself a reportable finding.

The window is set to **1627–1660** (wider than the strict 1627–1635 death-elimination band) to (a) catch a later Mary Wales burial, (b) recover the burial of **Peter Wales the father** and his wife (which fixes the household's terminus and may name surviving children), and (c) sweep for **any Gurney-variant burial** in the parish that adjoins East Dereham.

## Register structure (established in Packet 32)
The book is **sectioned, each section chronological**: Baptisms first (images ~1–40), then Marriages (images ~44–52), then **Burials (images ~53–77, not yet read)**. Latin secretary hand throughout. The burial formula will run "[name] sepult'/sepultus/sepulta … [N] die mensis [Month] Anno Dni 16NN," with relationship descriptors (uxor / vidua / filius / filia).

## Reading aids / terminology
- **Wales** = *Wales / Wallis / Walys / Wayles / Wale*; **Peter** = *Petrus*; **Mary** = *Maria*.
- Burial Latin: *sepultus est / sepulta fuit … die … mensis … Anno Dni 16NN*; **uxor** (wife), **vidua** (widow), **filius/filia** (son/daughter).
- Gurney-variant head-forms: *Gurney / Gurnay / Gournay / Gurnie* → genitive *Gurnei/Gournei*; first-letter corruption (G→C/J) common.
- Pages carry a **"Soiled Document"** flag; staining heaviest at top-left, right-hand page generally cleaner.
