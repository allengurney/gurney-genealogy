## Scope and status

This is a bounded staging transcription of Packet 18 from the paleography staging README. It covers two FamilySearch images from Norwich Consistory Court registered-copy will material:

- 18a, `gressenhall-will-1630-p1-9Q4B-D.jpg`, ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSJ6-9Q4B-D`.
- 18b, `gressenhall-will-1630-p2-9Q4F-M.jpg`, ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSJ6-9Q4F-M`.

The main result is corrective: the Gressenhall will is the will of **John Thornton of Gressenhall, Norfolk, husbandman**, dated **16 May 1629** and proved **6 February 1630/31**. Richard Rivett is named as a son-in-law and executor, but the will names Richard's wife as **Marian** (or Mary/Marion), not Faith. On this reading, the packet does **not** identify Faith Rivett's father or maiden surname.

This report is staging only. Nothing has been promoted into research files, corpus supplement, data, or media.

## Best-effort transcription

### 18a, image `9Q4B-D`: opening and first bequests

The left page of the image contains the end of another will and the will of Margaret Raven. The Gressenhall/Thornton/Rivett will starts on the right page.

Opening, best effort:

> In the name of God Amen. The xvjth day of May in the fifth yeare of the raigne of our soveraigne Lord Charles by the grace of God of England, Scotland, ffrance and Ireland King, defender of the faith &c, Anno Domini 1629, I John Thornton of Gressengell in the Countie of Norff[olk], husbandman, being sicke in body yet of good and perfect remembrance, thankes be given to Almighty God, revoking all former wills by me made, doe ordaine, make and declare this my last will & testament in manner and forme following.

The religious and burial clauses follow. The burial clause asks burial in the churchyard of Gressenhall. The will gives 2s. 6d. toward the reparations of the parish church of Gressenhall, to be paid within one month after the testator's decease, and 2s. 6d. to the poor people of Gressenhall, to be divided among them at the executor's discretion.

The first major property clause reads, in substance:

> Item I doe give and bequeath unto Edward Thornton my sonne my copyhold tenement wherein I now dwell with the appurtenances and all the land to the same belonging, situate, lying, and being in Gressengell aforesaid ...

The same clause excepts or preserves a limited copyhold interest connected with land lately surrendered to Thomas Blade and Elizabeth his wife and their heirs. The exact legal wording of that exception should be rechecked before promotion.

### 18b, image `9Q4F-M`: conditions, children, Rivett clause, Anne, executor

The continuation imposes payment conditions on Edward Thornton and his heirs or assigns. It refers to sums due to the testator's children named in the will.

The daughter Elizabeth clause reads, best effort:

> First I doe give and bequeath unto my daughter Elizabeth Blade, her executors and administrators, the sum of five pounds of lawfull money of England, to be paid by my sonne Edward his heirs or assigns ...

The Richard Rivett clause reads, best effort:

> Item I give and bequeath unto my wellbeloved sonne in law Richard Ryvett and to Marian his wife, their executors and administrators, in performance of the condition of a bond entered by me to the said Richard Ryvett, the sum of ten pounds of lawfull money of England, to be paid by the said Edward my sonne ...

`Marian` is my best reading of Richard Rivett's wife. `Mary` or `Marion` remains possible, but I do not read the name as Faith.

The son John clause reads, in substance:

> Item I give and bequeath to John Thornton my sonne the sum of twenty pounds of lawfull money of England, to be paid by my said sonne Edward ...

The payment timing appears staged over several years after the testator's death. The exact schedule is lower confidence.

The foster-child / parish-charge clause reads, best effort:

> ... if the said John Thornton my sonne shall not within one month next after my decease provide for Anne Sitho [?], whome I have brought up of a childe, soe that my executor and the township of Gressengell be freed and discharged of all such charges as hereafter they may be charged withall for the maintenance of the said Anne during her naturall life, then my said guift to him shall be void ...

The foster child's surname is uncertain. I read it as `Sitho` or `Sithe`, but this should be checked from the image before promotion.

Later bequests include:

> Item I give and bequeath to Jane Blade ...
>
> Item I give and bequeath unto Susan Lowes my grandchilde the sum of ten shillings ...

The will then gives the residue of goods and chattels, after debts, legacies, church and poor payments, funeral charges, and proving charges, to Richard Rivett:

> And I doe ordaine and make Richard Ryvett my sonne in lawe the sole executor of this my last will and Testament ...

Witnesses, best effort:

> John Johnson his marke; Richard Rivett his marke; witness Tho: Herrour [?]

The probate clause states that the testament was proved before Thomas Browne, clerk, surrogate, on 6 February 1630, i.e. 6 February 1630/31 by modern reckoning.

## Answers to the packet questions

1. Testator: **John Thornton of Gressenhall, Norfolk, husbandman**.
2. Will date: **16 May 1629**.
3. Probate: **6 February 1630/31**, before Thomas Browne, clerk, surrogate.
4. Page layout: 18a contains other wills on the left page; the Gressenhall will begins on the right page and continues through 18b.
5. Son and heir/property: Edward Thornton receives the testator's copyhold tenement where John Thornton then dwelt, with appurtenances and land in Gressenhall, subject to exceptions/conditions and payments to other named children.
6. Daughter Elizabeth: Elizabeth Blade, daughter of the testator; Thomas Blade and Elizabeth his wife also appear in the surrendered-land exception.
7. Richard Rivett: Richard Ryvett/Rivett is explicitly called the testator's son-in-law and is made sole executor. He also signs or marks as a witness.
8. Richard Rivett's wife: named as **Marian** (or Mary/Marion), not Faith. The will does not support identifying Faith Rivett's maiden surname.
9. Thornton son-in-law: I do not find a separate Thornton son-in-law. `John Thornton` is the testator; `Edward Thornton` and `John Thornton` are sons.
10. Foster child: Anne Sitho/Sithe [?], whom the testator says he brought up from childhood; maintenance language is designed to free the executor and township of Gressenhall from future charges.
11. Other legatees: Jane Blade and Susan Lowes, the latter called the testator's grandchild.
12. Occupation/status: John Thornton is styled `husbandman`.

## Confidence notes

High confidence:

- Testator: John Thornton of Gressenhall, Norfolk, husbandman.
- Will date: 16 May 1629.
- Probate date: 6 February 1630/31.
- Edward Thornton and John Thornton are sons of the testator.
- Elizabeth Blade is a daughter of the testator.
- Richard Rivett/Ryvett is son-in-law and sole executor.
- Richard Rivett's wife is not named Faith in this will.

Medium confidence:

- Richard Rivett's wife reads `Marian`; Mary/Marion is a plausible normalized form.
- Thomas Blade and Elizabeth his wife are tied to the copyhold exception/surrender.
- Susan Lowes is the testator's grandchild.

Low confidence / needs re-check:

- Foster child surname, read here as Anne Sitho/Sithe [?].
- Exact legal wording of the surrendered-land exception in the Edward Thornton devise.
- Exact payment schedule for the legacies due from Edward Thornton.
- Final witness surname, read here as Tho: Herrour [?].

No durable recognition note was added. The useful result is genealogical/source-identification rather than a reusable paleographic lesson: the packet's machine-derived `Richard Rivett son-in-law` clue was real, but the image read reverses the proposed Faith-maiden-name inference.
