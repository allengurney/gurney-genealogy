## Questions, in priority order

1. **Court dates.** For each opening in 1426–1485, read the court heading where present: court type (curia baronis / leet / generalis), the regnal-year date (anno regni Elizabethe N), and convert to calendar year. A simple image→court-date table is itself a deliverable (it calibrates the whole book).
2. **Rivet entries.** Find every entry naming **Rivet / Ryvett / Revett / Ryvet** (any spelling). The manor's courts governed Garveston copyhold. Specifically hoped for:
   - a **death presentment / heir admission for Robert Rivett of Garveston**, who died intestate by 1597 (his administration was granted 1597 to widow Agnes). His known children (bp. Garveston, by his first wife): Agnes 1563, Robert 1565, William 1571, Miles(?) 1571, Richard 1575, **Margarett 1577/8**. Any entry naming his heir(s), a guardianship of a minor heir, or a surrender-to-uses naming children would be decisive for the L-128 question (which Margaret Rivet married Francis Gurney in 1611).
   - any tenancy of **Francis Rivet** (m. Elizabeth Podmer 1584; children incl. Margaret bp 1586) — admissions, surrenders, essoins, suit-of-court lists.
3. **Podmer** — any occurrence (also Pitmer/Pytmer/Patmer/Petman).
4. **Gurney/Gurnay** — any occurrence (none expected this early; flag if seen).
5. In suit lists / homage lists (three-column tenant lists with "essoin/alleg." markers), note Rivet and Podmer names even without further text.

## Context and terminology

- Members of the manor: Garveston(e), Reymerston, Thuxton, Mattishall (+ Mattishall Burgh), Yaxham; the court book heads township sections "Ex'te Mattishall" etc. Margin notes include "fest" (fine), "adm[issio]", "sur[sum]red[didit]" (surrendered), "p[re]sent[atum]".
- Known neighbours/tenant families seen in later courts: Allen, Bacheler/Bacheler (Gregory), Futter/ffutter (Arthur), Howlett, Russell (Edward), Skoulding, Mussett.
