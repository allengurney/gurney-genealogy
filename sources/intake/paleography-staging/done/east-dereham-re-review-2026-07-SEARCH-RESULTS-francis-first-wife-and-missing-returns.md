# Two Norfolk searches — results, 26–27 July 2026

Answers to the two searches set against `FINDINGS-2026-07-22-deviation-audit.md`: a first wife for
Francis Gurney 1606–1611, and the fate of the East Dereham annual returns for 1611–1615. Plus the
Wainford provenance question.

**Headline.** Search 1 is a clean negative across every index that can be searched. Search 2 is also now
a hard negative: **the 1611–1615 East Dereham annual returns do not survive**, and no second witness
exists for those years. That is a permanent evidentiary limit, established by examining the register
file complete for the first time.

> **Revision, 2026-07-27 (afternoon).** An earlier draft of §2 argued that D9's premise was wrong and
> that the missing membranes might be filed out of order in the 34 leaves of PD 86/41 that had never
> been pulled. Those leaves have now been pulled and read. They are **1635–1641**, the file is strictly
> chronological, and nothing is misfiled. D9's observation was right; my proposed escape from it was
> wrong. §2 below is rewritten accordingly.

## 1 — A first wife for Francis Gurney, 1606–1611: NEGATIVE

No marriage of a Francis Gurney (any variant) exists in Norfolk between 1579 and 1628 other than the
known Gurney–Rybett marriage of 23 September 1611 at Norwich St Martin at Palace.

**Indexing artefact worth carrying forward:** Ancestry 61045 indexes the 1611 marriage as a **baptism** —
"Frant Gurney, Abt 1611, Baptism, 23 Sep 1611, Norwich, St Martin at Palace", no spouse field. An
event-type-filtered marriage search would have missed it.

### The residue that cannot be closed online

For 1606–1611 the surviving licence sources, per the Norfolk Record Office's own guidance, are the
**Norwich Consistory Court** bonds (microfiche 1558–1902, with gaps), the **Norwich Archdeaconry**
registers of licences granted 1590–1632 in the administration act books, and the **letters of matrimony
1590–1609** (ANW 22/2). The finding aids are **Bolingbroke's calendars, 1476–1708, NRO HMN 313–317**,
with name indexes on the searchroom shelves. Norfolk and Norwich Archdeaconry *bonds* proper begin
1670/1712, after the window. FindMyPast's *Norfolk Marriage Bonds 1557–1915* is image-browse with no
name index; FamilySearch's index of the same collection carries five Gurney entries in total, all 1749
or later. **No online name index reaches this window** — closing it means the NRO searchroom or a paid
NRO lookup against HMN 313–317.

### Consequence

The audit staked B's upside precisely here: *"if the Francis 1606–1611 marriage sweep returns a first
wife, B should jump sharply."* It returned none. The pro-B reading of the apprenticeship constraint gets
no support from the searchable record; the 1606–1608 birth sliver stands, and so does the three-to-five
year pre-marital birth Candidate B still requires.

Stated fairly: absence of a marriage record is not absence of a marriage, and the Norfolk licence layer
is genuinely untested. But the East Dereham household was producing children by 27 May 1610 with **no
located marriage of any kind** for Francis-A before September 1611, across two independent Norfolk
parish-register programmes, the England-wide IGI, the Bishop of London's allegations, and the Norfolk
probate-and-licence index. That pushes weight toward the anti-B reading — that the East Dereham
*ffrancis* is a different man.

## 2 — The missing 1611–1615 returns: THEY DO NOT SURVIVE

**Five annual returns — 25 March 1611 to 25 March 1616 — are lost. There is no second witness. This is
a permanent evidentiary limit.**

The Norfolk Record Office catalogues the file as **PD 86/41, "Indented register bills, 1593–1641"**,
digitised **image by image** as 110 contiguous Parts, `4034129-00692` – `4034129-00801`. The March–July
2026 analysis used `00693`–`00768` — 76 of 110. The remaining **34 leaves (`00692`, `00769`–`00801`)
were pulled from FindMyPast on 2026-07-27** and read. Masters at `sources/media/nro-pd-86-41/_local/`.

Every membrane carries a modern pencil year at its head, and most returns also carry an in-parchment
heading, so the whole file can be year-mapped without transcribing a line. It is **strictly
chronological — no membrane is out of sequence**:

| Images | Return |
|---|---|
| 00693 / 00694, 00697 | 1593-4 |
| 00704 / 00705 | 1601 |
| 00708 / 00709 | 1608 |
| 00712 / 00713 | 1609 |
| 00720 / 00721 → 00725 | **1610** — in-parchment "East Dereham 1610", through marriages and burials |
| *— nothing —* | **1611-12, 1612-13, 1613-14, 1614-15, 1615-16** |
| 00726 / 00727 → 00731 | **1616-17** — "25 of march 1616 unto the 25 of march 1617" |
| 00732 → 00734 | 1618-19 |
| 00735 / 00736, 00738 | 1620 |
| 00739 → | 1621 |
| 00752 | 1632-33 |
| 00770 / 00771, 00772, 00777 / 00778 | 1635-36 |
| 00779 / 00780 | 1636-37 |
| 00783 / 00784 | 1637-38 |
| 00793 / 00794 → 00798 | 1640-41; 00798 is the last membrane |
| 00799 → 00801 | END OF ITEM target card, then leader frames |

So the never-examined tail is **1635–1641**, not misfiled early membranes, and the file runs from the
1610 return straight to the 1616-17 return. **D9's observation was right.**

**Index-side corroboration.** FamilySearch's *England, Norfolk, Parish Registers (County Record Office),
1510–1997* (collection 1416598) — the index built on this same NRO material — returns 487 hits for
Dereham-standardised places in 1611–1615, and every one on the first hundred is **West** Dereham. Not a
single East Dereham event is indexed in the window. (FamilySearch standardises "East Dereham" to
"Dereham", and the place parameter ranks rather than filters, so this is corroboration, not an
independent proof.)

**No archdeacon's counterpart exists either.** FindMyPast's *Norfolk Archdeacon's Transcripts
1600–1812*, archive reference `AT EAST DEREHAM`, 243 images, opens on a film target card reading
"NORWICH DIOCESE / NORFOLK ARCHDEACONRY / HINGHAM DEANERY / ARCHDEACONS TRANSCRIPTS / ITEM 5 / **EAST
DEREHAM 1725—1811/12**". The archdeacon's series for this parish begins in 1725 and cannot cover the
window.

### What it means

The natural birth window for a first child of the 23 September 1611 marriage — roughly 1612–1615 — is
**permanently unobservable at East Dereham**. Candidate B is not refuted by the absence of a 1612–1615
Gurney baptism, because the pages that would carry one are gone and will not turn up; but that absence
can never now be converted into evidence either way. The line of enquiry is **closed, not pending**.

Full account, corrections forced, and the retrieval recipe:
[`packet-58-east-dereham-1611-1615.md`](packet-58-east-dereham-1611-1615.md). Whole-file year map:
[`../../../../sources/validations/east-dereham-pd-86-41-register-structure-and-chronology.md`](../../../../sources/validations/east-dereham-pd-86-41-register-structure-and-chronology.md).

### The pencil years are corroborated in-parchment, and the transcripts question is closed

**Corroboration, first sample.** Image `00793` carries the modern pencil `PD 86/41/20 · 1640-41` at the
head of the mount. Directly beneath it, in the contemporary hand and ink:

> "**Bills Indented** of all the Christinings, Marriages and Buriall[s] in y[e] towne of East Dereham in
> y[e] County of Norff[olk] **from our Ladie 1640 unto our Ladie 1641**, Mr Jo: Bretton being vicar of
> the [parish], Jo: Sturgeon gent and Edward Rudd being churchwardens for y[e] said yeere as followeth.
> **Christnings**"

Lady Day to Lady Day, matching the pencil exactly. The archivist's pencil years are transcriptions of
the in-parchment headings, not inferences, and the same pattern is visible on 00770/71 (1635-36),
00779/80 (1636-37) and 00783/84 (1637-38), each of which also carries its own in-parchment date span.

**No transcript alternative exists — confirmed from the NRO's own summary.** *Parish Registers and
Transcripts in the Norfolk Record Office* (summary PDF, last revised 12 October 2022), East Dereham
(PD 86):

| | Baptisms | Marriages | Burials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original registers | c.1549–1596 *(unfit for production)*, then **1679**–1902 | c.1549–1596 *(unfit)*, then **1679**–1914 | c.1549–1596 *(unfit)*, then **1679**–1908 |
| Archdeacon's and Bishop's transcripts | **1698**–1885 | **1698**–1837 | **1698**–1885 |

So there is **no bound parish register for East Dereham between 1596 and 1679**, and archdeacon's and
bishop's transcripts do not begin until **1698**. The loose register bills of PD 86/41 are the *only*
witness to the entire 1593–1641 period, which is why the loss of five of its annual returns is absolute.
(FindMyPast's `AT EAST DEREHAM` film item, at 1725–1811/12, is one item within that post-1698 series.)

### Two corrections this forces on existing repo statements

- **The chronology lattice is superseded.** Its interpolated rows (`00728/29/30 = 1617`,
  `00731/32 = 1618`, `00733/34 = 1619`) rest on a one-page-per-return assumption that is false.
- **"Page 00725 = the 1615 register year" is wrong.** 00724/00725 are the marriages-and-burials
  membranes of the **1610** return — which the year map makes unavoidable, since no 1615 return exists.
  This independently confirms D10's page reassignment, and every date derived from the old assignment,
  including the Marye and Agnes burial years, needs re-deriving.

## 3 — Philis Wainford's source: identified, and it does not support the Ryvett claim

In Charles E. G. Pease, *The Descendants of Anthony Gurney* (2016), the document's own numbered source
list gives:

- **#177** = Sir Bernard Burke, *Genealogical & Heraldic History Landed Gentry GB/I*, 1894 (Harrison,
  London) — as the repo's L-71 note records.
- **#254** = "Philis Wainford http://www.wainford.net/, 'Wainford & Gurney family connections'; report,
  June 2012–2016."

Her source is **her own family website**, a report compiled 2012–2016 — not an archival source. And
every `254` superscript in the genealogy sits on the **19th-to-21st-century Wainford branch**: the
illegitimate line of Richard Hanbury Gurney (1783–1854) by **Susan Wainford** of Saxlingham, running
down to **Philis Aster Wainford** herself, daughter of Philip Joseph Wainford and Mary Helen Aster, of
Georgia and Pennsylvania. She is a modern American descendant documenting her own recent line.

**The Francis Gurney entry carries no source superscript at all.** Verbatim:

> "Francis married Margaret Ryvett. They had two children: John and Anne. John Gurney was born in 1610
> in Circa and died in Chelmsford, Essex. John married Grizell Fletcher. Anne Gurney was born in 1610
> in Circa. Anne married John Gilman."

Neither #177 nor #254 nor anything else is attached, while entries on the same page do carry
superscripts (`Francis Gurney1`, `John Gurney2,3`). **The Ryvett claim is unsourced in Pease, and it did
not come from Wainford.** Her working papers are not traceable: wainford.net no longer resolves.

Two things the full quotation does show:

- **Pease's John is unambiguously the Braintree emigrant.** "Grizell Fletcher" is the colonial John
  Gurney's second wife, married at Braintree 12 November 1661. "Died in Chelmsford, Essex" is a compiler
  slip — the colonial John died at Braintree, and Chelmsford is also a Massachusetts town. So Pease does
  make the Candidate-B identification explicitly; he simply names nothing for it.
- **"Anne married John Gilman"** is a second, testable emigration-shaped claim in the same sentence.
  Tested against FindMyPast Norfolk Banns and Marriages (`lastname=gurn*` × `spouselastname=gil*`):
  four rows, none an Anne Gurney × John Gilman, none before 1667. Not found in Norfolk.

## 3a — Francis G14's death: the date holds, but it is transcript-level twice over

The repo states Francis G14 was buried at **St Botolph without Bishopsgate, London, 9 January 1646/7**,
citing FreeREG and noting it corrects Boyd's misread 1641. Checked 2026-07-27:

- **The FreeREG entry is real and was seen.** Searching FreeREG for surname Gurney, 1640–1650, County
  London (City), Place Bishopsgate returns exactly one exact result: *Francis GURNEY · Burial ·
  09 Jan 1646/47 · Bishopsgate : St Botolph*.
- **But its register type is "Other Transcript."** FreeREG's transcriber worked from a transcript, not
  from the parish register. So the chain to the original is: repo → FreeREG → an unnamed transcript →
  the register. Nobody has seen the register entry.
- **Ancestry's 18.15-million-record *London, England, Church of England Baptisms, Marriages and Burials,
  1538–1812* (collection 1624) does not carry it.** `Fran*_Gurn*` returns 52 rows across the whole
  collection; the only burial anywhere near is *Frances Gurney, 22 April 1649, St Dunstan in the West*,
  a woman. Either that collection's St Botolph Bishopsgate coverage has a Civil-War-years hole, or the
  transcript FreeREG used records something the register does not.

**Probate is negative in every jurisdiction searched, with one court left.**

| Court | Result |
|---|---|
| Prerogative Court of Canterbury | negative (Discovery, PROB 11, 1637–1665) |
| **Commissary Court of London (London Division), 1626–1649 & 1661–1700** | **negative** — the printed Marc Fitch index p. 296 gives five Gurney/Gourny records (Daniel 1690, Miles 1636, Tho. of Fulham 1676, Thomazine widow 1680, Wm. 1665) and no Francis. This court held St Benet Fink |
| Norfolk church courts, 1371–1858 | negative (Ancestry 62679, `Fran*_Gurn*` = 2 rows, both Gurnell 1717) |
| Essex Record Office, 1600–1700 | negative (Essex Archives Online, keyword Gurney) |
| **Archdeaconry Court of London** | **UNCHECKED — the one remaining jurisdiction** |

Two actions would close this: (1) the **original St Botolph without Bishopsgate burial register** at the
London Archives (LMA reference P82/BOT2), to convert the death date from transcript to record; and
(2) the **Archdeaconry of London** probate index. Bear in mind Francis was financially ruined by the
1634 total land sale, so no probate at all is a perfectly ordinary outcome and the negative may simply
be true.

## 4 — Bycatch and new open questions

- **A year conflict on the East Dereham Francis burial.** The repo carries Entry F — Francis Gurney,
  burial 8 November **1633**, East Dereham (FamilySearch VNN2-H8S, no parent) — read as a probable son
  of Francis G14 born c.1611–1618. Ancestry 61045 indexes what is almost certainly the same line as
  **"Francis Garndy, burial, 8 November 1649, East Dereham"**. Same day and month, sixteen years apart,
  surname read `Garndy` not `Gurney`. **PD 86/41 is now confirmed to end at 1640-41**, so a genuine 1649
  burial cannot sit in that file at all — either Ancestry's year is wrong, or the row comes from a
  different source. Still open. Note also that if Entry F's Francis was born in the lost 1611–1615
  window his baptism is unrecoverable; only a 1616–1618 birth remains testable.
- **No Francis Gurney probate in Essex.** Essex Archives Online, keyword `Gurney`, 1600–1700: two pages
  of results, no Gurney will or administration for a Francis. The catalogue indexes its probate series
  by testator name (a 1683 Gurney marriage licence bond, D/ALL 1683/58, shows name-level indexing works),
  so a Francis Gurney will there would have surfaced. With PCC negative, FamilySearch full-text negative
  for the London Commissary and Archdeaconry of Norwich, and Norfolk (Ancestry 62679) now negative,
  **Francis G14's probate is unlocated in every jurisdiction so far searched.** The remaining candidates
  are the LMA London Commissary and Archdeaconry of London registers, unindexed online.
- **A gentleman Gurney in Interregnum Essex.** Essex QS 1654: recognizance for "Adiell GURNEY,
  gentleman… of Chipping Ongar" (Q/SR 363/27). An unusual forename and a gentry Gurney in Essex in the
  right decade for the Maldon Gournay cluster. Not chased.
- **A Wainford–Gurney marriage in mid-Norfolk in 1579.** "France Gurnard × Mary Wainford, 5 October
  1579, North Elmham" — five miles from East Dereham — appears in both FindMyPast Norfolk Banns and
  Marriages and Ancestry 61045. Almost certainly coincidence given §3 above, but the surname is in play,
  so it is on the record.
- **L-153's ark is wrong.** The lead records a "Francis Gurnay marriage at **Mattishall Burgh**" at
  FamilySearch ark `3:1:S3HY-65RF-GL`. Resolved 2026-07-27 under an authenticated session: that ark is
  in *England, Norfolk, Parish Registers (County Record Office), 1510–1997* → **Caistor next Yarmouth →
  Baptisms, Marriages, Burials → 1563–1837**, a 273-image waypoint. Not Mattishall Burgh. Independently,
  no Francis Gurney-variant marriage at Mattishall or Mattishall Burgh appears in Ancestry 9852, Ancestry
  61045, or FindMyPast Norfolk Banns and Marriages. The lead needs re-basing on whatever the full-text
  hit actually was, or closing.
