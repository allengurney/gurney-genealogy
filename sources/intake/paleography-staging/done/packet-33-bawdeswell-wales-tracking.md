# Packet 33 - Bawdeswell Wales tracking

Date of this report: 2026-06-22

## Scope and status

Read the staged Bawdeswell baptism openings `bw-img-020.jpg` through `bw-img-027.jpg`, the marriage-section samples `bw-img-045.jpg` and `bw-img-046.jpg`, and the additional structure samples. Full-page and manual line-strip enhancements were generated under `snippets/packets-32-33/`.

The baptism images are low-resolution, faded, and affected by gutter loss and overwritten entries. Strong-ink entries are readable; some pale entries are not secure enough for name-level negatives.

## Result

### Wales-family entries

The packet does **not** image-confirm the expected Ann Wales baptism in 1618 or Peter Wales baptism in 1629. No secure Wales / Wallis / Walys / Wayles surname was identified in the staged baptism openings, and no Gurney-form surname was identified.

This is **not yet a reliable parish negative**. The staged date map was partly wrong:

- Mary Wales's 1611 baptism belongs to **Great Dunham**, so it should not have been expected in `bw-img-020.jpg`.
- `bw-img-024.jpg` begins at 1619 on manuscript page 32.
- `bw-img-027.jpg` reaches about 1631 on manuscript pages 38-39, not 1635.
- The darkest late-1617/1618 portion is faded and overwritten; no Ann Wales reading can be promoted from it.

The absence of the indexed Ann and Peter entries from their predicted openings raises a waypoint/date-range problem. Their exact indexed records should be used to recover the correct film image before the Wales household's Bawdeswell presence is treated as manuscript-confirmed.

### Post-1627 mother test

No Mary Wales, Mary Gurney-form, or securely identifiable Wales-family mother was found in the staged 1627-1631 baptism pages. Confidence is **moderate (~75-80%) for the legible entries**, but lower in faded sections. Because the packet stops around 1631 and contains unreadable patches, this does not eliminate or confirm the candidate.

No burial page covering 1627-1635 was staged. `bw-img-048.jpg` instead samples burials from much later periods, so the burial-elimination test remains unperformed.

### Marriage gap

The staged marriage-section samples support the reported gap:

- `bw-img-045.jpg` contains a continuous marriage run from 1562 through 1582.
- `bw-img-046.jpg` resumes with marriages in 1709 and the 1710s.
- No contrary 17th-century marriage run appeared in the staged baptism or structure samples.

This strongly supports a missing 1583-1708 marriage run. Because only selected images from the 111-image composite were staged, an absolute claim that no interleaved marriage page survives anywhere in the volume would require a full image-by-image structure sweep.

## Explicit negatives and limits

- No image-confirmed Ann Wales baptism in the staged 1617-1619 transition.
- No image-confirmed Peter Wales baptism in the staged 1628-1630 area.
- No secure Wales or Gurney-form entry in the staged pages.
- No post-1627 Mary-as-mother finding that eliminates the emigrant-wife candidate.
- No relevant burial run was supplied.
- The staged baptism sequence does not extend through 1635.

## Next step

Extend the baptism pull beyond manuscript page 39 to cover 1632-1635, and stage the relevant burial sequence before treating Mary's later absence as evidence.
