# Packet 34 - Bawdeswell Wales baptisms and burials

Date of this report: 2026-06-23

## Scope and status

Read the staged Bawdeswell masters: images 18-35 and 67 for baptisms and structure, and images 46-52 for marriages/burials. Enhancement sheets were generated under `snippets/packets-34-36/`.

The decisive work was resolving the three FamilySearch index records that had been normalized to Bawdeswell. Their original-place and film fields show that they are **Shimpling records, not Bawdeswell records**.

## Decisive index resolution

All three records point to digital folder `004008716_011_M9LD-F15`, microfilm `1657289`, indexing batch `V01545-0`:

| Record | Event | Displayed place | Event Place (Original) | Image |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ann Wales, `VNN6-7MQ` | Christened 28 January 1618; father P Wales | Bawdeswell | **Shimpling, Norfolk** | 14 |
| Peter Wales, `VNN6-7S2` | Christened 26 December 1629; parents Peter and Mary | Bawdeswell | **Shimpling, Norfolk** | 15 |
| Peter Wales, `VNN6-D2B` | Buried 20 November 1634; father Peter | Bawdeswell | **Shimpling, Norfolk** | 47 |

The record pages do not supply an accessible original image through the Bawdeswell volume. The image numbers belong to the Shimpling film identified above.

This explains why Packets 33-34 could not find Ann or Peter in the Bawdeswell register. The earlier claim that Peter Wales's household had moved to Bawdeswell by 1618 arose from FamilySearch's normalized display place and is not supported by these records' original-place fields.

## Bawdeswell image read

No secure Wales / Wallis / Walys / Wayles entry and no secure Gurney / Gournay / Gurnay / Gurnie entry was found in the staged Bawdeswell baptism pages.

No Mary Wales burial or Gurney-form burial was found in images 46-52. Those leaves are disordered and chiefly cover later seventeenth-century years; they do not form a continuous, demonstrable 1627-1640 burial run. The Bawdeswell death-elimination test therefore cannot be treated as exhaustive from this image set.

## Effect on the Mary Wales hypothesis

The Bawdeswell branch of the test was misdirected. Absence from these Bawdeswell pages is not evidence that Mary Wales emigrated or disappeared after marriage. The relevant later children and the 1634 child burial belong to **Shimpling**.

The Peter-and-Mary parent pairing on the 1629 Shimpling record is nevertheless useful household evidence: Peter Wales's wife was named Mary.

## Confidence and limits

- Shimpling reassignment: **very high (~99%)**, from explicit original-place and film metadata.
- No secure Wales/Gurney form in the staged Bawdeswell baptism pages: **high for legible entries (~90%)**, lower in faded and gutter-damaged lines.
- No Bawdeswell Mary Wales burial: only a **limited staged-page negative**, not a complete 1627-1640 parish negative.

## Follow-up

Use Shimpling microfilm `1657289`, digital folder `004008716_011_M9LD-F15`, images 14-15 and 47 to obtain the original Ann/Peter entries and neighboring leaves. Any further post-1627 mother or burial test for this household should include Shimpling before returning to Bawdeswell.
