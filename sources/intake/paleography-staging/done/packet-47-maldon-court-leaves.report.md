Working crops and locator sheets: `snippets/packet-46-48-analysis/`.

## Bottom line

The packet resolves into one real Francis Gurnay conveyance, one uncertain marginal/court-note Gurney-like token, and one priority false positive.

- The 1677 "John Gurney" FTS hit does not read as John Gurney on the image. The court entry names William Franny/ffranny and his daughters Elizabeth and Hannah; the "apprentice" language belongs to the Franny/Richard Eddye matter, not to John Gurney.
- The undated conveyance page is a real Gurnay hit: it names Francis Gurnay/Gurney as the grantee/within-named party in a deed involving Sarah Woodward and William Pollard.
- The 1676 election page includes a possible `Mr Jeffrey Gorney/Gourney` note near the lower portion, but this is not the main election return language and I would not promote it without a second pass or adjacent image context.

## 1677 leaf - `maldon-court-1677-9Q97-YSLK-2HC.jpg`

Crop set: `maldon-1677-right-court-contact-sheet.png`; sheet: `maldon-1677-right-court-sheet.png`.

The priority FTS target is a false positive for Gurney. The readable Latin court entry is headed for Maldon court of record and opens with an appearance/presentation by Elizabeth Franny of Boreham in Essex, widow, concerning William Franny of Maldon. Key names in the entry:

- Elizabeth Franny/ffranny of Boreham, Essex, widow.
- William Franny/ffranny of Maldon, deceased.
- Elizabeth and Hannah, daughters of William Franny.
- Richard Eddye/Eddy of Maldon, probably tied to the apprentice clause.
- Hannah Franny, infant, petitioning by guardian/next friend language in the same matter.

I read no John Gurney/Gournay in the priority passage. The surname forms that FTS likely turned into Gurney are Franny/ffranny. This means the 1677 leaf should not be used as evidence for John Gurney, indictment, recognizance, sureties, or an apprenticeship.

## 1676 leaf - `maldon-court-1676-9Q97-YSL8-9VR.jpg`

Crop set: `maldon-1676-right-election-contact-sheet.png`; sheet: `maldon-1676-right-election-sheet.png`.

The visible main text is an election/return context:

- It refers to a writ directed to the bailiffs/burgesses from John Moorecroft, esq., sheriff of Essex.
- It concerns choosing a burgess for Parliament in the place of Sir John Tyrrell.
- The dwelling-house phrase reads as a house "called the Bell" in Maldon.

I do not see a secure Gurney token in the body of the election return. Near the lower part of the page there is a marginal/court-note-looking line that may read `Mr Jeffrey Gorney` or `Mr Jeffrey Gourney`, dated around 19 March 1676/7. Because this is not in the main election sentence and the surname is not fully secure, treat it as a paleographic lead only.

## Undated leaf - `maldon-court-undated-9Q97-YSL8-9NC.jpg`

Crop set: `maldon-undated-right-lower-contact-sheet.png`; sheet: `maldon-undated-right-lower-sheet.png`.

This is the strongest Gurnay result in the packet. The lower-right deed memorandum reads, in substance:

> Sealed, delivered, and seisin given... by the within named Sarah Woodward and William Pollard... and by them delivered to the within named Francis Gurnay...

The final wording continues with delivery "to him and his heirs according to the purport, effect, and true meaning of this deed." Names visible around the execution/certification area:

- Sarah Woodward.
- William W. Pollard.
- Hannah Pollard.
- Francis Gurnay/Gurney, grantee/within-named party.
- Witnesses/marks include John Simonds, Robert Jounyngs/Jennings, William Carr, and William Slaughter.

The staged image does not expose the beginning of the deed cleanly enough to date it from the opening formula; it refers to "the day and year first above written."
