The will is Richard Podmer/Podmore of Leaton, in the parish of St Mary, Shrewsbury, Shropshire, dated 14 September 1614 and proved 22 May 1615. The family and legatee structure is local/Shropshire-facing. Visible names include:

- Wife Elizabeth Podmer.
- Sons and/or close male kin including Thomas, Richard, John, Henry, Edward, William, and Samuel Podmer/Podmore names in the bequest sequence.
- Daughters/younger women including Elizabeth and Mary/Margery forms in the family bequest sequence.
- Local or related names including Thomas Cleling/Clelling and other Shropshire-context names.

I saw no Norfolk place name, no Hingham, Garveston, East Tuddenham, no Rivett/Rivet/Ryvett token, and no Gurney/Gournay token. This will does not appear to connect the Leaton Podmers to the Norfolk Podmer strays.

Because the question was a long-shot kin scan, I did not produce a full word-for-word transcription of the whole will. If a full Podmer family reconstruction is later wanted, start from images `00736` lower will body and `00737` upper continuation.

### Mary Gurney passage

The manuscript confirms the NEHGR abstract's core wording and does not add a residence or explicit kinship qualifier for Mary beyond her father:

> ...unto Mary Gurney the daughter of John Gurney...

The line appears immediately after the Grisell Lloyd bequest. The visible sequence reads:

- A bequest to "my kinswoman Grisell Lloyd daughter of James Lloyd" of ten pounds current money, payable after news of the testator's death by order of the executor.
- Then the Mary Gurney clause: "unto Mary Gurney the daughter of John Gurney..." followed by a money bequest.

The amount after Mary is partly affected by image/line-strip overlap, but the old abstract's stand-alone wording "Mary Gurney the daughter of John Gurney" is image-supported. I would not add any further qualifier such as residence, "kinswoman," or New England for Mary from this image; those qualifiers belong to Grisell Lloyd and the Lloyd network, not visibly to Mary.
