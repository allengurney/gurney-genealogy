# Packet 36 — Maldon borough court books: which John Gournay? (L-14 / Bernau "Problem")

**Status:** Open brief, 2026-06-22. Images to stage (FamilySearch das/v2 pull from the arks below). Bears on lead L-14 (John Gurney of Maldon, 1681) and the Bernau "Problem" (whether the 1655 Maldon John became the Norwich Quaker banker).

## The question
FamilySearch Full-Text Search surfaces a **John Gournay** in the Maldon, Essex borough/court records dealing with property and goods that had been **Francis Gournay's** (Francis of Maldon, the salt-refiner, d. 1677). The machine transcripts are court-hand OCR salad — dates and relationship descriptors are lost or garbled. The decisive question: **which John is this?**
- the **bachelor John Gournay who died at Maldon in 1681** (Francis's *brother* — a son of Francis G14, hence a sibling-generation-mate of the emigrant G13; lead L-14), or
- the **son/heir John Gournay, b. 1655** (Francis-of-Maldon's eldest son, whom Burke's 1875 pedigree says "joined the Society of Friends" — i.e. the candidate for John of St Gregory's, Norwich, founder of the banking Gurneys; Bernau's "Problem").

An image read that fixes the **exact date** and the **relationship/abode descriptor** ("brother," "son," "gent of Maldon," "of Norwich," "the elder/younger") of each entry would discriminate the two and materially advance both leads.

## What the machine transcripts show (finding-aids only — confirm from image)
- **Goldhanger goods/estate entry:** *"…of him by said John Journay Situate in Golding [Goldhanger] County of Essex And as John Gournay being possest of said goods having disposed of … he doth now re[…]"* — a John Gournay possessed of and disposing goods at **Goldhanger**, the parish of Francis-of-Maldon's salt works (his 1677 estate schedule lists Goldhanger Marsh + Salt Coates). Reads as a probate/estate-administration matter.
- **Corporation-lot purchase:** *"…whereas John Gournay one of the [free…] lately bought of the said Corporation A certain lot of [land] … heretofore of Francis Gourn[ay]"* — a John Gournay buying from the Maldon Corporation a lot formerly Francis's. Post-1677 heir activity.
