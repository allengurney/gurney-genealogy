## Packet 13 — East Dereham & Costessey manorial court rolls

Goal: identify the **pre-1636 male Gurney** of East Dereham (Francis G14's home parish) whose death left the widow "Margaret/Margery Gurner/Gurney," and firm up the Costessey John Gurne family. The FTS transcripts hit their ceiling at 1636; these are image reads.

### 13a — Dereham court 1662 + 1665 (Margaret/Margery Gurney, free-bench?)
- **Image:** `images/packet13-dereham-1662-65-margaret-gurney-QX9.jpg`
- **Citation:** East Dereham (with Deopham/Denver) manor court book, 1623–1689. FamilySearch DGS **004389483**, ark **3:1:S3HY-D1S9-QX9**. Two courts on the spread: **1662** (left page) and **22 July 1665** (right page).
- **Finding-aid snippet (salad):** "…in Est Dearham… Margaret Gurney was proved…"; "…22 July 1665… Margery Gurney or abbath then…"
- **Questions:** (1) Transcribe the Gurney passages at the 1662 and 22 July 1665 courts. (2) Is Margaret/Margery Gurney a widow holding a copyhold by free-bench? (3) Name her **late husband** and the copyhold/messuage. (4) Any heir admitted, and the relationship stated.

### 13b — Dereham court 1640 (Gurney + Wagstaffe?)
- **Image:** `images/packet13-dereham-1640-gurney-wagstaffe-7H8.jpg`
- **Citation:** same book, DGS **004389483**, ark **3:1:S3HY-D1S9-7H8**, recordDate **1640**.
- **Finding-aid snippet (salad):** "…Gurney and interest as Wagstaffe takes…"
- **Questions:** (1) Does the entry couple a **Gurney** holding with a **Wagstaffe**? Walter Rye records *"1543 Michael Gurney, of E. Dereham, m. Marion Waggestar, widow"* — test whether the Dereham Gurney copyhold descends from that marriage. (2) Names, land, dates. **NOTE for Allen:** you previously image-read *a* 1640 Dereham card and found "no Gurney" — please confirm whether `7H8` is that same card before spending time.

(2) When did it pass from a Gurney to John Plough/Clough?

## Packet 16 — NCC will registers 1624–1637 — Gurney sightings (in-window)

New vector: the NCC registered-copy will registers for the **1624–1637 gap** (between Henry G15's 1623 will and the 1638 Earsham John) were never Gurney-swept. The films are confirmed FTS-covered. Three genuine Gurney sightings surfaced — each named *inside another person's will*, so identify both the testator and the Gurney, and read adjacent pages for the will's opening (testator's name/parish/date).

### 16a — "Clement Gurney, Gent" in a bond (1631/32 register)
- **Image:** `images/packet16-ncc1631-32-clement-gurney-gent-bond-YRB3.jpg` · NCC regd. wills vol. 124-125 (1631-32), DGS **008076514**, ark **3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-YRB3** · place tagged Norwich
- **Snippet:** "…I give and bequeath … my bond … obligatorie bearing date the **one and twenty day of November Anno Dm 1622** conteyning the penall sume of forty pounds … wherein **[Clement] Gurne Gent** [bounden] unto me for … twenty pounds…"
- **Questions:** (1) Confirm the forename **Clement** and style **"Gent"** — a *gentleman* Gurney bound in a £40 bond dated 21 Nov 1622. (2) Who is the **testator** (name/parish — read the will's first page)? (3) Any residence given for Clement Gurney. *(Clement is a rare Gurney forename — high tracing value.)*

### 16b — "John Gurney's house," Langley, 1634
- **Image:** `images/packet16-ncc1634-john-gurney-house-langley-T9KS.jpg` · NCC regd. wills vol. 127 (1634), DGS **008076861**, ark **3:1:3Q9M-CSND-T9KS-K**
- **Snippet:** "…lying in **Langley** … one acre of Meadow … next unto **John Gurneys house** & … abutting … To have & to hold … unto my said son **Robt Pagan** … after the decease of **Maryon my wife**…"
- **Questions:** (1) Confirm a **John Gurney** holding a house at/near **Langley** (S Norfolk, by Loddon) in 1634. (2) Testator's name/parish (the Pagan will — read its opening). (3) Exact location of the Gurney house and any further Gurney detail.

### 16c — Ellingham poor-charity (1630) — Gurney token uncertain
- **Image:** `images/packet16-ncc1630-ellingham-poor-charity-YF6X.jpg` · NCC orig. wills 1630, DGS **008076513**, ark **3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-YF6X** · place tagged Norwich
- **Snippet:** "…yearly … distribution … unto the poor sort of people of the [parish] of **Ellingham** aforesaid … and by [Gurnent — ?salad]…"
- **Questions:** (1) Whose will is this, and what is the tie to **Ellingham** (the core Gurney locus)? (2) Is there any genuine **Gurney** named, or is the "Gurnent" token OCR salad (e.g. "covenant")? Lower confidence — included because of the Ellingham locus.

## Packet 17 — Earsham John Gurney will body, 1638 (completes L-106/L-109)

The repo (L-106) had read only the *probate clause* of John Gurney of Earsham's will (proved Dec 1639). These are the **will-body pages** — they carry the family structure that the Sion/Lyon question (L-109) turns on. From NCC regd. wills 1638-39, DGS **008076858**.

### 17a — opening / dating clause
- **Image:** `images/packet17-earsham-john-gurney-will-1638-opening-3WWW.jpg` · ark **3:1:3Q9M-CSN6-3WWW**
- **Snippet:** "…The tenth day of August [in the] fourteenth year of ye Reign of our Sovereign Lord … King … **John [Gurney] of E[ar]sham in ye County of Norf[olk], Yeoman**, being in [health] of Body…" (the page also carries the probate note "Probatum … Joannis Edmond[?] surrogate").
- **Questions:** (1) Confirm the testator: **John Gurney of Earsham, yeoman**, will dated **10 Aug 14 Charles I (1638)**. (2) Transcribe the opening (health, parish, any occupation/abode detail).

### 17b — bequest clause (son John, brother, sister, nephew William)
- **Image:** `images/packet17-earsham-john-gurney-will-1638-bequests-3W7R.jpg` · ark **3:1:3Q9M-CSN6-3W7R**
- **Snippet:** "…if ye **John my Son** shall [die] before he shall accomplish age of one and twenty years then I give & bequeath ye messuage Lands [&] Tenement unto … **my Brother** … for paying **my Sister** her Legacies ye four pounds … my wife … to any **son of my Sister, W[illia]m** …"
- **Questions:** (1) Name the **brother** (is it **Lyon/Sion**? — the rare forename of L-109). (2) Name the **son John** and his age/contingency, the **wife**, the **sister** and her son **William**. (3) The messuage/lands devised (Earsham copyhold — ties to the "Tenement Gurneys"/Hallsty of Packet 15?). This maps the Earsham Gurney household at the 1638 anchor.
