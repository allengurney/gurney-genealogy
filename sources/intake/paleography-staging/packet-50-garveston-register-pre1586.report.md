## Bottom line

`garveston-register-61045-4033226_00540.jpg` confirms the competing Margaret entry:

> Margarett Rivet daughter of Robt christened first of ffebruarie

The entry is under the `Anno Dni 1577` heading and above the `Anno Dni 1578` heading, so it is best treated as **1 February 1577/8 old style = 1 February 1578 new style**. She would have been about 33 at the 23 September 1611 Gurney marriage, not 34.

The same image places Richard Rivet's christening above the `Anno Dni 1576` heading:

> Richard Rivet ye sonne of Robt christened ye last of November

Because this line precedes the 1576 year heading, the page structure supports **30 November 1575**, not 1576-1578.

The supplied `garveston-register-61045-4033226_00537.jpg` does **not** visibly support the indexed "Pagnell Rivet, father Thomas, 24 Oct 1569" target in the expected place. The left page has the 1569 heading and October entries, but the visible 24 October cluster reads as other surnames, not a Thomas Rivet child. This looks like either an image/record-link mismatch, an index target on an adjacent not-supplied image, or a severe index mislocation. I would not use this supplied image to settle Pagnell vs Francis.
