# Packets 54–55 — PCC wills, Gournay of West Barsham (report)

**Documents.** TNA PROB 11/303/284 (Henry Gourney of West Barsham, esq., proved 11 Feb 1661)
and PROB 11/188/136 (Edward Gournay of West Barsham, esq., proved 8 Feb 1642). Both read in
full from 300 dpi renders of the TNA facsimile PDFs.

**Outcome.** Both wills transcribed and abstracted to
`sources/corpus_supplement/tna-pcc-west-barsham-gournay-wills.md`, registered as
`tna-pcc-west-barsham-gournay-wills`, with a validation worksheet at
`sources/validations/tna-pcc-west-barsham-gournay-wills.md`. Masters parked in
`sources/media/tna-pcc-west-barsham-gournay-wills/_local/` (copyright-restricted facsimiles).

## Reading approach
Grid first on each page to locate the entry within the register folio, then per-line strips
(`line-strips --line-count`) at scale 3 for continuous reading, and 5–6× enhancement sheets
for individual name tokens. Automatic line detection was not used; the register hand is
dense and evenly spaced, so fixed `--line-count` bands over a grid-derived box segmented
cleanly.

Both entries share a page with unrelated wills — Henry's begins at the foot of folio 170
beneath the will of Francis Farren of Bishops Itchington, Warwickshire, and continues over
the page break. Locating the entry boundaries from the grid before cropping avoided
transcribing the neighbouring testator.

## Unresolved readings
- Edward's second-named sister carries an appended surname, probably a married name;
  *Mathewson* / *Mathison* both fit the letterforms. Not secure.
- *Helton alias Hault Hall in Helton* in Henry's settlement recital is not securely matched
  to a modern Norfolk parish.
- Edward's will date word before "date of November 1639" is partly obscured.

## Catalogue check
TNA's catalogue descriptions record neither testator's status. Both are styled **esquire**
in the register text. Register name and quire range agree with the UK Data Service PCC index
(SN 4816) for both items.
