## Headline findings

The inventory is headed:

> Braintre / March 16: 1663 / An Inventory of the goods & estate of John Gurney Senr deceased.

The inventory appraises movable goods, livestock, and land at about **55 pounds 14 shillings 6 pence** before debts. Debts charged against the estate total **37 pounds 12 shillings**. A lower note grants administration to **Richard Wharton**; I do not find a visible statement that he was kin, nor do I find Grizell, children, or another family relationship named in the administration note.

The note supports an administration grant to **Richard Wharton**, but I do **not** read a visible relationship statement. I also do not find the widow Grizell named in this administration note. The separate debt line "To the widow for goods impaired out of her estate" confirms that a widow was living and had a separate estate interest, but it does not name her.
