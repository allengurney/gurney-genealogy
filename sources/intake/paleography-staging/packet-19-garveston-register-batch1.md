## Scope and method

This was a visual paleographic pass over the Garveston register images, with enhancement crops and line strips generated for faint or target-rich pages. The main surname targets were Rivet/Rivett/Ryvett/Revett and Gurney/Gurnay/Gurne, with attention also to Bate, Thornton, and Blade where visible.

The viewer/image numbering is offset from the register content at the beginning of this packet: `image (17).jpg` is a modern FamilySearch title card, `image (18).jpg` is a register cover/title page, and the first register text spread begins at `image (19).jpg`.

Transcription conventions:

- Original spelling and letter forms are preserved where reasonably legible.
- `ffrancis` is retained for the initial double-f form.
- Square brackets mark unresolved or editorially supplied readings.
- Dates are given in the register's year heading where visible, with modern numeric expansion in the note.
