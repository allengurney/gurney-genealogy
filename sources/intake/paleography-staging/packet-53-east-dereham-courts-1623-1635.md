# Packet 53 — East Dereham manor court book, 1623–1635 (the G13 late-teen window)

Staged 2026-07-19. Lead **L-98 / L-124**; topic [`research/topics/john-gurney-candidate-others.md`](../../../research/topics/john-gurney-candidate-others.md) + [`research/people/g13-john-gurney/`](../../../research/people/g13-john-gurney/). Direct-line relevance: **John Gurney (G13) was baptised at East Dereham c.1609/10 and his father Francis (G14) was resident there through 1633.** This is the manor court of his childhood parish across his teens. Two abuttals already found (round 4) name **"land formerly of Margery/Margaret Gurney widow" at Ellingfield in East Dereham** (courts of 1627 & 1636) — a pre-Francis Gurney copyhold household. A page-by-page read is required because FamilySearch full-text finds only 13 Gurney tokens in the whole 2,189-image film (Latin/secretary hand OCRs as salad) and misses the abuttal mentions entirely.

## Source / provenance
- **Manor of East Dereham of the Queen, court book 1623–1852** (NRO **DC 12/5**; catalog koha:402769). Filmed as FamilySearch **DGS 004389483** ("Manorial court rolls, 1554–1907," 2,189 images), Items 16–20 = East Dereham 1623–1689. Original at Norfolk Record Office.
- **The film interleaves three manors (East Dereham + Deopham + Denver), non-chronologically** — image order ≠ date order across manors. Within the East Dereham court book it runs forward-chronological.
- **Images staged:** `images/packet-53-east-dereham-courts-1623-1635/` — 120 leaves, `edc-idx<N>.jpg` where `<N>` = FamilySearch image number (`?i=<N>`), full archival resolution (~1.4 MB). Range **idx 1505–1624**.

## Calibration (confirmed this session — use to fix each leaf's date)
- **idx 1525** = the East Dereham court-book **cover / start (≈1623)**.
- **idx 1600** = East Dereham court of **30 Sept 1633** (9 Chas I), folio 72.
- **idx 1629** = East Dereham court of **1635/1636** (11 Chas I), folio 98 — a court-leet homage/jury list (East Dereham + Dumpling-bury + Hardingham).
- Gradient ≈ 11 idx/year, forward. So **idx 1525–1624 ≈ courts 1623–1635** (target). idx 1505–1524 is the pre-book boundary (may be Deopham — notate by heading).

## The task
Read every leaf. For **each court**, capture the **court date** (regnal year, e.g. "N Car. I") to fix the gradient, then scan the whole leaf for any **Gurney / Gurner / Gurnay / Gournay** occurrence in either role:
1. **As a tenant/party** — surrender, admission, presentment, fine, homage/jury list, death/heir entry.
2. **As an abuttal** — a boundary clause "land late/formerly (nuper/quondam) of … Gurney," or "now (modo) … Gurney." *These are the ones OCR misses and the reason for the read.*

## Questions to answer
1. Any Gurney **admission or surrender** 1623–1635 — a Gurney copyhold tenant at East Dereham in John G13's teens? (Would materially change the "why Dereham" / fostering picture.)
2. For the **Margery/Margaret Gurney widow** parcel at Ellingfield: capture every abuttal or entry naming it — does any recital give her **late husband's forename**, or the parcel's descent (who held it before her / after her, e.g. Osborne)?
3. Record a clean **negative with coverage** ("courts of [dates] read, no Gurney entry") where absent — that itself sharpens whether the Gurney presence at Dereham was tenurial or only a remembered boundary.
4. Note any Rivett / Ryvett, Podmer / Petman, Elwyn, or Blomefield entries in passing (adjacent Gurney-orbit families).
