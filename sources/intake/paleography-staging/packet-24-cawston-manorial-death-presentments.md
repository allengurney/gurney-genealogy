## Findings

The packet is processed, but the genealogical crux remains unresolved from this pass.

The five local masters do appear to be the intended Cawston target pages, and several have plausible marginal or body-text loci for Gurney/Gurner-related entries. However, I could not safely recover the essential chain:

- the deceased Gurney/Gurner name;
- the date of death;
- the admitted heir;
- the heir's stated relationship;
- the land or copyhold description tied to the death presentment.

The `MFZ` page is particularly resistant. It is the right image family for the 1649 question, and the page has name-bearing marginalia and compact body text, but I could not confirm that the target name is `Wyand`, `William`, or another form. The generated grid improves orientation but not enough to support a durable transcription.

The `57P` page plausibly contains the Agnes Gurney/libertate hit identified by OCR, but I could not turn that into a confident legal reading or family relationship.

The three death-presentment images (`13K`, `6HZ`, `TC4`) remain plausible targets for a specialist Latin-court-hand pass. I could not extract a reliable deceased-and-heir answer from them in this bounded review.
