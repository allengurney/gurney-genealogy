**Status:** Processed 2026-06-22. The marriage gap is supported, but the staged baptism sequence does not image-confirm the expected Ann Wales (1618) or Peter Wales (1629) entries and reaches only about 1631, not 1635. Report: `done/packet-33-bawdeswell-wales-tracking.md`.

## The question
The emigrant **John Gurney (G13)**'s wife was a **Mary** of unknown family, married c.1627; the candidate is **Mary Wales** (chr. 1611 Great Dunham, dau. **Peter Wales**), whose household moved to **Bawdeswell** by 1618 (children there: **Ann Wales chr. 1618**, son **Peter Wales chr. 1629**). The test: does Mary Wales marry locally / disappear after ~1627 (consistent with emigrating as the wife who died in New England), or does she reappear at Bawdeswell — as a wife/mother or in a burial — which would **eliminate** her?

## Key structural finding — a disordered book, and an apparent marriage gap
This register is **not chronologically ordered**: it is faded, tight-bound, and its baptism / marriage / burial sections are interleaved and out of sequence (sampling found marriages on img45 and img46, burials on img48, baptisms on img23 and img67, all jumbled by date). Most consequential: **the surviving marriage entries jump from 1562–1582 (img45) straight to 1709+ (img46)** — there appears to be **no marriage record for the 1583–1708 window, including the 1620s–30s**. If confirmed, this means *Mary Wales's marriage cannot be tested at Bawdeswell because the register has no marriages for that period* — which also explains why the marriage indexes are blank for this parish (a true record gap, not an indexing miss). **The test therefore shifts to the baptism and burial sections**: does Mary Wales appear as a *mother* or a *burial* after ~1627?
