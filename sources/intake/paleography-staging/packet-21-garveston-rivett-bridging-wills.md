## 21a. Margaret Ryvett/Rivett of Garveston, widow, 1584

The will begins at the foot of image 442:

> In the name of god amen the five daye of June ... in the yeare of the raigne of our Sovereigne Ladie Queene Elizabeth ... I Margaret Ryvett of Garveston in the Countie of Norff widowe ...

The continuation on image 443 is legible enough for the packet crux, though I have not produced a full polished diplomatic transcription from the viewer screenshots.

The legatee set visible in the continuation is centered on Wright/Wryght, Clayton, Raymond, and Newton connections. Names read in the continuation include:

- Thomas Wright / Wryght, son of Robert Wright.
- Grace Wright / Wryght, daughter of Thomas Wright.
- Thomas Wright and his wife.
- Alice, daughter of [name uncertain].
- Robert Raymond and Elizabeth his wife.
- John Clayton, son of the said Elizabeth.
- Margaret Clayton.
- Elizabeth Clayton, daughter of the said Elizabeth.
- Frances Clayton, son of the said Elizabeth.
- Anthony Clayton.
- Parmel Newton.
- Marye Ryvett, described as daughter-in-law.
- Edward Newton.

I do **not** find a Francis Rivet/Ryvett, a Margaret Rivet/Ryvett as a younger legatee, or any Gurney/Gurnay/Gurne mention in the readable continuation.

### 21a answer

The will confirms a Margaret Ryvett/Rivett of Garveston, widow, but the locally reviewed pages do not provide the desired bridge to Margaret Rivet baptized in 1586. In particular, I found no named son or grandson Francis Rivet and no Gurney mention.

The `Marye Ryvett my daughter in lawe` clause is worth retaining as a family-structure clue, but it is not the Francis/Margaret bridge sought by the packet.

## 21b. Robert Ryvett/Rivett of Garveston administration, 1597

The relevant act-book film was located through the FamilySearch catalog:

- Catalog parent: `Probate records for the Episcopal Consistory Court, Archdeaconry of Norwich, 1370-1858`
- Act books vol. 8, 1588-1605
- British Film `173449`
- DGS `008077845`

A DGS-scoped FamilySearch full-text probe for `Rivett` produced one hit, ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSN6-R9RK-L`, image 204 of 366. The full-text transcript is machine-salad, but it correctly locates the manuscript entry.

Best-effort transcription of the relevant entry:

> Ryvett / xbr [?]
>
> Dicto die mensis et Anno pred[ictis] ... Comissa fuit administratio bonorum Roberti Ryvett nuper de Garveston defuncti Agneti Ryvett vid[uae] relictae dicti defuncti de bene et fideliter administrando eadem jurat.

Translation / sense:

> On the same day, month, and year aforesaid, administration of the goods of Robert Ryvett, late of Garveston, deceased, was granted to Agnes Ryvett, widow and relict of the said deceased, she being sworn well and faithfully to administer the same.

The exact calendar day is not recoverable from this cropped entry alone because it uses `Dicto die mensis et Anno pred[ictis]`; the page is in the 1597 act-book sequence. The marginal `xbr` may point to December, but the exact day should be checked against the immediately preceding dated entry before promotion.

### 21b answer

Robert Ryvett/Rivett of Garveston died before the 1597 act. Administration was granted to **Agnes Ryvett, widow/relict**. This places Agnes as Robert's surviving widow and household administrator, but the short act does not name children, including Francis or Margaret.

## Packet answers

1. Margaret (Bate) Rivett 1584: the will names Margaret Ryvett/Rivett of Garveston, widow, and a broad legatee network, but no Francis Rivet/Ryvett, no younger Margaret Rivet/Ryvett, and no Gurney.
2. Robert Rivett 1597: administrator is **Agnes Ryvett**, widow/relict of Robert Ryvett of Garveston.
3. Bridge value: packet 21 strengthens the older Garveston Rivett household map but does not prove that Francis Rivet, father of Margaret baptized 21 May 1586, was a son or grandson of Margaret Ryvett or Robert Ryvett.
4. Further value: Agnes Ryvett as Robert's widow is a new household anchor for the Garveston Rivett cluster and should be tested against the Garveston register and any later probate/index references.
