# Packet 41 — Manor of Whinburgh-with-Members court rolls (Garveston's manor), 1595–1663 film: two target entries + nine calibration leaves

Staged: 2026-07-16. Images in `images/packet-41-whinburgh-with-members-courts/` (11 files). Outsourced-read brief per the staging README.

## Why this packet matters

The Manor of Whinburgh-with-Members held court "on the part of Garvestone, Reymerston, Thuxton, Mattishall and Yaxham" — Garveston is the home parish of the Rivett family, the probable maternal kin of John Gurney (G13). Francis Rivet of Garveston (father of the probable Margaret, G13's mother) **left no will**; the copyhold court rolls are the last surviving record class that could name his heirs. This packet (a) reads two Gurney-flagged entries found by FTS, and (b) date-calibrates the film's 1595–1663 item so a follow-up walk can target the 1595–1630 courts precisely.

Film: FamilySearch **DGS 004389244** = British Film 1472614; items 10–11 = "Court rolls 1595–1663" (catalog koha:439083; the film also carries Manor of Weybourne rolls in earlier items and Whinburgh 1743–55 as item 9 around images ~1380–1390). The film's in-viewer image array is 2301 images; **image numbers below are 1-based positions in that array** (open the film's first image ark 3:1:S3HT-6X4K-11 at `?i=0` and use image-number navigation, or the arks given per file).

Language/hand: 1595–1630s courts will be Latin court hand; 1640s–1663 mixed Latin/English. High-reduction 42X microfilm — expect small script; zoom generously.
