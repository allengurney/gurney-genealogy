# L-46: Blomefield, Harpley (de Gournay manor descent + arms)

**Lead:** L-46
**Date searched:** 2026-06-14
**Source:** Francis Blomefield, *An Essay towards a Topographical History of the County of Norfolk*, vol. 8 (Freebridge Hundred and Half: Harpley), pp. 452–459.
**Online:** Available online — British History Online, https://www.british-history.ac.uk/topographical-hist-norfolk/vol8/pp452-459 (also archive.org `essaytowardstopo04blom` and successors).

## Manor of Gourney's (Harpley) — acquisition and descent

- **Acquisition by marriage (c. 30 Henry II, ~1184):** William de Burnham granted the moiety of his fee to his brother Reginald, whose "only daughter and heir, **Rose**, who was given in marriage by **Hameline Plantagenet, Earl Warren** … to **Matthew de Gurney**, who was lord in her right, about the 30th of Henry II." A fine of 30 Henry II settled the dispute: the manor ceded to Matthew and his heirs, held by half a fee, paying Philip 10 marks/ann.
- **William de Gournay** (son of Matthew).
- **John de Gournay** (son of William) — "with Sir Henry Hastings at the battle of Lewes" (1264); recovered the church advowson 3 Edw. I; granted a fair 35 Edw. I (1307).
- **John de Gournoy** (nephew, son of Catherine) — held the manor 27–34 Edw. III.
- **Edmund de Gurnay** — presented 1360; "lord and patron" 1360.
- **John Gournay** — presented 1387.
- **John Gournay, Esq.** — died 9 Henry IV; widow **Alice** held court 10 Henry IV.

## Departure from the Gournay line (Tudor)

After intermediary control (Robert de Morley, William Pakenham): "**Thomas Gurnay** presented to the church in **1443**, and **William Gurnay, Esq.** in **1485**, and **Anthony Gurnay**, in **1511**, who, with **Elizabeth, his wife**, was living in **1535**, and soon after conveyed it to **Sir John Allen, Knt.**" Manor then passed through Curson, Stubbs, Yelverton, Walpole.

## Rectors / church (relevant)

- Gravestone: "**Hic jacet corpus Joh'is de Gournay, quonda' rectoris et patroni hujus ecclesie**" — John de Gournay, rector, died 6 Edw. III.
- John de Gurnay, rector 35 Edw. I.
- **Edmund Gurnay, B.D., presented 1620** — this is **Edmund Gurney the Divine** (rector of Harpley 1620–48; cf. L-15, already confirmed via Venn/ACAD).

## Arms

- Church windows displayed: "**Gournay, argent, a cross, ingrailed, gules**" — the engrailed-cross arms (cf. the American-Gurney arms question, L-10; and the Harpley/Norfolk usage).

## Value / disposition

Corroborates the Matthew-de-Gournay Harpley acquisition narrative (cf. L-29, L-39 Harpley court rolls; G29 Matthew companion) and pins the Tudor exit (Anthony Gurnay → Sir John Allen, c.1535). Independent witness to the engrailed-cross arms in the Harpley church windows. Edmund the Divine's 1620 institution is independently confirmed here. Lead can move to **Partial/Done** (Blomefield's printed account fully read; only Harleian MS 970, the un-digitized companion, remains under the original L-46 wording).
