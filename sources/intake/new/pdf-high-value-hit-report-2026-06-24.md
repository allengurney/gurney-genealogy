# High-value PDF hit report - Calendar of Documents and Stewkley parish register

This report reviews the two high-value PDFs in `sources/intake/new/pdfs/` for Gurney/Gournay-relevant content using the repo broad-variant surname list and Latin/Norman forms.

## Calendar of Documents Preserved in France

**File:** `sources/intake/new/pdfs/Calendar_of_Documents_Preserved_in_Franc.pdf`  
**External reference:** <https://www.google.com/books/edition/Calendar_of_Documents_Preserved_in_Franc/DsqpulIVx0gC>  
**Work identified:** J. Horace Round, ed., *Calendar of Documents Preserved in France, Illustrative of the History of Great Britain and Ireland*, vol. 1, A.D. 918-1206 (London: HMSO, 1899).

### Search scope and variant control

Searched broad family forms and OCR-risk forms including `Gurney`, `Gurnay`, `Gourney`, `Gornai`, `Gornaco`, `Gurnaco`, `Gurnaio`, `Gournayo`, `Gurniaco`, `Guarniacum`, `Gornais`, and root-pattern checks for `Gorn*`, `Gurn*`, `Gourn*`, `Guarn*`, `Gern*`, `Girn*`, and `Gyrn*`. The index confirms Round consolidates the relevant personal-name forms under `Gornai`, including `Cornai (sic)`, `Gornaco`, `Gournayo`, `Gurnay`, and `Gurniaco`.

### Person and family hits

**PDF p. 67 / printed p. 2 - Cathedral Church of St. Mary, Rouen, [1111-1116].**  
Witness list includes `Hugone de Gournayo` among high-status witnesses to a royal/ecclesiastical act at Windsor on the Purification of St. Mary. Other witnesses include bishops of Salisbury, Lincoln, and Durham; Ranulf the chancellor; Robert count of Meulan; William de Warenne; William de Tancarville; William and Nigel de Albini; Hamon dapifer; and Ada de Port.  
**Value:** High contextual value for early 12th-century Gournay placement in Anglo-Norman royal witness circles. Likely relevant to the senior Norman line after Gerard de Gournay, but person-identification should be cross-checked before assignment to a specific Hugh.

**PDF p. 125 / printed p. 60 - Abbey of St. Wandrille, 1157.**  
Entry 172 is an agreement between the abbey of St. Wandrille and `Hugh de Gornaio` concerning lands and rights in Normandy. It is dated 1157 at Rouen in the cemetery of St. Ouen, in the presence of Roger, abbot of St. Wandrille, Roger, abbot of St. Ouen, and Hugh de Gournay. Witnesses from Hugh's side include Hugh Talbot, William de Sancto Lecheen, Anselm de Montengnei, Robert de Maregnei, Bartholomew Dallage, Hugh Davesnes, Oliver Dage, and Saueri and William Hoese.  
**Value:** Very high. This is a direct act/agreement involving Hugh de Gournay, not merely a witness-list occurrence. It is a prime candidate for a focused extract or source entry if the G32-G35 medieval evidence set is expanded.

**PDF p. 137 / printed p. 72 - Abbey of Bondeville, for Cistercian nuns.**  
Witness list includes `Walterus de Gornaco` among clerical and lay witnesses.  
**Value:** Medium. Potential collateral or place-derived personal-name hit. Do not treat as direct-line evidence without external corroboration.

**PDF p. 155 / printed p. 90 - Miscellaneous documents, [1174-1178] / 1190 material.**  
One passage notes that neither `Hugh de Gurnai` nor anyone else is to make unjust demands on the beneficiaries. Another nearby charter witness list includes `Hugone de Gurnay` with Hamelin earl Warenne and others.  
**Value:** High but needs calendar-entry separation before citation. The page contains adjacent material with close but not identical diplomatic contexts, so the exact entry number and date should be verified visually before promotion.

**PDF p. 156 / printed p. 91 - Seine Inferieure, 1190, 22 June / related charter material.**  
Witness list includes `Hugo de Gornay` with Hubert archbishop of Canterbury, Robert bishop of Bangor, John count of Mortain, Thomas de St. Valery, and others.  
**Value:** High for John/Richard-era elite witness context, likely relevant to the later Hugh de Gournay line. Needs exact regnal-year reconciliation before use.

**PDF p. 184 / printed p. 119 - Abbey of Preaux, [1187].**  
Witness list includes `Hugone de Gornai` in a charter context with Walter archbishop of Rouen, Baldwin count of Aumale, William Marshal, and William son of Ralph, seneschal of Normandy.  
**Value:** High. Useful as a high-status Norman witness-list occurrence, especially for late Henry II / Richard I chronology.

**PDF p. 189 / printed p. 124 - Abbey of Bec-Hellouin, [1101-1118].**  
Signa include Henry king, Adeliza queen, William bishop of Winchester, Roger bishop of Salisbury, Ranulf chancellor, Drogo de Munceio, Walter son of Richard, `Hugo de Gornaco`, Waleran count of Meulan, and Robert count of Leicester.  
**Value:** Very high for repo context because Drogo de Munceio/Monceio appears near Gournay parentage and affinity discussions. This deserves side-by-side review with the existing G32 medieval research files before any conclusion is hardened.

**PDF p. 204 / printed p. 139 - Department of the Eure, [1174-1186].**  
Witness list includes `Hugone de Gurnay` with Baldwin count of Aumale, William de Braose, Robert de Harcourt, Stephen de Longchamp, Warin de Glapion, and Peter de Stokes; the act was given at Rouen, 15 February in a fourth regnal year.  
**Value:** High. Another useful Plantagenet witness-context hit for the later Norman line.

**PDF p. 273 / printed p. 208 - Abbey of St. Andre-en-Gouffern, [1151-1154].**  
Witness list includes `Hugone de Gurnaio`, dated 1154, 7 kalends of September.  
**Value:** High. Potentially useful for the transition between Geoffrey/Angevin and early Henry II Normandy evidence.

**PDF p. 392 / printed p. 327 - Abbey of the Holy Trinity, Lessay.**  
Witness list includes `Hugonis de Gornai` among major ecclesiastical and noble witnesses.  
**Value:** Medium-high. Likely useful for an early Norman chronology, but the entry date and diplomatic context need close visual review before identifying the Hugh.

**PDF p. 427 / printed p. 362 - Abbey of St. Mary, Perseigne, for Cistercian monks.**  
Entry 1027 is a charter of Hugh de Gournay giving a house to St. Mary of Perseigne. Related confirmation material also includes `Hugone de Gornai` in a witness list.  
**Value:** Very high. This is the strongest direct-person hit after the St. Wandrille agreement because it preserves a charter of Hugh himself.

**PDF p. 452 / printed p. 387 - Abbey of Fontevrault, [?1191-1197] / 1199 material.**  
A queen's charter witness list includes `Hugone de Gornai`, with queen Joanna, Ralph count of Eu, Robert count of Leicester, William Maengot, Hugh de Surgeres, Ralph de Faia, Hamelin de Brolio, and Henry de Bernavalle.  
**Value:** High for the late 1190s political network, though the page's date presentation should be checked carefully before use.

**PDF p. 467 / printed p. 402 - Abbey of St. Florent, Saumur, [n.d.].**  
`Ralf de Gornaio` accompanies the grantor to the altar of St. Florent.  
**Value:** Medium. A potentially important collateral/person-name hit, but not direct evidence for Hugh or the main line without more support.

**PDF p. 490 / printed p. 425 - Abbey of Marmoutier, Tours, 1066.**  
Witness list includes `Hugo de Gurniaco` with Odo bishop of Bayeux, Hugh bishop of Lisieux, Richard de Courcy, Ralph son of Herluin, and others.  
**Value:** Very high if the dating and identification hold. It may provide an early post-Conquest Gournay witness occurrence, but it requires a careful check against existing Daniel Gurney and medieval-source chronology.

**PDF p. 531 / printed p. 466 - Royal charters, etc., [1156-1157].**  
Witness list includes `Hugone de Gurnaio` with Thomas the chancellor, count Giffard, Ralph de Toeni, John count of Eu, William chamberlain of Tancarville, and Nicholas de Stuteville, given at Bonneville-sur-Touques.  
**Value:** Very high for early Henry II royal-charter context.

**PDF p. 539 / printed p. 474 - Royal charters, etc., 1199-1200.**  
Records securities/bonds connected with King John and Philip of France, including Roger de Toeny, William de Kaeu, Baldwin earl of Aumale, `Hugh de Gornaco`, Warin de Glapion, John de Pratellis, and William de Humet.  
**Value:** Very high political-context evidence for the Anglo-French transition. This could be important for late Gournay positioning under John and Philip.

**PDF p. 561 / printed p. 496 - Documents at Lille, 1199.**  
Witness roster begins with `Hugo de Gornaio`, followed by William de Kaeu, Geoffrey de Cella, Roger constable of Chester, Robert son of Walter, William de Albini, Robert de Ros, Nicholas de Montfichet, Roger de Tosny, Saher de Quincy, William de Montchesney, Peter de Pratellis, and others.  
**Value:** Very high. Another strong John-era witness roster placing Hugh among major Anglo-Norman actors.

**PDF p. 583 / printed p. 518 - Appendix I, 1147.**  
Witness list includes `Hugone de Gornaco` in an act dated at Lions, 11 October 1147, with Matilda queen, Waleran count of Meulan, Robert count of Leicester, William de Roumare, Richard fitzUrse, William Peverel of Dover, Walkelin Maminot, and Ingram de Guascolio.  
**Value:** Very high. Useful for Angevin/Matildan context and mid-12th-century Gournay chronology.

### Index controls

**PDF pp. 638, 657-660 / index pages.**  
The index consolidates `Cornai (sic)`, `Gornai`, `Gornaco`, `Gournayo`, `Gurnay`, and `Gurniaco` under the Gournay personal-name entry. It lists Hugh de Gournay at printed pages 2, 57, 60, 90, 91, 119, 124, 137, 139, 145, 208, 327, 387 n., 425, 466, 474, 496, and 518; a charter of Hugh at p. 362; Ralf at pp. 401-402; and Walter at p. 72. It separately indexes Gournay/Gornacum/Guarniacum as a place.  
**Value:** Very high as a variant map. The index should be used as a cross-check when preparing any future extract set from this volume.

### Calendar assessment

This PDF is worth retaining and processing. It is not merely duplicative of Daniel Gurney or later baronage summaries: it supplies calendar-level access to charter evidence, witness lists, direct acts by Hugh de Gournay, seigneurial place context, and variant spellings across Latinized Norman forms. The strongest future corpus-supplement candidates are the St. Wandrille agreement (p. 60), the Bec-Hellouin signa with Drogo de Munceio (p. 124), the Perseigne charter (p. 362), the 1147 Appendix I act (p. 518), and the John-era securities/rosters (pp. 474, 496).

### Stewkley assessment

The Stewkley PDF is high-value but not because it supplies a simple emigrant-origin proof. Its main value is cluster control: it shows a dense local Gurney/Girney/Gerney/Gourney network from the 1560s through the 1650s, including multiple adult male lines named John, Robert, and Walter. That density makes the 21 February 1602/1603 John son of John baptism less safe as a standalone candidate and more useful as one entry inside a local reconstruction problem. The most important immediate extraction targets are the John household entries on printed pp. 25, 29, 33, 38, 41; the Robert household sequence on pp. 33, 39-42, 46, 54, 56, 64-65, 87; and the Walter household entries on pp. 49 and 55.

Before promoting any single Stewkley event into a fact sheet or person companion, the printed page should be checked visually because several entries are damaged, line-wrapped, or OCR-sensitive. The safest next research move is to build a small Stewkley household table by adult male head, child/spouse, event type, event date, printed page, and spelling.
