# Family pages handwriting transcription

Source images: `sources/intake/new/Family pages/Scanned_20260603-2217-01.jpg` through `Scanned_20260603-2217-07.jpg`.

Scope: Verbatim working transcription of the handwriting on the seven scanned pages. Fact-sheet and research-companion files for G2-G5 were used only as context for names and relationships.

Conventions:
- Line breaks generally follow the page.
- Bracketed text marks an uncertain reading or a brief layout note.
- `~~text~~` marks handwriting that appears crossed out.
- Blank-looking fields are preserved as blank when the page leaves space but no value.
