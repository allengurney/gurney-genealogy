## Task A - "Anne Gurney my Daughter" will

### Page correction

The following will, beginning on image 340, is clearly Robert Grene and should not be used as the testator identity for the Anne Gurney clause:

> In the name of God Amen / the third day of July in the xlj yeare of the Raigne of our soveraigne Ladie Elizabeth ... I Robert Grene ...

That Robert Grene opening follows the probate clause of the prior will. The `Anne Gurney my Daughter` clause belongs to the prior will, not to Robert Grene.

### Testator

I did **not** recover the testator's full name confidently from the available page sequence. Image 337 shows the will already in progress; image 338 continues the same will; image 339/340 carry the later continuation, Gurney clause, and probate. The probate clause is present near the bottom of image 340 left page, but the testator name in that clause is not legible enough for me to treat it as settled.

The will is not a Robert Grene will. Any downstream note should therefore avoid the bundle's original "Robert Gr-" identification for this item unless a fresh review of the probate clause or preceding images proves otherwise.

### Working transcription of Gurney-bearing passages

These are line-level working readings. Doubtful words are bracketed.

From image 338 right page, near the middle-lower continuation, there appears to be an earlier Anne-related bequest:

```text
Item I give and bequeath unto Anne my [second?] daughter [...]
[...] the said Anne my daughter with William [and/or Willm?] Gurney [...]
```

I can see `Anne my daughter` clearly enough, and I see a probable `Gurney` form in the adjacent wording, but I am not confident whether the phrase is naming **William Gurney**, **Gurney's son**, or a bequest payable through/with a Gurney. This passage should be re-read before using it as a child/husband identification.

From image 340 left page, in the clearest Gurney clause:

```text
Item I give and bequeath unto the foure followinge
[Ellen?] Shropham my Daughter and to her [husband?],
Anne Gurney my Daughter and to [Joane?/John?] Collier my Daughter,
to everie of them two silver spoones.
```

High-confidence elements:

- `Anne Gurney my Daughter` is present.
- The clause gives silver spoons, apparently `two silver spoones` to each recipient or recipient-pair.
- `Collier` appears nearby as another daughter/sibling-family name.

Lower-confidence elements:

- I do not see a legible Gurney husband's forename, occupation, or residence in the immediate clause.
- The first daughter name may be `Ellen Shropham`, but `Ellen` and `Shropham` should be treated as tentative.
- The adjacent Collier wording is not fully resolved; `Joane?/John? Collier my Daughter` is uncertain.

### Other family names in the will

Names or likely names visible in the surrounding continuation include:

- Anne Gurney, daughter of the testator
- [Ellen?] Shropham, daughter of the testator
- [Joane?/John?] Collier, daughter of the testator
- Elizabeth, Anne, and Margaret, daughters of the testator's sister Anne
- Robert [Casteline?/Castellow?] and children of a sister, uncertain
- Phillip Collier, Allen Collier, and [Prosper?] Collier appear in a later family/legal clause on image 339/340

The sister Anne clause is only partially legible. My best working reading:

```text
Item I give and bequeath unto Elizabeth Anne and Margaret
daughters unto my sister Anne [Bodney?/Codling?]
to every of them xijd.
```

The surname after sister Anne is not secure. It looks more like a `Bodney/Codling`-type form than Gurney.

### Answers to the bundle questions

1. **Testator full name/status/parish/date:** not confidently recovered. The will is not Robert Grene's. Probate appears at the bottom of image 340 left page and seems to be dated in 1599, but the exact probate date and testator name require a stronger read of the probate clause or earlier opening images.
2. **Exact Anne clause:** see working transcription above. The words `Anne Gurney my Daughter` are clear; no Gurney husband forename/occupation/residence is legible in the immediate clause.
3. **Other Gurney names:** one probable additional Gurney-form passage on image 338 near `Anne my daughter`, possibly involving `William Gurney`, but too uncertain to promote as a firm named person. No Gurney witness/overseer/debtor was confidently found in the retained crops.
4. **Other daughters / sister Anne:** probable daughters include [Ellen?] Shropham, Anne Gurney, and a Collier daughter. The sister Anne's daughters appear to be Elizabeth, Anne, and Margaret.

## Task B - Great Ellingham "Harney/Gurney"

This is **Harney**, not Gurney.

Relevant clause:

```text
... and to James Harney one of the sonnes of Isacke Harney
of Great Ellingham in the County of Norff yeoman and to his
heyres and assignes for ever ...
```

The broader page also contains Harvey/Harney-family context, including names such as James Harney, Abraham Harney, and James/Mary/Sarah Harvey. I see no reason to escalate this item as a Gurney record.

Disposition: reject as a Gurney lead.
