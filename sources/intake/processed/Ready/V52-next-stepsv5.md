page 344 - 

Gurney, Gurnay, Gurnee.— Local, ‘ of Gournai,’ probably Gournai-en-Brai, in the arrondissement of Neufchatel. We are told that there were two Hugh de Gournays at the battle of Hastings.

‘li viel Hue de Gornai

Ensemble o li sa gent de Brai.’ Roman de Rou (quoted by Lower).

The two Hughs received grants of land in Norfolk, in which county the name is still strong.
