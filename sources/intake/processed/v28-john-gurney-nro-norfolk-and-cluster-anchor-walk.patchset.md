### 0.3 NRO Norfolk Gurney baptism walk

The Ancestry collection "Norfolk, England, Church of England Baptism, Marriages, and Burials, 1535-1812" (in partnership with Norfolk Record Office) covers approximately three quarters of Norfolk's parishes. A walk for surname Gurney plus variants (Garney, Gurny, Grune, Gerney, Greney, Garne, etc.), birth year 1615-1645, returns 179 records. The complete baptism roster within the window is:

**Zero baptisms in the NRO collection 1615-1645 have father indexed as "John Gurney."** Per user direction this is not treated as definitive elimination; it is one strong negative across the most-authoritative Norfolk dataset.

### 0.4 Denton 1638 indexer disagreement

The Denton 1638 Mary Gurney baptism is read by NRO as **father Josiah, mother Rachelle**. The same physical register entry is read by FS England Births and Christenings and by Findmypast UK Parish Baptisms (the v25 source) as **father John, mother Rachell**. The NRO holds the original register, so its indexer reading is presumptively authoritative; the NRO image had "Soiled Document"/"Faded Document" overlays in the brief 2026-05-11 view and the script could not be independently parsed in this pass. The mother Rachell/Rachelle is consistent across all three indexes.

Implication: v25's "Denton John + Rachell" §8.4 row should be qualified to reflect the NRO Josiah+Rachelle reading and the unresolved father-identification.

### 0.5 FS England-wide John-father baptism walk 1615-1645

FS Records, surname Gurney + father John, baptism 1615-1645, returns 6,158 results. Filtered to "Principal" (baptized child) records, the controlling cluster matches case-file standing or v22-v25 additions except for three new items:

1. **Haddenham, Bucks ~25 February 1620** — unnamed Gurney child, father John Gurney. England, Buckinghamshire, Church Records, 1217-1994.
2. **Haddenham, Bucks 26 January 1622** — Joane Gurney, father John Gurney. Same collection.
3. **Aylesbury, Bucks 16 December 1638** — John Gurney, son of John Gurney. England, Births and Christenings, 1538-1975.

Haddenham is in Bucks ~7 miles SW of Aylesbury. The 1620 + 1622 baptisms identify a previously-undocumented Bucks "John Gurney + Haddenham" household. No mother indexed.

The Aylesbury 16 Dec 1638 John-Gurney-son-of-John baptism is the fifth indexed Aylesbury John-Gurney-father child alongside Cand A's v23-documented Sarah 1639, Daniell 1645, Jonathan 1647, Hannah 1653. The 1638 entry either extends Cand A's family group earlier by one year (giving a son John Jr.) or identifies a second contemporaneous Aylesbury John Gurney father. Mother field unindexed.

**Isaac Gurney + father John, England 1620-1650 (FS, all collections, 287 results):** Zero "Principal" Isaac Gurney baptisms with father John in England in this window.

### 0.7 Non-lead memorialized

Treat as a non-lead per the v26/v27 framing for the Puritan Great Migration Project tree source. Do NOT carry as an open lead unless a primary source for the Richards surname surfaces.
