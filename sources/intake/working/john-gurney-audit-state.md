## 2026-05-09 Pass 2 Findings (online lead-work)

- TAG 10:70-73 OBTAINED. archive.org `sim_american-genealogist_1933-10_10_2`, Holman, "Grissell of the Many Marriages." Confirmed full text. Two new concrete details: (a) the 12 Nov 1661 Braintree marriage was performed by Peter Brackett; (b) Holman supplies NO 1636 arrival/birth date for John Gurney. Anderson's 1636 cannot trace to TAG 10:70-73. Most likely Anderson source for 1636 is WJ 2:422 / Newgate-apprentice tradition. This sharpens the two-Johns reading rather than closing it.
- Mendon Proprietors' Records (1899 print) online at archive.org `proprietorsrecor00mend`. Direct mention: "John Gurny, a twenty acre lot" and separately "Grisel Gurney, a twenty acre lot." This complicates the prior G13 framing that John "died with no land" and that Grizzell merely "applied for John's lot." Both names are in the proprietor roster. The Mendon township was granted privileges 1667, so a proprietor entry for John is necessarily posthumous unless the list was a retrospective allottee record; reconcile against the chronology before promoting.
- ROLLCO does NOT currently index Haberdashers' or Merchant Taylors' Companies. The 1632 John Gurney apprentice lead is NOT online-accessible through the free livery-company database. Park as needs Guildhall Library / Findmypast London Apprenticeship Abstracts.
- St Ann Blackfriars 1615 baptism: no Harleian Society printed transcription found online in this pass. Original register access path is LMA/Ancestry/Findmypast (paywalled). Park.
- Pease pennyghael.org.uk PDF unreachable in this pass (ECONNREFUSED). Re-attempt later; prior Margaret Ryvett finding from the PDF is already in the case file.
- Daniel Shed genealogy (Frank Shedd, 1921) opening section read; no Mary Gurney content there. ## 2026-05-09 Pass 3 Findings (Cand A / C / other-origin pressure)

- Phillimore *Buckinghamshire Parish Registers — Marriages*, vol. 1, OBTAINED on archive.org `buckinghamshirep01phil`. Edlesborough section yields four Bucks Gurney marriages 1660-1685 plus the high-value "Joh. Gurney & Mary Kidgell" marriage at Edlesborough in 1661. A John Gurney with a wife named Mary, marrying at Edlesborough in 1661, is materially incompatible with the colonial John (who buried his Mary in MA 20 Sept. 1661 and married Grizzell on 12 Nov. 1661 at Braintree). Strongest single online eliminator yet for Candidate A: either the same individual as Cand A (which closes him out) or a separate Bucks "John + Mary" household that further depletes the residual "Unknown other origin" bucket.
- Phillimore vol. 1 Edlesborough also gives William Gurney + Martha Halsey 1660, Samuel Gurney of Bierton + Elizabeth Bunce of Padbury 1662, and Thomas Gurney of Hockliffe + Frances Norman of Houghton Regis 1680 — corroborating a continuing Bucks Gurney cluster well after 1641.
- VCH Bucks vol. 3 Edlesborough (BHO `vch/bucks/vol3/pp350-361`) reviewed: zero Gurney landholders. The Edlesborough Gurney presence is yeoman/non-manorial, consistent with the 1642 Contributions for Ireland £1 0s entry and the 1661 Mary Kidgell marriage being a working-class household.
- VCH Bucks vol. 3 Stewkley (BHO `vch/bucks/vol3/pp420-426`) confirms John Gurney holding the Stewkley manors by 1687 with son Isaac, both selling to Anne Robinson of Stepney in 1701. No Northamptonshire link in the VCH text; reconcile with the Findings V7 statement that Cand A's John was the same person who appears at Walgrave Northants 1650.
- Stewkley parish register printed transcription (Dickson, *Parish Register of Stewkeley, Bucks., 1545-1653*, Salem: Eben Putnam, 1897) confirmed to exist and to cover the entire Cand A diagnostic window. Online access in this pass is paywalled at Geneanet Premium and the Genealogy Store. Highest-value targeted future pull because it would index every John Gurney baptism, marriage, and burial at Stewkley through 1653.
- Cand C (Berkhamsted, Herts): FreeREG indexes a "GURNEY" surname at St Peter Berkhamsted but does not expose the 1626 Richard / 1634 Sara baptisms in a freely searchable record-level interface in this pass. Findmypast/Ancestry/Hertfordshire Archives required for a real Berkhamsted marriage-of-John-to-Mary search. Park as a targeted Findmypast/Hertfordshire Archives pull.
- Ackworth Yorkshire John + Mary Barton "Very unlikely" candidate produced no online context in this pass.
- Pre-existing TNA E 115/180/113 (1641 certificate of residence Aylesbury → Northants) and 1650 Walgrave Northants tenancy are recorded in the older `Gurney_Research_Findings_V7.md` and `Gurney_ProtestationReturns_Analysis.md` notes but are not currently referenced in the case file's §8 elimination table.
