### 1.1 [FINDING] Elizabeth Rivet buried 23 October 1626 at Garveston — the probable maternal grandmother of John G13 was alive at Garveston through his boyhood
Ancestry "England, Select Deaths and Burials, 1538–1991" (IGI-derived), record 16796808: **Elizabeth Rivet, burial 23 Oct 1626, Garveston, Norfolk** (FHL film 1702622, item 11 — the same film/item as the 1584 Rivet×Podmer marriage, i.e. the same Garveston register/BT extraction). URL: ancestry.com/search/collections/9840/records/16796808.
Analysis:
- Identity: almost certainly **Elizabeth (Podmer) Rivet, wife of Francis** — the only adult Elizabeth Rivet documented at Garveston; the extraction source is the same register item that carries her 1584 marriage and the Francis-and-Elizabeth children (Alice 1606, Francis 1609). (Alternative: an unrecorded Elizabeth, e.g. a child of Francis — but no Elizabeth christening appears among the IGI child set.) Index level; image-confirm needed.
- **Bearing on the fostering hypothesis (case file / L-128 complex): the probable maternal grandmother lived at Garveston until Oct 1626** — through the entire window after Margaret's death (c. 1616–17) when the motherless John (b. c. 1609/10) and Edward (b. c. 1611) needed raising, 4 miles from their East Dereham birthplace. A living grandmother is a far stronger fostering anchor than the generic "maternal kin" previously arguable. John was ~16–17 when she died — old enough by then for apprenticeship (the tailoring-trade route).
- **Bearing on the Wymondham Francis (round-1 §3.7):** Elizabeth's burial at Garveston in 1626 implies Francis Rivet's household was still Garveston-based through 1626 — while the "Wymondham Francis" was having children there 1622–26 by (implicitly) a different wife. **This strongly favours the Wymondham Francis being a DISTINCT man** (probably the Buxton Francis-and-Ruth line), cleanly protecting the Garveston chronology. Round-1's lead can be downgraded to a disambiguation note.
- **Register-survival note:** the June register read covered burials 1561–1625 and batch-2 1627–75 — **the 1626 burial falls exactly in the unread seam between the two batches.** The IGI extraction (film 1702622 item 11) caught it. This also re-frames L-131: Margaret's c. 1616–17 burial was NOT found in the 1561–1625 section that WAS read — so her burial genuinely is elsewhere (East Dereham route stands) — but any event dated 1626 at Garveston must be checked against film 1702622, not DGS 004033226's read sections.
- Sources: `fs-england-deaths-burials` (Ancestry copy, collection 9840) / FHL film 1702622 item 11. Also re-confirmed the 1584 marriage detail record (collection 9852 record 33546676: Elizabeth Podmer × Francis Rivet, 2 Jul 1584, Garveston; film 1702622 item 11) — no father given for the bride (nothing further recoverable at index level).

### 1.2 [FINDING — reopens candidate analysis] Ancestry's image-backed NRO collection (61045) covers early Garveston — and reveals a COMPETING Margaret Rivet (bp 1 Feb 1577, daughter of ROBERT) plus a third household head Thomas
Ancestry "Norfolk, England, Church of England Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials, 1535–1812" (collection **61045**, in association with the Norfolk Record Office, WITH register images), search `_riv*` keyword Garveston — the complete Garveston Rivet index set in this collection (11 primary rows; all carry View-Images):

- Marriage: **Robt Rivet × Agnes, Apr 1586, Garveston.**

Three-source register-survival map now coherent.
