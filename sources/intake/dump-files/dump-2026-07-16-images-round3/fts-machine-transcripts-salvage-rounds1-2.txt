===MUSSETT-WILL === Will of John Mussett of Garveston, made 28 Nov 1654, proved Nov 1660 (NCC, before Ro. Thaxton surrogate)
ark: 3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-T9PL-R  | URL: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-T9PL-R?view=fullText
Executors of this my last Will & Testamt . They taking my debts paying my debts, and bringing my Bodie last Will containing three sheets of paper Revoking all other Wills by me formerly made In witnessewhereof I have felt my hand seal the day and yeare within written the marke of Richard my hill Sealed & delided in the presence of : Clem Cubitt the wee of John Bushe : Rich : Shalder . Probatum fint hand Testamente Disinnon die mensis Novembris Anno and 1660 : Coram Ro - Thaxton Ohio, Surro Venire John Myles Leguntoris Curie Cout Epah Nortons at Collafiit Adm to Bondred Execut in heir Testamt norah A Santer des Evangelia de bene Salue Cottamentum The Musett : In the name of God Amen the Eight Ewentieth day of November In the year of our Lord God One Thousand Six hundred fifty four : I John Mussett of Garveston in the County of North doman, being well stricken in years, and not well in Bodie, but of good & perfect remembrance ; for ordaine Make this my present last Will and Testamt . in manner & forme following ( that is to say ) first & comend my Soul into the mercifull hands protection of God Almighty, steadfastly beleiveing the free pardon Remission of all my, Times, and injoyment of a better, and Eternal life purchased for me by the meritorious death & Passion of my blessed Saviour Jesus Christ my Bodie I comend to the - Earth whereof it was formed, to be therein Christianly Interred at the will of my Executors or Executrixes And as for those small Temporall Blessings & benefits with God hath given me I will & devise them in manner and form following 9 first first Will bequeath unto Elizabeth my beloved wife All that these my house and well meadow at Pasture, and whatsoever by being in Garveston aforesaid as well freehold as Copy hold for during the whole term of her naturall life, after the death decease of the said Elizabeth my wife Will devise give Bequeath them all with all and linguler their contented unto Mary Mussett my daughter to her heires & assignes for ever : on Condicon notwithstanding following as herein expled ( That is to say That she the said Mary Mussett her heirs Executors or Assigned, shall pay or cause to be paid unto Ann my daughter the wife of Simon dayes of Shipdham in the County aforesaid Wheeler, or to her heirs Executors or Assignes the true whole sume of Twenty Pounds of good morie of England in manner & form following That is to say ) five Pounds of good mound of England within one whole year next after the death & decease of the said Elizabeth my wife, At or in the now dwelling house of mee the said John Mullett Situate in Garveston aforesaid ; And likewise five Pounds yeard and early successively at the place aforesaid untill the full same of the said Twentie pounds be fully satisfied and paid But if the said Mary Mullett in said daughter her heirs or Assignes shall make defect or default in the payment of the said Twenty Pounds, five pound pearely after the decease of the said Elizabeth my wife, or of any part thereof in manner form before specified and Declared, then my mind will is that the sd Ann my daughter her heirs or Assignes shall inheritt occupy possess & injoy the said houses Lands and Tenements with all & singuler their apptenances to them & their heires for ever ; Lastly I Constitute and make the Elizabeth my wife, Mary Millett my daughter in andly
