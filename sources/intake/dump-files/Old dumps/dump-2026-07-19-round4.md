## §2 ⭐ East Dereham manor court: "land formerly of Margery/Margaret Gurney widow" — a pre-Francis Gurney household at Ellingfield, read from the pullable court films

The East Dereham manor-court films (DGS **004389483**) are das/v2-pullable (confirmed: S3 redirect, not 403). The two "undated Gurney" leaves round 2 staged for packet 53 were pulled at full resolution and read. **Both are boundary clauses (abuttals) naming a dead Gurney widow's former copyhold:**

- **CYG — folio 25, court of 3 April 1627.** Admission of **Christopher Fuller** to ~14 acres "apud **Ellingfeild in East Dereham**." The parcel abuts, on the west, **"terr[am] nup[er] Margerie Gurney vid[ue], modo [—]"** — *land formerly of Margery Gurney widow, now [another's]* — with William Bower (east), Blomefield and Hugh Bunker/Baker adjoining.
- **7DQ — folio 132, court of 10 Oct 1636.** A surrender/admission whose abuttal names **"terr[am] quond[am] Margarete Gurney vid[ue]"** — *land formerly of Margaret Gurney widow* — alongside "nup[er] Francisci Elwyn clerici," Osborne, and Roger Daynes.

*Margerie (1627) / Margarete (1636) are spelling variants of one forename.* The operative words are **nuper / quondam** ("late / formerly"): by 1627 the parcel is *remembered* as a Gurney widow's — so the Gurney tenancy at Ellingfield **predates 1627**, and the widow was gone (consistent with Margery Gurney widow, will proved 1602-03).

**Why this matters (analysis).** The repo currently starts the East Dereham Gurney copyhold chain at the **1636 "Gurner"** entry. These two abuttals push a **named Gurney copyhold at Ellingfield in East Dereham back before 1627** and tie it to the **widow Margery** — i.e. an established Gurney landholding household at East Dereham a generation before Francis G14 baptised his children there (John c.1609/10 … Francis 1633). This is the concrete, image-grounded form of the round-2/3 hypothesis: **there was a pre-existing Gurney presence at East Dereham, which is a plausible reason Francis settled his young family in that parish rather than at the Great Ellingham / West Barsham seat.** The will image being locked does not block this conclusion — the manor court supplies the residence, the parcel (Ellingfield), and the widowhood independently. Note "Ellingfield" is a field/locus within East Dereham parish (not Great Ellingham) — but the name coincidence with the Gurney gentry seat is worth a footnote, not a claim.

**Placement:** `research/topics/john-gurney-candidate-others.md` (Dereham copyhold pre-history — merge with the existing 1636-Gurner / L-98 material: the chain now begins with *nuper Margery Gurney widow* by 1627, not 1636) + `research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` (East-Dereham-origin / L-124: the "why Dereham" answer is now evidenced). SourceId: `familysearch-fulltext-search` for the DGS 004389483 court images (arks in raw-extracts §A). **Leads:** L-98 → the two undated Gurney tokens are read (Margery/Margaret widow abuttals, 1627 + 1636); L-124 → the East Dereham Margery anchor is now corroborated at the manor-court level.
