Sources: American Aristocracy "Grizzell (Eastwick) Apthorp" (https://americanaristocracy.com/people/grizzell-eastwick); Cobbold Family History Trust bio #9342 (https://family-tree.cobboldfht.com/biographies/9342/griselda-grizzel-eastwick); Boston1775 blog (https://boston1775.blogspot.com/2013/02/grizzell-apthorp-widow-employer.html).
