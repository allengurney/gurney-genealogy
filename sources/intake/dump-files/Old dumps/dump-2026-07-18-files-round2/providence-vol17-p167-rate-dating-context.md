# Providence vol. 17 p.167 rate — dating evidence (round 2)

Source: *Early Records of the Town of Providence* vol. 17, archive.org `cu31924092200694`, djvu.txt (`https://archive.org/download/cu31924092200694/cu31924092200694_djvu.txt`).

The undated rate at printed p.167 (vol.17 index: "Gurney John 167 211") is bracketed as the rate **finished 3 January 1697/8**. Verbatim OCR anchors (line numbers in the djvu.txt):

- L12151: `town of prouidence : this 24* of Aprill : 1697` (town meeting preceding the rate)
- L12887 → L12899 (the rate's name column, one token per OCR line):
  - `Widow feild` … `o` `3` (= 00-03-00, 3s)
  - `John Gurney` … `o` (John Gurney rated, immediately after Widow Feild)
- L13147: `This Rate was finished January: 3: 1698 by us` (closing line of the same rate list)

Conclusion: John Gurney was a rated Providence inhabitant (~3s), listed next to Widow Feild, in the rate finished **3 Jan 1697/8** — earlier than the 1705 rate (8s, printed p.211) found in round 1. Confidence ~85% (OCR-bracketed; page header itself not cleanly recoverable from OCR, so the date is inferred from the enclosing "24 Apr 1697" meeting and the "finished 3 Jan 1698" closing line).

The 1705 rate for comparison (same volume, L15062): `John Gurney 00 08 - 00` among Mathewson/Hopkins/Waterman households.
