### CYG — folio 25, 3 April 1627 (Christopher Fuller admission)
Admission of Christopher Fuller to ~14 acres of land "apud **Ellingfeild in East Dereham**." Boundary clause (working transcription; abbreviations expanded in brackets):

> "…inter terr[am] … Christofori nup[er] Willi[elmi] Bower ex p[ar]te orient[ali] **et terr[am] nup[er] Margerie Gurney vid[ue], modo [—] ex p[ar]te occident[ali]**; caput bor[eale] nup[er] abutt[at] sup[er] terr[am] [—] Blomefeild … et caput austr[ale] sup[er] … ; … nup[er] Hugonis Bunker [Baker?] …"

Key: **"terram nuper Margerie Gurney vidue"** = "land formerly of Margery Gurney widow." *nuper* (late/formerly) → the parcel was Margery Gurney widow's before 1627; she is past/dead by then. Neighbours: William Bower (E), Blomefield, Hugh Bunker/Baker.

### 7DQ — folio 132, 10 Oct 1636 (surrender by Hugh Osborne? gen.)
Heavy bleed-through leaf. Bottom-right abuttal (working transcription):

> "…una p[ar]cell[a] terr[e] arrabil[is] … in [East]dereham … [pro] … **terr[am] quond[am] Margarete Gurney vid[ue]** … et terr[am] modo … nup[er] **Francisci Elwyn cleric[i]** … abbutt[at] … modo Jos[ephi?] Osborne … nup[er] Rogeri Daynes …"

Key: **"terram quondam Margarete Gurney vidue"** = "land formerly of Margaret Gurney widow." Neighbours/prior holders named: Francis Elwyn clerk, Osborne, Roger Daynes.

*Margerie (1627) / Margarete (1636) = spelling variants of one forename. Both leaves record land REMEMBERED as formerly a Gurney widow's — i.e. the Gurney tenancy predates 1627.*
