## 6. Analyst insights, speculation, and synthesis notes (the thinking layer)

1. **The Bardolf key.** The single most consequential result this session: the junior line's Swathings tenure ran through the Bardolfs *because the Bardolfs were the senior Gournay line's heirs* (Juliana). CIPM vii.243 shows Bardolf's Norfolk caput manors (Caister, Cantley, Winburgh) as "parcel of the barony of Gurnay" with junior John de Gurnay acting as the trusted re-settlement feoffee AND holding Swathings of Bardolf by knight service. Combined with Farrer (Swathings within the senior fee via Hugh II→Burguignon) and DG-Supp Note 115 (Swathyng "de Hugone de Bardolf… et idem de Rege"), the architecture is: senior barony forfeited 1204→ restored → Juliana → Bardolf; junior line held fragments OF that barony. **Implication worth testing next: the junior line may descend from a cadet enfeoffed within the senior fee before 1204** — the "Hugh de Gurnay of Letton" Lewes-priory deed (A.2972) and the Clemency-de-Chesney marriage (Farrer pp.313-318) are exactly the kind of 12th-c. Mitford-hundred/Norfolk datapoints that could anchor such a cadet origin. This reframes L-151 from "reconcile two accounts" to "identify the cadet enfeoffment."
2. **The Gurney-Heydon-Townshend-Calthorpe quadrangle (1470s-1500s).** Paston 1472/3 (Heydon arms-support for Gurney at Saxthorpe; Townshend as proposed mediator) + A.7857 (Heydon→Gurney Flegg feoffment) + the CIPM Henry VII feoffee webs (Townshend, Calthorpe/Fortescue, Wynter, Geddyng/Drury) + "William Gurnay the younger" = the West Barsham Gurneys were embedded as *professional trustees* in the dominant post-Paston legal-gentry bloc.
