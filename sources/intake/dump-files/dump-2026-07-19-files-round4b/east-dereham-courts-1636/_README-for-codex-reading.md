# East Dereham manor court leaves — bulk pull for paleographic reading (Codex)

**Task:** read every leaf for any **Gurney / Gurner / Gurnay / Gournay** occurrence — as a **tenant** (surrender, admission, presentment, homage/jury list) OR as an **abuttal** (boundary: "land late/formerly of … Gurney"). The abuttals are the point: FamilySearch full-text found only 13 Gurney tokens in the whole film because the Latin/English secretary hand OCRs as salad, so a page-by-page read is the only way to catch them. Capture forename, relationship, copyhold description, court date, and folio.

**Known target pattern (from round 4):** two abuttals already found name **"terram nuper/quondam Margerie/Margarete Gurney vidue"** ("land formerly of Margery/Margaret Gurney widow") at **Ellingfield in East Dereham** (courts of 1627 and 1636). The widow = Margery Gurney (will proved 1602-03). Look for: her late husband's forename; any Gurney admission/surrender; the parcel's descent (who held it before/after her).

## Provenance
- Film **DGS 004389483** ("Manorial court rolls, 1554-1907"), FamilySearch. NRO original = **DC 12/5** (Manor of East Dereham of the Queen), catalog koha:402769.
- **This film interleaves three manors: East Dereham + Deopham + Denver**, each with its own foliation — image order is NOT global-chronological. Confirmed calibration points (read this session):
  - `edc-idx1470.jpg` = **Deopham** manor, court of 6 May **1656** (Sir Thomas Wodehouse lord). → idx ≲1540 shades into Deopham/Denver, later dates.
  - `edc-idx1600.jpg` = **East Dereham**, court of 30 Sept **1633** (9 Chas I), folio 72.
  - `edc-idx1629.jpg` = **East Dereham**, court of **1635/1636** (11 Chas I), folio 98 — a court-leet homage/jury list for East Dereham + Dumpling-bury + Hardingham.
  - Gradient ≈ 11 idx/year, forward-chronological within the East Dereham item: **idx 1555–1650 ≈ East Dereham 1633–1636** (the G13 late-teens window).
- Files named `edc-idx<N>.jpg` where `<N>` is the FamilySearch image number (`?i=<N>`). Full archival resolution (~1.4 MB each). 201 leaves, idx 1470–1689.

## Coverage caveats
- The East Dereham (Queen's manor) court book **starts 1623** — there is **no East Dereham coverage 1605–1622** on this film (the only earlier East Dereham manor court book at NRO is CHC 1562–1582). So 1605–1622 is a documentary gap.
- 1623–1632 East Dereham courts sit at idx below ~1600 but are interleaved with Deopham/Denver in the idx 1470–1599 band — read by date/manor heading, not by idx.
- The manor's own **index to court books 1623–1820** exists (NRO DC 12/5/22 = FS DGS 4389277 Item 8) — a faster route to Gurney *tenants* (not abuttals); not yet pulled.
