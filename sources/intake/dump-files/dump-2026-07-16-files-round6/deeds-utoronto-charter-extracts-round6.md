# DEEDS database (deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca) — charter full-text extracts, round 6 (L-232 residue)

Follow-up to round-5's `deeds-utoronto-charter-extracts-round5.md`. These are the not-yet-pulled index items from round-5 §2.6 + the two remaining L-232 citations. All five charters cite **"SCRIPTA – Base des actes normands médiévaux"** as their external source — DEEDS is mirroring SCRIPTA for the Norman acta, so DEEDS's shown dates ARE SCRIPTA's editorial assignments (this answers, in part, round-5's "check SCRIPTA's dating of the Jumièges 1040 charter": the assigned dates in DEEDS reproduce SCRIPTA's, flagged "Assigned").

Charter-page URL pattern: `https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/<8-digit id>` (server-rendered; WebFetch-readable).

## 03781569 — "Hugo senex de Gornaco" (Regesta Henrici Primi, 1123×1128)
- Cartulary: Regesta Henrici Primi 1100–1135 [Westminster]; ext. SCRIPTA. Royal confirmation (King Henry I).
- **"Hugo senex de Gornaco"** granted a house in **Escuceio**; his successors also granted property. The "senex" (the elder) styling is a contemporary generational disambiguator ~1123–1128 (Hugh III's floruit), separating him from a younger Hugh.
- Witnesses incl. Geoffrey abp of Rouen, John bp of Lisieux, Bernard bp of St Davids, Humphrey de Bohun, Robert earl of Leicester, Nigel d'Aubigny, Juhel de Medanta, Robert de Hagia; queens Adeliza + (dec.) Matilda.

## 05390078 — Hugh de Gournay witnesses William I's grant to Saint-Benoît-sur-Loire (April 1067)
- Cartulary: Recueil des chartes de l'abbaye de Saint-Benoît-sur-Loire (0539); ext. SCRIPTA. Date April 1067 (regnal yr VII of King Philip).
- William the Conqueror's donation of church properties + castle revenues to Fleury/Saint-Benoît. Attestation: **"Signum Hugonis de Gornaio"** among the witnesses (with Gerald de Novomercato, Stigand dapifer, Henry de Ferrers, etc.); royal family William + Matilda + sons Robert & Richard.
- A very early (1067) attestation of Hugh de Gournay (II) — a year after the Conquest.

## 03771092 — Hugo de Gornay + "Warnerio consanguineo" (Regesta Willelmi Conquestoris, 1068×1076)
- Cartulary: Regesta Willelmi Conquestoris et Willelmi Rufi 1066–1100 [Westminster]; ext. SCRIPTA. Royal decree; grant to a female monastery of the Virgin (Montivilliers?).
- Land-purchase clause "**concedente Hugone de la Ferteit et Hugone de Gornay**", with **"Warnerio consanguineo"** (a kinsman Warner) in the same transaction context. Confirms the round-4/5 "Warner kinsman" datum and a Gournay–de la Ferté co-attestation.
- Places: Monasterium Villare, Fécamp, Eschetot, Magneville, Caen, Longueville, Rouen.

## 08070002 — King John's Le Goulet-context act naming Hugh de Gurnay as security; maritagium clause (May 1200)
- Cartulary: Annales de Burton (0807), **BL Cotton MS Vespasian E III f.10v (p.34)**; ext. SCRIPTA. Done at **Guleton [Le Goulet]**, May 1200.
- The Anglo-French settlement: Philip II + John, homage for Brittany (Arthur), and a **maritagium** granting fiefs to Louis (Philip's son) on his marriage to the King of Castile's daughter, with reversion conditions. **"Hugo de Gurnay [Hugone de Gurney]"** stands among the English magnates providing security (with William Marshal earl of Pembroke, Baldwin count of Aumale). = round-5's "1200 Gaillon maritagium act."

## 05570059 — King John confirms Robert of Leicester's lands; Hugh de Gurnaco witness (23 Oct 1201)
- Cartulary: **Newly Discovered Charters of King John** [Westminster]; ext. SCRIPTA. Done "apud Rupem Andeliaci" [Les Andelys / Château-Gaillard], 23 Oct 1201 (regnal yr 3).
- **Verbatim:** "Iohannes Dei gratia rex Anglie … concedimus … quod Robertus comes Leircestr et heredes sui … teneant et habeant omnes terras suas et feoda sua cum omnibus libertatibus … Testibus **Willelmo Marescallo comite de Pembroc, Rannulfo comite Cestrie, Willelmo de Humet constabulario Normannie, Hugone de Gurnaco**. Dat per manum Simonis archidiaconi Wellensis apud Rupem Andeliaci xxiii die Octobris regni nostri anno tercio."
- **Hugh de Gournay (IV, d. 1214)** as one of the last pre-1204 witnesses at King John's Norman caput, alongside the greatest magnates — a terminal-Norman-generation attestation. = round-5's "1201 Les Andelys act."
