**The FamilySearch film (chaining resolved).** The ANF register-copy wills are filmed on FamilySearch (catalog `koha:291384`, "Wills and administrations, 1459-1857," Archdeaconry of Norfolk Court, 177 reels). Register **v. 35 (Offwood), 1602-1604 = British Film 167091, DGS 8045772**. Margery's will sits at folio 191 of that register.

**Image-only — not full-text.** An FTS coverage probe scoped to `q.groupName=008045772` returns **0** for `+wife` and **0** for `Gurn*` — the film is not full-text indexed (independently confirming round 2's FTS negative for Norfolk Gurney wills). **Why this matters (analysis).** John G13's own documented East Dereham baptism is **c.1609/10** (case-file v4, entry E, NRO PD 86/41, image 00715 — "John the sonne of ffrancis Gurnie"), and Francis G14's documented Dereham presence runs c.1609/10–1633. Margery's widowhood in **1602-03** puts a Gurney husband dead at East Dereham **before 1602** — a Gurney household at Dereham a **half-generation before Francis's children arrive there**. This is the strongest available lead on the unanswered "why did Francis settle his young family at East Dereham (not the family seat at Great Ellingham/West Barsham)?" question: a pre-existing Gurney kin-presence at Dereham. Her will can name her late husband and children/kin and test that link. (Note: the round-2 dump's "a decade before John's 1616 baptism" overstated it — John's baptism is c.1609/10, so Margery predates it by ~7 years, and predates the whole known Dereham copyhold chain, which the repo starts at the 1636 "Gurner" entry.)

## §3 Chaining #2 — Thomas Gurnay Esq. of Great Ellingham, inventory 1615-16: a CONFLICT with the g15 companion's Thomas-death-date, not an uncle-hearth confirmation ⭐

**The record.** Ancestry 62679/155780 confirms: Thomas Gurnay, **Esquire**, of **Great Ellingham**, record type **Inventory**, probate 1615-16. An inventory without a will = intestate → an administration (and, if a minor heir, a guardianship/wardship) should accompany it. (NRO catalogue reference for court/folio [REC] — the NRO permalink 502'd all session.)

**Placing Thomas in the pedigree (the real finding).** The Visitation of Norfolk (Harl. MS 1552, Harl. Soc. 32 p. 141) names Henry G15's eldest son as **Thomas of West Barsham** (m. Martha, dau. of Sir Edward Lewkenor of Denham, Suffolk — the senior heir), whose son **Edward** (b. 1608) succeeded Henry in 1623. The g15 companion (2026-06-11) concluded — from the packet-10 reading of Henry's 1 May 1621 will bequeathing to "**my eldest sonne Thomas**" (high confidence) — that this Thomas died **1621×1623**, and it *debunked* the older "Thomas d. 1614" tradition, attributing "1614" to the date of Henry's revoked earlier will.

**The conflict.** This newly-located primary probate event — **Thomas Gurnay Esq of Great Ellingham, inventory 1615-16** — lands squarely on the old "Thomas died ~1614/15" date the companion set aside. Two readings, exposed not reconciled:
- **(a) It IS Henry's eldest son Thomas**, who therefore died 1615-16 intestate (his son Edward, b.1608, then a ~7-year-old minor — which fits an inventory+admon+wardship far better than a will). This would **overturn** the companion's conclusion and require re-reading the packet-10 "my eldest sonne Thomas" bequest (a different Thomas? a grandson? a misreading?).
- **(b) It is a DIFFERENT Thomas Gurnay Esq** at Great Ellingham — but the Visitation shows no second adult armigerous Thomas there in this window, so (b) needs its own candidate.

**Decisive test.** Read the 1615-16 inventory + any accompanying admon/guardianship: if the administrator is **Martha** (widow) and/or the heir is **Edward** (minor), it is Henry's eldest son Thomas and the death-date conclusion flips. The gentry style argues the inventory is NCC or ANW rather than ANF; note the g15 companion's control that the NCC registered wills 1615-16 carry no Gurney testator — consistent, because a loose inventory (intestate) is not a registered will.

**Bearing on L-136.** The round-2 dump floated this Thomas as the "foster-hearth uncle Thomas" for John G13. But Thomas of West Barsham/Great Ellingham is Francis G14's **brother** = John's paternal **uncle**; if this inventory is his and he died 1615-16, his household could **not** have fostered John (who was a young child c.1609/10 and whose Dereham/London life ran later) — it would **weaken**, not support, the uncle-Thomas fostering branch. Reading the inventory settles which way L-136 moves.

**Placement:** `research/people/g15-henry-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` (Thomas-death-date conflict — merge into the existing "Two conflicts, both now resolved" treatment, which this reopens) + a one-line bearing note on `research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` (L-136). **Lead:** reopens the Thomas-death question (L-111 orbit); L-136 gets a concrete answerable probate/image target.
