## L-158 — Norfolk feet of fines: two new date-fixed William Gurney occurrences

### 4 June 1486 — CP 25/1/170/195, no. 2

Object page: https://www.medievalgenealogy.org.uk/fines/abstracts/CP_25_1_170_195.shtml

Image landing page (AALT): https://aalt.law.uh.edu/AALT4/CP25%281%29/CP25%281%29no170/IMG_0020.htm

> Date: Two weeks from Holy Trinity, 1 Henry VII [4 June 1486]. Parties: John Heuenyngham, knight, Edmund Bedyngfeld', knight, William Boleyn', knight, Henry Grey, knight, John Paston', esquire, Thomas Heuenyngham, esquire, **William Gurney the elder, esquire**, Richard Suth'well', esquire, William Yeluerton', esquire, Henry Spelman', James Hobert and John Wotton', querents, and John Groos and Margaret, his wife, deforciants. Property: 4 messuages, 200 acres of land, 20 acres of meadow, 60 acres of pasture and 60 acres of heath and 40 shillings of annual rent in Westwyk and Worsted'.

The calendar gives the standardised persons as including William Gurney and places Westwick and Worstead. The phrasing **the elder** is a useful chronological discriminator for the separate Gournay/Gurney collateral problem; it is not proof of parentage.

### 3 February 1491 — CP 25/1/170/195, no. 24

Object page: https://www.medievalgenealogy.org.uk/fines/abstracts/CP_25_1_170_195.shtml

Image landing page (AALT): https://aalt.law.uh.edu/AALT4/CP25%281%29/CP25%281%29no170/IMG_0042.htm

> Date: The day after the Purification of the Blessed Mary, 6 Henry VII [3 February 1491]. Parties: Thomas Wodehous, esquire, **William Gurney, esquire**, Edmund Harberd', clerk, and John Pegeon', querents, and Ralph Platfote and Alice, his wife, deforciants. Property: A moiety of 1 messuage called Towtons in Tytteleshale and Godwyk'.

This is a separate, precisely dated Norfolk occurrence. The calendar standardises the places as Tittleshall and Godwick (in Tittleshall).

## Great Ellingham / Hingham context: new creative-source objects

### Blomefield, *History of Norfolk*, Forehoe — Gurney's Manor

Object page: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/History_of_Norfolk/Volume_2/Forehoe

> "Was part of the great manor, granted to a younger branch of the family, before the forfeiture; it continued always in the family of that name residing at Barsham and Great-Elingham, in this county; **Henry Gurney was lord in 1572**; how it passed afterwards I do not find; but in 1715 it was owned by Mr. Larwood of Norwich, merchant."

This is a place/tenure pointer only. It supports an archive/search bridge between the Great Ellingham and Hingham holdings but does not resolve L-241's 1621x23 death presentment.

### British Museum astronomical watch, 1600–1610

Object page: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/H_1856-0429-1

The catalog dates the object 1600–1610 and prints an armorial attribution to the Norfolk Gournay/Gurney family. Its curator note says the arms with a rose difference likely belonged to Leonard Gournay/Gurney and characterises him as a seventh son. The note then repeats a pedigree/will assertion for Henry Gournay. It is a valuable *object-provenance lead*, not a primary proof of the asserted child count or descent; the underlying 1614/1623 will must be checked.

### CELM / Bodleian MS Tanner 175 — a manuscript-catalogue route not previously used in the repository source layer

Object page: https://celm.folger.edu/repositories/bodleian-tanner.html

> MS Tanner 175: A small folio volume of estate and personal records and of verse written by Henry Gurney (1549-1616), lord of the Manor of Great Ellingham, Norfolk, including his comments on other writers and inventory of his books, ii + 239 leaves. c.1570s-1608.

This is a creative-source route for L-241: not evidence of the post-1621 death presentment, but a named manuscript with estate records that may contain holding, tenant, or family-network clues. Later work should use the Bodleian catalogue/digital facsimile rather than rely on CELM's concise description.

### Mendlesham St Mary marriage transcription — surname-variant control sample (Rivett/Rivet)

Object PDF: https://essexandsuffolksurnames.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/mendlesham-marriages-grooms-1558-1749.pdf

The PDF identifies its transcription base as Suffolk Record Office references FB159/D/1/1, /3, /4 and reports the following distinct spellings in one parish series:

> RIVETT William — Sarah THROWER — 24 Nov 1580 — image 60.
>
> RIVETT Robert — Bridgett DUNKAN — 25 May 1622 — image 10.
>
> RIVET Tho. — Margret DAMUNT — 4 Jun 1655 — image 49.
>
> RIVETT William — Susan SHERWIN — 20 Sep 1654 — image 49.

These are not assigned to the direct Rivett line. The useful raw result is methodological: the same locally indexed register sequence uses both **Rivet** and **Rivett**, reinforcing that both must remain in the conservative search-variant set.
