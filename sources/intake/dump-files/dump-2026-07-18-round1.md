## §3 L-237 — Early Records of the Town of Providence (21-vol. printed series) Gurney sweep — G12-adjacent (Providence John & Sarah)

Method: archive.org full-text (djvu.txt) downloads of the printed series — 18 volumes obtained (1–6, 8–13, 16–19, 21; identifiers in `providence-volmap.json` pattern `cu31924…`/`earlyrecordsofto…`), swept for `gurn*/gourn*/garney/gurnney/surney`. Raw hit extracts (±3 lines): **`dump-2026-07-18-files-round1/providence-gurney-sweep-extracts.txt`**. Series URL pattern: `https://archive.org/details/<identifier>`.

**NEW FINDING — John Gurney was a rated inhabitant of Providence in 1705.** Vol. 17 (Cornell copy, <https://archive.org/details/cu31924092200694>), printed pp. 211–212: the **1705 Providence town rate** (constable's warrant dated 4 Oct 1705; produce-price schedule "Wheat att 3-8, Ry att 2-6…") includes **"John Gurney 00-08-00"** (8s), listed among Mathewson/Hopkins/Waterman households. This is the first documented event between his 1696 expulsion order and the 30 Apr 1714 will appearance: warned out 1695–96, he not only stayed in the Providence orbit but was a **settled, rated inhabitant by 1705**. A second, undated rate list in the same volume (printed p. 167 per the volume index "Gurney John 167 211") lists **"John Gurney"** rated ~3s **immediately after "Widow feild"** — physical adjacency on the list to the Field household he later headed by marriage (rate lists commonly run in neighbourhood order) [REC — date the p. 167 rate from the printed page (header outside the OCR window); vol. 17 covers town papers c. 1700s].

**Printed-edition locators for facts now cited only to the FamilySearch film** (cite-strengthening, no new facts): vol. 10 (<https://archive.org/details/cu31924092200629>) pp. 34–38 = the 1695–96 John Gurney warning-out/whipping orders (verbatim text matches companion); p. 136 = the 30 Apr 1714 council record of Sarah's will. Vol. 7 (<https://archive.org/details/earlyrecordsofto07prov>) **pp. 166–169 = the full recorded will (p. 166), inventory (p. 168, "departed this life the sixteenth day of Aprill … 1714"), and letter of administration to Joseph Field (p. 169)**, incl. the witness block "John X Gurney his mark / Sarah X Gurney her mark" and John's mark on the inventory attestation 30 Apr 1714.

**Negative:** vols 1–6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 16, 18, 19, 21 = zero Gurney-variant hits (vol 12's sole hit = "dept Gournr" = deputy Governor, false positive). **Residual:** vols **14, 15, 20** not obtained (vol 14 = the lead's named Google Books target `WMUTAAAAYAAJ`; archive.org identifiers for 14/15/20 either absent from the title search or failed volume-metadata parse) [REC — fetch vols 14/15/20 (Google Books plain-text or retry archive.org by direct identifier browse) and complete the sweep; also re-run the sweep for `field`-adjacent "Gurney" mentions once vol 14 is in hand]. **Suggested lead update:** L-237 → Partial (18/21 vols swept; 1705 rate finding to promote; vols 14/15/20 residual).
