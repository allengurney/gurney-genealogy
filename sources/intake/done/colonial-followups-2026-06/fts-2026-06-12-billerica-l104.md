## Findings Summary

### 1. Re-grant of the surrendered ten-acre lot

**YES — the surrendered lot was re-granted, to John Hall, by 19.1.1660.**

Image 15 (ark 3:1:3QS7-89Z8-TV2C), a town-meeting record page, carries the following entry:

> "19.1.60 day the Towne Doe grant to John Hall one halfe of the Lot yt was granted to John Gurney, it being divided and he to have his choice of the two halus when it is divided, and the Comite it have payd out the sevil : Lotts in the towne shall divide the same the Towne hath give the said John hall : untill to morrow fortnight to give his aust for his exceptence or refusall of the said grant"

Date in modern notation: 19 March 1660 (O.S. 1659/60; month 1 = March in the Puritan calendar). The surrounding context establishes this as one of a cluster of early-1660 town actions (the 7th [January] 1660 town meeting chose Samuell Champney as Constable, etc.). 

The grant is for **one half** of Gurney's lot, divided. No re-grant of the other half to any identifiable person appears in the FTS sweep. Note the entry says "the Towne hath give the said John Hall untill to morrow fortnight to give his aust [answer] for his acceptance or refusal" — John Hall's formal acceptance was presumably recorded elsewhere. This is the same image 15 that also carries the rate-list area from image 14.

**Timeline of the lot:**
- 15.6.1659 (15 Aug 1659): Granted to John Gurney, conditional on him inhabiting and on Richard Gurney being accepted as inhabitant
- 26.10.1659 (26 Dec 1659): Daniel Shead answers for his father John Gurney; John Gurney surrenders the lot back to the town
- 19.1.1659/60 (19 Mar 1660): Town grants one half of Gurney's lot to John Hall

### 2. Who holds the later abuttals — John Gurney as a neighbour

Several lot-description and meadow-allocation pages continue to name John Gurney as an abuttal/neighbour after the surrender of the ten-acre lot. This implies John Gurney remained a landholder in Billerica (holding upland and meadow granted under other entries) even after returning the October 1659 lot. The relevant abuttals are:

- Image 169 (parallel-copy survey page, header "308 X (16) X 173 183"): "A John Gurney South and ye ministers Land & Robert Parker & Will Pattin West" — in a lot description for an abutting proprietor; John Gurney is south boundary
- Image 179 (meadow allocation): "John Layed out to him four acors and a quarter more or Gurney Dess : beging at the bonds of James Paterson" — meadow lot laid out to John Gurney adjacent to James Patterson's meadow
- Image 180 (meadow allocation, parallel-copy area, page header "328"): "John Gurney , dayed out to him four acres and a halfe more or less being the second Lott in order, which begins at the North corner of the month of hors Brooke Lyinge Eight poles wide cross the Meddow bounded with John Rogers South"; also "bounded with John Parker North and John Gurney South" for an adjacent lot
- Image 329 (parallel copy): "Dudly James line three pole wind on the North John Gurney & Will Hamlet Coyle & with the marrow to the Bank" — upland lot abuttal

### 3. "Gurney Tanner" verdict

**NOT a person styled "Gurney the Tanner." It is a misread of "Towne Charges."**

Image 214 (ark 3:1:3QSQ-G9Z8-TK1S) shows: "John Gurney Tanne Charges - of - 10". This is a charges/debts list. The word "Tanne" is an OCR error for "Towne" — confirmed by:
- Image 13 (ark 3:1:3QS7-89Z8-TV6F), which is plainly the same list and reads cleanly: "John Gurney : Towne Charges of -10-7"
- The surrounding entries on both images follow the same pattern (Gookin, Sheldon, Paterson, Rice, Redinge all have "Towne Charges" or house valuations)
- Image 214 is a degraded copy or different-camera image of the same rate-accounting page

No occupation "tanner" attaches to John Gurney in any legible entry across the entire 17-card sweep.

### 4. Daniel Shed / Shead entries adjacent to Gurney

- **Image 173** (ark 3:1:3QS7-89Z8-T2GH, grant/surrender page): "26.10.1659 Daniel Shead in answer for his father John Gurney ; the said John Gurney surrendred up the Lott". Daniel Shead speaks for his father John Gurney — the transcript calls Gurney his father, making Daniel Shead apparently a son-in-law or step-son of John Gurney (or the scribal phrasing means Daniel answers on Gurney's behalf as an agent; the prior session's transcription read "father" in the possessive sense here).

- **Image 171** (ark 3:1:3QSQ-G9Z8-T2DZ, survey page, no direct Gurney): "ye & a Lott Sheads corner about two polls ; from the bounds the stump toward the brooke" — "Sheads corner" is a landmark on a survey line, indicating the Shead family held an identifiable lot whose corner served as a boundary marker.

- **Image 12** (ark 3:1:3QSQ-G9Z8-TVWM, lot-drawing list): "Daniel Shed" appears in a list of inhabitants including John Marshall, John Baldwin, Daniel Shed, John Sheldon, Will Sheldon, John Rogers. This is an early proprietors list (~1658–59). John Gurney also appears in the same area of the list: "John Gurney I" (the "I" is a lot-draw number or share indicator). Daniel Shed and John Gurney are sequential or proximate in the list, consistent with a familial or neighbourhood connection.

- **Image 570** (ark 3:1:3QS7-89Z8-TVY6, late-book town meeting ~1707): "Nathan Shead" appears in a list of assessors/sworn officers, long after 1659. The surname Shead persists in Billerica through the later book.

- **Image 328** (ark 3:1:3QS7-89Z8-T273, parallel copy of surrender): "her son ahead in answer for his father the said John Gurney Surrendred by the Lett" — badly garbled OCR of the same "Daniel Shead in answer for his father John Gurney" passage from image 173.
