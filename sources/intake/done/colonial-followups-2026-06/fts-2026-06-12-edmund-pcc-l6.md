## Verdict

**NOT FOUND.** No PCC will, administration, or other probate record has been located for Edmund Gurnay/Gurney of Harpley under any spelling variant across all queries conducted below. No Norfolk Record Office probate entry exists for him either. The absence is consistent with the NRO's known gap in Norwich Consistory registers 1643–1651; if a will was proved locally in that gap window it may simply be lost rather than absent. The PCC centralisation period (from 1653) falls after his 1648 death, so his estate would have been handled by whatever functioning church court he used in mid-1648 — which in practice during the Civil War may have been disrupted or informally resolved without formal probate. No positive evidence for intestacy administration has been found either.

This is not a probate record, but it may be genealogically significant: Edmund Gurnay was born at Great Ellingham (per the Harpley rectors register), and the case is dated the same year as his death. Frances Gurnay, widow, is possibly a sister-in-law or other family member; Henry Gurnay is likely a brother or nephew. The case may have arisen from estate or inheritance disputes following Edmund's death. **Worth examining C 5/609/120 at TNA for any mention of Edmund Gurnay's estate.** (Not online — requires in-person order or document download at TNA Kew.)
