# Unplaced and collateral Norfolk Gournay/Gurnay feet of fines (medieval)

Cross-cutting holding file for the medieval Norfolk Gournay/Gurnay final concords that surfaced in the Walter Rye feet-of-fines harvest but **cannot yet be tied with confidence to a single direct ancestor** — either collateral/same-name individuals, or direct-line manors where the exact Gurney generation is undetermined without a reign/date. The confidently-attributable fines live on their subject companions (Robert G22, entry #64; Matthew de Gournay G29, #236; Edmund Gournay G23, #1526/#262/#837/#489; Thomas Gournay II G20, #200). Full raw transcription of every entry, with the OCR/place-anchoring method, is in the corpus extract cited below. Identity resolution (precise regnal year, or an AALT manuscript image) is lead L-158.

## Direct-line manors — Gurney holder, generation undetermined

- **John de Gurney in Hardingham (and Reymerston)** — Rye Pt I, entry 1593 (v. William de Swatham). Hardingham is a direct-line manor (held G29 Matthew through G20 Thomas II — see [`research/places/hardingham.md`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/research/places/hardingham.md)), but which John (G27 Sir John I, G24, or G25) the fine names is undetermined without the reign.[^rye-fines-corpus]
- **William de Gurnai in Burnham** — Rye Pt I, entry 148 (v. Martin de Wertlai). Burnham ties to the Rose-de-Burnham / Harpley acquisition root (G29 — see [`research/people/g29-matthew-de-gournay-fact-sheet.research.md`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/research/people/g29-matthew-de-gournay-fact-sheet.research.md)); a William of the line (G30 William I or G28 William II) is possible but unfixed.[^rye-fines-corpus]

## Collateral / unplaced same-name

- **William son of Philip de Gurnay, and Elena his wife, in Reymerston** — Rye Pt I, entry 146 (temp. Edward II; v. Ela late wife of John de Calveley; "John de Gornay app. clam."). "Son of Philip" is a forename outside the main male line, so most likely a collateral branch.[^rye-fines-corpus]
- **John de Gurnay, of the manor of Hillington** — Rye Pt I, entry 1059 (v. Sarra wife of Roger de Kylby; entail, with remainder to Galfridus Wymer of Saham).[^rye-fines-corpus]
- **Ludovic de Gurnay** (named via his widow, Mabil) — Rye Pt I, entry 504.[^rye-fines-corpus]
- **Hugh de Gurnay** — entering a claim ("app. clam.") in a fine over Kineburgh and Carleton; a Hugh de Gurney also appears at Cantley (Rye Pt I).[^rye-fines-corpus]
- **[forename lost] de Gornay and Katrine his wife** v. Henry de Habelund — Rye Pt I.[^rye-fines-corpus]
- **[forename lost] Gurney and Havisia his wife, in Duneston** — Rye Pt I.[^rye-fines-corpus]

Each remains unattributed pending a date/reign or image read (lead L-158); promote to the relevant subject companion once the generation is fixed.

Internet Archive `ashortcalendarf02ryegoog` (Part I; local copy [`sources/corpus/rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk-part1.pdf`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/sources/corpus/rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk-part1.pdf)) and `ashortcalendarf00ryegoog` (Part II); underlying manuscripts TNA CP 25/1 (Norfolk), images at AALT. Source ID: `rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk`.
