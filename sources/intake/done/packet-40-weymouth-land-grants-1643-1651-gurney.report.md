## Transcriptions

Spelling and capitalization are retained where legible. Punctuation is editorial. Square brackets mark uncertain or supplied letters. The Nash transcription was used to localize and check faint words, but each John name below is visible in the manuscript image.

### 1. Robert Randoll — East Field, manuscript p. 12

Image: `007009659_00135.jpg`, left page, lower half.

> The land of Robert Randoll  
> Two acres in the East field first granted to John Gurny,  
> bounded on the East with the land of Edmond hart, the hiewaie  
> on the west, the land of Robert Cooke on the north, and of  
> Nathaniel Addames on the south.

**Confidence:** High. `John Gurny` is clear. The boundary syntax is compressed but the directional sequence is readable.

The same owner block continues:

> Two acres more in East field first giuen to Edward Bate, bounded on the East with  
> the land of Edmond Harte, with the land of Robert Cooke on the west, Nathaniel  
> Addames on the north, and his owne on the south.

This second parcel does not name John, but it reinforces the same East Field neighbor cluster: Edmond Hart, Robert Cooke, Nathaniel Addames, Edward Bate, and Robert Randoll.

### 2. Nathaniel Addames — East Field, manuscript p. 23

Image: `007009659_00140.jpg`, right page, middle-lower portion.

> Two acres in the East ffield first giuen to John Gurnie, bounded on the East  
> with the land of Jeffery Staple, on the west with the land of Robert Randoll,  
> on the north with the Ceder swampe, on the south with the highwaie.

**Confidence:** High. The surname is `Gurnie`, not `Gurny`. The full abuttal is image-supported.

The immediate Addames block also records other East Field parcels, including eleven acres assembled from earlier grants to Matthew Pratt, Thomas Clapp's wife, and William Brandon; an eleven-and-a-quarter-acre parcel first given to Clement Briggs; and three acres given in lieu of a highway. These entries show that the compiler was preserving earlier-grantee information inside a later ownership survey, not recording one contemporaneous grant event.

### 3. Thomas Richardes — Mill Field, manuscript p. 31

Image: `007009659_00144.jpg`, right page, top. The heading and preceding Richardes parcels begin on manuscript p. 30 (`007009659_00144.jpg`, left page); the John clause continues onto p. 31.

> ffower acres in the mill ffield first giuen to John Gurnie,  
> bounded on the East with Nicholas Norton land, on the west  
> with Richard Wulinges, on the north with John Gilles, on the  
> south with William Brandones.

**Confidence:** Moderate-high. The page is badly faded, but the clause structure and the name `John Gurnie` remain visible. `Wulinges`, `Gilles`, and `Brandones` agree with the letterforms as far as the image permits; they should not be silently modernized to Rawlings, Gill, or Brandon in a verbatim quotation.

The broader Richardes block also describes five acres of fresh meadow at the beginning of the Hingham bounds, first given to Mr. Busley, and eighteen acres abutting the Hingham bounds, Mr. Jefferies, and Aingell Hollard. The Mill Field parcel therefore sits inside a larger Hingham-boundary ownership description.

## Dating and document structure

- The ownership descriptions occupy manuscript pp. 9–31 in this packet. Page 9 begins mid-series under Edward Bate; no title or date heading survives in the staged opening.
- The record repeatedly uses `first granted` and `first giuen` to distinguish an earlier grantee from the person under whose name the land was later described.
- None of the three John clauses dates his original grant.
- Manuscript p. 32 appends sales dated 1644. One legible heading begins `Sould by Thomas Dyer unto Thomas Baylie the 21st of the 3 month 1644`; another begins `Sould by Edward ... unto William Reade the 25th of the 3 month 1644`. These dated sales show later additions to the land-record sequence but do not independently date the ownership survey to 1643.
- No separate 1636 `First Division` section is present.
- No 1651 or 2 February 1651–52 heading is present.

The image evidence therefore supports only this chronology: John had been the original grantee of the two East Field parcels and the four-acre Mill Field parcel before the ownership descriptions were compiled under Randoll, Addames, and Richardes. The manuscript images supplied here do not say when those original grants occurred.
