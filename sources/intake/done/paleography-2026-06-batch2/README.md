## Packet 10 — Henry Gurnay of Great Ellingham, Esquire: registered copy will, proved October 1623

**Citation:** Norwich Consistory Court registered copy wills, vols. 116–118 (1621–1623), FamilySearch DGS 008470484 (British Film 94937), images 594–596. Arks: image 594 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C39M-3](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C39M-3), image 595 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C3MP-N](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C3MP-N), image 596 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C3MJ-W](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-C3MJ-W).

**Layout:** two register pages per image. The will opens in the lower half of the RIGHT page of image 594, in display script: "In the name of God Amen, I Hen[ry] Gurnay of Greate Elingham in the Countie of Norff Esquie[r]…" (targeting crops `_crop594-right-mid.jpg`, `_crop594-dateline.jpg`, `_crop594-dateline2.jpg`). The body fills image 595 (both pages). The probate clause is at the top of image 596, followed by an unrelated will (widow of Peter Bower).

**Why it matters:** the project's Henry Gurney G15 (b. 21 Jan 1548/9, of Great Ellingham and West Barsham) is published as d. 1615/16 with "will proved 1623." This register will — made (per targeting read) "on the ffirste daie of May in the xx[?]th yeare of the raigne of … Kinge James, And in the yeare of our Lorde &c 162[?]" — may move his death to 1621×1623 and is in any case the registered will behind the probate-1623 tradition. Film-scoped sweeps of the 1615–16, 1617–18, and 1619–20 registers (all in FTS coverage) found NO other Gurney testator, so this is the only Gurney will in NCC registers 1615–1623.

**Machine-transcript finding aids (garbled; for locating passages only):** "I Hen Gurnar of Prento long in the Countie of Kettering" (= Great Ellingham / Norff); children tokens on image 595: "Tho : Antho : Son : Eliz :", "my daughter unmarried … Abigal … one hundred pound", "daughters Amy and Abigalo", "I give to my Eldest Sonne", "my Bason and [Ewer] of Sylv[er] [&] Gould", "all my books", "my Son --- London"; charitable loan-stock clause ("poore of the said town", churchwardens, bond or bill obligatory, Moderator); probate clause on 596: "Probate … die October … 1823 [=1623] … quod Anne Antonio Burnay [=Anthonio Gurnay?] … Administrand…".

## Packet 12 — Henry Spillman, Esq., of [Great] Ellingham: will made February, proved 1524 — Antony Gurney (G17) as executor

**Citation:** Norwich Consistory Court registered copy wills, vol. 36 (1520–1524, "Register Alabaster"), FamilySearch DGS 008076282 (British Film 94878), images 379–380. Arks: 379 [3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-T9QC-H](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-T9QC-H), 380 [3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-T93B-3](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSN8-T93B-3).

**Layout:** the will opens mid-page on image 379 (machine tokens: "…I [Hen?]ry Spilman … being in good memory … the [—] day of February make my last will … my bodie to be buried in the [Church of St] James [at El[ing]h[a]m] …") after another testator's executor clauses; it continues onto image 380.

**Why it matters:** the Norfolk wills abstract volume reads "HENRY SPILLMAN, Esquier, to be buried in the Church of St. James in [Great?] Ellingham … my wife, Antony Gurney and John Turrell, executors. D: 17 Feb – P. 1524" (lead L-101). An Antony Gurney as executor to a Spilman esquire in 1524, in the Great Ellingham orbit, is a personal Gurney–Spelman connection a century before Sir Henry Spelman the antiquary held the manuscript Gurney pedigree (lead L-5) — and the executor is almost certainly Anthony Gurney G17 (then ~30, a decade before the Lovell inheritance brought him Great Ellingham itself).

3. The testator's wife's name; children/legatees; the Ellingham property and church bequests.
4. Exact will date (17 February 15—?) and the probate clause date/court.

## Packet 11 — Mary Scamler of Norwich, widow: will dated 22 January 1688/9 — the Waveney Gurney kin map

**Citation:** Norwich Consistory Court registered copy wills, vols. 142–143 (1691–1698), FamilySearch DGS 008472233 (British Film 166892), images 199–202. Arks: 199 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BJWR](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BJWR), 200 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BN13](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BN13), 201 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BJQV](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BJQV), 202 [3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BJML](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C39Z-BJML).

**Layout:** will opens at the foot of the RIGHT page of image 199, after the probate clause of Richard Drury of Gooderston (targeting crops `_crop199-right.jpg`, `_crop199-name.jpg`): "In the Name of God Amen the Two and Twentieth Day of January … One Thousand Six Hundred Eighty Eight and in the fourth year of the Reign of … James the second … I Mary Scamler of the Citty of Norwich Widdow…" Body continues across images 200–201 and ends on 202.

**Why it matters:** Mary's legacies (image 202, located by the original FTS hit) include £5 each "to John Gurney, Thomas [& Ly]on Gurney and to Mary Farrow, my Sister Sarah Gurney['s] children" — the Earsham/Bungay Waveney Gurney family (the 1638 Earsham testator's line; Sarah Gurney of Bungay d. June 1710, her nuncupative will made at the Norwich house of her son Lyon in St Peter Parmentergate, the laceweaver Francis B's parish). Mary's will is the kin-map document for that network: burial "in Eaton Church so near the Body of my late dear Husband"; daughter Elizabeth wife of Nathaniel Smith (£50); a £50 provision toward "the purchase of an Office or place"; son-in-law Richard Nelson and Woolnough kin (per FTS snippets); lands at Barsham/Shipmeadow, Suffolk.
