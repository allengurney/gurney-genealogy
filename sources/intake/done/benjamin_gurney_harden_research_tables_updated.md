## Jane / Jean Harden — likely mother of Benjamin baptized 1730

| Fact | Observations / analysis / applicability | Source name (URL) |
|---|---|---|
| Abington birth records list Jean Harden/Hardin, daughter of John and Mary, born 19 Nov. 1709. | This gives Jane/Jean an age of about **20 years, 6 months, 11 days** at Benjamin’s baptism on 30 May 1730. She is biologically plausible as the mother. | [*Vital Records of Abington*, Vol. 1 — Internet Archive / Wikimedia PDF](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vital_records_of_Abington,_Massachusetts,_to_the_year_1850_.._(IA_vitalrecordsofab02abing).pdf) |
| Hobart says Mr. Niles of Braintree recorded the baptism of “Jane Harden, the daughter of John Harden, of Little Comfort,” on 22 Feb. 1711. | If this is the same Jean/Jane born 19 Nov. 1709, her baptism occurred roughly **15 months after birth**, showing that Harden-family baptism did not necessarily occur immediately at birth. | [Aaron Hobart, *Historical Sketch of Abington*, 1839, via LDSGenealogy](https://ldsgenealogy.com/MA/books/Historical-sketch-of-Abington-Plymouth-County-Massachusetts-With-an-appendix-part-2.htm) |
| “Jane” and “Jean” appear to refer to the same daughter of John Harden. | The Abington vital-record editor explicitly warns that original spellings are followed and that alternate spellings should be examined. “Jean” in Abington and “Jane” in Hobart are likely the same person, but the records should still be treated as variant forms rather than silently normalized. | [MassachusettsGenealogy, Abington Vol. 1 explanations](https://massachusettsgenealogy.net/plymouth/vital-records-of-abington-massachusetts-to-the-year-1850-vol-1.htm) |

It says Benjamin was son of Jean; it does not say son of Elizabeth. | [*Vital Records of Abington*, Vol. 1 — Internet Archive / Wikimedia PDF](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vital_records_of_Abington,_Massachusetts,_to_the_year_1850_.._(IA_vitalrecordsofab02abing).pdf) |
| Secondary compiled genealogy says Jane returned to Braintree with her parents after Benjamin’s birth. | This may explain why Benjamin was not raised in G10’s later Sarah Morse household, but I did not find independent primary confirmation of this movement or the “raised by aunt” detail. | [*The Neverending Hobby — John Gurney, US 1636*](https://sites.google.com/site/theneverendinghobby/home/those-connected-to-more-than-one-family/john-gurney-us-1636) |
