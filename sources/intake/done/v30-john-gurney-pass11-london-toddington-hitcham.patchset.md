## 0. Audit report

### 0.1 Scope

Pass 11 (2026-05-11) followed the user's correction that the colonial Richard cannot be Massachusetts-born (his son John died in the 1675 war, back-projecting Richard's birth to no later than about 1637). The colonial John was therefore in England through approximately 1640. Pass 11 walked four highest-value targets:

1. Saint Benet Fink (combined as Saint Peter le Poer with Saint Benet Fink in the FamilySearch index): the case file's documented Francis Gurney G14 London-side parish.
2. Bermondsey 1603 John Gurney + Roase Tayler marriage and any children of that couple.
3. The Toddington, Bedfordshire John Gurney + Elizabeth Moreton household: positive English-side death evidence to strengthen the §8 eliminator.
4. Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk: Banks's deprecated identification for the colonial John's origin.

### 0.2 Toddington Beds — strong positive eliminator confirmed

A John Gurney burial is indexed at Toddington, Bedfordshire in September 1641 in the FamilySearch England, Deaths and Burials, 1538-1991 collection. The Toddington John Gurney + Elizabeth Moreton marriage is independently indexed at Toddington 12 October 1624 in FamilySearch England Marriages, 1538-1973. The Toddington household's children Elizabeth 1625, Anne 1628, Audrey 1633, and a son John 13 February 1630 are indexed at Toddington in the same collection, with a separate son John 5 October 1630 to a Toddington Thomas Gurney indexing the parish's second Gurney father in that year. The September 1641 burial of John Gurney at Toddington is the controlling positive eliminator: the Massachusetts emigrant's Weymouth fine is dated June 1641, three months earlier, but the Massachusetts John continues to appear in the Weymouth and Braintree record set through 1662/3. The Toddington John buried in September 1641 cannot also be in continuous Massachusetts residence through 1662/3.

### 0.3 Hempnall Norfolk — cross-source verified

The Norfolk Record Office collection (Ancestry collection 61045) image-confirms the v25 Findmypast finding for the Hempnall John Gurney household. Anna Gurney baptized 6 April 1640, Hempnall, with father indexed in Latin as "Johannis Gurney." The Pass 9 NRO walk also surfaced an earlier Anna Gurney burial at Hempnall 6 April 1639 (same date, one year earlier) which is most plausibly an earlier child by the same name who died and was replaced by the 1640 Anna under the same family's replacement-naming custom. The Hempnall John household's father identification now has dual-source support (Findmypast UK Parish Baptisms and the Norfolk Record Office collection).

### 0.4 Saint Peter le Poer with Saint Benet Fink — Francis Gurney + Anne household

FamilySearch England Births and Christenings, 1538-1975 indexes a complete second-marriage family group for Francis Gurney at the combined Saint Peter le Poer with Saint Benet Fink, London. The mother is consistently indexed as Ann or Anne (the case file's existing endnote n30 cites Bernau's 1913 reading "Anne Browning"). Seven children appear:

- Dorithy Gurnoy, 2 March 1619, father Frauncis Gurnoy mother Ann.
- Roger Gurney, 20 December 1621, father Frauncis Gurney mother Ann.
- Frances Gurney (girl), 18 January 1625, father Frances Gurney mother Ane.
- Francis Gurnay (boy), 13 November 1628, father Francis Gurnay mother Anne.
- Lucretia Gurnay, 28 October 1630, father Francis Gurnay mother Anne.
- Thomas Gurnay, 19 April 1636, father Francis Gurnay mother Anne.
- Margaret Gurnay, 28 July 1637, father Francis Gurnay mother Anne.

The 1619 Dorithy and 1621 Roger entries match Daniel Gurney's 1858 Supplement Note 181 description ("Dorothy was the eldest child mentioned in the parish register of St. Benet Fink"; "Roger, born in 1621"), confirming the household is Francis G14's London-side family with his second wife Anne Browning. No indexed John Gurney child of this household appears in the parish register set. If Candidate B is correct, the colonial John must be a child of Francis G14's first marriage (recorded at East Dereham, Norfolk per the case file's existing primary record 3 and NRO PD 86/41), not a child of the London Anne Browning marriage.

### 0.5 Aylesbury Luke Gurney household — fourth Aylesbury Gurney patriarch

FamilySearch England Births and Christenings, 1538-1975 indexes a Luke Gurney household at Aylesbury active across 1621-1635:

- Thomas Gurney, 20 January 1621, Aylesbury, father Luke Gurney.
- Elizabeth Gurney, 15 June 1623, Aylesbury, father Luke Gurney.
- Luke Gurney, 1 August 1624, Aylesbury, father Luke Gurney.
- Prudence Gurney, 6 March 1635, Aylesbury, father Luke Gurney.

This is a fourth distinct Aylesbury Gurney patriarch in the same century as Candidate A's John (1638-1653 children at Saint Mary Aylesbury), the second Aylesbury John who married Anne Cowheard (25 October 1638), and Edward Gurny (1660s). Luke is not a John Gurney father, so he does not enter the §8 elimination table; he belongs in the research companion's Aylesbury cluster context.

### 0.6 Hitcham Buckinghamshire — single-record John household

FamilySearch England, Buckinghamshire, Church Records, 1217-1994 indexes a single 22 January 1631 baptism at Hitcham, Buckinghamshire of Mary Gurny to father John Gurny. The mother is unindexed. No siblings appear in the FamilySearch index in this pass. Hitcham is in south Buckinghamshire near Burnham, about 30 miles south of Stewkley and 20 miles south of Aylesbury; the geographic distance from the other Buckinghamshire John Gurney households argues for treating this as a distinct south-Bucks household rather than conflating with the Aylesbury Vale Johns. The single indexed child is insufficient to establish a controlling identification, and a Hitcham parish register pull would resolve whether further Gurney baptisms exist there.

### 0.7 Norwich Saint Peter Mancroft 10 February 1639 burial

FamilySearch England, Deaths and Burials, 1538-1991 indexes the burial of John Gurny at Saint Peter Mancroft, Norwich, 10 February 1639. The relative field reads "John Gurny, Marg." — most plausibly the deceased's parents (John Gurny and Margaret) rather than a wife. This burial is two years before the Massachusetts emigrant's June 1641 Weymouth presence and adds a positive Norwich-side John Gurney elimination to the case file. Note: this Norwich Saint Peter Mancroft burial is distinct from the Earsham John Gurney + Elizabeth Singler whose will was proved 1639 elsewhere in Norfolk, and from the Norwich (m. 1639) John Gurney + Jane Wright marriage at a different Norwich parish (Saint Benedict per Pass 9).

### 0.8 Bury St Edmunds — Banks Suffolk claim unsupported

FamilySearch England Births and Christenings, 1538-1975 surfaces a Bury St Edmunds Gurney baptism at the search window's late edge: Juda Gurny 23 February 1634 Bury St Edmunds, father Thomas Gurny. The father is Thomas, not John. No Bury St Edmunds John Gurney father baptism appears in the FamilySearch index 1620-1645. Banks's deprecated identification of Bury St Edmunds as the colonial John's origin (case file source 14 and section 13) does not match any indexed primary record reachable in this pass.

### 0.9 Bermondsey 1603 marriage — confirmed; no children indexed

FamilySearch England Marriages, 1538-1973 confirms John Gurney + Roase Tayler at Saint Mary Magdalene, Bermondsey, London on 4 July 1603 (a second index transcription gives the bride as Elizabeth Tayler; both transcriptions point to the same Reference Item 2 record). A search of FamilySearch for any Bermondsey Gurney baptism with father John in 1603-1620 returns no indexed children. The Bermondsey 1603 marriage does not yield further evidence in this pass.

### 0.10 Candidate implications

- **Candidate B (Francis G14 of East Dereham + Margaret Rybett):** The Saint Peter le Poer with Saint Benet Fink seven-child family group confirms Francis G14's London-side family is a second marriage to Anne Browning, with the earliest indexed Saint Benet Fink child Dorothy 1619 starting after the case file's documented 1611 Norwich marriage to Margaret Ryvett. If the colonial John is Francis G14's son (the case file's premise), he is a child of the first marriage at East Dereham, not the second marriage in London. Pass 11 reinforces the East Dereham reading and does not change Candidate B's probability.
- **Candidate A:** No direct effect.
- **Candidate C:** No direct effect.
- **Toddington Beds (case-file elimination row):** Status unchanged but elimination reasoning materially strengthened by the September 1641 burial.
- **Hempnall Norfolk:** Now cross-source verified; the household is firmly a John Gurney father household (no longer single-source).
- **Bury St Edmunds Suffolk (Banks's claim):** Materially weakened by the absence of an indexed John Gurney father at Bury St Edmunds in the FamilySearch search set.
