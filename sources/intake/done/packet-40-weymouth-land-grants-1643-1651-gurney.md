## Background (for localization only)

John Gurney Sr. (G13), tailor, appears at Weymouth in June 1641 and was "in Braintree before 1645"; he died at Braintree 1662/3. Gilbert Nash's *Historical Sketch of Weymouth* (1885), Appendix C, transcribes this Land Grants book. Nash's Appendix C is headed **"Property Owners and Description of Property in 1643… First Division, 1636; Second Division, 1651; List of 1663,"** and notes *"in 1643 a partial record of the then property owners was made which has been preserved."* The *History of Weymouth* (1923) cites this book at entries/pages **258, 270, 278, 282** for John's grants. Nash's wording at those points (to be confirmed against the manuscript):

- **p. 258** — under *"The land of Robert Randoll"*: *"Two acres in the East field first granted to John Gurny, bounded on the East with the land of Edmond hart the hiewaie, on the west the land of Robert Cooke, on the north … of Nathaniel Addames…"*
- **p. 270** — under *"The land of Nathaniel Addames"*: *"Two acres in the East fiield first giuen to John Gurnie, bounded on the East with the land of Jeffery Staple, on the west with the land of Robert Randoll, on the north with the Ceder swampe, on the south with the highwaie."*
- **p. 278** — under *"The land of Thomas Richardes"*: *"ffower acres in the mill ffield first giuen to John Gurnie, bounded on the East with Nicholas Norton land, on the west with Richard Wulinges, on the north with John Gilles, on the south with William Brandones."*
- **p. 282** — direct list entry in the **1651-2 great-lot list** (heading *"LIST OF 1651… under date of Nov. 26, 1651… NAMES OF THOSE TO HAVE LOTS 2 FEBRUARY 1651-2"*): *"John Gurney … 16."*
