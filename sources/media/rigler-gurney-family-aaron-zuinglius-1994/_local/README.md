# Rigler, *The Gurney Family from Aaron to Zuinglius* (1994) — local-only page scans

Page scans of Jean Gurney Rigler, *The Gurney Family from Aaron to Zuinglius: A Genealogical Dictionary; Some Descendants of Richard Gurney Who Settled at Weymouth, MA before 1656*, rev. and expanded ed. (Honolulu: J. G. Rigler, 1994).

**Reason for `_local`:** copyright-restricted. This is a modern (1994) in-copyright compiled genealogy; the page images are not redistributed. They are kept here as working-reference masters only.

**Canonical source:** an earlier edition is digitised at the Internet Archive ([`gurneyfamilyfrom00rigl`](https://archive.org/details/gurneyfamilyfrom00rigl)). The 1994 revised-and-expanded edition is the one scanned here (the author's signed gift copy, inscribed 6 May 1997). Source ID: `rigler-gurney-family-aaron-zuinglius-1994`.
