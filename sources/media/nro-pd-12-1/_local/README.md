  Palace, Norwich parish register opening covering 1611–1612, kept local because it is
  copyright-restricted Ancestry subscription content. It carries the marriage entry
  "franc: Gurney et Margarett Rybett nupt 23 Sept" (23 September 1611), left page, sixth line of
  the upper block. Canonical copy: Ancestry.com, "Norfolk, England, Church of England Baptisms,
  Marriages, and Burials, 1535–1812," image for St Martin at Palace, PD 12/1,
  <https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/61045/records/2879447>. Underlying original:
  Church of England Parish Registers, Norfolk Record Office, Norwich (PD 12/1).
