# Rigler — Gurney family-group worksheets (c. 1980) — local-only scans

Five scanned Family Group Record worksheet forms compiling the direct American line Richard² (G12) through Amos (G8). The printed forms are signed **Robert G. Rigler, M.D., 2117 Puuali'i Place, Honolulu, Hawaii** — the same Honolulu household as the later published genealogy credited to **Jean Gurney Rigler**, *The Gurney Family from Aaron to Zuinglius* (rev. ed., 1994; Source ID `rigler-gurney-family-aaron-zuinglius-1994`). The worksheets are undated; the form style and their relationship to the 1994 book place them roughly a decade or more earlier (c. 1980). They are the author's earlier working draft; the 1994 edition supersedes them where the two differ.

**Reason for `_local`:** copyright-restricted. Unpublished compiled research by a third-party compiler; the page images are not redistributed and are kept here as working-reference masters only. (Conservative call — relax to committed if the rights position allows.)

**Canonical source:** the physical worksheets supplied by the user. Source ID: `rigler-genealogy-notes-c1980`.
