## C6 — revised verdict

- **Prior verdict (initial pass):** UNCERTAIN — page-type and era plausible, specific line not stroke-verified.
- **Revised verdict (this pass):** CONFIRM the prior AI's revision. The target line on 00732 reads **"Marye the daughter of ffrancis Gurnoe bapt may 25"**, not "Margaret".
- **Evidence class:** D for the given-name word length and terminal, the verb "bapt", the date "may 25", and the surname body "Gurn-"; I for the surname terminal "-oe" vs "-ey/-y" and the page-year.
- **Stroke-level reasoning:**
  - In `page_00732_line_margaret_ffrancis_gurnoe_sweep.png`, the given name is short (5 character widths) and ends with a low terminal "e" loop. "Margaret" requires 8 character widths and a terminal "t" with the characteristic cross-stroke. The visible word lacks the medial "g-a-r-e" run, lacks the terminal "t", and matches the same-line-immediately-above "Marye the daughter of John Park[er?]" given-name in shape, length, and rhythm. Two adjacent "Marye" entries on the same May date cluster make the "Marye" reading effectively self-consistent.
  - The verb "bapt" (short form of "baptized") is legible across raw, autocontrast, and contrast+sharp views. This independently confirms the page is a christenings page rather than a burial page.
  - The numeral "25" is firm in raw-resized and existing-enhanced views.
  - In `page_00732_surname_terminal_comparison_00721_00725.png`, the 00732 surname terminal after "Gurn-" is an open rounded form followed by a short e-like finish — best read as "-oe" or "-oe[?]" — and does *not* show the descending y-tail seen on both 00725 surnames (Marye-line Gurny and Agnes-line Gurny). The 00721 Edward-line surname is too compact in the same comparator to settle the spelling, but it sits with the broader Gurney/Gurnie cluster.
- **Diverges from prior AI:** none on the headline reading. I would record the surname as `Gurnoe` paleographically and treat `Gurney` only as a downstream-normalization candidate; the in-line ink does not show the long descending tail of the 00725 `Gurny` forms.
- **Implication for the case-file Margaret-baptism lead:** withdraw. There is no Margaret-daughter-of-ffrancis baptism on 00732. The line that prompted the lead is a Marye-daughter-of-ffrancis baptism with a `Gurnoe`-spelled surname.

Two cautious framing notes:

- The Marye burial on 00725 (~Jan 1614–1615) **predates** the Marye baptism on 00732 (~May 1616–1617). These are therefore two distinct individuals named Marye, not the same person. The naming pattern (a subsequent daughter named after a deceased sibling or relative) is conventional 17th-century practice and consistent with what is visible.

The Marye burial may be a niece or other relative rather than a daughter of Francis, which would also bear on whether the 00732 Marye is a same-given-name replacement within the household or unrelated.
