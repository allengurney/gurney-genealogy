## Bottom line

No burial entry matching "Margaret wife of ffrancis Gurny/Gurney/Gurnie" was identified on page 00725. No Rybett/Ryvett form was identified.

The page does contain a useful ffrancis Gurny family cluster, but the readable entries are child-burial forms, not a wife-of-ffrancis form. The strongest two are:

## Line-level observations

- The page is a burial list running through a year-crossing sequence. The exact year is not stated on the visible page crop; earlier work estimated the 00724/00725 cluster around 1614/15, but this page alone should be treated as year-truncated.
- The Marye and Agnes entries sit in the lower third of the page and are close together. This supports a family-cluster reading for ffrancis Gurny, but it does not produce a Margaret wife-of-ffrancis burial.
- The Marye line is weaker than the Agnes line. The word after `Marye` and before `of ffrancis` is degraded by bleed-through/staining. It may be a relationship word, but I would not extract it as a clean "daughter" without a bracketed uncertainty marker.
- The Agnes line reads as `Agnes the daughter of ffrancis Gurny` with good comparative support from the same line group and the retained `candidate_724_725_mary_agnes_francis_gurny.png`.
- A Margaret-like entry in the upper/middle area appears to be a daughter-of-William entry, not a spouse of ffrancis. Another wife-of-Cooke line was checked because it can be mistaken on a fast pass, but the deeper crop does not support it as the requested Margaret/ffrancis target.
- I did not resolve a clear Susan/Susanna line on page 00725 itself. If the Susan cue comes from the FamilySearch waypoint or a neighboring-image overlap, it needs separate confirmation against the adjacent page images or the viewer metadata.

## Correction to earlier broad-pass wording

The first burial-analysis pass described an "apparent Margaret wife of Richard Cooke" as an unrelated Margaret entry. The deeper sweep does not support using that line as a Margaret reading. It remains a non-target wife-of-Cooke-type entry, but it should not be used as evidence for a Margaret on this page.

## Research implications

- Page 00725 should be treated as negative for the requested Margaret wife-of-ffrancis/Rybett burial.
- Page 00725 should be treated as positive for ffrancis Gurny household mortality context: probable Marye burial on January 25 and stronger Agnes daughter-of-ffrancis Gurny burial on January 31.
- Prior notes that labeled the 00724/00725 Marye/Agnes lines as baptisms should be rechecked before reuse. In this image context, they are on a burial page.
