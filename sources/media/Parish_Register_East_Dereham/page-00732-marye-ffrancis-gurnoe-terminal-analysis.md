## Focused 00732 reading

Evidence class: D for the crop and visible target line; I for the surname-terminal adjudication.

The given name is visually closer to `Marye` than `Margaret`. It has the shorter Marye word length and terminal shape, and it lacks the longer middle and final structure expected for `Margaret`. The entry is also in the christening section, so it is not a burial record for the Margaret-wife-of-ffrancis target.

## Surname terminal comparison

The 00732 surname body is comparable to the same-hand Gurney/Gurnie cluster: a capital `G`, followed by `urn`. The terminal is the issue.

On 00732, the final strokes after `Gurn` are best read as an open rounded `o` plus a small terminal `e` or e-like finish. The terminal does not show the long descending tail expected in the clearest `Gurny` examples.

On 00725, both the Marye and Agnes ffrancis-family burial entries show a clearer terminal `y` form in `Gurny`, with a darker descender below the baseline. Those forms are not a close match for the 00732 terminal.

On 00721, the Edward son of ffrancis comparator remains useful for the `ffrancis` and `Gurn-` body, but the terminal is more compact and not the same open `-oe` pattern seen on 00732. It supports the same surname cluster more than it settles the exact spelling.

## Working conclusion

Record 00732 as a useful same-register family-cluster comparator, not as the target burial. The line should be carried forward, if at all, as:

`Marye daughter of ffrancis Gurnoe`, christening, May 25, with `Gurnoe` preferred over `Gurney` for this line's terminal, and with the spelling still marked paleographically interpretive.

This resolves the earlier C6 uncertainty in a negative direction for `Margaret`: the focused line crop refutes the tentative `Margaret daughter of ffrancis` reading and replaces it with `Marye daughter of ffrancis`.
