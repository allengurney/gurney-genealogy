# Pages 00737-00768 Gurney review

Created 2026-05-16 as a follow-on review of East Dereham PD 86/41 images beginning at page 00737.

## Scope and method

Scope: `gbprs_norfolk_pd_86-41_00737.jpg` through `gbprs_norfolk_pd_86-41_00768.jpg`.

Target scan terms: Francis/ffrancis Gurney and close surname variants, plus any other Gurney-family surname occurrence.

Method: visual page scan only, using parchment crops, contrast-sharp enhancement, and focused six-state sweeps for Gurney-like lines. No OCR/HTR was used for the paleographic readings. Review confidence is line-level, not a claim that the pages cannot contain any missed entry.
