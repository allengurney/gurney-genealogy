# Daniel Gurney, *Record of the House of Gournay* - Part IV Image Extraction Catalog

## Scope and source

**Source used for extraction:** `Record of the House of Gournay_logical_part_3-Later Lines and Appendix.pdf`

**Source citation:** Daniel Gurney, *The Record of the House of Gournay*, Part IV / later lines and appendix (London/privately printed edition represented by uploaded logical Part III/Part IV PDF), printed pages 606-699 for this first image pass.

**Processing note:** Images were rendered from the uploaded PDF at 300 dpi, cropped from the page image, converted to grayscale, and lightly auto-contrasted. The process was intentionally conservative: no binarization, sharpening, or generative enhancement was applied. The goal is source-faithful working images suitable for later contrast cleanup or visual reconstruction.

**Selection note:** This first pass emphasizes Part IV images tied to the Somersetshire/East Harptree collateral Gournay branch, Robert de Gournay II, Sir Matthew de Gournay, associated monuments, arms, seals, castles, churches, and manorial places. A few contextual plates were retained with lower-confidence relevance flags rather than discarded.
