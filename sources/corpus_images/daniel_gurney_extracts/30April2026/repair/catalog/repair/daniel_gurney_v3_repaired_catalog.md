**Scope of this repair:** This file repairs the v3 continuation pass, especially the Part II / Norfolk-line and Part III / early Part IV crops. The prior crops are retained, but this catalog supersedes the thin metadata in the earlier v3 output.

**Source PDFs used in this pass:**

- `Record of the House of Gournay_logical_part_1-Norman Origins.pdf` — despite the filename, this uploaded segment begins with Part II front matter and the Norfolk line.

**Cataloging standard:** Each entry below includes filename, source PDF, PDF page, printed page or plate placement, caption, ancestor/place applicability, scope judgment, and a substantive adjacent-text narrative. Generation numbers are supplied where they are reasonably anchored from the project’s existing direct-line structure; otherwise the field uses named people and the scope judgment rather than a guessed generation number.
