# Vital Records of Taunton, Massachusetts, to 1850, vol. 2 (Marriages, 1928)

Source ID: `taunton-vital-records-to-1850-vol2-marriages-1928`

## Scope Examined

Full-text sweep of the printed marriages volume (archive.org `vitalrecordsofta02unse`, also
catalogued `vitalrecordsoftataun_5`) for Gurney-variant forms (Gurney, Garney, Surney), plus the
volume's own cross-reference entries, 2026-07-01.

## Findings Location

The Bundy-Gurney marriage entries (9 January 1676) and the Cooper-Gurney marriage (16 October
1706) are carried in
`research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/family/12-mendon-descendants.md`.

## Limits

Sweep covered the Marriages volume only (vol. 2 of 3); Births (vol. 1) and Deaths (vol. 3) were
not separately swept for this campaign. Text is OCR from the printed 1928 NEHGS compilation, not
the manuscript town records.
