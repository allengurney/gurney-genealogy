# Validation — A Descriptive Catalogue of Ancient Deeds (TNA, HMSO)

**Source ID:** `descriptive-catalogue-ancient-deeds`

**What was examined.** The printed *Descriptive Catalogue of Ancient Deeds in the Public Record Office* (HMSO), via British History Online, for Gurnay/Gurney appearances across the A/B/C/D series. Volume indexes (vol. 1 pp. 623–677; vol. 3 pp. 656–709; vol. 4 pp. 624–683) were harvested for deed numbers; individual abstract pages were then pulled.

**What portion.** For this promotion (W2), the single deed **A.7857** (vol. 4, pp. 218–235, abstract at pp. 229–230) was read and extracted: Henry Heydon's 25 March 1493 feoffment of the manor of Ormesby and Flegg lands, naming William Gurney among the feoffees. Verbatim abstract in `sources/corpus_supplement/ancient-deeds-a7857-heydon-gurney-ormesby.md`.

**Level.** Calendar/abstract, not the original deed. The catalogue gives parties, property, date, witnesses ("Witnesses. Seal.") but not the full text or the witness names.

**What remains.** The wider Gurnay index (A.1554 "the King's squire," B.991, B.1426, D.1053, A.6461, A.7196, A.7911, A.8939, A.8973, A.9266, A.9956, C.5895, etc.) is enumerated but not yet abstracted here — collateral-line material reserved for the collateral clusters.

**Where findings landed.** A.7857 → `research/people/g18-william-gurney-v-fact-sheet.research.md` (Gurney–Heydon trust bond). Corpus extract as above.
