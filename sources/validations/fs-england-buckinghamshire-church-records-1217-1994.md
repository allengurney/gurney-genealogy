# Validation — FamilySearch, England, Buckinghamshire, Church Records, 1217–1994

**Source ID:** `fs-england-buckinghamshire-church-records-1217-1994`
**Collection:** FamilySearch collection 4380170, indexed from the Centre for Buckinghamshire Studies.
**Examined:** 2026-07-26, authenticated session.

## What was examined

The whole Gurney holding of the collection, by surname variant with exact matching, plus a
per-parish sweep of twenty named parishes: Saint Mary Aylesbury, Cublington, East Claydon, Chesham,
Wing, Hitcham, Stewkley, Bierton with Broughton, Weston Turville, Haddenham, Great Kimble, Ivinghoe,
Marsworth, Chenies, Edlesborough, Wingrave, Waddesdon, Mursley, Linslade, Cheddington.

Variant totals across the collection's full run: Gurney 4,334; Gurny 128; Garney 120; Gourney 45;
Gorney 22; Girney 12; Gurnay 7; Gurnie 4; Gurnoe 0; Gernne 0. 359 records fall between 1570 and
1700.

## What remains unexamined or uncertain

- **Not exhaustive.** FamilySearch's search service returns at most about 1,500 unique results per
  query however deep the paging goes, and its `q.birthLikeDate`, `q.anyPlace` and `q.spouseSurname`
  parameters rank results rather than filtering them. The 4,334-record Gurney set was therefore
  covered by relevance-ranked and per-parish sweeps. A Gurney record in a parish outside the twenty
  queried, ranked below the cut, would have been missed.
- **Parish coverage is uneven and two gaps are load-bearing.** Every one of the 28 Aylesbury Gurney
  records in the collection is nineteenth-century; the 1638–1653 Saint Mary baptisms are not here.
  Amersham is likewise absent for the seventeenth century. Chenies and Great Kimble return no Gurney
  record at all. Per-parish coverage of the collection was not independently audited against the
  Centre for Buckinghamshire Studies holdings.
- **Mothers are indexed for some parishes and not others.** Cublington, East Claydon, Chesham, Wing,
  Cheddington, Linslade, Whitchurch, High Wycombe and Hulcott carry mothers; Stewkley, Haddenham,
  Weston Turville, Hitcham and Bierton carry the father alone. Whether that reflects the register or
  the indexing has not been tested.
- The registry previously gave this source's URL as FamilySearch collection 2376818. The collection
  that answers to the registered title in the live catalogue is 4380170, and the URL has been
  corrected to it.
