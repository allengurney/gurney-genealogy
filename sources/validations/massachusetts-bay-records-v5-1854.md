# Source validation: Massachusetts Bay Records, vol. 5

**Source ID:** `massachusetts-bay-records-v5-1854`

## Examined

Nathaniel B. Shurtleff, ed., *Records of the Governor and Company of the Massachusetts Bay in New England*, vol. 5, 1674-1686 (Boston: William White, 1854), p. 540, Internet Archive item `recordsofgoverno05mass`.

## Scope

- Page 540 sworn-freemen list entry naming "Rich'd Gurney" (Weymouth cohort, dated 1681 by position).
- Full-text sweep of v5 (F6, round-1 colonial dump) found no other Gurney entry beyond p. 540 and the distinct Gurnell/Burg items.

## Validation result

Usable as a second, independent page locator for Richard Gurney's (G12) 1681 freeman admission, corroborating Paige's *List of Freemen* citation (Colony Records Vol. V, pp. 320-321) already on the G12 companion. The printed line itself has not been checked against the page image.
