# Validation — Hampton (Pakington) of Westwood Park, Buckinghamshire property deeds

- **Source ID:** `worcs-archives-pakington-westwood-park`
- **Source:** Worcestershire Archive and Archaeology Service, Hampton (Pakington) of Westwood Park, Droitwich, collection 705:349, series *Buckinghamshire Property — Aylesbury and District*.
- **Examined:** 28 July 2026, at catalogue level, through the Discovery catalogue.

## What was examined

The complete Gurney-variant result set for the collection, reached by keyword-paired Discovery
queries (`Gurney … AND (Pakington OR "Westwood Park" OR Bedgrove)`, and by parish name) and read in
full. Twenty-three Gurney items, dated 1399 to 1663, all in the *Aylesbury and District* series and
its parish sub-series for Aylesbury and Weston Turville.

The collection is catalogued to item level, with parties, parishes, status and trade named in the
abstracts.

## Why this collection matters and how it was missed

The Pakingtons held an Aylesbury Vale estate from Worcestershire, and the estate's muniments were
deposited with the family's own papers at Worcester. A Buckinghamshire Archives search therefore
cannot see them, which is why a Gurney tenantry documented across two and a half centuries in
Aylesbury, Bierton, Broughton, Walton, Halton and Weston Turville did not surface in the July 2026
Buckinghamshire deed sweep.

## Scope and limits

Catalogue level only; no document has been examined. The abstracts are unusually informative for a
county-estate deposit — most name the grantor's and grantee's parish and status — but they are still
abstracts, so persons named only in witness lists or recitals will not appear.

One previously registered item, `tna-a2a-705-349-aylesbury-1483-grant` (705:349/12946/495200, the
1483 Aylesbury and Walton grant to Richard Gourney), belongs to this collection. Its registration
carried an open question about the depositing repository; that is now resolved and the entry has been
updated.
