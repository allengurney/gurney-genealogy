# Validation — Calendar of the Cecil Papers in Hatfield House (`cal-cecil-papers-hatfield`)

- **Source examined:** Calendar of the Cecil Papers in Hatfield House, British History Online (free series). Vol. 12 (1602–1603), pp. 518–528, "Cecil Papers: December 1602, 16–25"; Vol. 18 (1606), pp. 371–462, "Cecil Papers: Miscellaneous 1605"; and the February 1607/8 dispute dossier in Vol. 20.

Abstract level (English calendar of the manuscripts).
