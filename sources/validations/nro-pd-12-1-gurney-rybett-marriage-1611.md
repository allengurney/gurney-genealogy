# Validation — NRO PD 12/1, Gurney–Rybett marriage 1611

**Source:** Parish register, St Martin at Palace, Norwich, PD 12/1 (Norfolk Record Office),
digitized by Ancestry.com collection 61045. Source ID: `nro-pd-12-1`.

**Examined:** the register opening covering 1611–1612 (Ancestry image `4143432_01883`, held local at
`sources/media/nro-pd-12-1/_local/`). Left page, upper membrane block.

**What was examined and found:** the marriage entry reads **"franc: Gurney et Margarett Rybett
nupt 23 Sept"** — a Latin marriage record naming both parties, the verb `nupt` (nupserunt), and the
day `23`. It sits in the September run immediately above the register's "Anno Dm 1612" block,
fixing the year to **1611** → 23 September 1611. Read at scale 10–12 from the master.

**Provenance note:** the record was located via the Ancestry index (March 2026); the image was read
as confirmation then, and re-read independently 2026-07-22. Unlike the East Dereham "John" entry
audited alongside it, this is a whole-entry reading with every field legible and mutually
consistent — see `sources/intake/paleography-staging/east-dereham-re-review-july-2026/FINDINGS-2026-07-22-deviation-audit.md`.

**Findings recorded in:** `research/case-files/john-gurney-case-file-v4.md` §2.2;
`research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md`.

**Unexamined / open:** only the single marriage frame was examined; St Martin at Palace baptisms and
burials on the same opening were not swept for other Gurney/Rybett entries.
