# Validation — CIPM vol. 19 (Henry IV), John Gournay (Sir John V) IPM

**Source ID:** `bho-ipm-henry-iv-vol19-gournay-johnv`

**What was examined.** The *Calendar of Inquisitions Post Mortem*, vol. 19 (Henry IV), entries 489–494, on British History Online (`/inquis-post-mortem/vol19/pp170-185`) — the inquisition post mortem of John Gournay (Gurney) of Harpley and West Barsham, = the repo's Sir John V. TNA reference C 137/70/6.

**Scope and method.** Read the calendar abstract of the six returns (entries 489–94). Located via the vol. 19 Index of Persons "G" (`/vol19/pp424-427`), which groups "Gournay, John, and Edmund his son, 489–94."

**Findings landed.** Recorded on `research/people/g22-robert-gournay-fact-sheet.research.md` (2026-05-29 entry): writ 16 Feb 1409; inquisitions East Rudham 26 Mar, Holt 27 Mar, Attlebridge 28 Mar 1409; died 4 Dec 1408; heir Edmund his son aged 10 (b. c.1398); jurors found he **"held nothing in Norfolk"** (nothing in chief). Independent corroboration of Daniel Gurney *Supplement* Note 121. The corollary — no royal IPM for Edmund G23 (he held nothing in chief) — is recorded on the G23 companion.

**Unexamined / uncertain.** No separate inquisition for the minor heir Edmund appears in the vol. 19 index for G; given the family held nothing in chief, a substantive royal IPM for him is unlikely (resolves future-research lead L-33 as a probable dead end). Disposition of the manor portfolio must be traced via feet of fines and Blomefield, not IPMs.
