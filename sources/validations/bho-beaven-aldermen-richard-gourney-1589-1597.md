# BHO - Beaven aldermen, Richard Gourney validation

- Source ID: `bho-beaven-aldermen-richard-gourney-1589-1597`
- Examined: British History Online page for Alfred P. Beaven, "Chronological list of aldermen: 1501-1600," in *The Aldermen of the City of London Temp. Henry III - 1912*.
- Scope: Richard Gourney, Haberdasher, alderman entry dated 16 January 1589.
- Finding: identifies Richard Gourney as alderman of Portsoken and Bridge wards, Sheriff 1589-1590, Auditor 1587-1589, Master of the Haberdashers' Company 1589-1590 and 1596-1597, died 5 March 1597, with PCC will 35 Cobham dated 15 October 1596 and proved 8 April 1597.
