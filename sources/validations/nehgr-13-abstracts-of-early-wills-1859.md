# NEHGR 13, "Abstracts of Early Wills" (1859)

Source ID: `nehgr-13-abstracts-of-early-wills-1859`

## Scope Examined

Printed p. 13 read in full (archive.org `newenglandhistor1318unse`, djvu text
lines ~1763-1771), 2026-07-01: the abstracted 1664 will of Margery Laver of
Dorchester, naming a legacy "to Jane Gurnet, 2s for a memorandum." The volume's
own index was checked against the abstract text.

## Findings Location

The will text and the Gurnell-family resolution of "Jane Gurnet" are carried as
source_evidence `G13-RI-000142` and negative_result `G13-RI-000144` in
`research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/family/13-colonial-collaterals.md`.

## Limits

Read at printed-abstract level only, not against the original manuscript will.
No relationship language ("my servant," "my kinswoman," etc.) is given for Jane
Gurnet in the abstract itself; her identity as Jane Gornell rests on the separate
Dorchester town and church records, not on this will.
