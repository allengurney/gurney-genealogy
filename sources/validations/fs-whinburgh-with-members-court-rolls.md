# Validation — Whinburgh-with-Members manorial court rolls (FamilySearch films)

**Source.** Manor of Whinburgh-with-Members (Norfolk), court rolls "on the part of
Garvestone, Reymerston, Thuxton, Mattishall and Yaxham," 1595–1790; NRO originals filmed
1991. FamilySearch catalog koha:439083. Key film: DGS 004389244 (British Film 1472614;
items 10–11 = rolls 1595–1663, item 9 = 1743–55; 2,301 images; the same film also carries
Manor of Weybourne rolls — triage hits by title). Later rolls: DGS 004389566 (1664–1743),
DGS 004395112 (1756–1790). SourceId: `fs-whinburgh-with-members-court-rolls`.

**Examined (2026-07-16).** Full-text (FTS) film-scoped probes on DGS 004389244
(`+Gurn*` = 7; `q.fullName=rivett` = 1; `+Ryvet*`/`+Podm*` = 0); viewer image-array
hydration (2,301 images) with FTS-card position mapping; full-res pulls of the two
Gurney-bearing court leaves (arks `3:1:S3HT-6X4K-X5`, `3:1:S3HT-6X45-XZ`) and nine
calibration leaves (film images 1400–1750); expert triage read of all eleven (paleography
packet 41). The two leaf files are **date-inverted against their filenames and FTS card
dates** — cite by ark and visible heading only. Calibration read produced no secure
image→year map for the Latin-era leaves.

**Unexamined / limits.** The 1595–1663 item has not been image-walked (lead L-238; pre-1639
courts ≈ images 1395–1784). Name-extraction on the film is proven partial (one extracted
Rivett despite continuous tenancy), so **film-scoped full-text negatives mean nothing**.
The unfilmed NRO classes — surrenders and admissions 1564–1800 (NRS 21256–21355), rough
court books 1593–1790 (NRS 17009–18), early rolls 1594–1629 — are gated originals.
