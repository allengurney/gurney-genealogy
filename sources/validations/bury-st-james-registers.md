# Validation — Bury St Edmunds, St James parish registers (1561–1800)

**Source ID:** `bury-st-james-registers`

**What was examined.** The Bury St Edmunds St James parish registers, FamilySearch image group DGS 007413310 (681 images), via a film-scoped Full-Text `Gurn*` sweep (20 cards) and record-page reading, 2026-06-13.

**What portion.** All 20 Gurney-matching cards triaged; the Thomas-household entries (marriage image 297; baptisms images 60, 77, 83), the 1665 William baptism (image 86), the Henry burial (image 511), and the Elizabethan William/Bridgett (image 23) read in context.

**What remains uncertain.** Machine-transcript level; exact baptism days not recovered. The post-1653 register opening date affects the dating of the "John son to Thomas" entry.

**What it settles.** A documented negative for the emigrant-origin question: no John Gurney household at Bury in the 1607–1635 window; the only relevant household (Thomas × Lidda Broddish, m. c. 1633–35) is a generation too late to be John Gurney-1's natal family.

**Where findings landed.** `sources/corpus_supplement/bury-st-edmunds-gurney-registers-1561-1800.md`; `research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` (colonial follow-ups entry); promoted via patchset v93.
