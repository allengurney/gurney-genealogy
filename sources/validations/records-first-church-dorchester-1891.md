Source ID: `records-first-church-dorchester-1891`

## Scope Examined

Full-text sweep of the printed volume (archive.org `recordsoffirstch00firs`)
for Gurney-variant forms, 2026-07-01, including the false-friend-prone forms
Gornell, Gornhill, Garnesey, Garnsey, Gurnet. The volume's own index groupings
were checked directly against the body entries.
