**Source:** `hop-gurdon-brampton-1566-1650` (`data/sources.json`). History of Parliament Trust,
"GURDON, Brampton (1566-1650), of Assington, Suff.," in *The History of Parliament: the House
of Commons 1604-1629*, ed. Andrew Thrush and John P. Ferris (2010),
<https://www.historyofparliamentonline.org/volume/1604-1629/member/gurdon-brampton-1566-1650>.

**Examined:** the full online biography (Constituency, Family and Education, Offices Held, and
Biography sections), read directly 2026-07-11.

**Nature of the source:** an authoritative modern scholarly parliamentary biography (not a
primary record, but not a compiled-genealogy tertiary source either). Confirms Gurdon's family
purchased the Suffolk manor of Assington, four miles from Sudbury, in 1556; that his father
represented Sudbury in Parliament in 1571; that Gurdon himself was elected MP for Sudbury in
January 1621 (his one term); and that he was "friend and neighbour" of John Winthrop, recruiting
Winthrop in 1626 to canvass Suffolk gentry on his behalf, and continued corresponding with
Winthrop in New England as late as 1637.

parliamentary seat and personal friendship with Winthrop — cited in

Brackett-brothers/Gurdon-geography analysis) as context, not as a genealogical claim about any
Gurney or Brackett. Per the tertiary-sources citation rule, this authoritative biography stands
on its own for this contextual purpose.
