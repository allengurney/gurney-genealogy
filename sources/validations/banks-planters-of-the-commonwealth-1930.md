# Validation — Banks, *The Planters of the Commonwealth* (1930)

- **Source ID:** `banks-planters-of-the-commonwealth-1930`
- **Source:** Charles Edward Banks, *The Planters of the Commonwealth* (Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 1930). Internet Archive item `plantersofcommon00bank`.

## What was examined
Banks's arranged passenger/settler lists for the three ships nearest John Gurney's
1641 Weymouth appearance — the **Bevis** (1638), **Diligent of Ipswich** (1638),
and **Mary Anne of Yarmouth** (1637) — together with an OCR text search of the
whole volume for Gurney/Gourney/Gurny variants.

## Result
No John Gurney (or Gurney/Gourney/Gurny variant) appears on any of the three
lists or elsewhere in the New England passenger material. Negative for a
documented crossing.

## Scope and limitations
- Secondary compilation: Banks rearranged Drake and earlier transcripts into
  family groups, adding English homes and New England destinations.
- OCR of the Internet Archive text is imperfect; searches were run with variant
  spellings.
- Not an original customs manifest; no named Boston-area manifest survives for
  the 1639–mid-1641 window when John most likely crossed.
