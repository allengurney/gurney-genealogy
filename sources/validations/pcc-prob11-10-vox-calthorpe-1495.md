# Validation — PCC will of Sir William Calthorpe (PROB 11/10, Vox, 1495)

**Source:** `pcc-prob11-10-vox-calthorpe-1495` — register copy of the will of Sir William Calthorpe, knight (d. 1494), TNA PROB 11/10 (register Vox), fo. 23, via Ancestry dbid 5111 images 40611_311109-00370/00371/00372.

**Portion examined (July 2026, paleography packet 48-A):** all three leaves; targeted read of the Gurney passages and the closing/probate block, not a full diplomatic transcription of every bequest.

**Key readings:** "…by the handes of my son William Gurney…" (image 00371, line-strip 3); executor cluster "Dame Elizabeth my wife, Robert Clerke, Robert Fermer, William Gurney, Walter Aslake"; probate clause supporting 23 May 1495. The daughter's forename does not appear at the Gurney occurrences — her identification as Anne remains sourced to the Calthorpe-side pedigree evidence.

**Unexamined:** full bequest sequence; full Latin probate clause.
