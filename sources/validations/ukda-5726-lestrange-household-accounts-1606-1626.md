# Source validation: UK Data Archive Study 5726 — Le Strange household accounts

**Source ID:** `ukda-5726-lestrange-household-accounts-1606-1626`

**Citation:** Jane Whittle and Elizabeth Griffiths, *Household Accounts of the Le Strange Family,
1606–1626*, UK Data Archive Study 5726 (2006), study guide:
https://doc.ukdataservice.ac.uk/doc/5726/mrdoc/pdf/guide.pdf.

## Scope checked

- All 13 base-table exports in the local Access package, including the account-entry, people,
  place, category, document-reference, and relationship tables.
- Every entry linked to people record 718, Francis Gurney: 31 entries, dated 1612–1626.
- Conservative and broad Gurney spelling searches across exported text fields; Rivett, Lynn,
  apprenticeship, schooling, and selected-place checks.

## Limits

The transcription sample covers disbursements 1606–1626, selected receipts for 1606–1613 and
1619–1621, and kitchen accounts for 1619–1621. Database section references map to account-book
folio ranges, not an individual page for each entry. The underlying NRO account books are
`LEST/P6–P11`; the data do not transcribe the later 1634 material.
