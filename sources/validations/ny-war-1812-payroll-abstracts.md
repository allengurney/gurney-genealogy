# New York War of 1812 militia payroll abstracts — validation

Source ID: `ny-war-1812-payroll-abstracts`

## Source examined
One payroll card ("payroll abstract") for Willis Gurney, supplied via chat (Ancestry, "New York, U.S., War of 1812 Payroll Abstracts for New York State Militia, 1812–1815"; original New York State Archives Series B0810). Master held local-only (subscription image) at `sources/media/ny-war-1812-payroll-abstracts/_local/willis-gurney-payroll-card-1814.jpg`.

## Scope examined
The single card. Transcription:
- **Name:** Gurney, Willis. **Rank:** Private.
- **Organization:** 10th Brigade; Capt. George Englehart's company; Col. E. W. Laight's regiment.
- **Paid from** Sept. 2 **to** Oct. 2, 1814.
- **Remarks:** "Deserted and was recovered & Disc[harged] by Col. Laight Oct. 8th."
- Signed J. T. Watson. Roll 2378.

## Findings
Promote. Findings recorded on the Willis Gurney (G7) research companion and fact sheet. The card places a Willis Gurney in New York State militia service during the 1814 defense of New York, identifying him as a New York resident by 1814 — the earliest documented New York presence of the family. Identification with G7 is strong (the name is distinctive and the family is in the New York City / Queens area by 1821) but rests on name-and-place inference, not a stated parentage on the card.

## Limits
- The card gives no age, residence town, or parentage, so the identification with Willis G7 is inferential.
- Militia age conventions (typically 18–45) make a birth no later than about 1796 the comfortable reading, bearing on the contested G7 birth year.
