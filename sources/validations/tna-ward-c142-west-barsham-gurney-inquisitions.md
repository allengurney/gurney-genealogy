# Validation — TNA WARD 7 & C 142 inquisitions for the West Barsham Gurneys

- **Source ID:** `tna-ward-c142-west-barsham-gurney-inquisitions`
- **Source:** The National Archives (Kew), Court of Wards and Liveries inquisitions post mortem (WARD 7) and Chancery inquisitions post mortem, Series II (C 142), for the Gurneys of West Barsham, Norfolk. Consulted via TNA Discovery.

## What was examined
TNA Discovery catalogue descriptions (read 2026-07-01) for the Gurney entries in
WARD 7 and C 142, filtered to Norfolk, cross-checked against the Chancery series.
The documents themselves have **not** been pulled.

## Result
- **Edward Gournay** (Thomas III's son and heir, d. 6 Aug 1641): C 142/613/60
  (17 Chas I, 1641/2), "Gurney, Edward: Norfolk." The solid entry for the branch.
- Earlier "Gurney, Thomas: Norfolk" items — WARD 7/57/157 (14 Jas I) and
  WARD 7/57/80 (15 Jas I), with Chancery copies C 142/365/126 and C 142/370/10 —
  fall in 1616/17–1617/18. The source dump provisionally read them as **Thomas
  Gurney III's** death inquisitions, but that **conflicts with the repo's verified
  finding** (lead L-113 / the G15 companion) that Thomas III was living in Henry
  G15's 1 May 1621 will and died 1621×1623. Their attribution is therefore
  unresolved; they are carried as catalogue leads, not as Thomas III's death IPM.
- A "Thomas Gurney, knight: Essex" (WARD 7/81/91; C 142/479/95, 7 Chas I) has no
  known place in the Norfolk line and is logged only as an Essex comparator.
- **Antony Gurney: Norfolk** — C 142/106/46 (2&3 Ph&M, 1555/6) and C 142/154/67
  (12 Eliz, 1569/70). The first predates G17 Anthony Gurney's documented death
  (his will was proved Dec. 1557), so it cannot be his own death inquisition as
  read literally; promoted to the g17 companion as an open catalogue lead, not
  assigned to Anthony without the document itself.
- **William Gurney: Norfolk / Suffolk** — C 142/37/103 and C 142/37/122 (13 Hen
  VIII, 1521/2). Conflicts with both G19's IPM-attested death (16 Feb. 1507/8)
  and G18's predecease of his father — neither fits 1521/2 as read. Promoted to
  the g19 companion as an unresolved catalogue lead (2026-07-12).

## Scope and limitations
- **Catalogue-level only** — descriptions, regnal dates, and references are read;
  the inquisitions' texts (which would name guardians, lands, and the heir's exact
  age) are queued as offline / order-copy follow-ups.
- TNA Discovery normalises spellings to "Gurney" ("Gurnay"/"Gournay" return nil in
  WARD and C 142).
- The wardship chronology corroborates, but does not by itself prove, the reading
  of the 1627 Gurdon–Winthrop letter's "mrs. Gurny" as Martha (Lewkenor), widow of
  Thomas Gurney III, guardian of Edward.
