# Validation — The National Archives, Prerogative Court of Canterbury probate

**Source ID:** `tna-pcc-probate`
**Access route:** The National Archives Discovery catalogue, https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk
**Re-examined:** 2026-07-26. Prior examination 2026-06-20 (recorded in the registry note).

## What was examined

Catalogue-level sweep of the whole Prerogative Court of Canterbury series for Gurney testators
1590–1700, then item-by-item retrieval of the four references the project's John Gurney elimination
corpus depends on. Catalogue titles only; no will text was read at image level this pass.

## What was found — three references are misattributed in the project registry

| Reference | Discovery catalogue title | As previously carried |
|---|---|---|
| PROB 11/241/246 | Will of John Gurney, Shepherd of **East Claydon, Buckinghamshire**, 28 October 1654 | "East Chilton / East Chiltington, Sussex" |
| PROB 11/242/723 | Will of John Gurney, Shepherd of **East Claydon, Buckinghamshire**, 28 October 1654 | as above, counted as a second Sussex man |
| PROB 11/335/425 | Will of John Gurney, **Ironmonger of Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire**, 20 March 1671 | "husbandman, of Albury, Hertfordshire, 1676" |
| PROB 11/252/319 | Will of John Gurney, Gentleman of **East Greenwich, Kent**, 29 January 1656 | "yeoman of East Grinstead" |

The registry note for this source has been corrected. The same misattributions appear in
`sources/corpus_supplement/tna-pcc-gurney-elimination-corpus.md`, in the John Gurney case file and
in the identity topic files, where they remain uncorrected — that is a coordinated patchset, not a
registry edit.

Two further Aylesbury items were located and are not currently carried: **PROB 11/337/37**, a probate
sentence for John Gurney of Aylesbury dated 7 April 1671, and **PROB 4/69**, the inventory for the
same man. A sentence indicates a contested grant.

The complete Prerogative Court John Gurney set 1590–1700 is: East Claydon 1654 (two registrations),
East Greenwich 1656, Aylesbury 1671 (will, sentence, inventory), Winkfield, Berkshire 1683, and a
merchant 1686.

## What remains unexamined or uncertain

- **No will text has been read.** Every statement above rests on the Discovery catalogue title.
- **The Prerogative Court is one tier only.** The Archdeaconry of Buckingham probate series held at
  Buckinghamshire Archives (D/A/We, D/A/Wf) is not item-level indexed in Discovery, and most
  Buckinghamshire husbandmen and shepherds proved there rather than at Canterbury. Every "no
  probate" statement for a Buckinghamshire John Gurney is bounded accordingly.
- No Prerogative Court will exists for any John Gurney of Stewkley, Amersham, Ivinghoe, Hitcham,
  Haddenham or Weston Turville, 1590–1700.
