# Validation — Edmund Gurney the Divine, probate search (negative)

**Source ID:** `edmund-divine-pcc-probate-negative`

**What this is.** A documented negative-result source: a systematic, multi-repository search for any will or administration of Edmund Gurney, clerk, rector of Harpley (d. 1648), run 2026-06-12 (lead L-6). A high-value target (his will could name New England kin), so the search is recorded as a source rather than discarded.

**What was examined.** Norwich Consistory Court registers 1643–1651 (FamilySearch FTS, coverage-confirmed); TNA Discovery and beta catalogue (PROB 11 wills, PROB 6 admons, all Gurney spellings, 1640–1660); Matthews *Year Books of Probates* (Internet Archive); the Norfolk Record Office online catalogue; general web. Full query table in the corpus-supplement record.

**Result.** No probate located in any court under any spelling. The sole TNA "Will of Edmund Gurney" (PROB 11/739/515) is dated 1745, a different man.

**What remains unexamined.** FamilySearch collection 3259459 (PCC wills 1640–60) and Findmypast PCC admons vol. VI (both auth-gated); a complete pass of Matthews vol. 4; an NRO in-person check of the 1648 session files.

**Where it landed.** `sources/corpus_supplement/edmund-gurney-divine-pcc-probate-negative.md`; the negative and the two incidental records (NRO BL/O/G/1 grant 1630; TNA C 5/609/120 Chancery 1648, lead L-115) are recorded on `research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md`; promoted via patchset v93.
