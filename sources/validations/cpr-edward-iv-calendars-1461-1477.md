# Validation — Calendar of Patent Rolls, Edward IV, 1461–67 and 1467–77

**Sources:** `cpr-edward-iv-1461-67` (HMSO 1897; archive.org `calendarpatentr14offigoog`) and `cpr-edward-iv-henry-vi-1467-77` (HMSO 1900; archive.org `calendarofpatent00grea`).

**Portion examined (July 2026):** both volumes in full at index level and by full-text sweep of the downloaded djvu texts, using variant families (`G[ou][ou]?rn[ae]`), OCR-mangle families (rn→m: `G[ou]?um[ae]?y`), `Garney|Gerney`, and a wide surname-shape net filtered to John/Thomas. Index page references cross-checked against body pages.

**Result:** complete Gurn*-surname harvest — five substantive entries plus posthumous Matthew-Gurney manor references — extracted verbatim to `sources/corpus_supplement/cpr-edward-iv-1461-1477-gurney-entries.md`. No Thomas Gournay/Gurnay pardon or other entry in 1461–77 beyond the p. 284 London yeoman; no patent-roll trace of the 1471 death of Thomas Gurnay of Harpley.

**Limitations:** printed calendars, not the rolls; Google-scan OCR is rough (quoted text normalized from the printed page images' obvious mangles). Supplementary pardon rolls (TNA C 67) are not calendared in these volumes.

**Findings recorded at:** `research/topics/norwich-gurney-collateral-network.md` (clerk-convict section; King's-squire John Gurnay entry); `research/places/buckinghamshire.md` (Thomas Gourney of Halton).
