# Validation — Muskett, *Suffolk Manorial Families*, vol. 3 pt. 1 (1900)

Source ID: `muskett-suffolk-manorial-families-v3-1900`

## Scope examined
One passage read from the Internet Archive djvu text, item
`suffolkmanorialf31john`: the "CHAPLIN OF SEMER, BURY, ETC." pedigree,
printed p. ~109+ (djvu lines 12522–12542), carrying the 24 April 1672 will of
Thomas Chaplin of Bury St Edmunds (proved 25 Nov 1672, Arch. Sudbury Reg.
1672, fo. 349). This is a **transcript-level** read (OCR'd djvu text of the
printed edition), not an image-level read of the manuscript will or of the
printed page itself. No full-volume variant sweep was run for this
registration (contrast vols 1–2, which did get full-volume Gurney/Gourney/
Gournay/Garney sweeps); scope is limited to the single located passage.

## What it establishes / limits
Companion volume to vols 1–2 (both already registered), same imprint
(Exeter: William Pollard & Co.) and declared 1900 publication date per
Internet Archive metadata for this specific part (v.3 pt.1). One
bibliographic detail could not be fully confirmed: Internet Archive's
metadata lists **Frederic Johnson** as creator/editor for this item, with
Joseph James Muskett (1835–1910) listed as a contributing editor — the
project has not established how authorship/editorship was actually divided
between Muskett and Johnson for vol. 3 specifically, or whether the 1900
date on this part is reliable given Muskett's death in 1910. The citation is
registered matching the vol. 1/2 shape (Muskett as author) per the task
instruction to match those entries; this attribution should be revisited if
it becomes load-bearing.
