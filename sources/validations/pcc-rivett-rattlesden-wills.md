# Validation — PCC wills, Rivett/Ryvett of Rattlesden (1610, 1626)

**Source ID:** `pcc-rivett-rattlesden-wills`

**What was examined.** Two Prerogative Court of Canterbury registered wills, both read in full from the register images:
- Thomas Rivett of Rattlesden, 1610 — PROB 11/116 (Ancestry collection 5111 record 864421; images `40611_311272-00101/00102`).
- Thomas Ryvett of Woodhall in Rattlesden, 1626 — PROB 11/148/688 (Ancestry collection 5111 record 840263; images `40611_310876-00804/00805`).

**Scope and method.** Full secretary-hand transcription of both wills (the 1610 will lines span the full page width; left and right halves of each band were read). High-resolution Ancestry register images.

**Findings landed.** Negative result and the 1610 legatee list are recorded on `research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` (2026-05-29 session entry). Neither will names a Gurney; the 1610 household names Edward (sole executor), Thomas, and married daughter Dame Anne Glascock, with no Margaret — weakening, not eliminating, Rattlesden parentage for Margaret Rybett/Ryvett (lead L-7).

**Unexamined / uncertain.** Four wider-net Suffolk Rivett PCC wills (Bildeston, Stowmarket, Ipswich 1567, Walberswick 1580) were left untranscribed as low-prior after the two exact-match Rattlesden wills came back clean.

**Image note.** Source images are paywalled Ancestry subscription content; per `sources.md` they are not committed to the repo. The durable record is this transcription plus the Ancestry/TNA record references.
