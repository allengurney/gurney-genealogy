# Validation — TNA PCC wills, Gournay of West Barsham (1642, 1661)

Source ID: `tna-pcc-west-barsham-gournay-wills`

## What was examined
Two PCC registered-copy wills, downloaded as TNA PDF facsimiles and rendered to page images
at 300 dpi:

- **PROB 11/188/136** — Edward Gournay of West Barsham, esq., will November 1639, proved
  8 February 1642. Register Cambell, quires 1–42. Two images; the will occupies page 1
  (register folio numbered 162) and is complete on that page.
- **PROB 11/303/284** — Henry Gourney of West Barsham, esq., will 21 December, proved
  11 February 1661. Register May, quires 1–53. Two images; the will begins at the foot of
  page 1 (register folio numbered 170) and runs to the head of page 2, ending with the Latin
  probate clause.

Both entries were read in full, line by line, from the rendered page images.

## Method
Page images rendered from the TNA PDFs, then read via per-line reading strips and
enhancement sheets. Names and relationship terms were confirmed at token level rather than
inferred from context. The TNA catalogue description was checked against the register text;
the catalogue records neither testator's status, and both are styled *esquire* in the wills
themselves.

Independently corroborated against the UK Data Service PCC wills index (SN 4816): register
name and quire range agree for both items.

## What remains uncertain
- Edward's second-named sister carries an appended surname, probably a married name; the
  letterforms admit *Mathewson* / *Mathison* and the reading is not secure.
- The place-name in Henry's settlement recital, read as *Helton alias Hault Hall in Helton*,
  is not securely identified against a modern Norfolk parish.
- Edward's will date is *"The [last] date of November 1639"*; the day word is partly obscured.
- Henry's wife is never named in his will, only styled *"my deare wife"*; she is identified
  only as a daughter of William Adams of the Middle Temple, esquire.
- Neither will has been checked against the original (unregistered) will or against
  Norwich Consistory Court probate for the same family.
