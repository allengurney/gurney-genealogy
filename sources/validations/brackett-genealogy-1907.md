# Validation — Brackett Genealogy (1907)

**Source:** `brackett-genealogy-1907` (`data/sources.json`). Herbert I. Brackett, *Brackett
Genealogy* (Boston, 1907), Internet Archive `brackettgenealog00brac`.

**Examined:** the introductory Richard/Peter/Thomas Brackett discussion, pp. 109–119 (full text
read from the local djvu OCR transcription staged at
`sources/intake/dump-files/dump-2026-07-02-g13-colonial-round4-files/brackettgenealog00brac_djvu.txt`),
2026-07-11. The rest of the volume (the per-descendant genealogical divisions) was not read.

**Nature of the source:** an early-20th-century compiled family genealogy. Its own author
concedes the evidence for the immigrant brothers' shared parentage and English origin is
"meager"; treat as compiled tradition, not a primary record.

**Findings recorded in:** `research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/origin/22-migration-network.md`
(the Brackett-brothers/Gurdon-geography analysis). Verbatim extract at
`sources/corpus_supplement/brackett-genealogy-1907-origin-extract.md`.

**Remains / uncertain:** the volume's own claimed source, the 1885–6 *East Anglian Magazine*
matriculation extract, was not independently checked; the Wreningham Richard Brackett's
relationship (if any) to the immigrant is explicitly unproven in the volume itself and remains
so here. The rest of the Richard/Peter/Thomas Brackett descendant divisions (pp. 119ff.) are
unread.
