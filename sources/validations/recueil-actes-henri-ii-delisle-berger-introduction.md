## Findings recorded

- Two sealed Hugh de Gournay charters not printed by Daniel Gurney (Anchin abbey, 1176 and 1177), the 1177 one styling Hugh "Dei permissione dominus Gorniaci" and citing Ralph de Coucy "dominus et cognatus noster" — a primary Gournay–Coucy kinship statement. Promoted to `research/topics/senior-gournay-baron-line-collateral.md`.
- Norman exchequer entry "Osbertus de Gornaio mortuus usurarius" — a minor Gournay-named moneylender. Promoted to the senior-line topic.
- A Rouen judgment at Saint-Gervais witnessed by "Hugo de Gurnaio" (1160s). Noted on the senior-line topic.

## Remaining cautions

- The IA OCR is noisy (e.g. "Gorniaci"/"Gornaci"); check page images before quoting exact spellings.
- The two Anchin charters are only *flagged* by Delisle from Guesnon's copies; they are not printed in the Recueil and have not been seen in the original.
