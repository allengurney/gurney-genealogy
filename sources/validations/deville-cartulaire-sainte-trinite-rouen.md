# Validation — Deville, *Cartulaire de la Sainte-Trinité du Mont de Rouen* (1840)

**Source ID:** `deville-cartulaire-sainte-trinite-rouen`

**What was examined.** Achille Deville's edition of the cartulary of the abbey of the Holy Trinity (Sainte-Trinité du Mont) of Rouen, in the *Collection des cartulaires de France*, bound with Guérard's Saint-Bertin cartulary. Internet Archive `cartulairedelab00frangoog`, searched 2026-05-29 for "Gornaco"/Gerard de Gournay.

**Scope and method.** OCR full-text search located **Charter XCIV** (*Chartularium* p. 168), the act DG-Supp Note 16 had cited but not re-located. Read the charter abstract and attestation clause; OCR garbled "Girardi" as "Ginanpi" in the body but the index (p. 468 ref) confirms "Girardus de Gornaco."

**Findings landed.** `research/people/g32-gerard-de-gournay-fact-sheet.research.md` §2.12 — Charter XCIV: Richard de Redvers's grant to the abbey of the church and tithe of a vill (Opimensis); Abbot Walter and the brethren pay him 15 livres; attestation clause "Signum Ricardi de Rethvers. Signum Girardi de Gornaco. Signum Hugonis de Bellebeuf. Testes ex nostris: Ricardus senescal; Bernardus cocus." Resolves lead L-49.

**Unexamined / uncertain.** The Gournay mark is a *signum* (subscription), not a described pictorial seal — the lead's "seal" framing is refined accordingly. The act is undated internally; dated only by Abbot Walter's tenure and Richard de Redvers's lifetime (d. 1107). The place "Opimensis" is not yet identified to a modern locality. The original charter (vs Deville's printed text) has not been inspected.
