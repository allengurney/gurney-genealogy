# Massachusetts State Archives, colonial period (petition calendar)

Source ID: `massachusetts-state-archives-colonial`

## Scope examined
FamilySearch film/DGS 007702977, **image 947** (ark `3:1:3Q9M-C9Y5-F9M1-4`), read at full resolution via the das/v2 image API, 2026-06-27. One calendar entry transcribed: the 30 May 1641 petition of John Gurny, Richard Porter, and James Ludden of Weymouth for remission of the gunpowder fines.

## What it establishes / limits
The calendar entry's forename for Porter is **Richard**, confirmed at image level — the counter-reading to the printed MBCR 1:331 "John Porter." This is the **calendar/index**, not the petition document; it does not by itself resolve which forename the original petition bears. Image 948 (ark `3:1:3Q9M-C9Y5-F9M1-C`) is the adjacent calendar leaf.
