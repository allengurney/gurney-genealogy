# Validation — Muskett, *Suffolk Manorial Families*, vol. 1 (1900)

- **Source ID:** `muskett-suffolk-manorial-families-v1-1900`
- **Source:** Joseph James Muskett, *Suffolk Manorial Families*, vol. 1 (Exeter: William Pollard & Co., 1900). Internet Archive item `suffolkmanorialf01musk`.

## What was examined
Printed pp. 285–288 (Brampton Gurdon's will and the Gurdon-of-Assington /
Gurdon-of-Letton pedigrees) and pp. 289–290 (the 1591 will of Martha Heigham of
Denham), plus a full-volume OCR sweep for Gurney/Gurnay/Gournay variants (both a
case-sensitive tight net and a case-insensitive net over the djvu text).

## Result
- The Gurdon-of-Assington pedigree gives Brampton Gurdon m. (2) **Muriel**,
  daughter of **Martin Sedley of Morley, Norfolk** by **Abigail** (a Knyvett of
  Ashwelthorpe) — the household that produced the 1627 letter to Winthrop.
- The 1591 will of Martha Heigham of Denham names **Martha Lewkenor** — later wife
  of Thomas Gurney III of West Barsham — among the daughters of Susan (Heigham)
  Lewkenor of Denham, and endows an Emmanuel College scholarship, fixing the
  Denham puritan-gentry matrix behind "Mrs. Gurney."
- **No early-modern Gurney appears anywhere in Muskett vol. 1.** The Gurdon–Gurney
  link runs solely through the Sedley/Lewkenor channel.

## Scope and limitations
- A compiled visitation/pedigree work, not the original wills; it prints or
  abstracts the P.C.C. wills it cites (Gurdon 68 Pembroke 1647; Heigham 55 Nevell
  1591).
- The distinct Suffolk **GARNEYS of Mickfield** are a false-friend surname and are
  not part of the Gurney line.
