# Plymouth Colony Records, vol. 8 (Shurtleff & Pulsifer, 1857)

Source ID: `plymouth-colony-records-vol8-shurtleff-pulsifer-1857`

## Scope Examined

Full-text sweep of the printed volume (archive.org `recordsofcolonyo0708newp`, djvu text) for
Gurney-variant forms, including the corrupted form "Surney," 2026-07-01. The Taunton town
return of vital records (printed p. ~60) and the whole-volume index were both checked.

## Findings Location

The Taunton return of the 9 January 1676 Bundy-Gurney marriage ("Ruth Surney, of Mendum") and
its bearing on the John Jr./Ruth household are carried in
`research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/family/12-mendon-descendants.md`. The Isaac
Gurney 1663/4 Scituate court entry (already held) is on
`research/people/isaac-gurney-scituate-boston.md`.

## Limits

Full-volume sweep found no other Gurney-variant entry across the whole of the Plymouth Colony
Records (all twelve volumes were swept in the same 2026-07-01 campaign). Text is OCR from the
printed edition, not the manuscript.
