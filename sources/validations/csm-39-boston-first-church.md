# CSM 39 — Records of the First Church in Boston, vol. I (1961)

Source ID: `csm-39-boston-first-church`

## Scope examined
Full text of vol. 39 (Volume I: admissions to membership 1630–1778, church
votes/discipline 1656–1785, baptisms 1630–1847), read at **transcript level**
from the Colonial Society of Massachusetts's own hosted text,
`colonialsociety.org/node/1070` (717,285 characters in one page). Examined via
an in-browser regex sweep for `g[aeiouvy]rn` (case-insensitive) and `gourn`
across the entire volume, 2026-07-03. Companion volumes 40–41 (marriages and
further records, node/537 and node/585) were **not** examined and are not
covered by this registration.

## What it establishes / limits
This is the church's own admission/discipline/baptism register as edited by
Richard D. Pierce from the original record books — a transcript-level read of
a printed critical edition, not an image-level read of the manuscript. The
sweep result (dump finding F-R4.2) is recorded in the dump file, not repeated
here; see `sources/intake/dump-files/dump-2026-07-02-g13-colonial-round4-arch45-and-network.md`.
Coverage-check terms ("Newgate," "Braintree," "Brackett") confirmed the sweep
was reading real content rather than a truncated or malformed page.
