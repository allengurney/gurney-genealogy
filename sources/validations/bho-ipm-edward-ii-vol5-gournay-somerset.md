# IPM Edward II vol. 5, no. 538 — Somerset Gournay fees (Anselm, Thomas) validation

- Examined: *Calendar of Inquisitions Post Mortem*, vol. 5 (Edward II), entry 538, via the retained Internet Archive full text `cu31924011387838` (dump-images), accessed 2026-07-03. Calendar-abstract level, not the original membrane.
- Scope: the Somerset Gournay fee entries only — Inglescombe (1 knight's fee, Thomas de Gurneye) and Boketon (1½ fee) with Fodyngton and Northcote (2 fees), all held by Anselm de Gurnay. Index: "Gurnay, Anselm de, 538 (p. 342)"; "Gurneye, Thomas de, 538 (p. 339)."
- Also confirmed negative: no Norfolk Bardolf/Gurnay feoffment in this volume (that record is CIPM vol. 7, no. 243).
- Findings recorded in: `research/places/somerset-gournay-collateral.md` ("The Somerset Gournays in the printed calendars").
- Open: the entry fixes the fees at Anselm/Thomas level but does not itself resolve the internal Somerset genealogy beyond Pettigrew's reconstruction.
