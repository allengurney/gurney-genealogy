# Validation — Adam Winthrop's diary, 3 Oct. 1605 (Winthrop Papers I:153)

- **Source ID:** `mhs-winthrop-papers-adam-winthrop-diary-1605`
- **Source:** Adam Winthrop, diary/almanac note, 3 October 1605, in *Winthrop Papers*, vol. I, 1498–1628 (Boston: Massachusetts Historical Society, 1929), p. 153. Internet Archive item `winthroppapersvo0000comm`.

## What was examined
The single diary/almanac entry at printed p. 153 (djvu lines 15423–15425), read
from the Internet Archive text, with its facing editorial footnote 37; part of
the same printed volume as the 1627 Gurdon–Winthrop letter (`mhs-winthrop-papers-gurdon-to-winthrop-1627`)
but a distinct document. No further Gurney-variant hits appear in the volume beyond
the known p. 363 letter.

## Result
Adam Winthrop recorded the deaths of **Sir Edward Lewkenor of Denham** and his
wife on 3 October 1605 — "Sir Edwarde Lewkenor of Denham in Suff[olk] Knight died
of the smalpocks. *Vir bonus et doctus fuit et patriae amans.* The lady his wife
died 2 dayes before him." The couple are Sir Edward Lewkenor (d. 1605) and Susan
(Heigham), the parents of Martha (Lewkenor) Gurney — so the Winthrop family
personally memorialised the widowed Mrs. Gurney's own parents a generation before
the 1627 letter carried a Gurney suit to John Winthrop. The editors' note gives
Sir Edward's parliamentary career (Maldon, Essex, 1584–1604) and cites the 1606
memorial volume *Threnodia in Obitum D. Edovardi Lewkenor*.

## Scope and limitations
- Printed edition of a manuscript diary; the identification of the couple as
  Martha (Lewkenor) Gurney's parents rests on the 1591 Heigham will and the
  Denham Lewkenor pedigree (Muskett), not on the diary itself.
- *Threnodia* (1606) is a further full-text target, not yet examined.
