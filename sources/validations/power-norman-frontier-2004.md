# Power, *The Norman Frontier* (2004) -- validation

Source ID: `power-norman-frontier-2004`

## Source examined

Forty-two local scans from Allen Gurney's print copy of Daniel Power, *The Norman Frontier in the Twelfth and Early Thirteenth Centuries* (Cambridge University Press, 2004). The modern book is copyright-restricted; scans are retained only at `sources/media/power-norman-frontier-2004/_local/`.

## Scope

The scans cover pp. 11--13, 27, 53, 91, 105, 118, 162, 188--191, 219, 233, 355--359, 365, 368--370, 408, Map VII, 414--415, 424, 469, 504--505, 532--538, Map IX, and index p. 606. The supplied page set has been visually reviewed, including the p. 504 genealogy diagram; the two captures of p. 11 are retained as source scans.

## Findings location

The evidence-rich, page-labelled extract is `sources/corpus_supplement/power-norman-frontier-gournay-selected-pages.md`. Its research promotion is prepared in `sources/intake/processed/v127-power-norman-frontier.patchset.md`.

## Limits

Power's treatment concerns the senior Gournay lordship. The supplied pages do not name Walter de Gournay or document his parentage, Montigny-sur-Andelle, or a parage division for the Norfolk junior branch. The p. 504 diagram is likewise confined to the senior line.
