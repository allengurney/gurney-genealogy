# Validation — Visitations of Norfolk 1563/1589/1613 (Harleian Society vol. 32)

**Source ID:** `visitations-norfolk-harleian-32`

**What was examined.** Walter Rye's edition of the Heralds' Visitations of Norfolk (Harleian Society vol. 32), full text on Internet Archive (`visitacionievisi32ryew`), searched 2026-05-29 for Gurney/Gournay/Gurnay and Barsham; the West Barsham / Great Ellingham Gurney pedigree read at p. 141 (from Harl. MS 1552, fo. 53 / pencil 49).

**Scope and method.** OCR full-text read of the printed pedigree (a heralds' visitation pedigree, not a parish register); the multi-column child layout was reconstructed from the OCR.

**Findings landed.**
- **g15-Henry** — Working Notes entry + witness-list bullet: Henry's full child-set (Thomas, heir, m. Martha Lewknor; Henry; Edward; Anthony; **Francis "of London" = G14**; Leonard; Bassingbourne *ob. s.p.*; daughters Elizabeth, Mary, Anne, Abigail), the Blennerhassett marriage, the Holdich (G16) / Anthony (G17) ascent, and the arms with a cinquefoil difference.
- **g14-Francis** — the visitation lists "6. Francis, of London," Henry G15's sixth son (corroborating the G15→G14 hinge).
- **g16-Francis** / **g17-Anthony** — parentage and Holdich-marriage corroboration.
- The cinquefoil-differenced engrailed-cross arms bear on L-10 (recorded on the g13 thread by the repo owner).

**Unexamined / uncertain.** The "1633" in lead L-24 is an error for the **1563/1589/1613** series. The Browning-wills strand of L-24 is separate and not pursued here. OCR garbles some names ("West Ba88am" = West Barsham; "Job a Blenerhassett" = a Blennerhassett of Barsham); verify exact wording against the page image before quoting.
