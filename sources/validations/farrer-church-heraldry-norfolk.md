# Validation — Farrer, *The Church Heraldry of Norfolk*

**Source ID:** `farrer-church-heraldry-norfolk`

**What was examined.** Edmund Farrer's parish-by-parish survey of arms surviving in Norfolk churches. Vol. 1 (Internet Archive `churchheraldryof01farr`) and vol. 2 (`churchheraldryn03farrgoog`) searched, 2026-05-29, for Gurney/Gournay/Gurnay and the relevant parishes.

**Scope and method.** OCR full-text search of the digitised volumes. Farrer records arms present in the churches at the time of survey (1880s); absence from his lists does not prove an arm never existed (cf. the Armstrong-1781 "probably Wentworth" shield at West Barsham, not in Farrer).

**Findings landed.**
- **`research/places/west-barsham.md`** — chancel brass of Edward Gournay (d. 1641), Gournay arms "Argent, a cross engrailed gules"; De Warenne glass (chequy or and azure) in the East window; Clare shield; Calthorpe and Morley monuments. Second independent witness (after Armstrong 1781) to the Edward Gournay brass and the Gurney arms.
- **`research/places/harpley.md`** — Gournay arms (argent, a cross engrailed gules) with the note "John de Gournay was Lord… 35 Edward I" and a cross-reference to Daniel Gurney's *Record* p. 344.

**Unexamined / uncertain.** Great Ellingham (G15 Henry's residence parish) was **not present** in vols 1–2 examined (vol. 1 has Clavering's Ellingham St Mary; vol. 2 has Little Ellingham only) — likely an unscanned/unfinished part. A modern Gurney monument in vol. 1 quarters Gurney + the "Gournay ancient coat" (paly of six, or and azure) + De Warenne + Barclay — a 19th-c. claimed achievement, evidence of family heraldic tradition, not a medieval witness.
