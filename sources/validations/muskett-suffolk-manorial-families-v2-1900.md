# Validation — Muskett, *Suffolk Manorial Families*, vol. 2 (1900)

- **Source ID:** `muskett-suffolk-manorial-families-v2-1900`
- **Source:** Joseph James Muskett, *Suffolk Manorial Families*, vol. 2 (Exeter: William Pollard & Co., 1900). Internet Archive item `suffolkmanorialf02john`.

## What was examined
Three passages of the printed volume, read from the Internet Archive djvu text:
the L'Estrange-of-Hunstanton / Bozoune pedigree at printed p. 176 (djvu lines
21249–21277); the Hovell-of-Hillington visitation charts at printed pp. ~10 and
~36 (djvu lines 1039–1051 and 4021–4028); and the abstracted will of Mary Goodwin
of St Giles', Norwich, at printed pp. ~28–29 (djvu lines 3189–3210). A full-volume
OCR sweep for Gurney/Gurnay/Gournay/Garney variants accompanied the reads.

## Result
- The Bozoune pedigree names **Elizabeth, daughter of Thomas and sister of Edward
  Gournay of West Barsham**, married before 20 December 1636 to **Bozoune Crowe of
  East Bilney, Norfolk** — a daughter of Thomas Gurney III and Martha Lewkenor
  recorded in no Gurney source, and a senior-branch household five miles from East
  Dereham. It also dates Crowe's will (15 September 1658, proved 11 March following).
- The Hovell charts and Mary Goodwin's 1647 will independently confirm **Frances
  Hovell of Hillington as Edward Gournay's wife**, alive and acknowledged as kin
  ("Cozen Gurney") in November 1647.
- The same charts give **Anne Lewkenor of Denham as the wife of Sir Nicholas
  L'Estrange, 1st Bt.**, the jest-book compiler behind the Thoms 1839 Gurney
  anecdotes — making those anecdotes kin-sourced. Not assimilated here.

## Scope and limitations
- Compiled visitation and pedigree charts with abstracted wills, not original
  records. The Crowe will (PROB 11/289/177) and Carthew's *Launditch* ii, 621 are
  the primary follow-ups.
- Muskett writes "East Basham"/"East Barsham" for Edward Gournay's seat; West
  Barsham is meant, or the widow's later residence. Flagged, not resolved.
- Muskett renders the distinct Suffolk **GARNEYS** surname as "Gurneys" in his
  charts (e.g. Mirabel Garneys of Kenton). In this volume, "Gurneys" with a
  terminal -s is Garneys until proven otherwise.
- Page numbers for the Hovell material are approximate; the djvu line ranges above
  are the reliable locator.
