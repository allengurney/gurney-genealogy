# Cummington, Massachusetts — town register of births — validation

Source ID: `cummington-vital-records`

## Source examined
One page image: page 213 (letter **G**) of the Cummington town clerk's register of births, supplied via chat (Ancestry, "Massachusetts, U.S., Town and Vital Records, 1620–1988"). Master held local-only (subscription image) at `sources/media/cummington-vital-records/_local/cummington-births-page-213.jpg`. The same register is indexed by FamilySearch ("Massachusetts, Town Clerk, Vital and Town Records, 1626–2001," ark:/61903/1:1:F4V6-Z38).

## Scope examined
The Gurney entries on page 213 only — the children of Amos & Ruth Gurney (G8), with adjacent collateral households of Asa & Molly and Benjamin & Esther Gurney for page context. Other pages and surnames not examined.

## Method
Page is high-resolution; the Amos & Ruth cluster and the out-of-sequence Willis line were cropped and upscaled for legibility before transcription. The clerk's parenthetical "(?)" after Willis's year is on the page, not an editorial mark.

## Findings
Promote. Transcription at `sources/corpus_supplement/cummington-vital-records-gurney-births.md`; substantive findings on the G8 (Amos) and G7 (Willis) research companions and fact sheets. The five in-sequence children's dates match Rigler's 1994 edition. Willis is recorded only as a late, out-of-sequence entry with the year self-flagged "(?)," so the register does not settle his birth year.

## Limits
- Single page; image not yet checked against the FamilySearch index frame-by-frame.
- The Willis year reads "Feb. 23[?] 1793"; the FamilySearch index gives "25 Feb 1793." The day is uncertain and the year is flagged on the page.
