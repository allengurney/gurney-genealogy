# Early Records of the Town of Providence (21 vols, 1892–1915)

Source ID: `early-records-town-of-providence`

## Scope examined
Town-meeting and probate volumes read at transcript level from the Internet
Archive copies (`earlyrecordsofto01inprov` through `-05`, `cu31924092200603`
(v8), `cu31924092200637` (v11), `earlyrecordsofto16prov` (v16)) via a broad
Gurney-variant sweep, 2026-07-01. Volumes carrying the separate 1690s
Providence John-and-Sarah Gurney cluster were **not** in this batch and are
outside this registration.

## What it establishes / limits
The volumes swept carry no Gurney of any spelling; the only near-name is a
distinct Thomas Garnet family of Providence (v16 probate). The town-meeting and
deed volumes for 1655–1675 (vols 2–3) are clean, which bounds the search for
John Gurney's unrecorded Quinapaug (Providence-jurisdiction) interest carried
into his 1663 inventory. Transcript-level read of a printed edition, not an
image read of the Providence manuscript books. This is dump finding F-R6.
