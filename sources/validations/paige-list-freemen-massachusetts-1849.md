# Paige, List of Freemen of Massachusetts (1849) — validation

Source ID: `paige-list-freemen-massachusetts-1849`

## Source examined
Two index entries in the Ancestry database "Massachusetts, U.S., Applications of Freemen, 1630–1691" (collection 4296), which reproduces Lucius R. Paige, *List of Freemen of Massachusetts, 1630–1691* (NEHGS, 1849):

- **Richard Gurney** — application 12 Oct 1681, residence Weymouth, Massachusetts (original source: Colony Records, Vol. V, pp. 320–321).
- **John Gurnell** — application 10 May 1643, residence Massachusetts (original source: Colony Records, Vol. II, pp. 27–28).

## Scope examined
The two index records only (transcribed application date, name, and residence). The underlying Colony Records (MBCR / Shurtleff ed.) page images and Paige's printed volume were not pulled.

## Findings
- Richard Gurney 1681 → confirms the freeman admission on the G12 Richard companion (`research/people/g12-richard-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md`, Freeman 1681; resolves Open Question 4).
- John Gurnell 1643 → disambiguation/negative result on the G13 companion (`research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md`): a distinct surname, not the emigrant John Gurney-1. A 30 Jul 1675 Dorchester death entry, examined directly, reads "Gurnell," confirming Gurnell as the actual surname rather than a Gurney variant.

## Limits
Index-level evidence; not image-verified against the Colony Records. The Dorchester 1675 Gurnell death is recorded from a direct look at the entry image but its town/vital-record source has not been formally registered here.
