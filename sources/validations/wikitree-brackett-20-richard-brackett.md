# Validation — WikiTree Brackett-20 (Richard Brackett, 1610–1690)

**Source:** `wikitree-brackett-20-richard-brackett` (`data/sources.json`). WikiTree profile
Richard Brackett (Brackett-20), <https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Brackett-20>.

**Examined:** the profile's Family/Ancestors summary and Biography section, read directly
2026-07-11.

**Nature of the source:** a compiled, crowd-edited genealogy profile, not a primary record. It
gives Richard Brackett a baptism at St Gregory, Sudbury, Suffolk, 16 September 1610, son of
Peter Bracket and Rachel, with a Sudbury-based paternal line back three further generations; it
cites Anderson's *Great Migration Begins* only for the fact of emigration during the Puritan
Great Migration window, not for the Sudbury baptism itself. Treat as a compiled-genealogy lead
for the Sudbury origin, not an image-verified parish-register fact.

**Findings recorded in:** `research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/origin/22-migration-network.md`
(the Brackett-brothers/Gurdon-geography analysis), cited alongside the superseded Norfolk
(Wreningham) scrap in the 1907 Brackett Genealogy.

**Remains / uncertain:** the St Gregory, Sudbury parish register itself (image level) was not
examined, nor was the NEHGS *Early New England Families* Richard Brackett sketch that would
supply the modern scholarly citation of record for this baptism — both remain open verification
steps, carried as backlog in the coverage ledger rather than claimed as examined here.
