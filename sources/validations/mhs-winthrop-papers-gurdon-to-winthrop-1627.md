# Validation — Brampton Gurdon to John Winthrop, 20 Oct. 1627 (Winthrop Papers I:363)

- **Source ID:** `mhs-winthrop-papers-gurdon-to-winthrop-1627`
- **Source:** Brampton Gurdon to John Winthrop, "morly this 20 of 8ber [Oct.] 1627," *Winthrop Papers*, vol. I, 1498–1628 (Boston: Massachusetts Historical Society, 1929), p. 363; MHS Winthrop Family Papers Digital Edition, document `PWF01d256`.

## What was examined
The full text of the letter (digital-edition transcription and the printed
Winthrop Papers I:363), its three editorial footnotes, and the whole digital
edition's search results for Gurney-variant strings (`gurney`, `gurny`, `gurnie`,
`girny`, `gourney`).

## Result
The letter is the **only** Gurney-variant document in the Winthrop Family Papers
Digital Edition. Gurdon, writing from Morley, Norfolk, asks Winthrop — then an
attorney at the Court of Wards and Liveries — to further the suit of "mr. warford"
(an M.A. and eight-year schoolmaster in Martin Sedley's Morley household) to the
Master of the Wards over a church presentation held by "mrs. Gurny who is gardyon
to her sonn who wantethe a few monthes of being of ful age"; "yong mr. gurney"
attends him. Commendations to "mr. Downing" (Emmanuel Downing). The MHS editors
identified only Sedley; they did **not** identify Warford or the Gurney widow and
heir.

## Scope and limitations
- The "W. 1. 29" in the digital-edition citation is the manuscript number, not a
  page; the printed reference is Winthrop Papers I:363.
- The identification of "mrs. Gurny" / "yong mr. gurney" with the West Barsham
  senior branch (Martha née Lewkenor, widow of Thomas Gurney III, and her heir
  Edward Gournay b. c.1607) is the project's own reading, not the editors'; it
  rests on the wardship chronology and the West Barsham brass, cited alongside.
- Warford is not yet identified (see the Venn negative); his institution record
  would name the advowson the wardship estate held.
