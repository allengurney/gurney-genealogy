# Classified Record of Weymouth Births (Nash copy, 1879) — validation

Source ID: `weymouth-classified-record-births-nash`

## Source examined
One opening, **pp. 74–75**, of the manuscript "Classified Record of Weymouth Births to 1843–4" (alphabetised by surname; copied 1879 by Gilbert Nash, purchased by the Town of Weymouth March 1890), via Ancestry, "Massachusetts, U.S., Town and Vital Records, 1620–1988" (Holbrook, collection 2495, record 14720071). Title page and the pp. 74–75 opening supplied via chat. Page image (subscription) held local-only at `sources/media/weymouth-classified-record-births-nash/_local/weymouth-classified-births-p74-75-gurney.jpg`.

## Scope examined
The complete **Gurney** section at the head of p. 75 — thirteen entries, 1656–1732, each giving child, parents, and birth date. Adjacent surnames (Gould, Green, Greenough, Gregory on p. 74; "Harvey"/next surname on p. 75) not transcribed.

## Findings
Transcribed in full on `research/people/g12-richard-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` (the hub: most entries are Richard² Gurney's children and grandchildren). Town-record confirmation of dates otherwise carried from Rigler and the *History of Weymouth*. Specific cross-links: G11 daughter Rebecca b. 22 Nov 1702 (`research/people/g11-benjamin-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md`); Elijah s. Nathan & Sarah b. 15 May 1732 (`research/people/nathan-gurney-abington.research.md`).

## Limits
- Index/transcription-level reading of one opening; years read from the upscaled image (clear). No other pages of the register examined.
- The Gurney section omits Richard²'s eldest sons John (c.1654/8) and Zachariah³ (c.1660) and son Benjamin G11 (c.1676) — recorded elsewhere or unregistered; not resolved here.
