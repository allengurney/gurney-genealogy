# Massachusetts Wills and Probate Records, 1635–1991 — validation

Source ID: `ma-wills-probate-records`

## Source examined
One probate image: the will of **Benjamin Gurney Sen.r of Middleborough** (G11; Rigler's Benjamin-3), **Plymouth County Probate vol. 8, pp. 98–99**, supplied via chat (Ancestry, "Massachusetts, U.S., Wills and Probate Records, 1635–1991," collection 9069, record 716357). Master held local-only (subscription image) at `sources/media/ma-wills-probate-records/_local/benjamin-gurney-g11-will-plymouth-probate-8-98.jpg`.

## Scope examined
The complete will (both pages of the opening): the dispositive clauses, the signature/attestation, and the probate clause. The will is self-contained on pp. 98–99; the preceding entry on p. 98 is an unrelated estate and was not transcribed.

## Method
High-resolution page; the opening lines, the signature/witness block, and the probate clause were cropped and upscaled to confirm names and the date. A few readings are bracketed in the transcription (Sen.r/Son; Sa[m]son).

## Findings
Promote. Verbatim transcription at `sources/corpus_supplement/plymouth-probate-benjamin-gurney-g11-will-1738.md`; substantive findings on the G11 (Benjamin Gurney) research companion. The image confirms and extends the Rigler/Plymouth Probate 8:98 paraphrase: will dated 4 May 1738, proved 2 July 1739; signed by mark; executors wife Rebekah and son Benjamin; only son Benjamin (G10) and daughter Rebeckah Taylor named as legatees (no further children); witnesses James Whaley, Gershom Sa[m]son, Nehemiah Bennett; before John Cushing, Judge of Probate.

## Limits
- One will examined; the collection covers many other Plymouth and Massachusetts probates not yet pulled.
- "Sen.r" and the witness surname "Sa[m]son" are bracketed readings; an inventory or administration account for the estate (if filed separately) was not sought.
