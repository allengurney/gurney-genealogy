# PCC will of John Lewis of Nevis (PROB 11/461, register Dyer) — validation

- Examined: register images of PROB 11/461 (register Dyer, quire 99), Ancestry dbid 5111, images 40611_310640-00574 to -00576, read via paleography packet 48-C, 2026-07-16.
- Scope: opening leaf and the legatee sequence around the Grisell Lloyd and Mary Gurney bequests; not a full diplomatic transcription of the whole will.
- Confirms: the abstract's stand-alone wording "unto Mary Gurney the daughter of John Gurney," placed immediately after the £10 bequest to "my kinswoman Grisell Lloyd daughter of James Lloyd." Mary carries no residence or kinship qualifier at this occurrence. Sole executor Henry Lloyd, merchant of Bristol; island executors-in-trust Arthur Plomer and William Ling of Nevis.
- Unexamined / uncertain: the money amount following the Mary Gurney clause (image/line-strip overlap); full witness cluster and the remaining family bequests.
