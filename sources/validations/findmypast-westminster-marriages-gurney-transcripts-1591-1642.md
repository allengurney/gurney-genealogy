# Findmypast Westminster Marriages — selected Gurney-variant transcripts, 1591–1642

**Source ID:** `findmypast-westminster-marriages-gurney-transcripts-1591-1642`

## Scope and method

- Examined 1 August 2026: 13 Findmypast transcript pages selected from the
  first chronological page of an unbounded `lastname=gurn*` Westminster
  Marriages result.
- Retained exact transcript IDs, all displayed event and party fields, and the
  two apparent same-event transcript pairs in the corpus supplement.
- No original-register image was opened. This is transcript/index evidence;
  the transcript's blank parent, age, occupation, and residence fields are not
  negative evidence about the underlying register.
