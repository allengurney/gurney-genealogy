# Classified Record of Weymouth Deaths (Nash copy, 1879) — validation

Source ID: `weymouth-classified-record-deaths-nash`

## Source examined
One opening, **pp. 208–209**, of the manuscript "Classified Record of Weymouth Deaths" (alphabetised by surname; copied 1879 by Gilbert Nash, the deaths companion volume to the births register), via Ancestry, "Massachusetts, U.S., Town and Vital Records, 1620–1988" (Holbrook, collection 2495). Page image (subscription) held local-only at `sources/media/weymouth-classified-record-deaths-nash/_local/weymouth-classified-deaths-p208-209-gurney.jpg`.

## Scope examined
The complete **Gurney** section on p. 208 — six entries, 1719–1794, each giving decedent, relationship/qualifier where present, death date, and (for the last two) age. Adjacent surnames (Greenwood, Grove on p. 208; Hall, Harvey, etc. on p. 209) not transcribed.

## Findings
Transcribed in full on `research/people/g12-richard-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md`. Town-record death dates for three of Richard² Gurney (G12)'s children (Richard³ 1719, Joseph 1739, Mary 1736) and son Zachariah³ (1732). "Richard, Oct. 1719" is the primary entry behind the *History of Weymouth* death-year error (it is the son Richard³, not Richard² who died 1691) — supports the G12 companion's resolved death-year question.

## Limits
- Transcription-level reading of one opening; years/ages read from the upscaled image (clear). No other pages examined.
- **Mary (14 Feb. 1736)** is ambiguous: most likely Richard²'s unmarried daughter Mary (b. 1667) on the absent-"wife of" reading, but the *History of Weymouth* read this Mary as Zachariah³'s wife Mary (Benson). Preserved as a conflict, not resolved.
- "Senior" qualifiers (Zechariah, Joseph) distinguish father from like-named son/nephew; the register itself gives no parentage column, so identifications lean on the birth register and Rigler.
