# Validation — Foster, *Alumni Oxonienses 1500–1714*

- **Source ID:** `foster-alumni-oxonienses-1500-1714`
- **Source:** Joseph Foster, *Alumni Oxonienses: The Members of the University of Oxford, 1500–1714*, 4 vols. (Oxford: Parker & Co., 1891–1892). Internet Archive.

## What was examined
The Oxford alumni register searched for "Warford" (and near spellings) to test whether
the "mr. warford" of the 1627 Gurdon–Winthrop letter — "a master of artes of 6 years
standing" (M.A. c. 1620–21) — was an Oxford graduate.

## Result
No 17th-century Warford. The only Warfordes in Foster are the Elizabethan Bristol men
(William, the Jesuit, d. Valladolid; Henry, matric. 1581), too early to be the 1627
schoolmaster. With Venn's Cambridge negative, this leaves no standard Oxbridge match
under that spelling.

## Scope and limitations
- A compiled register, not a primary matriculation book; a genuinely mis-rendered name
  (Watford / Warforde / Wansford) or a Trinity Dublin degree would fall outside it.
- Does not exhaust the clergy record: the Norwich diocesan institution registers (the
  Clergy of the Church of England Database) remain the route to his institution and the
  advowson the Gurney wardship estate held.
