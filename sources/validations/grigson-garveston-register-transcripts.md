# Validation — Grigson, Garveston parish register transcripts, 1538–1845

**Source.** Francis Grigson, *Garveston parish register transcripts, 1538–1845* (parish
churches of Garvestone and Thuxton); microfilm of the original typescript at the Norfolk
Record Office. FamilySearch catalog koha:586425, British Film 1702622 items 11–12 =
DGS 004115503 (high-reduction 42X film; marriages, baptisms, burials, and indexes).
SourceId: `grigson-garveston-register-transcripts`.

**Examined (2026-07-16).** Catalog record only. The Film/Digital Notes row states "This
item is not currently digitally accessible due to access restrictions" — the signed-in
session exposes index search only, no image viewer (paleography packet 42, blocked).
No page images were transcribed.

**Significance established at catalog level.** Film 1702622 item 11 is the extraction
source behind the IGI's later Garveston rows — including the 2 July 1584 Rivet×Podmer
marriage and the 23 October 1626 Elizabeth Rivet burial — and the transcript continuously
bridges the surviving register's gaps (its burial section ends 1625).

NRO reproduction of the typescript (lead L-242).
