# Validation — NRO NCC will registers (register-Aleyn films, register Jekkys)

**Source:** `nro-ncc-wills-registers` — Norwich Consistory Court will and administration registers, examined via FamilySearch films DGS 008076270 (probate vols. 6–7, 1444–55, register-Aleyn period; 555 images) and DGS 008470476 (register Jekkys, 1464–72; 465 images).

**Portion examined (July 2026):**
- DGS 008076270: film structure mapped (act book ≈ imgs 245–255+, kalendar ≈ imgs 255–280, register text ≈ imgs 281–555 ≈ fols. 1–~250); register-text fo. 19 opening (imgs 300–301) read at image + expert level; act-book fols. 16v–20r (imgs 258–261) read at expert level (paleography packets 46 and 49).
- DGS 008470476: foliation anchored by numeral reads (fo. 211 = img 365); fos. 210v–213r imaged at full resolution and expert-read (paleography packet 51); act-book imgs 19–22 checked (Horne-de-Forncett false positive).

**FTS coverage caveat:** both films are Latin-register "salad" for full-text search, with no reliable name extraction — `gurn*`/`gournay`/`gurnay` scoped probes return 0 on DGS 008470476 despite the Gurnay will being present. Negative FTS results on these films are meaningless; locate entries by foliation walk.

**Unexamined / uncertain:** the great majority of both films' folios; the Jekkys ultima-voluntas day/regnal clause, third-executor line, godson surname, and Swathings vendor await a final Latin edition; the Aleyn act-book co-administrator's place ("D…") unresolved.
