# Validation — FamilySearch, England, Hertfordshire, Parish Registers, 1538–1912

**Source ID:** `fs-england-hertfordshire-parish-registers-1538-1912`
**Collection:** FamilySearch collection 2388825, indexed from Hertfordshire Archives and Local Studies.
**Examined:** 2026-07-26, authenticated session.

## What was examined

The Gurney holding, exact surname, by three sweeps: general, given-name John, and spouse-given-name
Mary. 546 of the collection's 1,437 Gurney records were harvested; 29 fall between 1570 and 1700, at
Bishop's Stortford, Datchworth, Hitchin, Kimpton, North Mimms, Northchurch, Puttenham,
Rickmansworth and Tring. A targeted Berkhamsted sweep was run separately.

## What remains unexamined or uncertain

- **Partial.** 891 of 1,437 Gurney records were not retrieved. FamilySearch's search service ranks
  rather than filters on date, place and spouse parameters and caps unique results at roughly 1,500
  per query, so the harvest is relevance-weighted toward John and Mary rather than exhaustive.
- **Berkhamsted holds no seventeenth-century Gurney record in this collection.** Its four Berkhamsted
  Gurney entries are 1888 and 1927. The Berkhamsted household of eight children 1610–1636 that the
  project carries as Candidate C is not reachable through this collection, and its mothers cannot be
  recovered from it.
- Hertfordshire probate was not searched here. The Prerogative Court of Canterbury sweep is recorded
  under `tna-pcc-probate`; the Hertfordshire archdeaconry and consistory probate indexes were not
  worked this pass.
