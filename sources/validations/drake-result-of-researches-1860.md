# Validation — Drake, *Result of Some Researches among the British Archives* (1860)

- **Source ID:** `drake-result-of-researches-1860`
- **Source:** Samuel G. Drake, *Result of Some Researches among the British Archives for Information Relative to the Founders of New England* (Boston: NEHGS, 1860). Internet Archive item `resultofsomerese00drak`.

## What was examined
Drake's near-primary transcripts of the English emigration/passenger
examinations for the three near-window ships — the **Bevis** certificate (1638),
the **Mary Anne of Yarmouth** examinations (1637), and the **Daniel Cushing
Hingham / Diligent** settler list (1638) — plus an OCR text search of the volume
for Gurney/Gourney/Gurny variants.

## Result
No New England Gurney passenger. The only "Gourney" hit is **William Gourney,
Mayor of Dartmouth**, on an unrelated St Christopher (Caribbean) list, not a New
England voyage. Negative for a documented crossing.

## Scope and limitations
- A transcript of the archival examinations, not the manuscripts themselves.
- OCR is difficult; some names and place-names are damaged or partially
  illegible.
- Provides no named Boston-area manifest for the 1639–mid-1641 window.
