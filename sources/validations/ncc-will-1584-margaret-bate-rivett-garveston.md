# Validation — Will of Margaret (Bate) Rivett of Garveston, widow, 1584

- **Source:** NCC registered-copy will register "Bate" (vol. 83, 1583–84), folio 399, will of Margaret Ryvett of Garveston, widow, dated 5 June 1584 (`ncc-will-1584-margaret-bate-rivett-garveston`).
- **Examined (June 2026, paleography packet 21a):** the will opening (foot of FamilySearch DGS 007904021 viewer image 442) and the continuation (image 443, ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSH9-GQ8V-R`), read from the register images.
- **Portion read:** opening clause and the legatee continuation; not a full polished diplomatic transcription.
- **Findings landed on:** `research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md`; extract in `sources/corpus_supplement/rivett-garveston-maternal-family-2026-06.md`.
- **Key result:** legatees are Wright/Clayton/Raymond/Newton kin plus "Marye Ryvett my daughter in lawe"; **no Francis/Margaret Rivet and no Gurney named** — does not bridge the widow to Margaret Rivet (bp 1586). Companion 1597 Robert Rivett administration (NCC admon act book vol. 8, DGS 008077845, image 204, ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSN6-R9RK-L`) grants administration to widow Agnes Ryvett.
- **Master image:** `sources/media/ncc-wills-registers/_local/bate-reg-fo399-img442-margaret-rivett-will.jpg` (off-GitHub per `_local` convention; FamilySearch viewing terms).
- **Unexamined / uncertain:** full diplomatic transcription; exact calendar day of the 1597 admon (act reads "Dicto die"; marginal *xbr* may indicate December).
