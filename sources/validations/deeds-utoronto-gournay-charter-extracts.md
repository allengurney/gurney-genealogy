# Validation — DEEDS (Toronto) Gournay charter extracts

Source ID: `deeds-utoronto-gournay-charter-extracts`

**What was examined.** The University of Toronto DEEDS database (deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca), July 2026: a systematic full-text variant sweep (`gour*` 29 docs, `gurn*` 46, `gorn*` 237 with ~30 substantive after Gournay-sur-Marne priory noise), followed by full-text pulls of eleven charter pages. Verbatim transcriptions and a sweep index of ~50 further substantive document IDs are preserved at `sources/corpus_supplement/deeds-utoronto-gournay-charter-extracts.md`.

**Method notes.** Search results are client-rendered (browser required); charter pages are server-rendered and WebFetch-readable at `https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/<8-digit id>`. For the Norman acta DEEDS cites SCRIPTA (Université de Caen) as external source and mirrors its texts; "Assigned" dates are editorial, and DEEDS's assignment can differ from SCRIPTA's own record (e.g. 06400021 "assigned 1040" vs sc_151 [1049–1087]).

**What remains unexamined.** Variant stems `gerne*`/`gurnei*` and phrase queries (`de gornaco`); the "alternative spelling" search toggle; the two 1203 Rouen King John originals (05560028/29); the Avranches 1037×1048 act 06081055 (identified, not pulled); direct SCRIPTA `sc_` lookups for most mirrored acts. DEEDS coverage is cartulary-bounded and mostly pre-1300 — negatives are not evidence of record absence.

**Where findings landed.** G32/G33/G34 companions (`research/people/g3{2,3,4}-*.research.md` — Basilia charter attestation, the Jumièges date analysis, the 1112/13 Bec chain, Senex usage, attestation harvest); `research/topics/senior-gournay-baron-line-collateral.md` (Milesent/Broughton 1167, King John acts, Wendover sons clause, attestation table); `research/places/lessingham.md`, `research/places/fordham.md`, `research/places/harpley.md`; G30 companion (1198 fine). Lead trail: L-232 (closed 2026-07-17).
