# Validation — East Dereham manorial court books (FamilySearch films)

**Source.** East Dereham (the Queen's manor, East Dereham with members; NRO DC 12/5),
manorial court books 1623–1852 with index 1623–1820; NRO originals, filmed. Key films:
DGS 004389483 ("Dereham, Norfolk. Court Records 1623–1689" — bundled with Deopham and
Denver material, non-chronologically interleaved) and DGS 004389277 ("Court Records
1689–1820," manor index at item 8). SourceId: `fs-east-dereham-manor-court-books`
(earlier reads cited via the `familysearch-fulltext-search` catch-all; both remain valid).

  22 cards, all reviewed); expert reads of the 10 Oct 1636 "quondam Margarete Gurner"
  surrender (packet 07), the 1697 Matthew Gurney admission (packet 09), and the 1758
  "Michael Gurney" recital; the 1662/1665 Margaret page attempted, unresolved (packet 08);
