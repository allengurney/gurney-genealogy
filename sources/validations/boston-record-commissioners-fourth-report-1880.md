# Boston Record Commissioners, Fourth Report — Dorchester Town Records (1880)

Source ID: `boston-record-commissioners-fourth-report-1880`

## Scope Examined

Full-text sweep of the printed volume (archive.org `fourthreportofre04bost`) for
Gurney-variant forms, 2026-07-01, including the false-friend-prone forms Gornell,
Gornill, Gornehill, Gurnel, Gurnett, Gurnit. The volume's own index and its
editorial footnotes were checked alongside the body text.

## Findings Location

The Gurnell-family identification (John Gurnell rendered under a dozen
spellings; the "Jane Gornell...married John Burge" footnote) is carried as
source_evidence `G13-RI-000143` and negative_result `G13-RI-000144` in
`research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/family/13-colonial-collaterals.md`.

## Limits

OCR/printed-edition text, not the manuscript town book. The volume's coverage
runs into the 1670s only; no later Dorchester-Gurnell trace was pursued beyond
what this and the companion First Church volume (`records-first-church-dorchester-1891`)
carry.
