# Source validation: Pope, The Pioneers of Massachusetts (1900)

**Source ID:** `pope-pioneers-of-massachusetts-1900`

## Examined

Charles Henry Pope, *The Pioneers of Massachusetts, a Descriptive List, Drawn from Records of the Colonies, Towns and Churches, and Other Contemporaneous Documents* (Boston: Charles H. Pope, 1900), Internet Archive item `pioneersofmassac00pope`.

## Scope

- Main entry under head-form "GURNET, GORNET" (print p. ~204; `_djvu.txt` lines ~27264-27280), the John Gurney of Braintree descriptive list.
- Index cross-references "GORNET, see Gurney" and "GORNELL, see Gurnell and Cornell"; the BUNDY and LAVER entries (worked for other topics, not here).

## Validation result

Independent 1900 compiler evidence for the wives-marriages topic. Pope's Braintree John Gurney entry states outright that the printed Braintree town record's "John Cheny, Sen." is "a typographical error" for John Gurney, and reads both the 20 (7) 1661 wife-death line and the 12 (9) 1661 Grizzell (widow of Henry Kibbee) marriage line as the Gurney family. A records-based authority siding with the Torrey/Sprague/TAG reading, independent of the manuscript image evidence.

Also carries a distinct lead not assimilated in this topic: "Signed a petition about the meadows in 1646. [Arch. 45.]" — a Massachusetts Archives vol. 45 (Lands) petition routed to the colonial record classes.

## Limits

Pope is a compiled descriptive list, not the original record; his typo ruling is an editorial judgement, not a re-transcription of the manuscript (the manuscript-image reading is carried separately via `familysearch-fulltext-search`, DGS 007009769). Pope conflates the 1636 John Newgate apprentice with the Braintree John — a conflation the case-file two-Johns de-conflation argues against, and which post-dates Pope 1900.
