# Records of the Suffolk County Court, 1671-1680 (CSM 29-30, 1933)

Source ID: `suffolk-county-court-records-1671-1680-csm-29-30`

## Scope Examined

Printed p. 481 (session c. January 1676/7-July 1677) read in full: the Prise
bastardy-case entry naming "Goodman Gurney of Dorchester" as surety, supplied as
verbatim text 2026-07-01. The court's own general index entry ("Gurney,——, 481")
was checked against the volume's Gornell/Gurnell index entries.

## Findings Location

The surety record and its resolution to the Dorchester Gurnell family are
carried as source_evidence `G13-RI-000143` and negative_result `G13-RI-000144`
in `research/people/_staging/g13-john-gurney/topics/family/13-colonial-collaterals.md`.

## Limits

Only the one printed page (p. 481) has been read; the rest of the two-volume
series has not been swept for other Gurney-form entries. No digitized copy has
been linked in this repo — the p. 481 text was supplied directly from the
printed volume, not pulled from an online scan.
