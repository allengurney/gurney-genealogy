# Validation — L'Estrange/Rye, Calendar of the Freemen of Norwich, 1317–1603

**Source:** `lestrange-rye-norwich-freemen-1317-1603` — L'Estrange (comp.) / Rye (ed.), *Calendar of the Freemen of Norwich from 1317 to 1603* (1888); archive.org Cornell scan `cu31924029785528` (the Google scan `freemennorwich1317` was checked and discarded for dirtier column OCR).

**Portion examined (July 2026):** full alphabetical run swept at Gurn*/Gourn*/mangle level from the downloaded djvu text.

**Result:** exactly two Gurney admissions (Geoffrey, barber, 20 Ric. II = 1396/97; William, tanner, 9 Hen. IV = 1407/08), plus John Gurnell, cordwainer, 5 Edw. VI = 1551 (distinct Gurnell family) and the distinct Garneys gentry surname. No Tudor Gurney freeman. Entries and Rye's completeness caveat extracted verbatim to `sources/corpus_supplement/norwich-freemen-calendar-1317-1603-gurney-entries.md`.

**Limitations:** L'Estrange's transcript printed unchecked against the original roll (Rye's own disclaimer); pre-1450 roll structure means earliest admissions may be under-captured.
