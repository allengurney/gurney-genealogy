# Boston Vital Records 1630–1699 — Ninth Record Commissioners' Report (1883)

Source ID: `boston-record-commissioners-ninth-report-1883`

## Scope examined
Full printed volume of Boston births, baptisms, marriages, and deaths,
1630–1699 (Ninth Report of the Record Commissioners of the City of Boston, 2nd
ed. 1883), read at transcript level from the Internet Archive copy
`reportofrecordco09bost` via a broad Gurney-variant sweep of body and index
(case-sensitive tight net plus case-insensitive net for all-caps index heads),
2026-07-01. Coverage confirmed against the printed name index ("Gurney, 210").

## What it establishes / limits
The register carries no Gurney of John Gurney's own generation. Its single
Gurney entry is the 26 October 1693 marriage of Samuel Gurney and Sarah
Shapley (printed p. 210) — a grandson two generations on, out of the emigrant's
window — so the volume is, for the elder John, a Boston vital-record negative.
Transcript-level read of a printed record commission edition, not an image
read of the Boston town clerk's manuscript. The year-block confirmation (1693)
is dump finding F-R3.4; the negative is dump finding F-R4.
