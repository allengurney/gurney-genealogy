# Early Records of Dedham, Massachusetts, vol. 4 (1894)

Source ID: `early-records-town-of-dedham-vol4-1894`

## Scope Examined

Full-text sweep of the printed volume (archive.org `earlyrecordsded00masgoog`,
Dedham town-meeting records), both a case-sensitive tight net and a
case-insensitive net for Gurn-variant forms, 2026-07-02.

## Findings Location

The 2 January 1670/1 town-meeting entry naming "Goodman Gurney of Dorchester,
a Tanner" as apprentice Thomas Walker's former master is carried, alongside
the Dorchester Gurnell resolution it extends, in
[`research/topics/gurney-false-friends-registry.md`](../../research/topics/gurney-false-friends-registry.md).

## Limits

Printed-edition text only. Only volume 4 was swept; the printed p. 198 entry
is the sole Dedham-side hit — the rest of the volume, and Dedham vols. 1-3,
carry no Gurn-variant of any kind.
