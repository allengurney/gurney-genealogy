# Source validation: Œuvres de Rigord et de Guillaume le Breton (Delaborde, SHF)

**Source ID:** `oeuvres-rigord-guillaume-breton-delaborde`

**Source:** Henri-François Delaborde, ed., *Œuvres de Rigord et de Guillaume le Breton, historiens de Philippe-Auguste*, 2 vols. (Société de l'histoire de France; Paris: Renouard, 1882–1885). Vol. 2 = Internet Archive `uvresderigordetd02rigouoft`.

## Examined scope

Volume 2, read from the Internet Archive DJVU text: Guillaume le Breton's *Philippide* book VI (the Gournay set-piece, verse lines 210–262, printed pp. 160–162) and the volume index/table entries for Gournay and Hugues de Gournay. Targeted passages extracted verbatim to `sources/corpus_supplement/oeuvres-rigord-guillaume-breton-delaborde-vol2-gournay-philippide.md`.

## Findings recorded

- The *Philippide*'s Gournay set-piece is in **book VI** (lines 210–262), not "lib. xi" as transmitted via Daniel Gurney *Record* Part I p. 24. It describes the triple-walled defences, names Hugh de Gournay as commander (VI, 216), and recounts Philip Augustus's 1202 capture by breaching a mill-pond dam to flood the walls (VI, 221). Arthur of Brittany knighted at Gournay is VI, 262.

## Remaining cautions

- The IA OCR carries scan noise (marginal verse-numbers, garbled diacritics); the verbatim extract retains it. Rigord's prose §138 and the *Chronicon* c. 112–113 parallel the account but were not extracted in full.
