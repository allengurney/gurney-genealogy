## Transcription status

The continuous text is a **Nash-assisted** reading: Gilbert Nash's 1885 printed
transcription (Appendix C) was aligned to the manuscript leaves to recover wording
now too faded to read independently. The three Gurney clauses, the owner headings,
the manuscript sequence, and many clearer parcel lines were checked against the
primary images. Original spelling is retained where legible; square brackets mark
supplied or uncertain letters; the surname at each Gurney clause is visible in the
image.

## The three parcels — John Gurney was the original grantee of three distinct lots (~8 acres)

The book is a **possession survey** (Nash dates it 1643): each parcel is listed
under the man who held it when the survey was made, with the memory of who had been
granted it "first." John Gurney is named as the **first grantee** of three separate
parcels, all of which had passed to other men by the time of the survey. The two
East-Field references are **not** the same parcel — they carry entirely different
abutters and lie under two different later owners.

| # | Field / size | Manuscript page (image) | Name form | Later holder | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | East Field, 2 acres | p. 12 (`007009659_00135`, left) | **John Gurny** | Robert Randoll | "first granted to John Gurny" |
| 2 | East Field, 2 acres | p. 23 (`007009659_00140`, right) | **John Gurnie** | Nathaniel Addames | "first giuen to John Gurnie" |
| 3 | Mill Field, 4 acres | p. 31 (`007009659_00144`, right) | **John Gurnie** | Thomas Richardes | "first giuen to John Gurnie" |

Total: **about 8 acres across three parcels** — two 2-acre East-Field lots and one
4-acre Mill-Field lot. (Earlier summaries that read "2 acres East Field + 4 acres
Mill Field" counted only parcels 1 and 3 and dropped parcel 2.)

## Verbatim clauses and abutters

### 1. East Field, 2 acres — under "The land of Robert Randoll" (p. 12)

> The land of Robert Randoll
> Two acres in the East field first granted to John Gurny,
> bounded on the East with the land of Edmond hart, the hiewaie
> on the west, the land of Robert Cooke on the north, and of
> Nathaniel Addames on the south.

- **East:** Edmond Hart · **West:** the highway · **North:** Robert Cooke · **South:** Nathaniel Addames.
- The same Randoll block continues with "Two acres more in East field first giuen to **Edward Bate**" (bounded by Edmond Hart, Robert Cooke, Nathaniel Addames) and "Three acres more … first granted to **James Rogeres**" (bounded by the sea and Nathaniel Addames) — so John's first East-Field lot sat in a tight cluster of Hart / Cooke / Addames / Bate / Rogers parcels.

Confidence: high; "John Gurny" is clear in the image.

### 2. East Field, 2 acres — under "The land of Nathaniel Addames" (p. 23)

> Two acres in the East ffield first giuen to John Gurnie, bounded on the East
> with the land of Jeffery Staple, on the west with the land of Robert Randoll,
> on the north with the Ceder swampe, on the south with the highwaie.

- **East:** Jeffery Staple · **West:** Robert Randoll · **North:** the Cedar swamp · **South:** the highway.
- The surrounding Addames block records other East-Field land assembled from earlier grants to **Matthew Pratt**, the **wife of Thomas Clapp**, and **William Brandon**; an 11¼-acre parcel first given to **Clement Briggs** (abutting Robert Randoll, Aingell Hollard, William Reade); and three acres "giuen him in lieu of a highwaie" (back River, Aingell Hollard, Steephen French).

Confidence: high; surname reads "Gurnie."

### 3. Mill Field, 4 acres — under "The land of Thomas Richardes" (p. 31)

> ffower acres in the mill ffield first giuen to John Gurnie,
> bounded on the East with Nicholas Norton land, on the west
> with Richard Wulinges, on the north with John Gilles, on the
> south with William Brandones.

- **East:** Nicholas Norton('s land) · **West:** Richard Wulinges (Rawlings) · **North:** John Gilles (Gill) · **South:** William Brandones (Brandon).
- The Richardes block places this Mill-Field land near the Hingham boundary: it also describes five acres of fresh meadow "at the begininge of the Hingam boundes" first given to **Mr. Busley** (with Aingell Hollard's lot) and eighteen acres abutting the Hingham bounds, **Mr. Jefferies**, and **Aingell Hollard**.
- Spelling note: "Wulinges," "Gilles," and "Brandones" are retained as written and should not be silently modernized to Rawlings, Gill, or Brandon inside the verbatim quotation.

Confidence: moderate-high; the page is badly faded, but the clause structure and "John Gurnie" remain visible.

## Neighbour and associate set

Every person named on or immediately around John's three parcels:

- **East-Field parcel 1 (→ Randoll):** Robert Randoll, Edmond Hart, Robert Cooke, Nathaniel Addames, Edward Bate, James Rogers; the highway.
- **East-Field parcel 2 (→ Addames):** Nathaniel Addames, Jeffery Staple, Robert Randoll, Matthew Pratt, the wife of Thomas Clapp, William Brandon, Clement Briggs, Aingell Hollard, William Reade, Steephen French; the Cedar swamp, the back River, the highway.
- **Mill-Field parcel 3 (→ Richardes):** Thomas Richardes, Nicholas Norton, Richard Wulinges/Rawlings, John Gilles/Gill, William Brandones/Brandon, Mr. Busley, Mr. Jefferies, Aingell Hollard; the Hingham bounds.

A connecting detail: John's two former East-Field lots ended up with the very men
whose lands adjoined them — Robert Randoll (whose lot bounds parcel 2 on the west)
took parcel 1, and Nathaniel Addames (whose lot bounds parcel 1 on the south) took
parcel 2 — so the two parcels were neighbours within the Randoll / Addames / Cooke /
Hart / Staple cluster around a cedar swamp. **Robert Randoll** and **William
Brandon**, both abutters/successors here, are among the Aylesbury-Vale
Buckinghamshire settlers profiled in
`research/topics/g13-john-gurney-immigration-by-association.md`.

## Dating and structure (what the manuscript does and does not say)

- The land descriptions in this batch occupy manuscript pp. 9–31. Page 9 begins
  mid-series under Edward Bate; no title or date heading survives in the staged
  opening.
- Each Gurney clause says only "first granted" / "first giuen"; **none states the
  year of John's original grant.**
- No surviving heading in this batch dates the property descriptions; the "1643"
  date is Nash's. Manuscript p. 32 appends sales dated in the third month of 1644
  (e.g. "Sould by Thomas Dyer unto Thomas Baylie the 21st of the 3 month 1644"), so
  the survey was compiled no earlier than 1644.
- This batch contains **no 1636 First-Division section** and **no 2 February
  1651–52 great-lot list** — so the separately attested "John Gurney … 16" great-lot
  entry (Nash p. 282) is on later images not supplied here.

## Negative result

No additional Gurny / Gurnie / Gurney occurrence was found across the staged pp.
9–32 beyond the three clauses above. Several leaves are faint, but the owner
headings and the repeated "first giuen" clauses were reviewed across the full run —
a best-effort image negative.
