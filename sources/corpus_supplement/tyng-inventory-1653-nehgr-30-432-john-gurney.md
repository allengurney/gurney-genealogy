# Capt. William Tyng inventory (1653) — John Gurney's 48-acre Braintree leasehold

Abstract of the probate inventory of Capt. William Tyng of Boston, printed in the *New England Historical and Genealogical Register*, vol. 30 (1876), p. 432. The Register abstracts the manuscript inventory at Suffolk County (Massachusetts) Probate Register, vol. viii, p. 62. Source ID: `nehgr-30-432`.

## Verbatim extract (NEHGR 30:432)

> Capt. William Tyng. — Inventory of the Goods and Chattells of Capt William Tyng, made 25. .3. 1653. See Register, vol. viii. p. 62. Houses, warehouses, etc. cattle at the Farme at Brantree called Salters Farme, at Goodman Mattocks, at George Speres Farme; 600 Akers of land at Rockstones Farme, **48 Akers land at Brantree, and Marsh in the possession of John Gurney**; 80 Akers of land at Monoctecott, £16: 30 Akers at Winchester's Neck, 4; 26 Akers vpon the Plaine, £13; 20 Akers of Marsh in Knight Neck, £40. Whole am' of lands, cattle, furniture, etc. 2774-14-04. Appraised by Natha: Duncan, Anthony Stoddard, Wm. Dauis. Plate afterwards mentioned.

"25. .3. 1653" is 25 May 1653 in Old-Style month numbering (March = first month).

## What the source records

The abstract records 48 acres of land and marsh at Braintree "in the possession of John Gurney" as of the 25 May 1653 inventory, enumerated as a separate item from the Tyng "Salters Farme." It is a secondary calendar of a manuscript inventory (Suffolk Probate Register vol. viii, p. 62); it states no lease-start date or terms. A separate secondary study, the National Park Service *Cultural Landscape Report, Adams National Historical Park* (1997), gives the leased tract as 45 acres with a 1647 lease start.

## Related primary material

- *History of Weymouth, Massachusetts* (1923), vol. 4, p. 151 (John Read entry): Read "made an agreement with William Tyng of Boston to carry on Tyng's farm at Mount Wollaston for ten years from 14 Apr. 1639 (Lechford's Note-Book, 94–99)," had a 44-acre Braintree grant in 1640, and married Sarah Lessie of Blyborough, Suffolk.
- *Suffolk Deeds, Liber IV* (the Tyng estate-division indenture of 28 March 1661), which describes one of the two Tyng Braintree messuages as "in the Occupation and by lease in the hands of John Gurney," the other in Gregory Belcher's hands.
