# ROLLCO — Gurney events in companies beyond Drapers and Stationers (Clothworkers, Tallow Chandlers, Mercers), 1573–1653

- Source ID: `rollco-other-companies-gurney-1573-1653`
- Search base: Records of London's Livery Companies Online (ROLLCO), https://www.londonroll.org/search — surname `Gurney`, all companies, 1400–1900 (172 Gurney events total across the 11 ROLLCO partner companies).
- Captured: 2026-06-20 (authenticated session).

This file records the Gurney livery events in the ROLLCO companies **not** already captured in `rollco-drapers-gurney-old-change-cluster.md` and `rollco-stationers-gurney-1613-1626.md`. The Drapers' "Old Change" tailoring cluster (Robert Gurney, and his son John freed by redemption 1623/4, apprenticeship-master 1630) is held in the Drapers file; the 1613 Stationers binding of John Gurney is held in the Stationers file. Those are not repeated here.

> Note on capture: ROLLCO renders results in a table whose per-record links carry query strings that the capture tool strips, so the numeric ROLLCO **event IDs are not recorded below** — each event is identified by year, name, company, role, and any stated occupation/residence. The forename forms are ROLLCO's own index spellings (Latin/abbreviated in the register: *Francus* = Francis, *Thome* = Thomas, *Rici* = Richard, *Edri* = Edward, *Ws* = William[?]).

## Neutral observations

- The 1622 Clothworkers' apprenticeship is the only one of these events that records a paternal **occupation and residence**: **Thomas Gurney, tailor, of Weston Turville, Buckinghamshire**, whose son **Francis** was bound to the Clothworkers' Company at London in 1622. Weston Turville lies in Aylesbury hundred, Buckinghamshire (about four miles south-east of Aylesbury town).
- The 1641 Clothworkers events show a London **Edward Gurney, citizen and haberdasher**, placing a son as a Clothworkers' apprentice, and a separately-bound John Gurney apprentice; the Tallow Chandlers' Ralph/William/Christopher events of 1650 form a single household (Christopher apprenticed to his father); the 1653 Mercers' Thomas and Francis are bound the same year.
- ROLLCO's partner companies do **not** include the Merchant Taylors' Company; Merchant Taylors' apprentices and freedoms are recorded separately (UK Data Service set ukda-9263; see `sources/validations/ukda-9263-mt-apprentices-scott-2024.md`).
