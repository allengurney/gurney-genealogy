## 1. Biographical note "Hugo de Gornaio" — two Hugh de Gournay charters not printed by Daniel Gurney (Anchin abbey, 1176 and 1177), with a Coucy kinship statement

From the "Notes biographiques" section of the Introduction (near IA text line 23356). Delisle records that, in addition to the many Gournay acts Daniel Gurney printed, he flags two further sealed charters of Hugh de Gournay preserved in the Archives du Nord, from copies supplied by M. Guesnon:

```text
Hugo  de  Gornaio,  de  Gurnaio.  —  Sur  ce  baron  et  les  autres  membres  de
sa  famille,  on  doit  consulter  les  deux  beaux  volumes  in-quarto  que  M.  Daniel
Gurney  a  publiés  sous  le  titre  de  The  Record  of  tlie  house  of  Gournay  (Lon-
don,  i8/i8  et  i858).

En  dehors  des  nombreux  actes  publiés  par  Daniel  Gurney,  je  signalerai,
d'après  des  copies  de  M.  Guesnon,  deux  pièces  des  archives  du  Nord,  éma-
nées de  Hugues  de  Gournai  témoin  à  plusieurs  chartes  de  Henri  II.

Charte  scellée  de  «Hugo  de  Gornaio»  datée  de  1176  pour  l'abbaye
d'Anchin,  touchant  les  biens  que  l'abbaye  d'Anchin  possédait  à  «  Noviant  »
(Nouvion-le-Vineux).

Autre  charte  scellée  de«  Hugo,  Dei  permissione  dominus  Gorniaci  »,  touchant
les  mêmes  biens,  datée  de  1  1  77.  Il  cite  dans  cette  seconde  charte  :  «  Radulfus
de  Cociaco,  dominus  et  cognatus  noster,  a  quo  id  totum  quod  ibidem
nostrum  est  in  feodum  tenemus ...»
```

Working translation: *"Hugo de Gornaio, de Gurnaio. — On this baron and the other members of his family, one must consult the two fine quarto volumes M. Daniel Gurney published under the title* The Record of the house of Gournay *(London, 1848 and 1858). Besides the many acts printed by Daniel Gurney, I signal, from copies of M. Guesnon, two pieces from the archives of the Nord, emanating from Hugh de Gournay, witness to several charters of Henry II. A sealed charter of 'Hugo de Gornaio' dated 1176 for the abbey of Anchin, concerning the goods the abbey of Anchin held at 'Noviant' (Nouvion-le-Vineux). Another sealed charter of 'Hugo, by God's permission lord of Gournay,' concerning the same goods, dated 1177. In this second charter he cites: 'Ralph de Coucy, our lord and kinsman, from whom we hold in fief the whole of what is ours there…'"*

Neither Anchin charter is printed in the Recueil; Delisle only notices their existence and quotes the Coucy clause from the 1177 piece. The properties lie at Nouvion-le-Vineux, in the Laonnois near Coucy. The 1177 style "Dei permissione dominus Gorniaci" and the datings place both charters in the tenure of the senior baron then lord of Gournay.

## 2. Norman exchequer roll — "Osbertus de Gornaio mortuus usurarius"

From an appended Norman exchequer-roll extract (near IA text line 20779). Among chattels of deceased persons accounted to the crown:

```text
De  cataillis  Osberti  de  Gornaio  mortui  usurarii,  x solidos.  De  cataillis  Radulfi
Elborc  mortui  usurarii,  c  et  viij  solidos.  Summa  :  xxix  libre.
```

Working translation: *"From the chattels of Osbert de Gournay, deceased usurer, 10 shillings. From the chattels of Ralph Elborc, deceased usurer, 108 shillings. Sum: 29 pounds."*

Osbert de Gournay is otherwise unrecorded in the repo; the entry attests a minor Gournay-named moneylender in Normandy whose forfeited chattels fell to the ducal exchequer.

## 3. Witness appearance — Rouen judgment at the monastery of Saint-Gervais

From the biographical note on Rotrou (bishop of Évreux, later archbishop of Rouen), quoting a judgment rendered at Rouen in the monastery of Saint-Gervais shortly after Rotrou ceased to be bishop of Évreux (near IA text line 26975):

```text
Jugement  prononcé  à  Rouen,  dans  le  monastère  de  Saint-Gervais :
Me  présente,  Rainaldo  de  Sancto  Walerico  justicia  in  curia  existente  plenissima  pluri-
morum  virorum. . . scilicet  Arnulphus  Luxoviensis  episcopus,  Frogerius  Sagiensis  epi-
scopus,  Henricus  abbas  Fescanui,  Hugo  de  Gurnaio,  Godardus  de  Vallibus,  Robertus  de
Freschenes . . .
```

A witness appearance of a Hugh de Gournay in a Rouen court of the 1160s (Rotrou was bishop of Évreux to 1165). One of several such Norman-court subscriptions of the senior baron; the identity (Hugues III's late years or Hugues IV) is not fixed by the extract.
