# Commissary Court of London — Gurney/Gourny testamentary records, 1626–1649 & 1661–1700

Verbatim transcription of the Gurney entries on **page 296** of the printed *Index to the Testamentary Records in the Commissary Court of London (London Division) now preserved in the Guildhall Library, London*, vol. IV (1626–1649 and 1661–1700), ed. for the British Record Society (Marc Fitch). The page columns are **year / register / folio**; "admon." marks an administration, "O.W." an original will.

| Name | Parish / status | Type | Year | Register | Folio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daniel Gurney | St James Garlickhythe | admon. | 1690 | 26 | 61v |
| Miles Gurney | St Sepulchre | admon. | 1636 | 18 | 295v |
| Tho. Gourny | Fulham, Middlesex | admon. | 1676 | 22 | 275v |
| Thomazine Gurney | widow, St Lawrence Jewry | O.W. | 1680 | 37 (also 23) | 227v (also 143v) |
| Wm. Gurney | St D[unstan in the] W[est] | O.W. | 1665 | 32 (also 20) | 109 (also 165) |

These five are the **only** Gurney/Gourny entries on the page, i.e. the complete Gurney holdings of the Commissary Court of London for the volume's range. **No Francis Gurney appears** in the 1626–1649 portion (or anywhere in the volume), so the Commissary Court did not prove a will or grant an administration for any Francis Gurney in the decade of Francis Gurney of St Benet Fink's death (9 January 1646/7).

The Commissary Court of London (London Division) held testamentary jurisdiction over most City-of-London parishes, including St Benet Fink, so a will proved there would be the expected venue for a middling City liveryman; its silence on Francis is therefore a substantive negative rather than a coverage gap.

Source: *Index to the Testamentary Records in the Commissary Court of London (London Division)*, vol. IV, A–G / H–S / T–Z, 1626–1649 and 1661–1700 (British Record Society, vols. 102, 108, 111), p. 296 (surname Gurney/Gourny); digitised copy (Guildhall Library) at the Internet Archive, [`indextotestament0004chur`](https://archive.org/details/indextotestament0004chur).
