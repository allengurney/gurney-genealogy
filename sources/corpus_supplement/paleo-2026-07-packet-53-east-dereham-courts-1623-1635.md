Source ID: `fs-east-dereham-manor-court-books` (East Dereham manor court books, DGS 004389483; accessed via FamilySearch Full-Text Search / film viewer, `familysearch-fulltext-search`). Expert paleographic report, packet 53, 2026-07-19.

Staged review completed 2026-07-19 against `packet-53-east-dereham-courts-1623-1635.md`.

## Scope and method

I reviewed the full staged range `edc-idx1505.jpg` through `edc-idx1624.jpg`, not only the late 1633-1635 run and not only abuttal-style passages. The review used full-leaf contact sheets, page halves, entry-band sheets, header sheets, and targeted enhancement crops. Where a combined entry-band derivative failed to render, I reviewed the corresponding left and right page-half derivatives instead.

The reading target was any visible `Gurney`, `Gurner`, `Gurnay`, or `Gournay` form anywhere on the leaf: headings, margins, homage or jury lists, presentments, admissions, surrenders, fines, death/heir notes, memoranda, and boundary/abuttal clauses. I also watched for the packet's orbit names: Rivett/Ryvett, Podmer/Petman, Elwyn, and Blomefield.

This is not a diplomatic transcription of every entry. It is a full entry-level target-name review, with source-structure notes and finding coordinates.

## Main finding

### Secure Gurney positive

`edc-idx1559.jpg`, right page, folio 25, court of 3 April 1627 (3 Charles I), contains the packet's secure Gurney occurrence. The entry is a Christopher Fuller admission for land at Ellingfield/East Dereham. The relevant boundary clause reads:

> `terram nuper Margerie Gurney vidue`

Meaning: land formerly of Margery Gurney, widow.

This is an abuttal/former-tenure reference, not a Gurney admission, surrender, presentment, fine, homage-list appearance, or death/heir entry. In this entry I did not see a husband forename for Margery/Margaret, nor a recital of the parcel's descent before or after her.

### Negative result for other target occurrences

After full review of `edc-idx1505.jpg` through `edc-idx1624.jpg`, I did not find any other secure `Gurney`, `Gurner`, `Gurnay`, or `Gournay` occurrence in the staged packet.

That negative includes:

- the non-East-Dereham/interleaved front matter and late-17th-century Deopham material in `idx1505-idx1526`;
- the East Dereham 1623 opening material around `idx1529-idx1534`;
- the 1624-1626 court run;
- the 1627 court run before and after the confirmed `idx1559` positive;
- the 1628-1632 run;
- the 1633-1635 run through `idx1624`;
- headings, marginal notes, homage/jury lists, ordinary entries, and boundary clauses.

Several later leaves contain Margaret/Margery/Margareta-style given names, especially in the 1633-1635 run, but the readable surnames/context are not Gurney. The earlier `idx1600` upper-right concern resolves this way: the enhanced sheet shows a Margaret/Margareta-type name in another entry/context, not a secure Gurney occurrence.

The known 1636/1636-37 form `terram quondam Margarete Gurney vidue` is not in packet 53 as staged; it falls outside the `idx1505-idx1624` range.

## Answers to packet questions

1. I found no Gurney admission or surrender in packet 53.
2. The only Margery/Margaret Gurney parcel reference found in this packet is the 3 April 1627 `terram nuper Margerie Gurney vidue` abuttal on `idx1559`. It does not give her husband's forename or a descent chain.
3. The staged range has a clean target-name negative apart from that single 1627 abuttal: all leaves `idx1505-idx1624` were reviewed at entry level, including headings, margins, lists, presentments, fines, admissions, surrenders, and abuttals.
4. Blomefield/Blomfield appears repeatedly in both the non-target front material and the East Dereham court run. Possible Rivet/Ryvet-like forms occur in the 1626-1630 vicinity, but I would not cite them from this pass without a targeted crop. I did not find a clear Podmer/Petman or Elwyn result worth promoting from this packet review.

## Limitations

Some leaves are faint, cramped, crossed, or partially obscured by bleed-through. The negative result is therefore a paleographic visual negative for the target surname forms, not an OCR negative and not a claim that every non-target name on every page has been fully transcribed.
