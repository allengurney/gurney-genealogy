# Bury St Edmunds (St James) parish registers — Gurney sweep (1561–1800)

Durable extract of the film-scoped Full-Text sweep of the Bury St Edmunds, St James parish registers for the surname Gurney, run 2026-06-13 (lead L-96). FamilySearch image group **DGS 007413310** (681 images). Entries are machine-transcribed; classification and dates read from the result cards and record pages. Source ID: `bury-st-james-registers`.

## Headline finding — Bury St Edmunds is cleared as John Gurney-1's natal origin

The sweep returns **no John Gurney household at Bury in the 1630s**, and no baptism that could be the emigrant John Gurney-1 (b. c. 1607–12). The Bury Gurney presence in the relevant period is a single household — **Thomas Gurney and Lidda (Lydia) Broddish**, married at St James c. 1633–35 — which is a full generation too late to have fathered the emigrant. Banks's attribution of John Gurney-1 to Bury St Edmunds therefore finds **no supporting natal household** in these registers, consistent with the reading that Banks tracked an *apprenticeship* placement at Bury rather than a birth (see `research/people/g13-john-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md` and case-file §8.5).

## The Thomas Gurney × Lidda Broddish household

- **Marriage:** Thomas Gurney & Lidda Broddish, St James, c. 1633–35 (image 297).
- **Children (baptisms):**
  - **Juda** Gurny, dau. of Thomas — c. 1633/34 (image 60); also in a 1633/34 communicant list (image 494).
  - **John** "son to Thomas" — registered c. 1653/54 (image 77). The St James register opened 29 September 1653 under the 1653 Civil Registration Act ("Booke for St James Parish … By an Act of Parliament ye 24 of August 1653"), so this entry may register a birth that occurred somewhat earlier; either way it is far too late to be the emigrant.
  - **Thomas** fil. Thomas — c. 1661/62 (image 83).
- **Possible further:** a **Henry** Gurney buried 1658–60 (no parent named, image 511) may be a child dying young.
- **Next generation:** **William** Gurney, son of **John and Elizabeth**, baptized 1665 (image 86) — a separate John × Elizabeth household, most likely the John born c. 1653/54 grown and married.

## Unrelated/earlier Bury Gurneys

- **Bridgett** Gurny, dau. of **William** Gurny — baptized September 1588 (image 23). A distinct Elizabethan household, no traced connection.
- **Mary** Gurny — in a 1620–24 communicant/confirmation list (image 294; name only, no parent).
- 18th-century: Robert Gurny s/o Charles & Sarah (1710s); a Thomas & Ann Gurney household (1760s–70s); plus published-index page-reference cards.

## Negative-result note

Searched the whole digitised St James register run for every Gurn* variant; no John Gurney baptism, marriage, or household in the 1607–1635 window beyond the Thomas household (married c. 1633–35). This is a documented negative for the emigrant-origin question, not merely an absence of search.
