# Suffolk Deeds, Liber XII — Gurnell of Dorchester extracts

Source ID: `suffolk-deeds-liber-12-1902` (proposed)

Source: *Suffolk Deeds, Liber XII* (Boston: Rockwell and Churchill, 1902) — the printed edition of the manuscript Suffolk County (Massachusetts) registry deed book Liber XII, covering conveyances of c. 1681–1684. Internet Archive identifier `suffolkdeeds11suff` (full djvu text). The printed edition preserves the original liber page numbers in running heads and margins.

Liber XII is the only volume of the printed Suffolk Deeds series (Libers I–XIV, 1629–1697) carrying the surname Gurnell; it appears 35 times, all referring to one **Dorchester, Suffolk County** family — **John Gurnell, tanner** (deceased by 1682) and his widow and sole executrix **Jane Gurnell**. The volume's indexes name **Anne, George, John, and Richard Gurnell**. No occurrence is spelled "Gurney"/"Gourney" in the printed text. (Libers XI and earlier carry no Gurnell or Gurney occurrence at all.)

## Deed text (Jane Gurnell, executrix; Lib. XII pp. 308–309)

The 1682 conveyance recites a Dorchester property "formerly belonging unto John Gurnell Tanner deced," and the grantor:

> Know Yee that Jane Gurnell of Dorchester in the County of Suffolke in the Massa[chusetts] … sole Executrix of the last will & Testament of John Gurnell of the aforenamed Town and … County Tanner …

> … that Shee the sd. Jane Gurnell is the proper and right Owner of the sd. bargained [premises] … the proper inheritance in fee simple of her the sd. Jane Gurnell … against … the sd. Jane Gurnell her heires Exec[uto]rs or Assignes …

(OCR renders the first surname occurrence "Gurncll"; the form is "Gurnell" throughout.)

## Index entries (printed indexes of Liber XII)

Grantor / grantee indexes of the printed volume:

> Gurnell, Anne, 308, 309. George, 308, 309. John, 84, 310. Richa[rd] …

> Gurnell, John, 200, 308, 309.

The same family appears in the volume's grantor index with 1682 conveyances by "Gurnell, Jane exrx." and the estate of "John" Gurnell to grantees Joshua Henshaw, John Baker, and Richard Baker (deeds dated 8 May 1682 and 20 December 1682).

## Source notes

- Liber XII (1902 print) is public-domain and page-faithful to the manuscript liber; the original manuscript registry images are separately on FamilySearch.
- The deed-body text is quoted from the archive.org djvu OCR of the printed edition; minor OCR slips (e.g. "Gurncll" for "Gurnell," "deced" for "deceased") are noted but not silently corrected inside the quotation marks.
- The family is consistently styled **of Dorchester** with John's trade given as **tanner**.
