## Bottom line

The visible date at the head of the page is **13 May 1697**. I do not find a secure admission for **Michael Gurney** in the staged image. The clearest Gurney-form on the page is better read as **Mathei/Matthei Gurney** in a property recital near the top of the left page: the property was "lately in the tenure of Matthew Gurney." This is a tenure reference, not a visible Margery-to-Michael admission chain.

The lower left-page recital names **John Castleton senior** and **Dame Margaret Castleton his wife**, then continues to **John Castleton junior** after Dame Margaret Castleton's death. This is probably the source of the machine association with "Margaret," but the surname in that recital is Castleton, not Gurney.

Several nearby lower-page names that can look superficially like Gurney appear instead to be **Wern/Worn** or similar, especially in the right-page parcel sequence. I would not use those as Gurney evidence without an external index or another image.

## Answers to packet questions

### (a) Entry type and date

The visible date is **13 May 1697**. The page reads like a manorial court-book property/admission sequence with parcel descriptions and prior-tenure recitals. For the Gurney target specifically, the visible item is a **tenure reference**: a customary messuage and associated land were lately in Matthew Gurney's tenure.

### (b) Whom does Michael's tenure recite as predecessor?

I do not find a secure **Michael Gurney** entry in the staged image. The clearest Gurney passage reads **Mathei/Matthei Gurney**, apparently Matthew Gurney, and only says the property was lately in his tenure. It does **not** recite Margery/Margaret Gurney as predecessor.

The substantial predecessor chain visible nearby is Castleton: John Castleton senior and Dame Margaret Castleton his wife, then John Castleton junior after Dame Margaret Castleton's death. That should not be treated as a Gurney chain.

### (c) Parcels and abuttals

The Gurney-linked upper-left entry mentions a customary messuage, meadow/pasture, and roughly five acres in several pieces in East Dereham. The immediately visible wording does not yield a clean Moorgate Street/Daynes abuttal for Gurney.

The same page contains many East Dereham abuttals and parcel names, including Moorgate Street, Eastwood Moor, Massingham land, Blomfield, Palmer, Holland, and others, but I would not attach all of those to the Gurney tenure reference without a fuller professional transcription of the whole spread.

## Confidence notes

- `13 May 1697`: high confidence.
- `Mathei/Matthei Gurney`: moderate-high confidence. `Gurney` is the clearest Gurney-form on the page; the first name is much more consistent with Matthew/Mathei than Michael.
- No Margery-to-Michael recital: moderate confidence from the staged image. I do not see it in the visible spread; another page or the FamilySearch text card may have created the "Michael" target.
- `Dna Margaretta ... Castleton`: moderate-high confidence. The surname Castleton is repeated in the passage and the relationship to John Castleton junior is visible.
- Right-page `Wern/Worn` readings: moderate confidence as "not Gurney," lower confidence on the exact surname.
