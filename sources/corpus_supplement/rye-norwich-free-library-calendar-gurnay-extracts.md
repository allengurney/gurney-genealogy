# Walter Rye's calendar of the Norwich Free Library MSS — Gurnay/Gurney/Gurnell extracts

- Source ID: `familysearch-fulltext-search`
- Source: a printed *Calendar of the Topographical and Genealogical Books and MSS. in the Free Library at Norwich* (compiled by Walter Rye), digitised as FamilySearch image group **DGS 004389182** (clean printed OCR; FamilySearch catalogues the film under Norwich deeds). The volume's contents-page lists, among other sections, "**The Gurneys of Norwich, pp. 68–96**" and a calendar of "**The Gawdy Papers** … from the collections of Le Neve and the late D[aniel] Gurney" (3,276 original letters from 1509 relating to the Gawdy, Knyvet, Hobart and allied families).
- Captured: 2026-06-20 (authenticated FTS session).

## A medieval Gurnay co-feoffment at Great Melton, 1463

In a recited descent of land in **Argerysthorp field, Great Melton, Norfolk**, the calendar abstracts a 1463 feoffment (FamilySearch ark `3:1:S3HY-6DQ9-C91`):

> "*… 4 [acres] in Argerysthorp field, Great Melton, formerly of Thomas Chapeleyn. Conveyed by Thomas Kyteman to Maud, widow of John Breton, Thomas Jermyn and Simon Bavet 1463, and by Jermyn and Bavet to **Sir William Calthorp, William Gurnay, Thomas Bachecroft, John Wyndham and Simeon Bachecroft**, with quitclaim of Robert Breton …*"

A William Gurnay (elsewhere in the calendar's index styled "Sir William Gurnay") thus acted in 1463 as a co-feoffee of Great Melton land alongside **Sir William Calthorp** (knight of the Bath, d. 1494) and **John Wyndham**, with the Bachecrofts. Calendar/abstract level; the original deed has not been examined.

## Index of Norwich city Gurney / Gurnell persons

The calendar's alphabetical surname index lists a run of sixteenth- to eighteenth-century **Norwich** Gurney/Gurnell people by parish and date (page references are the calendar's own):

> "*Gurneham 76 — Gurnell (orig. written Gurney) Jno., St. Jno. Sepul[chre], 139 — Gurnell Jno., 1556, St. Jno. Sep., 160 — Gurnell (Miles) (St. Saviours, 1570), 139 — Gurney 157, 418, 440 — Gurney Arthur (abt. 1591), 123 — Gurney Benjn (St. Marg[aret]., 1734), 154 — Gurney Edward (St. Aug[ustine]., 1719), 117 — Gurney Geo. (St. Aug., 1719), 117 — Gurney Geo. (St. Andrews, 1579), 140 — **Gurney al's Gurnell Geo. (St. Peter Mancroft, 1579), 140** …*"

The entry "Gurney alias Gurnell George (St. Peter Mancroft, 1579)" and the parenthetical "Gurnell (orig. written Gurney)" record that **Gurney and Gurnell were interchanged forms of one surname in Norwich city**. The persons indexed are spread across the Norwich parishes of St John Sepulchre, St Saviour, St Margaret, St Augustine, St Andrew, and St Peter Mancroft, c. 1556–1734. Index level; the underlying register/MS entries were not examined.
