# Cummington, Massachusetts — town register of births, page 213 (Gurney entries)

Source ID: `cummington-vital-records`

Working transcription of the Gurney birth entries on page 213 (letter **G**) of the Cummington town clerk's register of births, from the page image supplied via chat (Ancestry, "Massachusetts, U.S., Town and Vital Records, 1620–1988"; the same register indexed by FamilySearch as "Massachusetts, Town Clerk, Vital and Town Records, 1626–2001," ark:/61903/1:1:F4V6-Z38). The entries follow the register's form "[child] son/daughter of [father] & [mother] [surname] born [date]." Readings are faithful to the page; uncertain characters are marked `[?]`.

---

## Children of Amos & Ruth Gurney (in sequence)

- Amos, son of Amos & Ruth Gurney, born Nov. 5th 1792.
- Willard, son of Amos & Ruth Gurney, born March 6th 1794.
- Hannah, daughter of Amos & Ruth Gurney, born May 7th 1795.
- Ruth, daughter of Amos & Ruth Gurney, born Jan. 4th 1800. *(Ancestry index, [collection 2495, record 10709636](https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2495/records/10709636); parents Amos and Ruth Gurney.)*
- Nathan, son of Amos & Ruth Gurney, born April 2nd 1802. *(Ancestry index, [collection 2495, record 10709637](https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/2495/records/10709637); parents Amos and Ruth Gurney.)*

## Willis (entered out of sequence, foot of the section)

After the children of Asa & Molly Gurney (Amos's brother's family) and other later entries, a single line returns to Amos & Ruth's household:

> Willis, son of Amos & Ruth Gurney, born Feb. 23[?] 1793 **(?)**

The "(?)" is the clerk's own mark on the page, written immediately after the year. The entry is out of chronological sequence — placed at the foot of the Gurney section rather than among Willis's siblings — and the flagged year (1793) collides with the in-sequence birth of his brother Amos on 5 Nov. 1792, since the two cannot both be birth children of Ruth ~15 weeks apart. The register therefore records Willis's birth only as a late, self-doubted insertion; it does not settle the year. (The 1796–98 bracket Rigler's 1994 edition prefers falls neatly in the register's gap between Hannah, 1795, and Ruth, 1800.)
