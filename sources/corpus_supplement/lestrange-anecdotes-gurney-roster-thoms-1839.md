# Gurneys in Sir Nicholas Lestrange's "Merry Passages and Jests" (Thoms, 1839)

The Gurney members named in Sir Nicholas Lestrange's anecdote manuscript (BL Harley 6395),
printed by W. J. Thoms as *Anecdotes and Traditions* (Camden Society, o.s. 5, 1839). The
G14 companion previously used only the editor's prefatory notice; this is the roster from the
numbered jests themselves. Internet Archive: `anecdotesandtra00thomgoog`. Source ID:
`thoms-anecdotes-traditions-1839`.

The Gurneys were Lady Lestrange's maternal cousins and more distantly related to Sir Nicholas
himself; Martha, daughter of Sir Edward Lewkenor, married Thomas Gurney, Esq.

## Roster (by anecdote number)

- **Fra. Gurney** (No. 120) — "an uncle of Edward, and a merchant in London" = **Francis
  Gurney G14**, here documented as actively present in the Lestrange / West Barsham orbit
  while a Londoner.
- **Ed. / Ned Gurney** (No. 135) — **Edward Gurney, Esq., of West Barsham (d. 1641)**,
  Francis G14's nephew (grandson-heir of Henry G15).
- **Tho. Gurney** (Nos. 99, 271, 273) — Thomas Gurney, Edward's younger brother.
- **"my couz. Dol. Gurny"** (Nos. 169, 234, 343, 354, 359, 364) — Dorothy Gurney, sister to
  Ned, died single.
- **Ed. Gurney** (No. 30, the mathematician joke) — Edmund Gurney, the rector (Edmund the
  Divine, G14b).

**No "John Gurney" appears anywhere in the anecdotes.**

## Bearing

This documents the pattern of West Barsham Gurney children (Edward, Thomas, Dorothy —
children of Thomas III, Henry G15's eldest son) embedded in an aligned gentry household (the
Lestranges of Hunstanton), with their uncle Francis G14 tied into the same circle. John
Gurney G13, if Francis's son, was these three's first cousin. The absence of John from the
jests is a weak negative (a curated jest collection is not a household census), but it shifts
the search for a fostered/placed John toward documented households rather than this source.
