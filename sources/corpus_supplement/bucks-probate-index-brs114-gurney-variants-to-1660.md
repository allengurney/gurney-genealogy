# Buckinghamshire probate index, Gurney-variant testators to 1660 — full extract

Verbatim transcription of the GURNEY block in the printed index, plus the surrounding cross-references
that establish the block is complete.

**Source.** *Buckinghamshire Probate Records to 1660* — Sir Nathaniel Lloyd, Bruce Hopkins and Hugh
Hanley (eds), **British Record Society vol. 114**, *Index to Buckinghamshire Probate Records 1483–1660,
and Buckinghamshire Peculiars 1420–1660*. Read at page-image level 2026-07-27 through FindMyPast's
*England & Wales Published Wills & Probate Indexes, 1300–1858*, records `OR/BRS/333/0197` (printed
p. 173) and `OR/BRS/333/0198` (printed p. 174). Source ID: `england-wales-published-probate-indexes`.

**Court abbreviations used in the index.** `We` = Archdeaconry of Buckingham wills register (Bucks
Archives D/A/We); `Wf` = the corresponding register series D/A/Wf; `Pec` = a Buckinghamshire peculiar
jurisdiction; `PR` = probate register; `INV` = inventory; `HRO` / `A25` = Hertfordshire Record Office
class holding certain Buckinghamshire inventories; `ORO` = Oxfordshire Record Office. Two figures after
a court letter are volume and folio.

## Cross-references confirming the block is complete

Printed p. 154 carries `GERNEY, see GURNEY` and `GERY, see GEARY`; the volume gathers the Gorney,
Gournye, Gurnie, Gurny(e), Gurneye and Gerny spellings under the single GURNEY heading, as the entries
above show. A separate GARNEY heading returns no Buckinghamshire Gurney-line testator in the window.

## What the block establishes

**Forty-two Gurney-variant probate records survive in the Buckinghamshire courts to 1660.** Nine
testators are named John, and their dates are 1523, 1552, 1554, 1556, 1589, **1592**, **1623**,
**1630** and **1636**.

**A Buckinghamshire Gurney tailoring trade runs across four parishes and eighty-five years.** William
Gurneye of Aylesbury, tailor (1571); Luke Gurneye of Aylesbury, tailor (1591); Thomas Gurney of
Rowsham in Wingrave, tailor (1628); Richard Gurny(e) of Mursley, tailor (1631); Thomas Gurney of
Weston Turville, tailor (1633). Four of the five fall inside the emigrant's own working life, and the
colonial John Gurney of Braintree was himself a tailor.

**The Stewkley Gurneys did not vanish in 1614.** Three Stewkley testators follow: Robert Gurnie,
husbandman, 1618; Robert Gorny/Gerny, husbandman, 1631; Robert Gurney, yeoman, 1651 — the last
rising in status. A John of Stewkley proved in 1592, a generation before the household active
1602–1613.

**The parishes that carry no John Gurney probate at all** are Haddenham, Weston Turville (which has a
Thomas but no John), Hitcham, Amersham (a Thomas of Woodside 1584 only), Ivinghoe, and Bierton after
Henry Gorney of Broughton in 1559.
