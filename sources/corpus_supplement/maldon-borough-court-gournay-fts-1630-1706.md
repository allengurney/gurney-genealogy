## Coverage

The `+Gournay +Maldon` query returns **92 Maldon, Essex court entries**, of which **26 fall before 1700**, running from **1630** to the early eighteenth century (a 1706 court is also present). The `+Gurney +Maldon` spelling returns mostly nineteenth-century Essex voter-lists (a later, unrelated use of the surname). The court-record machine transcripts are dense secretary/court hand and transcribe as salad; the surname is the reliably-indexed token, so forenames, dates, and the substance of each entry require the page image.

> **Recognition note (packet 31):** in this Maldon court hand FamilySearch Full-Text can collapse a manuscript **1676** to **"1636"** — so the manuscript heading must control the date before any identity analysis.

## The 1 December 1630 court — Francis Gournay, gentleman, a juror

Image ark `3:1:9Q97-YSLK-YTV` is a genuine Maldon court baron with view of frankpledge, its date line reading **1 December 1630** (sixth regnal year of Charles I). In the sworn-juror list the target name reads, at high confidence:

> Franciscus Gournay, gent.

This is an office/attendance entry (a sworn juror), not an admission, debt, or presentment against him. The 1630 court therefore records an **adult Francis Gournay, gentleman, resident at Maldon** — chronologically consistent with **Francis Gurney G14** (and not with his son Francis, then about two years old), though the entry itself states no parentage or London identity.

## The image the machine dated "1636" is a 1676 court — the younger Francis as bailiff

Image ark `3:1:9Q97-YSLZ-BQG`, indexed by the machine transcript as 1636, is in fact a **1676** court: its heading reads **1676**, and the opening formula names the borough bailiffs —

> Coram Will[ielm]o Wyseman Milite et Baronetto et Francisco Gournay gent., Ballivis …

— i.e. Sir William Wiseman, knight and baronet, and **Francis Gournay, gentleman**, as bailiffs of Maldon. This is the documented younger **Francis Gurney of Maldon (baptized St Benet Fink 1628; drowned at Maldon 1677)**, holding civic office near the end of his life; it does **not** place Francis G14 at Maldon in 1636.

## Later seventeenth-century cluster

The other pre-1700 "Gournay" Maldon court entries cluster at **1660, 1669, 1670, 1672, 1673, 1675 (three), 1676 (four)** and onward (a 1706 court is also indexed). This 1669–1676 cluster is contemporary with the younger Francis Gournay of Maldon, gentleman, whose Maldon civic career and Chancery suits of 1676–77 are recorded in C. A. Bernau, "Francis Gournay (or Gurney), of Maldon, Essex," *The British Archivist* I.7 (1913) (extract at `sources/corpus_supplement/The_British_Archivist-Unrecorded-Biographies-Francis-Gurney.md`).

Net: the **1630** court is the early, G14-consistent sighting (an adult Francis Gournay gentleman at Maldon a decade before his London children's baptisms end in 1637); the rest of the read material belongs to the younger Francis and his generation.
