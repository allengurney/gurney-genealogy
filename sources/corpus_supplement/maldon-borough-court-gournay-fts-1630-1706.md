# Maldon (Essex) borough court records — "Gournay" entries, FamilySearch Full-Text Search

- Source ID: `familysearch-fulltext-search`
- Collection: Maldon, Essex borough court records (FamilySearch Full-Text Search; "Maldon, Essex, England" court books).
- Query: `+Gournay +Maldon` (and `+Gurney +Maldon`); captured 2026-06-20, authenticated session.
- Full-resolution masters of the two earliest courts downloaded to `sources/media/maldon-borough-court-gournay/_local/` (1630 and 1636 court pages); see that folder's README.

## Coverage

The `+Gournay +Maldon` query returns **92 Maldon, Essex court entries**, of which **26 fall before 1700**, running from **1630** to the early eighteenth century (a 1706 court is also present). The `+Gurney +Maldon` spelling returns mostly nineteenth-century Essex voter-lists (a later, unrelated use of the surname). The court-record machine transcripts are dense secretary/court hand and transcribe as salad; the surname is the reliably-indexed token, so forenames and the substance of each entry require the page image.

## Later seventeenth-century cluster

Further pre-1700 "Gournay" Maldon court entries cluster at **1660, 1669, 1670, 1672, 1673, 1675 (three entries), 1676 (four entries)** and onward (a 1706 court is also indexed). This 1669–1676 cluster is contemporary with Francis Gournay of Maldon, gentleman (baptized St Benet Fink, London, 13 November 1628; found drowned at Maldon 12 August 1677), whose Maldon business activity and Chancery suits of 1676–77 are recorded in C. A. Bernau, "Francis Gournay (or Gurney), of Maldon, Essex," *The British Archivist* I.7 (1913) (extract at `sources/corpus_supplement/The_British_Archivist-Unrecorded-Biographies-Francis-Gurney.md`).

The 1630 and 1636 courts pre-date that Francis (who would have been an infant/child); whichever Gournay they name is therefore an earlier member of the family at Maldon, recoverable only from the images.
