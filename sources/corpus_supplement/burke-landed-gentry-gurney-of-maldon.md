# Burke's *Landed Gentry* — "Gurney of Maldon" pedigree extract

Extract from John Burke and John Bernard Burke, *A Genealogical and Heraldic Dictionary of the Landed Gentry of Great Britain and Ireland* (London: Colburn / Harrison; mid-19th-century editions), the "Gurney" pedigree, consulted via FamilySearch Full-Text Search, June 2026. Source ID: `burke-landed-gentry-gurney`. A **tertiary compiled pedigree — a collector, not a primary authority.**

## Extract (as transcribed by full-text search)
The pedigree sets the Maldon branch within the wider Gurney / de Gournay descent. The relevant fragments, as OCR'd (the dictionary's two-column print interleaves the Maldon entry with the adjacent Keswick pedigree, so the connected wording is approximate):

> FRANCIS GURNEY, of Maldon, in Essex, was 2nd son … b. in 1628. He was a merchant at that place, and one of the bailiffs of the [borough] …

> … [m. a daughter] of Jeremy Browning, of Maldon, and had issue, with other children, JOHN GOURNEY, … his eldest son, b. 1655, who joined the Society of Friends; he established himself as a merchant …

The same pedigree carries the famous Norwich-Quaker / Keswick descent in adjacent lines (Richard Gurney of Keswick married to a daughter of David Barclay; the Hoare, Fowell Buxton, and Cunningham marriages; Sir John Gurney, baron of the exchequer).

## Source caveats
- Tertiary compiled pedigree, OCR via full-text search; word order, punctuation, and the join between fragments are approximate.
- The pedigree links the Maldon line into the Norwich-Quaker banking descent; that linkage is asserted by the compiler and is not shown from primary record in this extract.
