# Rivett family of Garveston, Norfolk — maternal family of John Gurney G13

Evidence dossier for the **Rivet family of Garveston, Norfolk** — the maternal family of
John Gurney G13 through **Margaret Rybett**, first wife of Francis Gurney G14. The Garveston
register carries two candidate baptisms for the 1611 bride: **Margaret Rivet, daughter of
Francis Rivet, 21 May 1586**, and **Margarett Rivet, daughter of Robert Rivet, 1 February
1577/8**. Garveston adjoins East Dereham (≈4 miles), where Francis Gurney settled and where
John was born. Record extracts and the controlling negatives are gathered here; the
analysis, the weighing of the two candidates, and the bearing on John G13 live on
[`research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md`](../../research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md),
with crosslinks from the G13 and G14 companions.

Captured from authenticated FindMyPast, Ancestry, and FamilySearch sessions, June–July 2026.

## The 1586 candidate

**Margaret Rivet, christening 21 May 1586, Garveston, Norfolk — parents: Francis Rivet.**[^fs-margaret]

- Born 1586 ⇒ **age 25 at the 23 September 1611 marriage** to Francis Gurney at St Martin at
  Palace, Norwich (NRO PD 12/1), and dead by ≈1616–17 (≈age 30), consistent with the known
  chronology of Francis G14's first wife.
- A FindMyPast Norfolk-baptisms sweep across spellings returned no other Margaret in the
  c.1583–1593 window (nearest were Aslacton 1609 and Wymondham 1624) and **no Rivett
  baptizing family in Norwich city at all** — but that set omits Garveston itself, and the
  register's earliest book carries the second candidate, **Margarett Rivet, daughter of
  Robert, baptized 1 February 1577/8** (expert-read entry below).
- "Rybett" (the 1611 marriage clerk's spelling) is ordinary variation of Rivet/Rivett
  (v/b, single/double t); see the marriage index entry below. The Garveston family spells
  itself **Rivet**.

This is an index-level christening (parish-register derived; the mother is not named in the
index). Primary documents bearing on the family: Francis Rivet of Garveston's will; the
Garveston parish-register image for the 1586 entry and a c.1616–17 burial; the 1584
Margaret Bate Rivett will (below).

### Garveston parish register — image read of the 1538–1675 register

A visual paleographic pass over the Garveston register images confirms and extends the
index:[^reg-garveston-p19]

- **Margaret Rivet, christened 21 May 1586** — confirmed at image level. The register reads
  *"Margaret Rivet daughter of ffrancis christened the xxjth of May."* The entry names the
  father (Francis) but **does not name the mother**.
- **Elen Rivet, christened 11 April 1593, daughter of Edmund** — the register supplies the
  father the index omits: **Edmund Rivet**, a second household head at Garveston, and plausibly
  the "Edmund Rivett" whose 1613–14 inventory stands in the Garveston Rivett probate cluster
  below.
- **A Rivet child of Robert, christened October 1571** — a partly-legible entry (the child's
  forename reads *Miles / Milius*, the day unresolved) attributing a child to a **Robert Rivet**,
  a third household head, and plausibly the **Robert Rivett of Garveston** whose 1597
  administration appears below.

The parish thus carried **at least three Rivet households** in the parental generation —
**Francis, Edmund, and Robert** — none of whose register entries names a mother. A focused
read of the burial section (1561–1625) found **no Margaret Rivet or Margaret Gurney burial**
c. 1616–17, and **no Gurney/Gurnay entry of any kind** surfaced in the c. 1615–1640 pages.
These are negatives for this visual pass over the local images, not a proof of absence in the
register.

- **Image `4033226_00540`** (year headings *A° Dni 1576 / 1577 / 1578*):
  - right page, under the 1577 heading and above the 1578 heading: *"Margarett Rivet
    daughter of Robt christened first of ffebruarie"* — **1 February 1577/8 old style
    (1 February 1578 new style)**, from the page structure;
  - left page, top line, above the 1576 heading: *"Richard Rivet ye sonne of Robt christened
    ye last of November"* — **30 November 1575**, from page position;
  - also visible: *"George Rivet sonne of John christened tenth of November"* (1576 block);
    *"Thomas Rivet sonne of Wm"* under the 1577 heading (day/month not settled); a probable
    Thomas-household Rivet entry under the 1578 heading (likely the indexed Frances daughter
    of Thomas, 1 April); **no Gurney/Gurnay/Gournay token** on the opening.
- **Image `4033226_00537`** (the *Anno Dni 1569 / 1570* opening): **no Rivet child is
  visible at 24 October 1569** — the visible late-October 1569 entries read as Cross and
  Fryer lines — so the indexed "Pagnell Rivet, 24 Oct 1569, father Thomas" row does not
  resolve against this leaf: the entry, if genuine, lies on an adjacent leaf, or the index

The Ancestry index of the same collection carries the complete Garveston Rivet set for the
first register book (11 primary rows): baptisms Frances 1547 (no parent indexed); Agnes

## Garveston Rivett probate, 1584–1636

The Norfolk wills/probate index records the Garveston Rivetts who left probate. **"Rivett,
Margaret, Widow, of Garveston," will 1584** — a widow's will, predating the 1586 baptism, and
the likely namesake of Margaret Rivet (1586); the parallel UK extracted-probate index renders
her maiden name *Bate* (a Bate–Rivett marriage). Her will survives as **NCC will register Bate,
folio 399** (Norfolk Record Office microfilm MF 66; FamilySearch DGS 007904021, viewer image
442) and is abstracted in **Norfolk Record Society vol. 21**.[^probate-bate-index] **"Rivett,
Robert, of Garveston, Norfolk," administration 1597** — a household head of the same generation
who died intestate; the grant is in the **NCC administration act book 1588–1605** (NRO MF
170–171; FamilySearch DGS 008077845, act books vol. 8, image 204).[^probate-robert] Around
them, in the index, sit mid-Norfolk Rivett wills of John (1585), James (1589), John (1592),
Thomas (1596) and William (1636), with inventories of Edmund Rivett (1613–14) and Katherine
Rivett (1626–27).[^probate-cluster]

### Image reads of the two bridging wills

Both probate documents were read from the register and act-book images.[^bate-will-p21]

**Margaret Ryvett of Garveston, widow — will 5 June 1584.** The will opens at the foot of
viewer image 442: *"In the name of god amen the five daye of June … in the yeare of the raigne
of our Sovereigne Ladie Queene Elizabeth … I Margaret Ryvett of Garveston in the Countie of
Norff widowe …"* The legatee network read from the continuation centres on **Wright, Clayton,
Raymond, and Newton** kin: Thomas Wright (son of Robert Wright) and his wife; Grace Wright
(daughter of Thomas); Robert Raymond and Elizabeth his wife; John, Margaret, Elizabeth, Frances,
and Anthony Clayton (children of the said Elizabeth); Parnell and Edward Newton; and **"Marye
Ryvett my daughter in lawe."** The reviewed pages name **no son or grandson Francis Rivet, no
younger Margaret Rivet, and no Gurney** — so the hoped-for descent from the widow to Margaret
Rivet (bp 1586, daughter of Francis) is **not present in the readable text**. "Marye Ryvett my
daughter in lawe" is the will's one Rivett family-structure clue (a son's widow, or a
step-relationship), not the Francis/Margaret link.

**Robert Ryvett of Garveston — administration 1597.** The act reads: *"Comissa fuit
administratio bonorum Roberti Ryvett nuper de Garveston defuncti Agneti Ryvett vid[uae] relictae
dicti defuncti de bene et fideliter administrando eadem jurat"* — administration of the goods of
Robert Ryvett, late of Garveston, deceased, was granted to **Agnes Ryvett, widow and relict** of
the deceased, sworn to administer faithfully. The short act names **no children** (so neither a
Francis nor a Margaret), but it fixes **Agnes** as Robert's widow — a household anchor for that
Garveston Rivett line. The exact calendar day is not recoverable from the act alone (it reads
*"Dicto die mensis et Anno"*); a marginal *xbr* may point to December 1597.

**No Francis Rivett or Revett probate stands in the index between 1551 and 1770.** The only
early Francis is a 1551 will indexed "Rivett, Francis, Clerk, of Hingham" — a clergyman of a
different parish.[^probate-francis] The collection is an index compiled in association with
the Norfolk Record Office and carries no will images; the texts are at the Record Office or in
the registered copies on FamilySearch.

## The 1611 marriage as indexed

FindMyPast "Norfolk Banns and Marriages" indexes the marriage under the **bride**:
**Margaret Rybett m. "Frans" [Francis Gurney], 1611, Norwich, St Martin At Palace** —
"Rybett" being a one-off clerk rendering (zero other "Rybett" records anywhere in the
index).[^fmp-marriage] This confirms the register is covered in the index and fixes the
spelling problem that had hidden the family under the wrong search term. The primary record
is the St Martin at Palace register, NRO PD 12/1 (`nro-pd-12-1`).

## Richard Ryvett of Gressenhall — the adjacent Rivett presence

**Richard Ryvett married Faith, 1602, at Gressenhall**; their daughter **Alice was baptized
1611 at Gressenhall**.[^fmp-richard] Gressenhall, like Garveston, adjoins East Dereham
(≈3 miles). Richard is roughly Margaret's generation, and he is the documented "Richard
Ryvett of Gressenhall" previously only hypothesized in the case file as the source of John
Gurney's son **Richard**'s name. His origin is the **Richard Rivet baptized 30 November 1575
at Garveston, son of Robert** (expert-read register entry above), which makes him a full
brother of the 1577/8 Margarett and — if Robert and Francis Rivet were brothers — a first
cousin of the 1586 Margaret.

The image-backed Norfolk collection carries his full Gressenhall child sequence, all father
Richard: **Alice, baptized 1 November 1602; William, 30 December 1604; Elizabeth, 19 April
1607; Faithe, 8 October 1609; Alice, 26 January 1611[/12]** (the repeated Alice implies the
first died young; FindMyPast independently indexes the 1609 daughter as "Fay Revett"). No
baptism follows January 1611/12. Later Gressenhall Rivett rows: an Alice Rivett burial,
5 November 1646, and a Katherine Rivett marriage, 15 April 1675.[^gressenhall-rows]

The **will of John Thornton of Gressenhall, husbandman** (dated 16 May 1629, proved 6 February
1630/31 before surrogate Thomas Browne) places a **Richard Ryvett at Gressenhall**: Thornton
makes "my wellbeloved sonne in law Richard Ryvett and … Marian his wife" a legatee, names
Richard Ryvett his sole executor, and Richard marks as a witness. The testator's other children
are a son and heir Edward Thornton (the copyhold tenement in Gressenhall), a daughter Elizabeth
Blade, and a son John Thornton; further legatees are Jane Blade and a grandchild Susan Lowes,
with a foster child Anne [Sitho/Sithe] charged on the township.[^ncc-1630] **Richard Ryvett's
wife is here named Marian (Mary/Marion) Thornton, not Faith** — so Richard Ryvett of Gressenhall
married into the Thornton family (with the Blades as co-in-laws); whether this is a remarriage
after the Faith of the 1602 Gressenhall marriage, or a distinct Richard, is unresolved. The
will is registered in FamilySearch film DGS 008098653, page 1 ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSJ6-9Q4B-D` and
page 2 ark `3:1:3Q9M-CSJ6-9Q4F-M`.

## Will of John Mussett of Garveston, made 28 November 1654, proved November 1660

A Garveston neighbour household, documenting mixed freehold-and-copyhold tenure in the
parish. Machine transcript (raw full-text output — a locator aid whose names and syntax are
unreliable at the character level), key clauses:[^mussett-fts]

> "I John Mussett of Garveston in the County of Nor[folk] … being well stricken in years,
> and not well in Bodie, but of good & perfect remembrance … first [I] Will bequeath unto
> Elizabeth my beloved wife All that … my house and … meadow [and] Pasture, and whatsoever
> … being in Garveston aforesaid **as well freehold as Copy hold** … during the whole term
> of her naturall life, after the death [and] decease of the said Elizabeth my wife [I]
> Will devise give [and] Bequeath them … unto Mary Mussett my daughter to her heires &
> assignes for ever: on Condicon … That she … shall pay … unto Ann my daughter the wife of
> Simon Dayes of Shipdham in the County aforesaid Wheeler … the true whole sume of Twenty
> Pounds …"

Witnesses: Clem Cubitt, the wife of John Bushe, Rich[ard] Shalder. Proved November 1660
before Ro. Thaxton, surrogate. **No Rivett or Gurney is named in the will.**

## Controlling negatives

- **No Margaret Rivett/Revett/Ryvett baptism c.1583–1593 anywhere in Norfolk** in the
  FindMyPast Norfolk Baptisms index — searched exact and by wildcard (R?vett) and under the
  marriage spelling "Rybett" (0 baptisms).[^fmp-bapt-neg] The Norfolk Rivett baptizing
  families in 1540–1620 cluster at **Aslacton, Gressenhall, Garveston, Wickmere/Wolterton,
  King's Lynn (Michael Rivett, children 1568–79), Wymondham (Francis Rivett), Dickleburgh,
  Catfield** — **none in Norwich city**. (The Garveston christenings are absent from this FMP
  Norfolk-FHS set — a coverage gap; they surface only in the IGI-based Ancestry/FamilySearch
  collection. This is why earlier Norfolk-baptisms searches missed Margaret.)
- **No Margaret Gurney burial c.1616–17 in Norfolk** in the FindMyPast Norfolk Burials index
  (all "Margaret Gurney" burials there are 1812+).[^fmp-burial-neg] Her burial remains
  unlocated; the Garveston and East Dereham register images are the next test.
- **No Gurney baptisms at St Martin at Palace** in the index; the Norwich Gurneys present
  (William & Robert families at St Giles / St Lawrence / St Stephen, 1619–29) are a distinct
  group, not Francis G14. So the 1611 venue does not reflect a Gurney parish there either —
  consistent with St Martin at Palace being a neutral/convenient Norwich church rather than
  either family's home parish.[^fmp-gurney-norwich]
- **No pre-1700 printed Gurney–Rivett pedigree, deed, or compiled source** exists in
  FamilySearch Full-Text Search: every Gurney↔Rivett/Revett co-occurrence is 19th–20th
  century (directories, 1832–1953).[^fts-neg] (A 17th-century *manuscript* deed would
  transcribe as secretary-hand salad and would not surface via co-occurrence, so this rules
  out a ready-made *printed* pedigree, not a manuscript deed.) A second full-text pass scoped
  to the 1600s (century filter) confirms this: the only pre-1700 Norfolk-Legal documents that
  co-occur a Gurney variant with Garveston or its Mitford-Hundred neighbours are manorial-court
  transcriptions reading as word-salad — a 1648 Whinburgh court ("Henry Gurnip," with
  Garveston and Mattishall), a 1677 Garveston/Rudston court tenant-list ("Brampton Gurn…"), and
  a 1640 Denver court ("Gurney" buried in court-hand) — all post-dating John's emigration and
  none yielding a readable, promotable Gurney–Rivett link.[^fts-neg]
- **No Gurney left probate at Garveston** in the Norfolk wills/probate index: a Gurney +
  Garveston keyword search re-ranks nothing (whereas the same keyword lifts the Rivett widow
  and Robert of Garveston to the top), consistent with Francis Gurney marrying *into* Garveston
  rather than residing there. The county's John-era Gurney probate sits elsewhere — John Gurney
  of Ashwellthorpe (administration 1615), Wolstan Gurney of Aylsham (administration 1607), and
  John Gurney, yeoman of Earsham (wills 1630 and 1639, inventory 1630–31) — with Henry Gurnay,
  Esq., of Great Ellingham (will 1623, the West Barsham line); none at East Dereham.[^probate-gurney]

## Method note (reusable)

- **FindMyPast authenticated URL-param search.** The results URL accepts parameters directly:
  `https://www.findmypast.co.uk/search/results?datasetname=<set>&sid=103&lastname=<name>&yearofbirth=<YYYY>&yearofbirth_offset=<N>` — far more reliable than the autocomplete form
  under automation. Record-set slugs: `norfolk+baptisms`, `norfolk+banns+and+marriages`,
  `norfolk+burials`. Wildcards work in the name param (`r%3Fvett` = R?vett). The place
  field binds only via the form autocomplete, not free text. A year with **±40** "give or
  take" is the practical way to bound a surname sweep.
- **Spelling discipline.** The marriage clerk's "Rybett" is not the family's baptismal
  spelling (Rivet); the Suffolk gentry line is "Ryvett"; FMP-indexed Norfolk baptisms use
  "Rivett/Revett." Search all of Rivet/Rivett/Revett/Ryvett/Rybett.
- **Coverage gap.** The FMP "Norfolk Baptisms" (Norfolk FHS) set omits some parishes
  (Garveston; Francis G14's own East Dereham children); the IGI-based Ancestry/FamilySearch
  "Births and Christenings 1538–1975" set covers them. Cross-check both.
