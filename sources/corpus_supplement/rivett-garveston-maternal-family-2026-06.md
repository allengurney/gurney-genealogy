# Rivett family of Garveston, Norfolk — maternal family of John Gurney G13

Evidence dossier behind the June 2026 identification of **Margaret Rybett** (first wife of
Francis Gurney G14, mother of John Gurney G13) as **Margaret Rivet, daughter of Francis
Rivet of Garveston, Norfolk, baptized 21 May 1586**. Garveston adjoins East Dereham (≈4
miles), where Francis Gurney settled and where John was born. Record extracts and the
controlling negatives are gathered here; the analysis and the bearing on John G13 live on
[`research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md`](../../research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md),
with crosslinks from the G13 and G14 companions.

Captured from authenticated FindMyPast, Ancestry, and FamilySearch sessions, June 2026.

## The identification

**Margaret Rivet, christening 21 May 1586, Garveston, Norfolk — parents: Francis Rivet.**[^fs-margaret]

- Born 1586 ⇒ **age 25 at the 23 September 1611 marriage** to Francis Gurney at St Martin at
  Palace, Norwich (NRO PD 12/1), and dead by ≈1616–17 (≈age 30), consistent with the known
  chronology of Francis G14's first wife.
- She is the **only** Margaret Rivet/Rivett/Ryvett of the right age in the region: an
  exhaustive FindMyPast Norfolk-baptisms sweep across spellings returned **no** Margaret in
  the c.1583–1593 window (nearest were Aslacton 1609 and Wymondham 1624, both too late), and
  **no Rivett baptizing family in Norwich city at all**.
- "Rybett" (the 1611 marriage clerk's spelling) is ordinary variation of Rivet/Rivett
  (v/b, single/double t); see the marriage index entry below. The Garveston family spells
  itself **Rivet**.

Status: **Probable, not proven** — this is an index-level christening (parish-register
derived; mother not named). Confirming pulls: Francis Rivet of Garveston's will; the
Garveston parish-register image for the 1586 entry and a c.1616–17 burial; the 1584
Margaret Bate Rivett will (below).

## Margaret Bate Rivett, probate 1584, Garveston

**"Rivett, Margaret Bate," death 1584, Garveston, Norfolk** — an entry in the Norfolk wills /
probate index (and UK extracted probate records).[^probate-index] Predating the 1586
baptism, she is the likely **namesake** of Margaret Rivet (1586); "Bate" reads as a maiden
name (a Bate–Rivett marriage). The will, if pulled, should name the family head and tie
Francis Rivet into the household. Garveston probate is the most direct unpulled lead to
Margaret's grandparents' generation.

## The 1611 marriage as indexed

FindMyPast "Norfolk Banns and Marriages" indexes the marriage under the **bride**:
**Margaret Rybett m. "Frans" [Francis Gurney], 1611, Norwich, St Martin At Palace** —
"Rybett" being a one-off clerk rendering (zero other "Rybett" records anywhere in the
index).[^fmp-marriage] This confirms the register is covered in the index and fixes the
spelling problem that had hidden the family under the wrong search term. The primary record
is the St Martin at Palace register, NRO PD 12/1 (`nro-pd-12-1`).

## Richard Ryvett of Gressenhall — the adjacent Rivett presence

**Richard Ryvett married Faith, 1602, at Gressenhall**; their daughter **Alice was baptized
1611 at Gressenhall**.[^fmp-richard] Gressenhall, like Garveston, adjoins East Dereham
(≈3 miles). Richard is roughly Margaret's generation (a plausible brother or close cousin),
and he is the documented "Richard Ryvett of Gressenhall" previously only hypothesized in the
case file as the source of John Gurney's son **Richard**'s name. His own origin and his
wife Faith's surname remain to be pulled.

## Controlling negatives

- **No Margaret Rivett/Revett/Ryvett baptism c.1583–1593 anywhere in Norfolk** in the
  FindMyPast Norfolk Baptisms index — searched exact and by wildcard (R?vett) and under the
  marriage spelling "Rybett" (0 baptisms).[^fmp-bapt-neg] The Norfolk Rivett baptizing
  families in 1540–1620 cluster at **Aslacton, Gressenhall, Garveston, Wickmere/Wolterton,
  King's Lynn (Michael Rivett, children 1568–79), Wymondham (Francis Rivett), Dickleburgh,
  Catfield** — **none in Norwich city**. (The Garveston christenings are absent from this FMP
  Norfolk-FHS set — a coverage gap; they surface only in the IGI-based Ancestry/FamilySearch
  collection. This is why earlier Norfolk-baptisms searches missed Margaret.)
- **No Margaret Gurney burial c.1616–17 in Norfolk** in the FindMyPast Norfolk Burials index
  (all "Margaret Gurney" burials there are 1812+).[^fmp-burial-neg] Her burial remains
  unlocated; the Garveston and East Dereham register images are the next test.
- **No Gurney baptisms at St Martin at Palace** in the index; the Norwich Gurneys present
  (William & Robert families at St Giles / St Lawrence / St Stephen, 1619–29) are a distinct
  group, not Francis G14. So the 1611 venue does not reflect a Gurney parish there either —
  consistent with St Martin at Palace being a neutral/convenient Norwich church rather than
  either family's home parish.[^fmp-gurney-norwich]
- **No pre-1700 printed Gurney–Rivett pedigree, deed, or compiled source** exists in
  FamilySearch Full-Text Search: every Gurney↔Rivett/Revett co-occurrence is 19th–20th
  century (directories, 1832–1953).[^fts-neg] (A 17th-century *manuscript* deed would
  transcribe as secretary-hand salad and would not surface via co-occurrence, so this rules
  out a ready-made *printed* pedigree, not a manuscript deed.)

## Method note (reusable)

- **FindMyPast authenticated URL-param search.** The results URL accepts parameters directly:
  `https://www.findmypast.co.uk/search/results?datasetname=<set>&sid=103&lastname=<name>&yearofbirth=<YYYY>&yearofbirth_offset=<N>` — far more reliable than the autocomplete form
  under automation. Record-set slugs: `norfolk+baptisms`, `norfolk+banns+and+marriages`,
  `norfolk+burials`. Wildcards work in the name param (`r%3Fvett` = R?vett). The place
  field binds only via the form autocomplete, not free text. A year with **±40** "give or
  take" is the practical way to bound a surname sweep.
- **Spelling discipline.** The marriage clerk's "Rybett" is not the family's baptismal
  spelling (Rivet); the Suffolk gentry line is "Ryvett"; FMP-indexed Norfolk baptisms use
  "Rivett/Revett." Search all of Rivet/Rivett/Revett/Ryvett/Rybett.
- **Coverage gap.** The FMP "Norfolk Baptisms" (Norfolk FHS) set omits some parishes
  (Garveston; Francis G14's own East Dereham children); the IGI-based Ancestry/FamilySearch
  "Births and Christenings 1538–1975" set covers them. Cross-check both.
