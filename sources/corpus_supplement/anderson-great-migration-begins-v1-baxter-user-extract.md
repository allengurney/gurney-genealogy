## Extracted research value

The user-supplied image crop from the Gregory Baxter profile shows John Gurney in two 1659 probate-administration roles.

- On 19 June 1659, Gregory Baxter voided the bequest to "my son Dearing" and gave that land to his son John.
- Baxter's will was witnessed by John Gurney, Moses Payne, and Richard Brackett. Payne and Brackett also witnessed the codicil.
- The will was sworn 14 [sic] June 1659; Anderson notes this should probably be 24 June 1659, citing Suffolk Probate Records 1:323.
- The inventory was taken 7 July 1659 by John Gurney, Moses Paine, and Edmund Quinsey.
- The inventory totaled 417 pounds 19 shillings. Of that, 315 pounds was real estate: dwelling house, barn, orchard, and 2 1/2 acres of pasture ground valued at 70 pounds; twenty acres of marsh at 120 pounds; twenty-four acres of upland at 40 pounds; six acres of plowland at 25 pounds; and eighty acres of woodland at 60 pounds.
- The inventory was sworn by Margaret Baxter, widow of Gregory Baxter, and John Baxter, their son, citing Suffolk Probate Records 3:146.
