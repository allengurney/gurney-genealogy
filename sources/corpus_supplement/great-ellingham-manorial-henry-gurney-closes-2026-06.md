# Great Ellingham manorial court rolls — Henry Gurney Esq.'s closes (boundary recitals)

Verbatim extracts from the **Buckenham manorial court rolls** ("Lathes, Close, Castle and
Buckenham Priory, 1595–1847"), which carried copyhold business for land in **Great Ellingham
(Ellingham Magna)**, the parish where **Henry Gurney, Esquire** (of West Barsham and Great
Ellingham; died 1621×1623) held the family's Lovell-inherited estate. The court rolls preserve
his landholding as a recurring **boundary abuttal** — a "formerly of Henry Gurney Esqr" parcel —
in admissions recorded long after the Gurney estate had passed out of the family. Captured from
the FamilySearch Full-Text collection "England, Norfolk, Legal, from 1081 to 1925," films
**DGS 004389549** and **DGS 004389550**, June 2026.[^src]

These particular admissions are clean-OCR English entries of the 18th–early-19th century
(the court rolls run to 1847); the boundary clauses recite older descents and are quoted as
they read. The Latin entries elsewhere in the same series transcribe as court-hand salad and are
not quoted here.

## The Tenement Bovells parcel — "formerly of Henry Gurney Esqr"

A copyhold messuage of the **Tenement Bovells** (also written *Hovells*) with one acre in its
croft, in Great Ellingham, is repeatedly bounded on the south by land "formerly of Henry Gurney
Esqr":

> "…One Messuage Copyhold built of the Tenement Bovells with One [ac]re of Copyhold land in the
> croft of the same Messuage Situate lying and being in **Great Ellingham between the lands
> formerly of Henry Gurney Esqr late of Robert Morley and afterwards of [Hannah] Spiall** on the
> part of the South and the land late of William Wright since of Roger Se[t]h on the part of the
> North and ab[b]utting upon the co[mmon] Pasture of Great Ellingham aforesaid called **Town
> Green** towards the East and upon the Kings highway and the head Meer towards the West…"
> — admission of 1751 (ark `3:1:S3HT-DW22-RD`); the same boundary recurs verbatim in the
> admissions of 1813 (ark `3:1:S3HT-D474-YHZ`, "Hannah Sprall") and 1814 (ark `3:1:S3HT-D474-RBD`).[^src]

The descent of that south-bounding parcel is therefore: **Henry Gurney Esq. → Robert Morley →
Hannah Sprall/Spiall** — i.e. on the dispersal of the Gurney estate the land fell to the Morleys
(the chief lords of the manor) and thence to the Sprall/Snell family.

## "Lay Close" — "formerly of Henry Gurney Esqr late of Roger Potts Bart"

A second Gurney parcel, a named close, is recited in the Congerfield part of the parish:

> "…and abutting upon **a close formerly of Henry Gurney Esqr late of Roger Potts Bart: called
> Lay Close** towards the South…"
> — admission of 1787 (ark `3:1:S3HT-D474-1LL`); the close also appears in the 1727 court
> (ark `3:1:S3HT-DW2K-ZY`, "abbutteth… [land of] Henrie Gurney… [Roger] Pott[s]… vocat Lay
> Close").[^src]

Here the close called **Lay Close** descended **Henry Gurney Esq. → Sir Roger Potts, Baronet**.

## Notes

- The "Henry Gurney Esquire" of these abuttals is the family's last lord of Great Ellingham,
  consistent with the manor having passed from the Gurneys after the early 17th century (the
  parcels are styled "**formerly** of Henry Gurney Esqr"). No intervening Gurney holder is named
  between him and a later **Joseph Gurney**, who was admitted to a Great Ellingham copyhold in
  this same series on 5 November 1788 (arks `3:1:S3HT-DW2V-GB`, `3:1:S3HT-D474-B19`,
  `3:1:S3HT-D474-YRL`) — a distinct, later holder.
- The chief-lordship marker throughout the Ellingham Magna entries is **Morley** ("Robert
  Morley," "Anne Morley"), the family of whom both the Gurney manor of Great Ellingham and the
  Gurney manor of Hingham were held.
- "Town Green," the "Head Meer," and "Congerfield" are the local Great Ellingham topographic
  anchors for the Gurney parcels.
