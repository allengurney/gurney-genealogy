# Missenden Abbey cartulary — Dammartin charters (Jenkins ed. 1938), full texts via the DEEDS database

Full Latin texts of the Dammartin charters from J. G. Jenkins, ed., *The Cartulary of Missenden Abbey*, vol. 1 (Buckinghamshire Record Society [Records Branch] 2, 1938; from BL Harley MS 3688; Davis 670), as transcribed in the University of Toronto DEEDS database (Documents of Early England Data Set, deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca, cartulary 0013). Retrieved July 2026. DEEDS assigns its dates from Jenkins's edition.

These are the charters Douglas Richardson cited (soc.genealogy.medieval, 11 September 2002) as "Jenkins pp. 70–75" — the witness lists could not previously be read (HathiTrust holds the volume search-only).

## 1. Manasser de Dammartin's grant of the hide at Missenden, with Walter de Gurnay as witness (DEEDS 00130071; assigned date 1150)

URL: https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/00130071

> Manasser de Dammartin omnibus hominibus et amicis suis salutem sciatis quod ego consilio et concessione Galiene uxoris mee et Bartholomei filii et heredis mei dedi et concessi ecclesie Sancte Marie de Messend et canonicis ibidem deo seruientibus terram quam habebam apud Messend que Hida appellatur illam scilicet quam Hunfridus de me tenuit et ipse in manu mea reddidit cum omni iure quod in eadem terra habuit et liberam ab omni calumpnia clamauit [...] Teste Theobaldo archiepiscopo Cantuar Hilario episcopo Cicestr Willelmo episcopo Norwic Wigodo priore de Oseneia et Hardingo sub priore Radulfo canonico Willelmo abbate de Lilehul Willelmo priore eius Magistro Roberto de Burenham Rogero clerico Willelmo clerico de Cyncg Ricardo clerico Graelendo de Tani Henrico des Escalieres **Waltero de Gurnay** Manassero nepote meo Radulpho filio Hunfredi

(Full text on the DEEDS page; the ellipsis above omits only the internal rent and warranty clauses, which concern the hide's 20s. rent, the hidage/danegeld reservation, and the settlement with Rainer's wife.)

**What it establishes.**
- Walter signs **"Waltero de Gurnay"** — the one document in which Walter's own name is recorded outside the 1166 exchequer return, and the anchor for "living 1150."
- He witnesses with the consent-parties being **Galiena** (Manasser's wife) and **Bartholomew, son and heir** — the same family the 1166 carta and the later Dammartin descent documents show.
- The witness list is headed by **Theobald, archbishop of Canterbury (1139–61), Hilary, bishop of Chichester (1147–69), and William [Turbe], bishop of Norwich (1146/7–1174)** — an unusually senior bench for a one-hide grant, consistent with the charter being executed at a major assembly; the episcopal dates independently bracket the charter to 1147×1161, enclosing Jenkins's assigned 1150.
- Walter stands in the lay tail with **Graelend de Tany** and **Henry des Escalieres**, immediately before "Manasser my nephew" — placement among the grantor's familiares (household connections), consistent with Walter as a Dammartin tenant by this date.
- The grant itself is the hide later confirmed to Missenden by Henry II: "Ex dono Manasseris de Dammartin j hidam apud Messend" (DEEDS 00130006, royal confirmation, 1155×1166).

## 2. Manasser, Galiena, and their sons confirm the hide arrangement — William de Gournay as witness (DEEDS 00130070; assigned date 1150–1178)

URL: https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/00130070

> Manassir de Dommartin et Galiena sponsa eius omnibus hominibus suis et amicis salutem Sciatis nos et **Bartholomeum filium nostrum et heredem et Odonem et Hamonem et Willelmum filios nostros** cum ceteris filiis nostris concessisse [...] Hiis testibus magistro Hamone cancellario ecclesie Nicol Alberico de Dommartin Willelmo de Dommartin Alano de Dommartin Roberto de Valoniis Graalen de Taneia Willelmo Torel **Willelmo de Gornaio** Nicholao decano Roberto filio Roberti Willelmo filio Willelmi Hugone de Hou Raueningo de Messendene Reynaldo filio suo

(The omitted middle regulates the canons' tenure of the hide under Thomas and Mabilia and converts the quarter-fee service owed from the tenure.)

**What it establishes.**
- A generation on from no. 1, the witness in the same Dammartin context is **"Willelmo de Gornaio" — William de Gournay**, matching the 1166 carta's statement that "now William his son holds that part." A previously unrecorded attestation of William (G30) inside the Dammartin affinity.
- The consent clause names Manasser and Galiena's sons **Bartholomew (heir), Odo, Hamo, and William, "with our other sons"** — adding Odo and Hamo to the known sons (Bartholomew and William were already documented from the Hatton Book of Seals notes).
- Witnesses include three further Dammartins (Aubrey, William, Alan) and again Graelend de Tany — the same circle in which Walter had stood.

## 3. Galiena (II) de Dammartin's quitclaim, 42 Henry III (DEEDS 00130072; internal regnal date 1257–58)

URL: https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/00130072

> Sciant presentes et futuri quod ego **Galiena de Dammartin filia Willelmi de Dammartin que fui uxor Ernulphi de Maundeuilla domini de Mimmes** dedi et concessi et quietos clamaui [...] assensu et consensu **domini Petri de Burgate filii mei et heredis** x solidos annui redditus [...] De quadam terra que **Hyda Hunfridi** appellatur apud eandem Messendenam [...] Facta autem fuit hec carta anno regni regis Henrici filii regi Iohannis quadragesimo secundo

**What it establishes.**
- The younger Galiena styles herself in her widowhood ("in ligia uidiutate") **daughter of William de Dammartin and widow of Ernulph [Ernald] de Mandeville, lord of [South] Mimms**, acting with her son and heir Peter de Burgate — a first-person confirmation of the descent chain assembled from the Hatton Book of Seals editorial notes (William de Dammartin d. 1195 → Galiena II → Mandeville marriage), and proof she was living 1257/8.
- The land is still "the hide of Humphrey" ("Hyda Hunfridi") — the same hide Manasser granted in no. 1, closing a 107-year documentary loop on one parcel.
