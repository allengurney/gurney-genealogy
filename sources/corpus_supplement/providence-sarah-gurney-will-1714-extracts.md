## The will, 31 March 1714

FamilySearch film 007717858, image 412, [ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9B8-GSPG-8](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C9B8-GSPG-8?view=fullText):

> …as my last Will and Testament; first I bequeath my spirit to God that gave it me, and my body to the Earth to be decently buried at the discression of my Executor here after named; 2 I give to my loving son **Zachariah ffield** five shillings to be paid by my Executor in one yeare after my decease, 3 I Give to my loving son **John ffield** five shillings …, 4th I give to my loving son **James ffield** five shillings to be paid by my Executor after my decease, **if he live to come home**, 5th I give to my loving son **Daniell ffield** five shillings …, 6th I give to my loving daughter **Sarah field** all my brass and Pewter & Iron vessells, and Bedding and other utensells for house keeping. 7th I order and appoint my loving son **Joseph ffield** my Executor to execute this my last will & Testament, to whome I give all my Cattell sheepe & swine & working Tooles, they being the product of his Care & dilligence; and in Confirmation of this my last Will & Testament I have hereunto set my hand & seale this last day of March in the yeare of our Lord 1714 and thirteenth year of the Reign of our sovereign Lady Anne by the Grace of God Queene of Great Brittan;
>
> Signed and sealed & declared to be her last will & Testament in the presence of **John Gurney** [and another] — his mark — **Sarah X Gurney her mark**
>
> Aprill the last day 1714: **John Gurney the husband of the above named Sarah Gurney personally appeared before the Town Councill of Prov[idence]** — William Hopkins, Josiah Thornton…

## Reading notes

- All five sons and the daughter bear the surname **Field** — Sarah's children by her Field marriage; no Gurney children are named.
- **Her husband John Gurney survived her** ("John Gurney the husband of the above named Sarah Gurney personally appeared," 30 April 1714) and took nothing under the will; he both witnessed the will and co-appraised the inventory, each time **by mark**.
- "If he live to come home" (son James) suggests a son at sea or otherwise abroad.
- Josiah Thornton (co-appraiser, council member) is the likely source of Torrey's "Sarah (Thornton), widow" association — to be checked against Torrey's actual entry.
