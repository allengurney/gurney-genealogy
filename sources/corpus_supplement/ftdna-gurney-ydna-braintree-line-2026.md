## Source caveats
- Terminal and parent branches have very few testers; "earliest known origin" countries are self-reported; these branches predate surnames. Country and project signals at this resolution are indicative, not determinative.
- Individual testers' earliest-known-ancestor and subclade details beyond the public summary (e.g., kit 365744's lineage) are visible only to the kit owner / project administrator.
