# Will of John Thornton of Gressenhall, Norfolk, husbandman — 1629 (proved 1630/31)

Working image-read transcription of the Norwich Consistory Court registered-copy will of **John Thornton of Gressenhall, husbandman**, dated **16 May 1629** and proved **6 February 1630/31** before Thomas Browne, clerk, surrogate. The will is the document that places **Richard Ryvett at Gressenhall** as Thornton's son-in-law and sole executor — captured here because Richard Ryvett of Gressenhall is an adjacent figure to the Garveston Rivetts (maternal family of John Gurney G13).

## Page 1 (`9Q4B-D`) — opening and first bequests

The left page carries the end of another will and the will of Margaret Raven; the Thornton will begins on the right page.

> In the name of God Amen. The xvjth day of May in the fifth yeare of the raigne of our soveraigne Lord Charles by the grace of God of England, Scotland, ffrance and Ireland King, defender of the faith &c, Anno Domini 1629, I John Thornton of Gressengell in the Countie of Norff[olk], husbandman, being sicke in body yet of good and perfect remembrance, thankes be given to Almighty God, revoking all former wills by me made, doe ordaine, make and declare this my last will & testament in manner and forme following.

Religious and burial clauses follow: burial in the churchyard of Gressenhall; **2s. 6d. toward the reparations of the parish church of Gressenhall**, to be paid within one month after the testator's decease; and **2s. 6d. to the poor people of Gressenhall**, divided at the executor's discretion.

First property clause:

> Item I doe give and bequeath unto Edward Thornton my sonne my copyhold tenement wherein I now dwell with the appurtenances and all the land to the same belonging, situate, lying, and being in Gressengell aforesaid …

The clause excepts or preserves a limited copyhold interest connected with land lately surrendered to **Thomas Blade and Elizabeth his wife** and their heirs (exact legal wording uncertain).

## Page 2 (`9Q4F-M`) — conditions, children, Rivett clause, foster child, executor, witnesses, probate

The continuation imposes payment conditions on Edward Thornton and his heirs/assigns toward the testator's other children.

> First I doe give and bequeath unto my daughter Elizabeth Blade, her executors and administrators, the sum of five pounds of lawfull money of England, to be paid by my sonne Edward his heirs or assigns …

> Item I give and bequeath unto my wellbeloved sonne in law Richard Ryvett and to Marian his wife, their executors and administrators, in performance of the condition of a bond entered by me to the said Richard Ryvett, the sum of ten pounds of lawfull money of England, to be paid by the said Edward my sonne …

> Item I give and bequeath to John Thornton my sonne the sum of twenty pounds of lawfull money of England, to be paid by my said sonne Edward …

(The payment timing for the legacies appears staged over several years after the testator's death; the exact schedule is lower confidence.)

Foster-child / parish-charge clause:

> … if the said John Thornton my sonne shall not within one month next after my decease provide for Anne Sitho [or Sithe?], whome I have brought up of a childe, soe that my executor and the township of Gressengell be freed and discharged of all such charges as hereafter they may be charged withall for the maintenance of the said Anne during her naturall life, then my said guift to him shall be void …

Further bequests:

> Item I give and bequeath to Jane Blade …
> Item I give and bequeath unto Susan Lowes my grandchilde the sum of ten shillings …

Residue of goods and chattels (after debts, legacies, church and poor payments, funeral and proving charges) to Richard Ryvett, made sole executor:

> And I doe ordaine and make Richard Ryvett my sonne in lawe the sole executor of this my last will and Testament …

Witnesses (best effort): John Johnson his marke; **Richard Rivett his marke**; witness Tho: Herrour [?].

Probate clause: proved before **Thomas Browne, clerk, surrogate, 6 February 1630** (i.e. 6 February 1630/31 modern).

## Kin and in-law structure recovered

- **Testator:** John Thornton of Gressenhall, husbandman.
- **Sons:** Edward Thornton (son and heir; the copyhold tenement) and John Thornton.
- **Daughter:** Elizabeth Blade (m. — Blade); Thomas Blade and Elizabeth his wife also appear in the surrendered-land exception; Jane Blade is a further legatee.
- **Son-in-law and sole executor:** Richard Ryvett, whose wife is named **Marian (Mary/Marion)**, not Faith.
- **Grandchild:** Susan Lowes.
- **Foster child:** Anne Sitho/Sithe [?], "brought up of a childe," charged on the township of Gressenhall.

## Note on identity

The will names Richard Ryvett's wife as **Marian Thornton**, so it does **not** supply a maiden surname for the Faith who married Richard Ryvett at Gressenhall in 1602; either Faith had died and Richard remarried a Thornton, or two Richard Ryvetts need disentangling. The will gives the Gressenhall Rivett in-law network as **Thornton and Blade**. Read June 2026 (paleography packet 18).
