#### Opens Private Office

Visible text:

> Opens Private Office
>
> Lester Gurney
>
> Time and again it becomes increasingly evident to us that the heavy construction industry has a great variety of lures and attractions that keep men busy and interested for the full span of their active working lives.
>
> Consider the case of Lester Gurney. For close to fifty years, man and boy, Gurney has been actively involved in some phase or other of heavy construction. As of this month, he retires as Construction Manager of the New England Power Service Company in Boston, Mass. The retirement will not be to the hammock in the garden, however. Far from it.
>
> Making full use of the tremendous, top level experiences that he has gathered through a half century in the construction field, Lester Gurney has announced that he is immediately opening a private office at 271 Dartmouth Street in Boston in the capacity of a consulting construction engineer, specializing in estimates, schedules, cost analysis, appraisals, contracts, investigations, and construction supervision.
>
> As a young man, Gurney entered the construction field as a rodman and instrumentman on electric railway construction, back in 1906.
