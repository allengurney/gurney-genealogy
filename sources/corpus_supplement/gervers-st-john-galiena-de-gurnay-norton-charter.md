# Galiena de Gurnay's grant to the Hospitallers at Norton (Mandeville), c. 1170 — full text via the DEEDS database

Full Latin text of the charter Michael Gervers printed in *The Cartulary of the Knights of St. John of Jerusalem in England, Part 1: Secunda Camera, Essex* (Records of Social and Economic History, n.s. 6, British Academy, 1982) — the "Gervers p. 216" reference carried on the G31 research companion — as transcribed in the University of Toronto DEEDS database (cartulary 0088, doc 00880374). Manuscript: BL Cotton MS Nero E VI, f. 339r. DEEDS assigned date: 1170. Retrieved July 2026.

URL: https://deeds.utsc.utoronto.ca/charters/00880374

## Verbatim text

> Notum sit omnibus sancte matris ecclesie fidelibus presentibus et futuris quod ego **Galiena de Gurnay** dedi et presenti carta confirmavi Deo et sancte Marie et sancto Iohanni Baptiste et beatis pauperibus hospitalis Ierusalem et fratribus eiusdem domus totam terram quam Osbertus clericus tenuit de me in **villa de Northuna** et quam terram ipse michi sponte reddidit et quietam **in meo halimot** clamavit Hanc donacionem feci ego pro salute anime mee et **Maneserii de Dammatin viri mei** et heredum meorum et omnium amicorum et antecessorum meorum in liberam et puram et perpetuam elemosinam liberam et quietam ab omni seculari servicio et exaccione et in bosco et plano et in pascuis et pasturis et in omnibus locis et in omnibus rebus sicut aliqua elemosina melius vel liberius dari potest Hiis testibus Petro capellano Normanno novo homine Ricardo diacono Willelmo palmero Radulpho serganto

In plain English: Galiena de Gurnay gives the brethren of the Hospital of Jerusalem all the land that Osbert the clerk held of her in the vill of Norton, which Osbert voluntarily surrendered and quitclaimed "in my hall-moot" (her own manor court); she makes the gift "for the salvation of my soul and of Manasser de Dammartin my husband," in free, pure, and perpetual alms. Witnesses: Peter the chaplain, Norman "the new man," Richard the deacon, William the palmer, Ralph the sergeant.

## What it establishes

1. **Manasser's wife used the style "de Gurnay" in her own charter.** Alongside the Rochester register charter ("Galiena de Gurnay ... Willelmus de Gurnay, avus meus"), this is a second independent first-person attestation that she was a Gurnay by birth and kept the natal surname in her married years.
2. **The place is Norton (Mandeville), Essex** — the Dammartin manor whose church her son Bartholomew and "Galiena his mother" granted c. 1181–90 (VCH Essex vol. 4). Her holding land there "in meo halimot" shows her with her own court within the family's Essex estate, most naturally dower or maritagium administered in her own name.
3. **Dating.** The pro-salute clause covers both her own soul and her husband's; Manasser died 1178/9, and Gervers's assigned c. 1170 sits within his lifetime — the phrasing does not require him dead.
4. The unusual witness list (chaplain, deacon, palmer — a returned pilgrim — and sergeant) is a household bench, not a baronial one: a domestic act of piety done at the manor.
