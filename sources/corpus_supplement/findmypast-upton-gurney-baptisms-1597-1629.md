# Upton on Severn Gurney baptisms, 1597–1629 (Findmypast transcripts)

## Source and scope

Findmypast, *England Births & Baptisms 1538–1975*, transcript records for four
Upton on Severn, Worcestershire, baptism entries. The displayed transcripts
identify the collection as an IRI index used by permission of FamilySearch
International. This extract preserves the indexed fields as displayed on 31
July 2026; it is not a transcription of the parish-register images.

Stable record URLs:

## Related primary material

Worcestershire Archive & Archaeology Service's parish-register guide lists
Upton upon Severn, Old Church baptisms from 1546 onward. The surviving-register
route is therefore distinct from, and potentially more complete than, the
Findmypast derivative index used here.
