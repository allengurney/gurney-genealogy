# Selected county-sweep Gurney-variant baptism and marriage transcripts, 1600–1644 (Findmypast)

## Source and scope

Findmypast county baptism- and marriage-index transcript records selected from the July–August
2026 Gurney-variant county sweep. This extract preserves every displayed data
field from thirty-three records reopened on 1 August 2026. The selection
includes source-checked entries with an indexed father John/Johis, direct
target-period Gurney comparison records that distinguish a different indexed
parental pair, and two Norfolk marriage records material to the established
Hempnall/Waveney comparator work. It is not a transcript of the original
registers and it is not a complete extract of the underlying county result
sets.

The FMP records supply indexed/transcribed fields only. No `View original
record` link was opened, no original image was read or retained, and a literal
`-` means only that the corresponding field was not indexed in that displayed
transcript. Archive names, references, document labels, and year ranges below
are likewise provider-displayed metadata.
