## Reading notes

- The card heading is `Robert Gurny`; the supplied Findmypast metadata points to `John Gurny`, evidently because John is the child/cross-reference subject.
- `S Augustine`, `S Magnus`, and `S Michael le Querne` are strong readings.
- The marriage date is image-confirmed as `1611 Apr. 4`; the earlier `Oct. 4` reading was corrected after review of the St Magnus original register image.
- The profession line is substantially legible as `citizen + draper`, `free 1581 Dec. 16`, and `Tailor Old Change`. The final name after `by` reads most plausibly as `Robert Mason`, but that surname should be treated as provisional. The `Tailor Old Change` wording should be read as an occupation/location note for Robert Gurny unless the original Drapers’ Company source proves it modifies the preceding master/sponsor name.
- The will line appears to read `1625 Archd. Lo. VI 185` followed by `S Augustine`; the exact archival abbreviation and number should be checked before being used in a citation.
- The `4924[?]` cross-reference appears to correspond to the `John Gurny` card in `gbor_bil_sog59_0240.jpg`, whose upper-right reference number was also read as `4924[?]`.
