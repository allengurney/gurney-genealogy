# Gurney References Extracted from `proprietorsrecor00men.pdf`

## Full citation
*The Proprietors' Records of the Town of Mendon, Massachusetts: Incorporated May 15, 1667*. Boston: Rockwell and Churchill Press, 1899.

**Source filename:** `proprietorsrecor00men.pdf`

## Scope and method
I searched the PDF for `Gurn` and also checked variant forms connected to John and his widow/second wife, including **Gurny**, **Gurney**, **Grisel**, **Grisell**, and **Grizel**. Extracts below are transcribed from the page images, preserving original spelling and capitalization as closely as practical, with line breaks regularized for readability. For several entries, I also used the surrounding page context to explain what kind of record is being abstracted.

## Extracts and contextual notes

### 1. Printed p. 13 (PDF p. 23)
**Verbatim extract**

> John Gurny, a twenty acre lot.
>
> Grisel Gurney, a twenty acre lot.

**Explanation / context**

This page sits within the early list headed by the compiler as **"The names of those that purchased lots of the Town after they were a township and granted town privileges are as follows"**. In that context, both **John Gurny** and **Grisel Gurney** appear as separate twenty-acre lot holders, which is important because it suggests that Grisel was remembered in the record tradition as holding her own allotment, not merely appearing incidentally under John's name.

### 3. Printed p. 43 (PDF p. 53)
**Verbatim extract**

> [29.] A Coppy of the Will of Grisell Gurny
>
> ... Thes are to Inform you by us William flecher and John Burge both of us of the Town of Chelmsford Apointed by the Courts order to bee Gardians to the Child of the Widdow Gurny that Was And Last of all Wife to the Abouesd John Burge she being of A sound understanding did Will unto her son Joseph Juell, all that Acomodation that was there att the Town of Mendon Laid out and given unto her ye sd Widdow Gurny With all the Apurtinances and Priuiliges In any wise Apertaining or be longing there unto upon this condition that the sd Joseph Juell doe Pay to Nathanil Juell and Mercy Juell fiftene Pounds and to Sherabiah Keby seuen pounds ...

**Explanation / context**

This is one of the most genealogically useful entries in the volume. The page is not merely a stray mention of Grizel/Grisell. It records a copy of her will, conveyed to the Mendon selectmen through guardians in Chelmsford. The wording strongly suggests that the woman remembered here as **"the Widdow Gurny"** had later become **the wife of John Burge**. It also identifies **Joseph Juell** as her son, and names **Nathanil Juell**, **Mercy Juell**, and **Sherabiah Keby** as beneficiaries to receive specified payments. In other words, this entry ties the Mendon accommodation to a later family network after John Gurny's death.

### 4. Printed p. 46 (PDF p. 56)
**Verbatim extract**

> [31.] Josiah Chapin
>
> Granted to Grisel Gurny A twenty Acre house Lott with all Rights and Priuileges there unto belonging and by her giuen to her son Joseph Juell by her Last Will upon Record in pag 29) and by sd Joseph Juells Lawfull Attorney as Appears upon Record in pag 29) And Now Josiah Chapins as Appears by A deed of Sale baring Date the twenty fift of Aprill one thousand six hundred and Eighty and three and now In Possion of sd Chapin ...

**Explanation / context**

This page turns the will on p. 43 into a chain-of-title statement. The compiler is abstracting the history of a specific Mendon lot: first **granted to Grisel Gurny**, then **given by will to her son Joseph Juell**, then passing by deed into **Josiah Chapin's** possession. For genealogical purposes, this is valuable confirmation that Grisel's Mendon holding was treated as a discrete lot with a traceable succession.

### 7. Printed pp. 152-153, record [108.] (PDF pp. 162-163)
**Verbatim extract**

> [108.] John Gurnys twenty Acre House Lott Now Josiah Thayers As Appears by A Deed of Sale baring Date the third Day of May one thousand six hundred Ninty and two, and Acknowledged before Edmond Quincy: Esqr: Justice of the peace
>
> 13 febuary 1692/3 and Recorded with the Record of Deeds for the County of Suffolke Liber: 19th Pag 356-7
>
> The House Lott being Twenty Acres is butted and bounded as followeth Easterly upon Muddy brook Southerly upon the House Lott of fardinando Thayer And with Comomon Northerly and Weasterly upon Comon
>
> The Dubling Lott belonging to the Aboue sd Lott being Twenty Acres is butted and bounded as followeth Southerly upon the Aboue sd House Lott Easterly upon Muddy brook Northerly by the Land of William Holbrook Weasterly partly upon A four Rod highway ner Joseph Steuenses Lott and partly with Comon

**Explanation / context**

This is the clearest title-style entry for John himself. The compiler identifies the parcel as **John Gurny's twenty-acre house lot**, but notes that it was **now Josiah Thayer's** by deed dated **3 May 1692** and recorded **13 February 1692/3** in Suffolk County deeds. The following page continues with the meadow divisions and third division upland of the same former Gurny holding. Taken together, this entry strongly indicates that John's Mendon land had passed out of the Gurney family by the early 1690s.

## Consolidated observations

1. **John Gurny/Gurney had a distinct twenty-acre house lot and associated meadow/swamp rights.** The clearest title-style abstract is the pp. 152-153 entry, which shows the lot later in **Josiah Thayer's** possession by deed of 1692.

2. **Grisel/Grisell/Grizel Gurney also appears as a distinct Mendon lot holder.** She is listed independently on p. 13, later appears in the copied will on p. 43, and her twenty-acre lot is summarized again on p. 463.

3. **The will material is genealogically important.** The p. 43 entry indicates that the woman remembered as **the widow Gurny** was later **wife to John Burge**, and that she willed her Mendon accommodation to **Joseph Juell**, with obligations to **Nathanil Juell**, **Mercy Juell**, and **Sherabiah Keby**.

4. **The printed index under-reports the true number of Gurney references.** Many additional references survive as abutters in meadow and swamp descriptions, especially where later proprietors' land is bounded by **John Gurny's meadow**, **John Gurny's house lot**, or **John Gurny's swamp lot**.

## Bottom line

The Mendon proprietors' records preserve two overlapping but distinct lines of evidence:

- **direct ownership/chain-of-title evidence** for **John Gurny** and **Grisel/Grisell/Grizel Gurney**, and
- **secondary boundary evidence** showing that John's meadow, house lot, and swamp lot remained recognizable landmarks long after title had passed to others.

For genealogy, the most valuable entries are the **p. 43 will**, the **p. 46 title follow-through for Grisel's lot**, and the **pp. 152-153 abstract of John Gurny's house lot now in Josiah Thayer's possession**.
