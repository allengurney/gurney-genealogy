# Calendar of IPM, Henry VII — William Gurnay, esq., as feoffee in Sir Roger Townshend's estate (d. 1493)

**William Gurnay, esq.**, of the West Barsham family, recurs as a **feoffee-to-uses (trustee)** in the inquisitions post mortem of **Sir Roger Townshend, knight** (the Norfolk lawyer-judge of Raynham), who died 9 November, 9 Henry VII (1493). The trust was still active at Townshend's death, so William Gurnay was holding Townshend lands to the family's use in the early 1490s. The calendar is an English-language abstract of the Latin inquisitions; the family forename appears as "William Gurnay, esq." throughout, without a distinguishing descriptor — most economically **William Gurney IV of West Barsham (the family head, escheator for Norfolk, d. 1508)**, though his same-named son William V (d. *vita patris*) was also living in 1493.

His co-feoffees are the top tier of late-fifteenth-century Norfolk society and church: Sir Roger Townshend himself, **William Pekenham, Archdeacon of Suffolk**, **Thomas Wodhous (Woodhouse)**, **Edward Knyvet**, **John and Thomas Blakeney**, **Thomas Gygges/Gyggys, gent.**, Edmund Harberd (clerk), William Wayte, and John Pegeon.

## Extracts (Calendar of Inquisitions Post Mortem, Series 2, vol. 1, Henry VII, entries 1101–1150)

> …and Thomas Appylton were seised of the under-mentioned messuage and land in Hadle [Hadleigh, Suffolk], and being so seised enfeoffed the said Roger Touneshend, knt., William Pekenham, Archdeacon of Suffolk, Thomas Wodhous, and **William Gurnay**, esqs., Thomas Gygges, gent., and William Wayte thereof in fee, to the use of the [said Roger]…

> …and being so seised enfeoffed the said Roger Touneshend, knt., and Dame Eleanor his wife, who survives, thereof, to hold to them and the heirs of their bodies, with remainder to John Blakeney and **William Gurnay**, esqs., and Thomas Gyggys in fee, to the use of the said Roger and Eleanor…

> …Manor called 'Havyles' in Est Reynham, West Reynham, and South Reynham, Helloughton, Toftes, Oxwyk, Wyssyngset [Whissonsett], Hornyngtoft [Horningtoft], and Gatele [Gateley], held of William Pekenham, clk., Thomas Wodhous, Edward Knyvet, and **William Gurnay**, esqs., Thomas Blakeney, Edmund Harberd, clk., William Wayte, and John Pegeon, as of…

## What the source establishes

- William Gurnay, esq. (West Barsham family) was a trusted feoffee in Sir Roger Townshend's principal Norfolk estate (the Raynham group of manors and Hadleigh in Suffolk), the trust live at Townshend's death in 1493.
- It places the West Barsham Gurneys inside the Townshend–Heydon–Knyvet–Blakeney–Pekenham trust circle at documentary, primary-record level — the same affinity recorded on the marriage side (Sir Roger Townshend's wife Amy Heydon was sister to Anne Heydon, wife of William Gurney V).

The calendar gives the abstract, not the Latin original; "Gurnay" is the calendar editors' spelling. The entries name Gurnay as a feoffee/trustee, not as a tenant-in-chief, so there is no Gurney inquisition of his own here.
