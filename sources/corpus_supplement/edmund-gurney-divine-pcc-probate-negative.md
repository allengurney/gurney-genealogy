# Edmund Gurney the Divine (d. 1648) — probate search, negative result

Durable record of a systematic search for a will or administration of **Edmund Gurney (Gurnay/Gournay), clerk, rector of Harpley, Norfolk** — the Puritan divine (author of *Gurnay Redivivus*), buried 14 May 1648 at St Peter Mancroft, Norwich; wife Ellen alive June 1643. He was a brother of Francis Gurney (G14) and so a great-uncle in the candidate line to the emigrant John Gurney-1. Search run 2026-06-12 (lead L-6). Source ID: `edmund-divine-pcc-probate-negative`.

## Verdict

**No will or administration for Edmund Gurney the Divine has been located in any court, under any spelling.** His will is **not** in the Norwich Consistory Court registers for 1643–1651 (coverage-confirmed Full-Text sweeps; the NCC registers then gap 1652–1659), and the **Prerogative Court of Canterbury** (TNA series PROB 11 wills, PROB 6 administrations) returns nothing for 1648–1660. His probate most likely does not survive — consistent with the Civil-War disruption of the church courts in 1648 and the known NCC register gap.

## Disambiguation

The only "Will of Edmund Gurney" in TNA Discovery is **PROB 11/739/515, dated 1745** — an 18th-century Edmund Gurney, worsted weaver of Norwich, not the rector.

## Incidental records surfaced (logged as leads, not probate)

- **NRO BL/O/G/1** (Bradfer-Lawrence Collection): grant of 27 April 1630 by "Edmond Gurnay of Harpley, clerk" to Edward Gurnay of West Barsham, esq., and Edmund Dethick of Harpley, gent. — Yeveringe close in Guist in exchange for tenements in Great and East Barsham. Confirms the rector's own spelling "Edmond Gurnay" and a property tie to the West Barsham heir Edward Gournay.
- **TNA C 5/609/120** (1648): Chancery suit *Gurnay v Cocke*, plaintiffs **Frances Gurnay, widow,** and **Henry Gurnay** vs. Edmund Cocke, concerning Great Ellingham — plausibly Edward Gournay's widow and his heir Henry II of West Barsham (lead L-115; would name Edward's otherwise-unrecorded wife). Not online — TNA Kew order required.
