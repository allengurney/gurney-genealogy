# Gurney-variant equity, Exchequer and estate records in the Discovery catalogue, 1590–1685

Catalogue-level extract. The National Archives' Discovery catalogue indexes TNA's own holdings and
the catalogues contributed by some 2,500 other archives. This file carries the Gurney-variant
descriptions returned by a systematic sweep, verbatim as catalogued, for the classes that name and
individuate a person: equity-court pleadings, Exchequer certificates and depositions, Star Chamber,
and deposited estate deeds. Source ID: `tna-discovery-catalogue`; the Worcestershire items are also
registered under `worcs-archives-pakington-westwood-park`.

## Sweep parameters

Discovery search API, `/API/search/records`, run 28 July 2026.

- **Query terms:** `Gurney`, `Gurny`, `Gurnay`, `Gournay`, `Gourney`, `Gurnie`, `Gorney`, `Garney`,
  `Gerney`, `Girney`, `Gurneye`, `Gournaye`, `Gurnoe`, `Gorny` — each run separately.
- **Date filter:** `1590-01-01` to `1685-12-31`, applied on covering dates, so records whose range
  overlaps the window are returned.
- **Result:** 753 unique records across all repositories; 92 of them name a John of a Gurney
  variant in the title, description or context.
- **Supplementary runs, undated:** the same variant list without a date filter, to reach the complete
  E 115, REQ 2 and STAC blocks; plus keyword-paired runs (`… AND "East Dereham"`, `… AND Hockliffe`,
  `… AND (Pakington OR "Westwood Park" OR Bedgrove)`, `… AND (tailor OR taylor)`, `… AND
  (Cheddington OR Edlesborough OR Ivinghoe OR Marsworth OR Stewkley OR Stuteley)`, `… AND (Playstow
  OR "Stoke Mandeville")`, `… AND ("Weston Turville" OR Bierton OR Broughton)`).

What Discovery indexes and does not index bounds every negative below. It carries catalogue
descriptions, not document text: a person named only inside an uncalendared bill, deposition or deed
does not surface. TNA's own item-level description is dense for Chancery pleadings (C 1–C 10), Court
of Requests (REQ 2), Exchequer certificates of residence (E 115), Exchequer depositions by
commission (E 134), Star Chamber (STAC), Court of Wards deeds (WARD 2) and PCC wills (PROB 11).
Contributed catalogues vary: some county offices are described to item level with full abstracts,
others only to collection level. Manorial court rolls and parish registers are catalogued as
volumes, so their contents are invisible here.

## 1. Exchequer: Certificates of Residence (E 115)

## 8. What the sweep did not return

Bounded by §Sweep parameters: these are absences from catalogued descriptions, not from the
documents themselves.

- No description in the window names a Gurney of the **tailoring trade**. The paired
  `Gurney … AND (tailor OR taylor)` run returns ten records, all of them surname *Taylor/Tayleur* or
  unrelated.
- No description names a **John Gurney with a wife Mary** anywhere in England 1590–1685.
- No description names a **John Gurney as son of a Francis Gurney**, in any court or archive.
- No **certificate of residence, Chancery bill, Requests bill, Star Chamber suit or Exchequer
  deposition** names any of the five unaccounted-for Buckinghamshire men — the Stewkley baptisms of
  1602/3 and 1611, the Haddenham head, the Weston Turville head, the Hitcham head, or the John
  baptised at Cheddington in 1608.
- No **Francis Gurney certificate of residence** survives in E 115, in either direction between
  Norfolk and London.
