# Ryvett / Rivett pedigrees — Visitation of Suffolk (Metcalfe)

Verbatim transcription of the Ryvett/Rivett pedigrees in the printed Visitation of Suffolk
(Hervey 1561, Cooke 1577, Raven 1612; Metcalfe ed.), pp. 63 and 162–163. These are the
Suffolk gentry Ryvetts — distinct from, and to be tested against, the mid-Norfolk Rivetts of
Garveston/Gressenhall identified as the maternal family of John Gurney G13 (see
[`research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md`](../../research/people/rivett-family-of-garveston.md)).
The Suffolk line spells the name **Ryvett**.

Source: Metcalfe, *The Visitations of Suffolk* — `suffolk-visitations-metcalfe-1882`. Internet
Archive: `visitationsofsuf00harvuoft`.

## p. 63 — RYVETT of Bricet

> ARMS: Per pale, Argent, and Sable, on a chevron between three mascles as many martlets all
> counterchanged. CREST: An arm erect couped at the elbow, per pale Argent and Sable, in the
> hand proper a broken sword of the second hilt and pommel Or.
>
> THOMAS RYVETT of Stowmarket, co. Suff., Gent., mar. Jone, da. of Thomas Raven, and had by
> her issue,—JAMES, son and heir; Thomas, second son; John, third son; William, fourth son;
> and Myrable, mar. to William Burd, Esq., Customer of London.
>
> JAMES RYVETT of Brysset (Bricet), co. Suff., Esq., son and heir to Thomas, mar. Dorothe,
> da. of John Goone in co. Suff., Esq., and by her hath issue,—Thomas Ryvett, son and heir;
> and Susan.

## p. 162 — RYVETT of Rishangles

> JOHN RIVET of Rishangles, descended of a third brother, mar. Margery, one of the da.'s and
> coheirs of … Beck of Norf., and had issue,—THOMAS, son and heir; Roger, second son;
> Edward, third son.
>
> THOMAS RIVET, son and heir, mar. Jane, da. of Bartholomew Bolney of Bolney in Sussex, and
> had issue,—JOHN, son and heir; Elizabeth; Mary, mar. to John Eldred of London, Esq.; Ann,
> mar., to her first husband, Anthony Bates of London, merchant; to her second husband,
> Thomas Talbot of London, Gent.; to her third husband, Richard Bowle of Great Chesham,
> co. Bucks, Esq.
>
> JOHN RIVET, son and heir, mar. Elizabeth, da. of George Brooke of Aspall in Suff., and had
> issue,—William, son and heir apparent, age 20; John, second son, age 14; Thomas, third
> son, age 10; George, fourth son, age 7; Elizabeth, age 21; Mary, age 18; Jane, age 16;
> Dorathy, age 15; Alice, age 12; Ann, age 8; Martha, age 4; Margaret, age 3.

## p. 162 — RYVETT of Ratlesden

> THOMAS RYVETT of Rattelsden in Suff., Esq., son and heir of James, mar. Katherin, da. of
> William Cotton of Panfield in Essex, Esq., and had issue,—James, obiit; THOMAS, son and
> heir; Edward, second son; Ann, mar. to Sir Henry Clovile of Essex, Kt.
>
> THOMAS RYVETT of Rattelsden in Suff., Gent., son and heir of Thomas, mar. Joane, da. and
> coheir of John Savill of Netherton in Yorkshire, and hath no issue, 1612; maryed 1611; his
> father obiit at London, 1611, apud St. George's in Southwark.

## pp. 162–163 — RYVETT of Brandeston

> JOHN RYVET of Molenden (Monewden) Park in Suff., Gent., mar. Crisstan (Christian) the da.
> of … Bedell of Essex, and had issue,—ANDREW, son and heir; William and Thomas, Clerks.
>
> ANDREW RYVET of Branson (Brandeston) in Suff., son and heir of John, mar. Elizabeth, da.
> and heir of Thomas Huntingfield and of Ann his wife, the da. and heir of Thomas Dove of
> Stradbroke in Suff., and had issue,—JOHN, son and heir; Anthony and George, sans issue.
>
> JOHN RYVETT of Branson, Esq., son and heir of Andrew, mar. Ann, da. of James Bacon of
> London, Alderman, and had issue,—NICHOLAS, son and heir; Robert, second son; John, third
> son; Thomas, fourth son; Ann, mar. to John Trence (Trench) of Gresnall in Norf.; Margery,
> mar. to Edmond Hills of London; Eliz, mar. to Robert Armiger of Friston in Suff.; Mary;
> Margaret; and Amy, unm.
>
> NICHOLAS RYVET, Esq., son and heir of John, mar. Mary, the da. of Thomas Sickelmore of
> Ipswich, and hath issue,—John, son and heir, age 2.

## Gurney-relevance notes

- **The Brandeston branch is the only one with a daughter "Margaret"** (John Ryvett of
  Brandeston × Ann Bacon). It also carries a documented Norfolk tie — daughter **Ann married
  John Trench of Gressenhall, Norfolk** — and a London-gentry network (Bacon of Friston via
  the Armiger and Bacon marriages). Nicholas Ryvett (1573–1643) was MP for Aldeburgh
  (History of Parliament). Whether the Brandeston Margaret is connected to the mid-Norfolk
  Garveston Rivetts (the maternal family of John G13) is open; the Garveston Margaret (bp.
  1586, dau. Francis Rivet) is the stronger candidate for Francis Gurney's wife.
- **Correction flag:** the printed Visitation states **Myrable [Mirabella] Ryvett married
  William Burd, Customer of London** — *not* Sir John Heydon of Baconsthorpe as asserted in
  the G14 fact sheet and John Gurney case file. The Rivett–Heydon leg of the cousinage claim
  is therefore in conflict with the primary Visitation;
  the Gurney–Heydon tie via Anne Heydon m. William Gurney V is independent and unaffected.
- The Rishangles branch's Margaret is **age 3 in 1612** (b. ≈1609) — too young to be Francis
  Gurney's 1611 bride; the Rattlesden, Bricet, and Stowmarket branches have no Margaret of
  the right generation.
