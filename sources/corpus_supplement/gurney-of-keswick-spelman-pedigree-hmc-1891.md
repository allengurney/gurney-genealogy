# Gurney of Keswick manuscripts (Spelman–Macro collection) — HMC 1891 description

Extracts from the Historical Manuscripts Commission's report on the manuscripts of **John Henry Gurney, Esq., of Keswick Hall, Norfolk**, printed in *Twelfth Report, Appendix, Part IX: The Manuscripts of the Duke of Beaufort, K.G., the Earl of Donoughmore, and Others* (London: HMSO, 1891). The Gurney section begins at **p. 116**; the Gurney/Spelman pedigree volume is described at **p. 161**.

## Provenance of the collection (p. 116)

The report records that the collection at Keswick Hall belonged to the trustees of the late **Hudson Gurney**, and that many volumes had earlier been in the library of **Dr. Cox Macro** (d. 1767). When Macro's collection was sold in **1820** (then owned by John Patteson of Norwich), forty-one of its volumes passed to Dawson Turner of Yarmouth and the remainder to Hudson Gurney; **fourteen** of the volumes described consist wholly or partly of papers once owned by, or written by, **Sir Henry Spelman**. The three items the report singles out as most notable are a twelfth-century *Liber Angliae* (laws of England), the minute-book of a company of Puritan ministers ("classes") meeting in Essex (Dedham and neighbourhood) in 1582–3, and the 1616–1652 diary of Robert Horton, judge of Common Pleas.

## The pedigree volume (p. 161)

Under heading **XXXIV. Miscellanea**, manuscript **No. 122 (16), pp. 1–124**, is described as:

> "Numerous pedigrees, with arms, &c.; [con]cerning **Bardolf, Calthrop, Gurney, and especially Spelman**; epitaphs; various churches on members of the Spelman family; copies of **Spelman wills, 1485–1641**."

This is the surviving Spelman-circle pedigree compilation. It contains a **Gurney pedigree with arms**, grouped with the allied Norfolk families Calthorpe and Bardolf and centred on Spelman — the armigerous Norfolk Gurney line of West Barsham. The HMC entry attests the manuscript's survival and content at description level; it does not transcribe the Gurney pedigree's individual generations.
