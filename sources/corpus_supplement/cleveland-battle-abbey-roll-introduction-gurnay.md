# Cleveland, Battle Abbey Roll introduction - Gurnay/Gurney variants

Source ID: `cleveland-battle-abbey-roll-introduction`  
URL: https://www.1066.co.nz/Mosaic%20DVD/library/Battle%20Roll/introduction.html  
Raw intake copy: `sources/intake/archive/v09-1066-gournay-texts/Introduction-Roll of Battle Abbey.md`

## Scope searched

Searched the user-supplied 1066.co.nz markdown for `Gurney`, `Gurnay`, and `Gournay`.

## Extracted findings

- The introduction's discussion of Battle Abbey Roll lists says one 168-name list duplicated Hugh under variants including Hue de Gourney and Hue earl of Gournay. The introduction notes that the earl form is the only instance where he appears with that title.
- The same introductory list-comparison material explicitly warns that the list tradition is incomplete and unstable. This supports using Battle Roll forms as name-variant evidence, not as standalone proof of separate people or titles.
- A tabular copy in the introduction preserves both `Gurnay` and `Gurney` forms.
- A later list line preserves `Gurnay et Greilly`.

## Intake disposition

Applied in v09 to the Anderson/Yvery/Gournay collateral topic file as a name-variant and roll-criticism source. No separate person was created from the duplicated Hugh forms.
