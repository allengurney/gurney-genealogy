## Calendar entry: 17 October, 20 James I (1622), p. 247

> Indenture of an Agreement, made between Francis Gurney citizen and merchant-taylor of London, Ambrose Tompson of Thetford co. Norfolk glover, and Martin Hill of Ellingham co. Norfolk woolchapman, of the one part, and the Mayor and burgesses of the borough of Lenn of the other part: Whereby, for considerations set forth in the writing, the said Francis, Ambrose and Martin agreed to instruct the poor children of the said town in the art of spinning worsted-yarn, providing the wool needful for the same instruction, and also to give employment to the poor of Lenn in the same industry of spinning worsted-yarn, paying those of them that are not mere learners fit wages for their work.

## Source character and limits

This is the Historical Manuscripts Commission's calendar of an indenture among the King's Lynn Corporation muniments, not a transcription of the sealed indenture. It identifies the parties, describes the instructional and employment terms, and dates the instrument 17 October 20 James I. It does not give the full covenants, witness subscription, seals, or a manuscript image. The public-domain HMC text is also available through the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/manuscriptsofcor00grea_0) and British History Online URL above.

For Daniel Gurney's printed full-text transcription of an indenture dated 11 October 1622, including its witness subscription, see [`dg-record-part3-appendix-lxxxvii-lynn-indenture-1622.md`](dg-record-part3-appendix-lxxxvii-lynn-indenture-1622.md).
