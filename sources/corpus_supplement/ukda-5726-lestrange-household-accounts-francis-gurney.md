# Francis Gurney in the Le Strange household accounts, 1612–1626

Jane Whittle and Elizabeth Griffiths, *Household Accounts of the Le Strange Family, 1606–1626*,
UK Data Archive Study 5726 (2006). Source ID:
`ukda-5726-lestrange-household-accounts-1606-1626`.

This extract joins the account-entry table to its people, places, categories, and document-
reference tables. The account books are Norfolk Record Office `LEST/P6–P11`. The database
transcribes selected material through 1626; its documentation of later account-book coverage
does not supply later entry transcriptions. A blank entered-amount field is shown as `—`; it is
not an amount of zero.

## Search coverage and negative results

- Conservative and broad surname searches returned 31 account entries indexed to Francis Gurney
  [718]; no further Gurney-variant account entry survived the broad pass.
- No account or people-table entry names John Gurney, Edward Gurney, Anne Gurney, Margaret
  Rybett/Rivett, or a Gurney child as a ward, apprentice, pupil, servant, or household member.
- The account text and people table contain no Ambrose Tompson/Thompson or Martyne/Martin Hill
  occurrence. The database therefore does not itself identify the 1622 King’s Lynn contract
  partners in its transcribed entries.
- No private ward or wardship entry was found. The word `ward` occurs only in Court of Wards
  legal-account context and as the surname Ward.
- The text contains no Great Ellingham, Hingham, West Barsham, Deopham, Attleborough, Garveston,
  or Whinburgh occurrence. The sole `Barsham` hit is a person named Mr Barsham, not a place.
- Gressenhall occurs repeatedly in estate transactions—court profits, timber, rent, house and
  sluice work, letters, servants, and unidentified boys—but none of those entries names Francis,
  John, or Edward Gurney.
