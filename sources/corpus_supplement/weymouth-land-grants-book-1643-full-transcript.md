# Weymouth Land Grants book — full transcript, manuscript pp. 9–32

## Scope and transcription status

This covers images `007009659_00128.jpg` through
`007009659_00145.jpg`. Images `00133` right through `00145` left contain the
continuous land-description series on manuscript pp. 9–32. The full text below
is a **Nash-assisted working transcription**: Gilbert Nash's 1885 transcription
was aligned to the manuscript leaves and used to recover wording that is now
too faded to read independently. Owner headings, page order, the three John
Gurney clauses, and many clearer parcel lines were checked against the primary
images. It is not represented as a fresh diplomatic transcription of every
letter. Internet Archive OCR artifacts in Nash's printed text may remain,
especially `m'`/`m''`, capitalization, and occasional names.

Square brackets are editorial. Blank or unreadable leaves are described rather
than silently omitted. Original spelling is retained where the printed
transcription preserves it.
