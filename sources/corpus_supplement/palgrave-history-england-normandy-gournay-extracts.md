## Scope searched

## Extracted findings

- Palgrave lists the Gourneys with the Giffards, Tankervilles, Baskervilles, and other Norman families as branches associated with the Forest of Arques context under Richard le Bon. This is broad kinship/geographic context only.
- In the Mortemer narrative, Palgrave presents Hugh Gournay as a major Pays de Bray power. He says much of the rebellious baronage held lands in the Pays de Bray, that a large share had been won by the Gournays, and that the Pays de Bray was an essart of the ancient Forest of Lyons with rich pasture and many towns and villages.
- Palgrave places Gerberoi about five miles from Gournay and says Robert Curthose established headquarters there during his quarrel with William I. After the fighting, Hugh de Gournay appears among mediators between father and son, with Roger de Montgomery, Hugh Lupus, Grantesmesnil, Beaumont, and Beaumonts sons.
