## Transcription

> **Anthonie Gurney**
>
> In the name of god Amen the Sixte daie of december in the yere of our lorde god a thousande fyve hundreth fiftie and seven I Anthonie Gurney of greate Elingham in the countie of Norff Esquier being in good and hole mynde thankes be unto god make & declare this my p[re]sent Testament and last will in manner & forme followinge ffirst I bequeathe my Soule unto almightie god trusting to have the fruition of his glorious presence emonges the saintes of heaven & my bodie to be buried in the churche of Elingham aforesayde unto the high alter whereof I give and bequeathe for my tithes negligently forgotten & not payd ij s viij d Item I will that at my daye of my buriall Seaventh daye thirtie daie & twelmonth daie shalbe done some dede of charitie unto the relieff of the poore as by myn executors shalbe thought most expedient
>
> And I will that my executors shall have two partes of all my mannors londs and Tenements with their appurtenances in three partes equallye devided lying and being w[ith]in the sayde countie of Norff being holden by knightes service to have and to holde the sayde two partes of all my sayde mannors londs and tenements with their appurtenances holden by knightes service to myn executors and their assignes from the daye of my deceas untill thend of the terme of forte[n]e yeres then next followinge be fullie complete and ended and the profyghtes and yssues cominge of the same two partes by the sayde terme of forte[n]e yeres to be employed and bestowed in and towarde the payment of my debtes legacies and performance of this my testament and last will
>
> Item I will that my sayde executors shall have the residue of all my sayde mannors londs and tenements with their appurtenances lying and being in the sayde countie being not holden by knightes service to have and to holde all the sayde mannors londs and tenements with their appurtenances so being not holden in knightes service to my sayde executors and their assignes from the sayde daie of my deceas untill thend of the sayde terme of forte[n]e yeres be fullie completed and ended & the yssues & profights therof coming by the same terme together with all my goods and cattall to be imployed and bestowed in and towarde the payment of my sayde debtes legacies and performannce of this my p[re]sent testament & lastwill
>
> Item I give and bequeathe unto **Elizabeth Gurney my dowter** two hundreth poundes of lawfull money of Englande to be payd to the same Elizabeth or to hir assignes as sone and in as redie spede as it maye be made raysed & taken of & for my goods and Cattalls thissues & profightes of the sayde two partes of my sayde mannors & other the premisses being holden by knightes service and of the Issues and profightes of all other my sayde mannors londs and Tenementes being not holden by knightes service as is aforesayde my debtes being ffyrst payd
>
> Item I give and bequeathe to **Cicelie Gurney my dowter** twentie poundes of lawfull money of Englande to be payd as it maye be made raysed and taken of the sayde Issues and profightes of the premisses assigned to my executors in this my p[re]sent will and Testament
>
> and I will that so much of the Residue of the Issues and profightes of the same premisses as shalbe thought meete by myn executors shalbe bestowed in and towarde the **educacion and bringing upp of Anthonye Gurney Thomas Gurney Elizabeth Gurney ffrannces Gurney Anne Gurney and Anthonye Drurye my Grandchildren** and forther I will That the over plus of the same yssues remayninge at thende of the sayde forte[n]e yeres shalbe equallie devyded and payd by my sayde executors or their assignes to my sayde Grandchildren then being alive
>
> The residue of my goods and cattalls not before given And bequeathed I committe to the good order and disposicion of myn executors whom I ordeyn and make **Sir Christopher Heydon knight and Robt Notarye Esquier** in witnes whereof to this my p[re]sent testament and lastwill I have sette my Seale and subscribed my name the daye and yere above written witnes to this p[re]sent will & Testament Anthonye [Twayghtes?] Thomas fflegge John Cadie and [Fannys?] Smyth

### Probate clause

> Probatum fuit huius testamentum apud Norwicum coram magistro Michaele Dunninge legum doctore Decimo die mensis Decembris anno d[omi]ni millesimo quingentesimo quinquagesimo septimo Et commissa fuit administratio bonorum etc. prefato Christofero Heydon militi et Roberto Notarye armigero executoribus in dicto testamento nominatis in debita iuris forma iuratis.

[Proved at Norwich before Master Michael Dunninge, doctor of laws, the tenth day of December 1557, and administration was committed to the aforesaid Sir Christopher Heydon, knight, and Robert Notarye, esquire, the executors named in the said testament, sworn in due form of law.]

## Reading notes

- Will made 6 December 1557 and proved 10 December 1557. The four-day gap supports treating this as a deathbed or near-death will. This conflicts with Blomefield's death date of 4 January 1555/6 for Anthony Gurney of Great Ellingham; see the conflict discussion in the [G17 research companion](../../research/people/g17-anthony-gurney-fact-sheet.research.md).
- No wife and no son is named in the visible will text. The will does **not** name Francis as Anthony's son.
- The only Francis/Frances reading is `ffrannces Gurney` in the grandchildren clause. The will's stated relationship for that person is grandchild, not child. The spelling does not by itself resolve whether the name should be normalized as Francis or Frances.
- The omission of son Francis should not be treated as a missed line. It is a real feature of the registered copy. It does not disprove external evidence that Francis was Anthony's child; it means this will does not itself state that relationship.
- The land-trust term now appears more likely to be `forte[n]e` (fourteen) years than forty years. The reading is still the hardest repeated English word in the will, but both occurrences show an n-like minims cluster, and the context is an education/support trust for grandchildren followed by division of overplus to those then living.
- The grandchildren named are Anthony Gurney, Thomas Gurney, Elizabeth Gurney, ffrannces Gurney, Anne Gurney, and Anthony Drury. No Henry appears in the list.
- Executor Sir Christopher Heydon (of Baconsthorpe, d. 1579) was the leading north-Norfolk knight of the day and a Gurney kinsman through Anthony's Heydon mother.
- The page following the probate (third staged image) begins the unrelated will of Stephen Ayott of North Runcton, tailor. The machine-text "Gurney unto the well land" hit there is a false positive for "given unto them as well lond."
