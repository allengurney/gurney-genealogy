# Anselm, *Opera Omnia* (Schmitt) - Hugh de Gornaco and Basilia locators

Source ID: `anselm-opera-schmitt`

## Source

Franciscus Salesius Schmitt, ed., *S. Anselmi Cantuariensis Archiepiscopi Opera Omnia*, vols. 3 and 5 (Edinburgh: Thomas Nelson and Sons, 1938-1961).

Internet Archive locators:
- Vol. 3: `sanselmicantuari03anse`
- Vol. 5: `sanselmicantuari05anse`

## Relevant locators

- Vol. 3, Ep. 68, "Ad Gondulfum monachum": the title and body connect Anselm's thanks with Hugh and Basilia; the editorial note identifies Hugh as de Gornaco and connects Basilia to the Bec service tradition.
- Vol. 3, Bec-monks salutation letter near Schmitt's Ep. 118 sequence: controls the DG/*Neustria Pia* tradition that Anselm asked Bec's monks to greet Hugh de Gornaco and Basilia.
- Vol. 5, Ep. 420, "Ad Basiliam": letter addressed directly to Basilia; the vol. 5 index identifies her as wife of Hugh de Gornaco.
