## Extract

Beaven's chronological aldermen list includes the 16 January 1589 entry:

```text
Richard Gourney, Haberdasher
Portsoken, 1589-94, Bridge, 1594-7
S. 1589-90
vice Garton
Auditor 1587-9; Master Haberdashers 1589-90, 1596-7.
Died March 5, 1597; Will [P.C.C. 35 Cobham] October 15, 1596; proved April 8, 1597.
```

## Research value

This entry gives a firm civic identity for Richard Gourney, haberdasher: alderman of Portsoken and Bridge wards, Sheriff in 1589-1590, Auditor in 1587-1589, and Master of the Haberdashers' Company in 1589-1590 and 1596-1597. It also supplies the key probate target: PCC will 35 Cobham, dated 15 October 1596 and proved 8 April 1597.

The Beaven entry strongly strengthens the London Richard Gurney/Gourney haberdasher cluster. Given the shared name, trade, date range, and London commercial context, this Richard is a strong candidate for the same man as the Richard Gurney, haberdasher, who appears in the 1567-1568 London port-book index and Danzig/Baltic import entries. That identification remains a working correlation until a livery, probate, or other record explicitly bridges the port-book merchant to the alderman.

The PCC will is the most valuable next pull. It may test whether this Richard connects to the Haberdashers' Gournay charity, to the known St Michael Crooked Lane family noted by Daniel Gurney, or to any Robert/John Gourney/Gurney household in the City.
