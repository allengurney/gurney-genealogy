# Norfolk feet of fines naming Gurnay/Gournay — Walter Rye's Short Calendar (both parts)

Gurnay/Gournay/Gurney entries from Walter Rye, *A Short Calendar of the Feet of Fines for Norfolk*:
- **Part I** (Richard I, John, Henry III, Edward I) — local text-searchable copy at [`sources/corpus/rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk-part1.pdf`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/sources/corpus/rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk-part1.pdf); Internet Archive [`ashortcalendarf02ryegoog`](https://archive.org/details/ashortcalendarf02ryegoog). Reign headers run Henry II → 35th Edward I (≈1307).
- **Part II** (Edward II → Richard III, including Henry IV–VI) — Internet Archive [`ashortcalendarf00ryegoog`](https://archive.org/details/ashortcalendarf00ryegoog); full OCR text now held locally at [`sources/corpus/rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk-part2.txt`](https://github.com/allengurney/gurney-genealogy/blob/main/sources/corpus/rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk-part2.txt) (each entry's regnal year is recoverable from the nearest preceding reign sub-header).

**Method note.** The calendar is arranged by hundred/place with a name-index at the back; the OCR mangles abbreviated surnames badly (the 1405 Robert Gurnay entry below is OCR-rendered "Eobert Qximay"), so these entries were recovered by **anchoring on the cleanly-printed place and party names** and by reading the **name-index head-forms** — not by a surname pattern alone. Asterisk (*) is Rye's deforciant mark; numbers are Rye's entry numbers.

## Part II — Edward II to Richard III (incl. Henry IV)

Rye groups the calendar by hundred/place, but each entry sits under a **regnal-year sub-header** ("9th Edward II," "8th Edward III," "23rd Henry VI," etc.), so the precise date of any entry is recoverable from the nearest preceding sub-header even though the entry numbers are not chronological. The regnal years below were read from those sub-headers.

- **#64 (Henry IV, 1405):** "Robert [Gu]rnay of Parva Cressyngham and Thomas Stodhagh v. *Edward Howard and Katherine his wife, in Parva Cressyngham and Hopton." The surname is OCR-mangled "Qximay"; recovered via Stodhagh / Cressingham / Hopton / Howard. The querent is identified only by residence, "of Parva [Little] Cressingham" (≈25 miles south of West Barsham); **no parentage recited**.
- **#489 (9 Edward II, 1315/16):** "John Gurnay, parson of the church of Harpele [Harpley], v. *Hugo, parson of P'va Massingham, and *Steph. de Estle, of the manor of Harpele and land in Geywode [and Wytton]. (Simon de Cresping and Margaret his wife app. clam.)" — a Gurnay cleric holding at Harpley (a West Barsham–line manor) in the reign of Edward II.
- **#200 (23 Henry VI, 1444/45):** "Nich. Bokkyng and John Aleyn of Castelacre v. *Thos. Gurnay, Armiger, and Margaret his wife, in Westlexham, Estlexham, Castelacre, Neuton, and Magna Dunham." — **Thomas Gurnay, esquire, and wife Margaret** (gentry; West/East Lexham, Castleacre, Newton, Great Dunham), 1444/45. The surrounding entries (#196 ff., 23 Hen VI) are a dense Fastolf–Paston Norfolk-gentry network (Oldhall, Fastolf, Yelverton, Heydon, Inglose, Calthorp, Clere); the querents Nich. Bokkyng and John Aleyn of Castleacre recur there as professional feoffees, so this is an enfeoffment-to-use settling the Gurnay West-Norfolk manors.
- **#262:** "John Wynter, John Yelverton, Rich. Creyk, and Thos. de Lexham v. *John Gournay and Alicia his wife, of the manors of Heylesdon and Drayton, and advowsons of the said manors." — John Gournay and **Alicia** (the Heylesdon settlement, Sir John Gurney V × Alice Heylesdon).
- **#1526:** "Edm. Gournay, William Pette parson of Haywardyn, Wm. Cursun, and John R[...] of Rougham v. *Wm. de Morle, Knight, of the manor of Hengham [Hingham]." — Edmund Gournay as a feoffee.
- **#146 (Edward II):** "*Willm. fil' Phi. de Gurnay and Elena his wife v. Ela who was the wife of John de Calveleye, in Reymerston. (John de Gornay app. clam.)"

**Candidate variant head-forms (verify at image level before treating as Gournay):**
- **#329 (8 Edward III, 1334/35):** "*Wm. Germye, Chivaler, and Elena his wife v. Galfr. de Boteler and Thos. de Felsham, in Therston [Tharston], Stratton juxta Therston, Forncett, and Wacton." — a knight ("Chivaler") **Wm. Germye** of Tharston with wife Elena, dated firmly to 1334/35. The 14th-century date is more than a century before the 15th-century Paston-correspondence "William Gurney esq. of Tharston," so this fine cannot be that man; the spelling "Germye" (with the early date and knightly rank) more likely belongs to the distinct Norfolk **Germy(e)** family of Tharston than to Gournay.
- **#432 (9 Edward III, 1335/36):** "*John de Mereworth and Margery his wife v. Rad. Gereneye, parson of the church of Mapelestope, of the manors of Saxthorp and Swatthyng. (John de Rokelound app. clam.)" — Ralph ("Rad.") Gereneye, parson, holding Saxthorpe (a Gournay manor) in 1335/36; the surname form "Gereneye" does not map cleanly onto the Gournay head-forms (Gurnay/Gornay/Gourney), so the Saxthorpe tie is the only Gournay signal and the identification stays uncertain.
- Read and **excluded** as distinct surnames: Thos. Geney (manor of Gunton — the Geney family) and John Gene (of Tilney).

## Part I — Richard I to Edward I

- **#236:** "*Emma de Herpelay [Harpley] v. Matthew de Gurnay, in Herpelay."
- **#837/838:** "*Simon de Creppinge and Matilda his wife v. John de Gurney, of the manor of Saxthorp."
- **#1593:** "*John de Gurney v. Wm. de Swatham, in Hardingham and Reymerston [Ravensholm mentioned]."
- **#1059:** "John de Gurnay v. *Sarra the wife of Roger de Kylby, of the manor of Hillington" (entail; remainder to Galfridus Wymer of Saham).
- **#148:** "William de Gurnai v. *Martin de Wertlai, in Burnham."
- **#504:** "*Mabil widow of Ludowic de Gurnay by Waren de [...]"
- Further: a Hugh de Gurney (in Cantley); "(Hugh de Gurnay app. clam.)" in Kineburgh and Carleton; "de Gornay and Katrine his wife v. *Henry de Habelund"; "Gurney and Havisia his wife, in Duneston."

Source: Walter Rye, *A Short Calendar of the Feet of Fines for Norfolk*, Parts I and II (Norwich: Goose, 1885–1886). Source ID: `rye-feet-of-fines-norfolk`. The underlying manuscript fines (TNA CP 25/1, Norfolk) carry the precise membranes and dating, with images at AALT.
