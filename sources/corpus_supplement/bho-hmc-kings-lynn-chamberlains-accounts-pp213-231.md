## Extracted research value

A continuous run of the Lenn (King's Lynn) borough Chamberlains' account abstracts, 1 Edward III to 4/5 Philip and Mary, followed by extracts from the Trinity Gild rolls. The 46–47 Edward III (1373–74) and 48–49 Edward III (1374–75) accounts record **Edmund Gurney/Gournay (G23)** twice: first as a guest of civic hospitality (wine spent on him in the Mayor's presence, alongside the Duke of Lancaster's steward Geoffrey de Folcham), then, the following year, retained for his counsel by the borough during a crisis — men banded together against the peace, some imprisoned, the Mayor's letter sent to Edmund for his advice, and a payment "for making a session for delivering the said prisoners." This is primary-account corroboration, independent of Blomefield and Daniel Gurney, of Edmund's documented role as legal counsel to the borough of Lynn in the mid-1370s.
