# Buckinghamshire Archives — Gurney entries in deeds and estate papers, 1570–1660

Extracts from the Buckinghamshire Archives online catalogue, searched 2026-07-27 with the date filter
1570–1660. Source ID: `bucks-archives-catalogue`.

**Variant coverage.** The catalogue is modern-catalogued and normalises the surname: `gurnay`, `gorney`,
`garney`, `gournay` and `gurnie` each return **zero** results in this window, while `gurney` returns the
full set. A variant sweep is therefore unnecessary here, and a `gurney` blank is a real blank rather than
a spelling artefact. The catalogue indexes descriptions, not document text, so a person named only inside
an unabstracted deed will not surface.

## The Bishopstone / Stone family — a gentry-rising line that sold up in 1649

**D-LE/2/31 — Fine, 1590.** "Stephen Gurney, junior, plaintiff. Antony Greneway, esquire, and Winifred
his wife, deforciants. 2 messuages, 1 dovecote, 120 acres land, 20 acres [meadow]…"

**D-BAS/49/91 — Deed poll, 25 November 1573 / 21 April 1574.** "…husbandman, for 'a certain sum of money'
assigning to **Stephen Gurney of Bishopstone, yeoman** the remaining term of years in the site of the
Manor of Stone by lease of 30 December 1549 (inherited by will…)"

**D-BAS/49/92 and D-BAS/49/94 — Deeds to lead the uses of a fine, 7 March 1598/9.** "…Common Pleas in
quindene of Trinity 1590 assured (a)-(f) to **Stephen Gurney of Bishopstone, younger son and heir
apparent of Stephen Gurney of Bishopstone, yeoman** — after the death of Stephen Grenewey…"

**D-LE/1/30 — Exemplification of Recovery, 14 June 1570.** "…Fletewoode, esquire, Mathew Gale,
gentleman, demandants. **Stephen Gurney, tenant.** John Howell, vouchee. 4 messuages, 4 cottages, 4 tofts,
2 dovecote, 4 gardens, 4 orchards, 200 acres land, 30 acres meadow…"

**D-LE/1/61 — Assignment, 22 February 1596/7.** "…residue of term of 21 years. Consideration: £7 to be
paid to **Stephen Gurney of Bishopstone**."

**D-LE/2/39 — Fine, 1626 (Michaelmas Term).** "**John Gurney, gentleman**, plaintiff. Robert Moore and
Susanna, his wife, deforciants. 1 messuage, 1 cottage, 2 gardens, 2 orchards, 14 acres land, 1 acre
meadow, 3 acres pasture in **Stone, Bishopstone, Great Kimble, and Marsh**."

**D-BAS/49/99 — Declaration of trust, 4 May 1649.** Parties: (1) Mary, Lady Croke, late of Water Stock,
Oxfordshire, widow; Sir Harbottle Grimstone of Bradfield, Essex, baronet; John Bennett of Grays Inn,
esquire; and John Fenner of Stone, yeoman. (2) Richard Ingoldsby of Hartwell, esquire, and Elizabeth his
wife, relict of Thomas Lee, esquire.

> Reciting: indenture of **1 March 1648–1649 in which (a) was conveyed by John Gurney, gentleman** to (1).
> Property: (a) **capital messuage in Bishopstone, in the parish of Stone**, with "divers cottages,
> closes, lands tenements and hereditaments" in Bishopstone and Stone, Hartwell, Great Kimble and
> Aylesbury.
> Habendum: to (2) for their lives for maintenance and education of the younger children of Thomas Lee;
> if any younger child of Thomas Lee paid **£1,700** to (1) then (1) would convey (a) to them.

**D-LE/2/50 — Fine, 1649 (Michaelmas Term).** "John Bennett, esquire, John Fenner, plaintiffs. **John
Gurney and Ann his wife, Edward Gurney, son and heir of John Gurney**, Edward Farmborough and Agnes his
wife, deforciants. 1 messuage, 2 cottages, 1 dovecote, 80 acres land, 15 acres meadow, 20 acres pasture,
1 acre wood and common pasture in **Stone, Bishopstone, Hartwell, Great Kimble and Aylesbury**."

**D-BAS/49/100 — Feoffment, 2 March 1648.** "…chief lord of the fee. Witnesses to delivery of seisin:
**Richard Gurney**; Thomas Fisher; Francis Henenel [in Latin]."

## The Owlswick / Monks Risborough family — a 1619 marriage settlement

**D-BAS/49/96a — Conveyance by feoffment, 20 September 1619.**

> Parties: (1) **John Gurney the elder of Owlswick, husbandman**; (2) Michaell Horton of Hartwell,
> yeoman and John White of Aston Sandford, husbandman.
> Operative part: (1), in consideration of **an impending marriage between John, son of (1), and Alice
> Hewett, daughter of John Gyles of The Marsh, yeoman**, enfeoffed (a)-(b) to (2).
> Property: (a) messuage with appurtenances in the occupation of (1) in **Owlswick** with a little close
> adjoining; (b) 3 half acres of arable belonging to (a) in the common fields of Owlswick.
> Habendum: to (2) to uses — half to use of **John Gurney the younger and Alice** for their lives,
> remainder to the heirs of (1) for ever; half to use of (1) and **Elizabeth, his wife**, for their
> lives, remainder to use of John Gurney the younger and Alice for their lives, remainder to the heirs
> of John Gurney the younger for ever.
> Witnesses: Jhon Mylles; John Pewtrace.

## Out of window, noted for provenance

**D-BAS/45/154** and **D-BASM/10/5** are 1945 correspondence and notes by **Frederick G. Gurney of
Claridges, Eggington, Bedfordshire**, an antiquary who transcribed and translated a medieval deed for
Aylesbury Museum and commented on a Great Brickhill court-roll fragment. **Ma/264/1** is a c.1660 map of
Moreton House, Dinton, surviving as a tracing "made by F. Gurney in 1932". These are twentieth-century
scholarly papers, not family records.
