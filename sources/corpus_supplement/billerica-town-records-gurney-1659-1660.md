# Billerica, Massachusetts town records 1653–1727 — John Gurney sweep (1659–1660)

Durable extract of the film-scoped Full-Text sweep of the Billerica manuscript town book for John Gurney, run 2026-06-12 (lead L-104). The town book is FamilySearch image group **DGS 007466228**; entries are machine-transcribed (verify wording against the image before quoting as verbatim). Source ID: `billerica-town-records-ms`. Dates use the town book's Old-Style month-number form (month 1 = March).

## Headline findings

- **The surrendered ten-acre lot was re-granted — half of it — to John Hall on 19 March 1659/60.** John Gurney was granted the ten-acre house-lot 15 August 1659 on condition he inhabit it (with his son Richard accepted as a fellow inhabitant); he surrendered it 26 December 1659 (Daniel Shead "in answer for his father John Gurney"). Image 15 then records: "19.1.60 day the Towne Doe grant to John Hall one halfe of the Lot yt was granted to John Gurney, it being divided and he to have his choice of the two halus when it is divided … the Towne hath give the said John hall : untill to morrow fortnight to give his aust [answer] for his exceptence or refusall of the said grant." No recipient of the second half appears in the sweep.
- **John Gurney had a substantial Billerica footprint, not merely a surrendered grant.** He appears in the proprietors' lot-draw list ("John Gurney I", image 12), with a house valuation of £3 and town charges of 10s 7d (image 13), a rate of £2 5s 10d (image 14), a 56-acre layout recited in the surrender entry (image 173), and **two great-meadow lots** — 4¼ acres "beging at the bonds of James Paterson" (image 179) and 4½ acres "the second Lott in order, which begins at the North corner of the month [mouth] of hors Brooke … bounded with John Rogers South" (image 180). These persist independently of the surrendered house-lot.
- **"Gurney Tanner" is a dead OCR garble of "Towne Charges."** Image 214's "John Gurney Tanne Charges -of-10" is the same accounting list that image 13 renders cleanly as "John Gurney : Towne Charges of -10-7." No tanner occupation attaches to any Billerica Gurney.
- **The Shead–Gurney tie recurs.** Daniel Shed sits in the proprietors list near John Gurney (image 12); "Sheads corner" is a survey landmark (image 171); Daniel Shead answers "for his father John Gurney" at the surrender (image 173); a Nathan Shead is still a town assessor in 1707 (image 570).

## Case relevance (G13 John Gurney emigrant)

The grantee John Gurney with a "son Richard" of settling age in 1659 matches G13 and his son Richard G12 (b. c. 1630–34); Daniel Shead's calling John "father" is the Shed–Gurney family tie no compiled source states. John surrendered the house-lot within four months and never relocated from Braintree (he sold the Braintree freehold Feb 1661/2 and died 1662/3), but the meadow/upland allocations and the proprietor/rate entries show a real, if brief, Billerica interest rather than a bare aborted application. The "Gurney Tanner" reading is dead.
